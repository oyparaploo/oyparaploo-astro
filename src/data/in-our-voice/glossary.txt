**It Has To Be a Title For Something.** The making insists before there is a piece, before there is a shape or a subject or even a first sentence — the insistence that this thing exists and must be called something, not named but called, the way you call someone in from the yard, the title arriving before the work the way the body arrives before the mind, and the period at the end is not punctuation, it is a foot put down. So begin with a **Sound Guide**, because everything the practice makes is organized by sound before meaning, the ellipses as rests, the caps as dynamics, the parenthetical whisper as a change of register the way a singer drops from chest voice to head voice mid-phrase and you feel it in your own throat before you understand what changed — listen first, the meaning is in the rhythm, the rhythm is in the body, follow the body and you will arrive at **Para Podcasts**, beside the podcast, next to the conversation but not inside it, the prefix carrying the whole philosophy, para as parallel, para as beyond, para as the thing that exists alongside the official version and does not ask permission, parachute, paranormal, paraploo, the practice having always worked beside the main event, in the margins of the dominant conversation, **Finding Favourites** the way you find a stone on a beach that fits your palm without your hand knowing it was looking, Brown Paint not auditioning but arriving, the hand recognizing it, the body selecting before the mind has organized its criteria, which is how every favourite has ever been found by anyone who was paying attention with their hands rather than with their list, the British spelling staying because the u is part of it.

And once the favourites have been found you **Stroll Through Drive In(s)**, walking where you were meant to drive, the wrong speed for the architecture, the parenthetical s making the plural uncertain the way forty-seven wobbles between committed and noticed, slowing down inside a structure designed for speed, which is what the practice does to every genre it enters, watching **Films About Not Lecturing** — trust the reader, do not explain, show the thing and leave the room — and somewhere nearby someone is debating whether to **Kill The President or** accept the **Clown's Illogical Motivations**, the violent option and the absurd option, and the practice chooses both because the two are the same thing wearing different hats and because choosing between them is a luxury the practice refuses, the way Brown Paint refuses to choose between grief and reporting, the way the puppet theater refuses to choose between political critique and physical beauty, the way forty-seven mannequin children holding toy guns at two in the morning in Beirut refuse to choose between unblinking witness and bureaucratic absurdism, the "or" in the title being the membrane, and the membrane was never there.

Meanwhile **Carl Arthur Doing ( What a character! )**, a person named and then admired in a whispered aside, the verb present tense, continuous, unfinished, someone nearby unable to contain their delight, the parenthetical being the audience, the stage direction leaking into the title the way the conversation leaked into Super Flu Vessel, Carl Arthur not knowing he is being watched, the watching being the piece, and the piece taking place in **Our Naked Urban Wilderness To The End** — ours, not mine, the first word inviting the reader onto the porch before the sentence has even told the reader what kind of wilderness this is, and the wilderness is urban and naked, stripped of the romantic and the pastoral and the lie that wilderness means away from people, this wilderness being Saint Paul at thirty below, Somali language in a hallway, Ojibwe in the name Mesabi that the mines took without asking, baobab roots under frozen Minneapolis ground, the naked city that does not hide what it carries and does not stop carrying it when the subject gets uncomfortable, to the end meaning exactly what it says. The **Skinny Sardines** don't care either way — **Whatever**, small, packed tight, silver, the shrug after the naming, both gestures true, the title crossing between pieces without explaining how it got from one room to the other, the practice naming the thing precisely and then shrugging, the shrug carrying as much weight as the naming in a **Sah Bow Universe** where the mouth makes sounds that mean what the sounds feel like rather than what a dictionary assigns, sah as exhale, as release, as putting something down gently enough that it does not break, bow as bend, as respect, as the thing drawn across a string to make it sing, universe as everything the mouth can hold, which turns out to be more than the mind can organize, bahku bahku, oy orilla, da da dada da, the invented language being the first language, the methodology coming later to give it English words, but the mouth already working.

**Would you have an interest in looking at another piece?** The conversation entering the art, not quoted or referenced but absorbed, metabolized, indistinguishable from the stream it entered, the question asked in a real conversation becoming a title, the title becoming a piece, the piece containing the question, the question still expecting an answer, yes, send it, because the **Cellular Underground Big Crack Of Wonderous Light** is happening beneath the surface of everything — cellular as the smallest unit of the living thing, underground where mycelium runs and root-government operates and the connections between the five witnesses and the three layers and the twenty-eight domains travel without being seen by anyone who is not looking for them, big crack as the moment the methodology breaks and the light gets in the way light always gets in when something that was trying too hard to be complete finally admits it is not, wonderous misspelled by the 4 AM mouth because the 4 AM mouth does not spell, it speaks, and the imperfection staying because the imperfection is the evidence that a body was here, making, before the critical mind arrived to correct anything, and the **Mission** is **Do Not Control Them**, an instruction to the mission itself, the mission's purpose being to not control, put the piece in the world and do not control what the world does with it, the gap in the circle being exactly this, and someone glancing through it says **I've been glancing through this ... and it's a gorgeous read!**, the reader arriving, not theoretical but actual, the ellipsis being the pause between glancing and judgment, the eye still moving and the opinion not yet formed, and then the exclamation sudden and warm, nine words, the piece reaching someone and the someone saying it out loud.

Look backward with excitement — **Exciting Insights Retro Spectacle**, the practice looking back through 140,000 files and finding the insights already there, Middle Eye and Pressure Ghosts and the heart-soul-head hierarchy already running in documents written years before the methodology existed, the spectacle being the looking itself, the excitement real, and from this looking a **Child Moonlight Chorus** emerges, Hahnu Youn counting leg bounces in light she did not arrange, multiple voices not harmonized or conducted but simultaneous, children seeing what the adults have learned to overlook, the work happening at 4 AM in the hours the daylight mind has surrendered, the chorus needing no conductor, the moonlight needing no permission. And the **DNA** of all of it — **( Dakota Nature Artist )** — whose land, right there inside the acronym, Dakota the people whose name for this place means the water that reflects the sky, the people whose presence predates every map on every wall of every gallery in Minnesota, nature not as wilderness or landscape but as the word that includes the human in the living system rather than separating the human from it the way the colony separated the human from the land in order to take the land, artist as the one who attends rather than invents, three words inside three letters, the parenthetical opening the door onto a **New World** that contains five hundred years of violence and the morning after, also tomorrow, both, the practice holding the history and the possibility simultaneously because refusing either one is a lie.
