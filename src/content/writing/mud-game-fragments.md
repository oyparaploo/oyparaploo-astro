Numbers are you game? ... Night panther backs up ... Wet mud salt ... Of sand and time ... Tinker ... Whoa mud yellow orange ... Rise and fall stinking joker ... The child returns quaffing dirty suds ... All together against laughing wet weed ... Never urgent or desperate soil ... Mud at the sea's edge ... Curved palette lines of all shades ... An artist's cliff slides ... Skinned beaded mind what do you have in the game. What absolutes did you invest in this mire.
