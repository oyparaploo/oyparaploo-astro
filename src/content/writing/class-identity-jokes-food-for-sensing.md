There is something quietly honest about the way food exposes class ... The things we ate growing up ... that we later learned were Poor Food or Fancy Food ... depending on who was encountering it ... The meal made from whatever was in a tipi or pantry ... Specific shame or pride attached to assimilation boarding school lunch or western world noon dinner ... Sensing instead of just analyzing with thoughts what feels true ... Class is not something we think our way into understanding first ... It is in the human body ... That hesitation ... The way we hold a tool or utensil ... Whether we immediately don't or do know everything about sacred bread

Seek that special ability shift on the spot and open completely ... Understand more about how class intersects with the lightness and darkness of being ... It is about whose knowledge counts ... regarding whether a certain food is wrong ... primitive ... and dirty or ... on the other hand ... a cut above everything middle class ... more refined ... and exclusively clean ... As also abstract fragmentary ellipses ... Breathing like sharp or foggy memory ... Inversions doing something different ... Sweet confidence of not knowing leading ... Assumed knowing of dominant colonial culture exposed as assumption ... Ink (elegant blood, crude oil) for pages and intuitive speaking for this Oyparaploo voice ...

Para parenthetical ... doing its own work ... Extraction ... What gets pressed out of something ... Living to make a hundred or more definitive marks ... that add up to be an individual or collective legacy ... Holding ... Race ... Class ... and ... Spirit ... all at once without preaching ... For an example ~ A human or animal (wild domesticated hybrid) body that walks into a room and is studied before ... she ... they ... he ... it ... even makes a sound before eating or drinking something ... The wild animal butcher's language is hidden in his aspiration ... We rank food the way we rank flesh ... Choice or not ... Grading system that only pretends to be neutral ... We ... Oyparaploo ... also find our medium in the silent mouth everyone now dating back to ancient times can see from where they are ... Ten second pause ... We all know which foods are jokes ... Ones we apologize for serving ... Ones we lie about ... Ones we pretend to know how to pronounce ... Bodies are punchlines belonging to something ...

The off cut asides carry the weight ... Stage direction living inside the text ... A middle eye reading alone ... learned to see both ways ... That is where ownership lands ... where membership becomes the joke ... We laugh at the body caught not knowing because we have been that body ... Relief when it is not us this time ...

Reading happens before taste ... before thought ... that sensing ... Class recognized in three seconds ... Not poverty exactly ... not extreme privilege ... but exposure ... The moment someone sees what you actually eat ...

Set up a split ... mind over here and body over there ... then affect both ... Eating is psychological ... Something chewing from the inside ... Worries ... Something we can't stop thinking about ... Verbs applied to our mind's troubles ... Bring it back to survival ... The question of ... precarity ... class ... access ... What sustains us ... What we depend on? Class ... food ... shame ... and knowledge ... live in the body before the mind is able to catch up ...

You can tell Class by how long someone stares at a menu ... The Wealthy decide fast because they have had it all many times ... People experiencing and from Poverty also decide fast because they already know what the cheapest items are ... People in the Middle need a minute or two because they are discussing and calculating what they can afford ...

Our people's food does not photograph well ... It is the color of getting by ... Brown ... beige and the grayness of stretching something into next week ... Instagram wants bold contrast ...

Eating hasn't been taken as a serious enough topic ... Maybe because it implicates everyone ... It is difficult to maintain a distance when it is dissolving on the tongue in our mouth ... Seeing lets us stand apart from what we observe ... We can close our eyes, and the world is still there ... waiting for when we open them again ... We cannot close our mouths and continue tasting something ... Eating is also sound ... The crunch ... the quietness of soft bread ... It is ... touch ... temperature ... texture ... the weight of a soup spoon ... Eating uses every sense ... Hierarchies need a top and a bottom ... Someone has to lose ... The senses we can share at a distance get promoted ... sight ... hearing ... and oddly the ones that require more closeness get demoted ... Intimacy becomes too suspicious ... Food is ethereal ... a strange word for something so material ... But it's true ... food is always becoming something else ... Becoming your identity ... Becoming ... after enough chewing ... gone ... We cannot revisit a meal the way we can revisit a painting ... Memory has to do all the work ... And memory ... like food ... changes every time ... It is perishable ... So is the body ... Food is thought about in the most serious terms ... by the people who do not have enough of it ... Philosophizing is easier on a full stomach ...
