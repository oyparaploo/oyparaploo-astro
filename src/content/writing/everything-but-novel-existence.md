December 15th, 2019. I was making my disappearance notes—yes, I keep a journal of everything that vanishes. Started with small things. Paper clips. The good pen. The ability to see faces without calculating survival odds. Then bigger: glaciers (faster than expected), species (Latin names like funeral hymns), the will to get out of bed (Tuesday through Friday). But that day, I noted: "Mrs. Chen from 4B disappeared." Thought she'd died. The way she watered plants suggested someone saying lengthy goodbyes. Turns out she'd moved to Phoenix to become a DJ at 87. Sometimes disappearances are just transformations with bad communication. Sometimes death is just life wearing a disguise so good it fools even itself.

From beyond, I can confirm: death smells like pool chlorine and birthday cake. Plan accordingly. Bring snacks to funerals. The living get hungry watching the dead not eat.
