**Visitor's Clarity (Revised)**

*"A visitor's witnessing"*
_________________

We arrived to find you all 
secretly googling "is it normal to cry about everything"
at 3 AM, finding out yes, 
everyone else is barely holding on too—

how your cells remember being ocean
*why you feel pulled by tides*
how your bones hum calcium psalms
*why you ache before storms*
how every grief you've carried 
has already grown wings
*you just can't feel them yet*

We watch you weep for endings
while your lungs perform their miracle,
*in and out, even when you forget to notice*
trading molecules with trees
who learned their breathing from the same first wind
*the one that taught everything alive how to continue*

Loved ones, friends, your sorrow is composting
into tomorrow's blackberries
*sweetness from shit, as always*
Your grandfather's last whisper
lives in your daughter's laugh
*she has his exact way of tilting her head*

From where we come from, we could see it—
how light bends around your sadness
making halos you can't witness,
*like how everyone else sees your strength but you*
how your tears prism into rainbow
*though all you feel is the falling*

Here inside your aching,
swimming in your same bewildered love,
*checking your phone for signs that anyone understands*
we still carry our visitor's eyes:
You are all becoming butterflies.

You just call it breaking
when your wings push through
*because transformation feels like dying from the inside*
