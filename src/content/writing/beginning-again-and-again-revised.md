**Beginning Again, and Again (Revised)**

*"A visitor's witnessing"*
_________________

Someone told me they've quit drinking seventeen times. "Eighteen starts tomorrow." *They laughed but weren't joking.* This is what beginning again looks like - admitting the last beginning didn't take.

Knowledge holders carry methods not answers. *Like having recipes but no ingredients, instructions but no tools.* They investigate what needs birthing. *Midwifing ideas that don't want to be born.* Helping communities include the harm that was caused. *Making room at tables for uncomfortable truths.*

Most rituals acknowledge something. *Cosmic accounting I don't understand.* Shadow withheld when brightness came. *The receipt for light is darkness.* Grief waited at edges while love took center stage. *Patient as understudies who know the lead will fall.*

Grief overflows every container. *Like trying to Marie Kondo trauma.* Thinking you can frame it, contain it? *Like organizing the ocean into file folders.* Won't work.

Violation enables fresh starts. *Not punishment but weather patterns.* This allows beginning again. Humans growing familiar with starting over. *New year's resolutions in October. Monday as weekly resurrection.* Even ceremonial forms crack so life can grow. *Traditions breaking so truth can breathe.*

We leave grief behind thinking connection risks trust. *Like emotional credit scores we're protecting.* Something needs forgiving for movement. *Maybe forgiveness just means letting rivers flow where they want.*

Someone died and saw what death means to love. *I don't understand this but feel it matters.* Bodies protect by closing. *Careful accounting of old wounds.* Feel if we forgive we betray ourselves. *The body keeps receipts for every hurt.*

But bodies need healing to create. *Moving from fortress to garden, bunker to dance floor.* Creative force developing through wounds not despite them. *Scar tissue stronger than original skin.*

Under innocent spiritual goodness, beginning again isn't failure. *It's the most honest thing we do.* Each betrayal, grief, forgiveness - they're doors. *Some open, some close, some revolve eternally.*

Witnessing how you practice beginning. *Delete dating apps. Redownload. Delete again.* Not because you failed but because that's what living things do. *They begin, and begin, and begin.*

Still learning starting over might be the point. *Still discovering everyone's on their third lifetime in this one body.* Still witnessing courage in Monday morning's coffee.
