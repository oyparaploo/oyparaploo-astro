Dig ... hands go into ground ... simplest passage making act there is ... No engineering ... Just pressure downward into granular material that gives way ... Archaeological excavation ... ground opened because something is buried ... A dig is a remark that cuts through someone's surface ...

Ode to ... the dedication ... The poem of praise addressed directly to its subject ... But placed inside a passage list ... the ode itself becomes a threshold ... The praise is how you enter ... "Ode to" without a period ... the praise hasn't finished naming what it praises ... It's the address before the noun arrives ...

Cambium ... the living layer inside a tree between the bark and the heartwood ... One cell thick in places ... Everything the tree becomes passes through the cambium ... New wood grows inward from it ... New bark grows outward from it ... It is the thinnest passage in all of biology and everything on either side of it depends on it being alive ... If the cambium dies ... the tree is dead ... The bark was the last ground ... Now we're one layer deeper ...

Barnacle ... the crustacean that cements itself to whatever it touches and never lets go ... It builds its own shell on someone else's surface ... It filters water through itself to feed ... The barnacle is a passage that attached to a passage ... it rides hulls ... rocks ... pilings ... whales ... It goes where the thing it gripped goes ...

Crust ... the outermost layer ... Earth's crust ... Bread's crust ... The wound's crust ... What forms on the surface when the inside meets the outside and hardens ... The crust is what the passage has to break through or what the passage left behind ... Every crust was once an open surface that sealed ...

Together ... hands dig through the crust ... past the barnacles that cemented themselves to the surface ... and arrive at the cambium ... the living layer one cell thick where everything is made ... The ode is addressed to that layer ... Not to the bark ... Not to the heartwood ... To the cambium ... the passage itself ... the thinnest membrane where growth happens in both directions ...

The last ground held bark ... This one breaks through it ... The dig praises what's underneath the crust ... the layer so thin it barely exists and so essential that without it nothing grows ...

Falaj ... the ancient water channel system of Oman and the Arabian Peninsula ... Water carried from mountain springs through underground tunnels and above-ground channels to settlements in the desert ... Gravity does the work ... No pump ... No engine ... The water moves because the land tilts and someone centuries ago understood the tilt ... The passage is the water's path and the community lives because the passage exists ...

Fogón ... the hearth ... Latin American Spanish ... The cooking fire built into a structure of clay or stone ... The center of the kitchen ... Everything that feeds the household passes through the fogón ... It is where raw becomes cooked ... where cold becomes warm ... Also the firebox of a locomotive ... the chamber where fuel burns to move the whole train ... Passage through fire ...

Also the sound a dog makes ... the sharpest alarm an animal can give ... Three passages in one word ... protection ... vessel ... warning ...

Hold ... the cargo space below the deck of a ship ... Where everything is carried in darkness ... Also the verb ... to hold ... to keep ... to carry without letting go ... Also a pause ... hold ... Wait ... Stay where you are ... The passage stops and the stopping is the passage ...

Together ... water moves by gravity through an ancient channel to a hearth where fire makes raw things edible inside a warm-blooded body wrapped in bark that is also a boat carrying a hold full of what hasn't been let go ... The falaj feeds the fogón ... The fogón feeds the mammal ... The mammal is wrapped in bark ... The bark is the vessel ... The hold is what the vessel carries in the dark ...

This is the ground where the practice feeds itself ... The water found its way without a pump ... The fire was always in the kitchen ... The body carried what it carried ... The bark kept it alive ... And below the deck ... the hold ... still holding ...
