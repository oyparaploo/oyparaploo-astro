### Characters

**ZIEGLER** - 40s, the Unchanged witness, carries a notebook everywhere  
**BAIK** - 30s, transforms between forms, becoming the settlement itself  
**WOOZY PUPIL** - 7-8, child who sings matter into consciousness  
**GRANDMOTHER** - 70s, appears in memory/vision, voice of ancient wisdom  
**CHEN** - 30s, refugee from the floods, carries perpetual grief  
**ANYA** - 40s, prophet who fled from tomorrow  
**KAI** - 20s, storm meteorologist reading impossible weather  
**CHORUS OF WALLS** - The settlement itself, speaking in unison or fragments  
**THE UNCHANGED** - Seventeen stubborn humans refusing transformation  

### Setting

Tomorrow's Edge settlement, multiple levels descending into earth. Time is fluid—past, present, and future bleed together. The stage should suggest depth through platforms, with projections showing the settlement's organic architecture.

---

## ACT ONE

### Scene 1: Surface

*(Dawn. Three wooden poles stand upstage, each with a songbird perched—two real, one clearly dead but positioned as if alive. ZIEGLER stands at a simple table, writing. The walls breathe visibly—slow expansion and contraction of the set itself.)*

**ZIEGLER:** *(To audience)* Three songbirds left. I count them each morning, though counting suggests they'll stay counted. Yesterday five. Tomorrow... *(Shrugs)* Time flows like water through hands—you feel it but can't hold it.

*(BAIK enters, her movement fluid yet stuttering, as if frame-jumping through animation. She shifts between forms—sometimes upright, sometimes crawling, sometimes almost flying.)*

**BAIK:** The walls asked me something today.

**ZIEGLER:** The walls ask you something every day.

**BAIK:** No, they usually answer. Today they asked: "What were songbirds?"

**ZIEGLER:** *(Writing)* And what did you tell them?

**BAIK:** I couldn't remember. *(Pause)* Ziegler, what happens when we forget what we're forgetting?

**ZIEGLER:** We become something else.

**BAIK:** Or nothing.

**ZIEGLER:** That's also something.

*(GRANDMOTHER appears in a pool of light, separate from the main action. Only BAIK sees her.)*

**GRANDMOTHER:** Child, you're reaching in eight directions again.

**BAIK:** *(To Grandmother)* Each arm wants something different.

**GRANDMOTHER:** Like an octopus grasping for eight different deaths. Eight different gods who might not exist.

**BAIK:** What if they all exist?

**GRANDMOTHER:** Then you'll need more arms.

*(GRANDMOTHER fades. WOOZY PUPIL enters, dragging her hand along the wall. Where she touches, the wall illuminates briefly.)*

**WOOZY PUPIL:** I taught the concrete to breathe last night.

**ZIEGLER:** What does concrete breathe?

**WOOZY PUPIL:** Memories of when it was mountain.

### Scene 2: The Service Road

*(Lights shift. BAIK collapses center stage, her form indeterminate—neither human nor other. ZIEGLER finds her.)*

**ZIEGLER:** *(Approaching cautiously)* Baik?

**BAIK:** *(Voice layered, multiple tones)* I'm between. Can't... decide... what...

**ZIEGLER:** The transformation sickness.

**BAIK:** Not sick. Just... all possibilities at once. It hurts. It's beautiful. It's...

*(ZIEGLER pulls out a canteen, kneels beside her.)*

**ZIEGLER:** Water. Still works the old way. Down the throat, into the body.

**BAIK:** *(Drinking)* Why do you stay unchanged?

**ZIEGLER:** Someone has to remember the before.

**BAIK:** What if the before was just another after?

**ZIEGLER:** *(Placing a rose beside her)* Then I'm keeping track of circles.

**BAIK:** *(Transforming, rising)* Your rose... its thorns grow inward.

**ZIEGLER:** Or outward. Depends on perspective.

**BAIK:** Everything does. *(Stronger now)* I'll be all the answers, Ziegler. You be the question.

**ZIEGLER:** I thought I was the memory.

**BAIK:** Same thing.

### Scene 3: The Descent

*(The stage transforms—platforms emerge showing depth. CHEN enters, soaking wet despite no rain. He's always dripping.)*

**CHEN:** The water follows me inside now.

**ANYA:** *(Entering with bottles of strange liquid)* Because you carry the flood in your lungs.

**CHEN:** My partner still drowns in my dreams. Every night, same flood, same reaching, same failing.

**ANYA:** In tomorrow—where I come from—the floods reach the mountains.

**ZIEGLER:** How do you flee from tomorrow?

**ANYA:** By walking backward through today. *(Offers bottle to CHEN)* Sacred water. From before water learned to drown.

**CHEN:** *(Drinking)* It tastes like... forgetting?

**ANYA:** Forgetting is a kind of healing.

*(KAI bursts in with makeshift weather equipment.)*

**KAI:** The barometric pressure is impossible! It's reading emotions now—grief at 30.2, falling. Hope holding steady at denial!

**BAIK:** *(To walls)* Is god the weather or the weatherman?

**CHORUS OF WALLS:** Yes.

### Scene 4: Memory Cathedral

*(Lights shift to suggest great depth. The stage fills with overlapping voices—the CHORUS OF WALLS speaking memories.)*

**CHORUS OF WALLS:** 
—remember when birds meant morning—
—remember when morning meant beginning—
—remember when beginning wasn't ending—
—remember when we could forget—

**WOOZY PUPIL:** *(To audience)* I found the frequency that makes concrete remember it was mountain. Mountains remember they were ocean floor. Ocean floor remembers it was star dust. Star dust remembers...

**BAIK:** Nothing. Star dust remembers nothing. That's its freedom.

**GRANDMOTHER:** *(Reappearing)* Women who survive must be unfaithful to their child-selves.

**BAIK:** You taught me that.

**GRANDMOTHER:** No, child. You taught yourself. I'm just the voice you gave the lesson.

---

## ACT TWO

### Scene 1: The Unchanged Quarter

*(Deep below. Seventeen actors representing THE UNCHANGED stand in tableau. ZIEGLER addresses them.)*

**ZIEGLER:** Report. Day 4,017 of refusing transformation.

**UNCHANGED 1:** Sarah tried to change yesterday. Her skin started scales.

**UNCHANGED 2:** We held her down. Forced her to remember her name.

**UNCHANGED 3:** She's Sarah again. Mostly.

**UNCHANGED 4:** Her dreams have gills now.

**ZIEGLER:** *(Writing)* Transformation creeps in through dreams. Note taken.

**UNCHANGED 5:** Why do we resist?

**ZIEGLER:** Because someone must. Because if everyone becomes everything, then nothing means anything.

**BAIK:** *(Voice from walls)* Or everything means everything.

**THE UNCHANGED:** *(In unison)* The walls are speaking again.

**BAIK:** *(Through walls)* I'm not the walls. I'm becoming them. Different thing.

**UNCHANGED 6:** What's it like?

**BAIK:** Like being a thought thinking itself. Like being the question and the answer and the silence between.

### Scene 2: The Storm Prophet

*(Surface level. KAI has set up elaborate equipment. ANYA watches.)*

**KAI:** Tomorrow's forecast: impossible sunshine after mandatory storms.

**ANYA:** You're reading my yesterday.

**KAI:** Your yesterday is our tomorrow?

**ANYA:** Time isn't a line, boy. It's a web. I'm the spider who got tangled.

**KAI:** The barometer says you don't exist.

**ANYA:** Exactly. That's how I escaped tomorrow—by not existing in it.

*(CHEN enters, still dripping.)*

**CHEN:** The water's rising in my dreams. Soon I'll drown awake.

**ANYA:** Then learn to breathe water.

**CHEN:** I can't transform. The grief keeps me solid.

**BAIK:** *(Entering, now part-wall, part-human)* Grief is just love with nowhere to go.

**CHEN:** Then I'm full of love with bad directions.

### Scene 3: The Teaching

*(WOOZY PUPIL stands center stage, hands on the floor. The stage itself begins to pulse with her heartbeat.)*

**WOOZY PUPIL:** Feel that? I'm teaching the building to have a pulse.

**ZIEGLER:** Buildings don't need heartbeats.

**WOOZY PUPIL:** Everything needs rhythm. Even death has a beat. *(Singing)* 
*Concrete, remember mountain*
*Mountain, remember ocean*
*Ocean, remember starlight*
*Starlight, remember nothing*

*(The walls begin to glow and shift.)*

**CHORUS OF WALLS:** We... remember... we... forget... we... remember... forgetting...

**BAIK:** *(Now more wall than woman)* I'm spreading through the foundation. Each room is a different thought. The basement is sadness. The attic is next Tuesday.

**ZIEGLER:** What's the kitchen?

**BAIK:** The last place I was completely human. It refuses to change.

### Scene 4: Night Visions

*(Darkness except for pools of light. Each character speaks from their own light.)*

**GRANDMOTHER:** The world ends not with bang or whimper, but with transformation.

**CHEN:** Every night I save him. Every night I fail.

**ANYA:** In tomorrow, we've already had this conversation. It ends badly.

**KAI:** The weather says we're inside a metaphor that's inside a storm that's inside someone's dream.

**WOOZY PUPIL:** I can hear the concrete crying. It misses being mountain.

**THE UNCHANGED:** *(Chanting)* We remain. We remain. We remain.

**ZIEGLER:** I write it all down, but the words keep changing. "Love" becomes "loss" becomes "less" becomes "lesson."

**BAIK:** *(Voice everywhere)* Does god exist?

**CHORUS OF WALLS:** 
- God is the question
- You are becoming the answer  
- We are god forgetting ourselves
- [SILENCE]
- [STATIC]
- Yes/No/Maybe/Always/Never

---

## ACT THREE

### Scene 1: The Collapse

*(The stage is fragmented—platforms at angles, walls leaning. Time is breaking.)*

**ZIEGLER:** *(To audience)* Three songbirds remain. Or is it five? Was it ever any number? Numbers assume separation. Assume things can be counted. Assume...

*(A songbird falls from its pole. WOOZY PUPIL catches it.)*

**WOOZY PUPIL:** It's still warm.

**ZIEGLER:** Death is warm at first.

**WOOZY PUPIL:** *(Singing to the bird)* 
*Little song, little silence*
*Little death, little dance*
*Transform to sound*
*Transform to memory*
*Transform to chance*

*(The bird dissolves into light in her hands.)*

**BAIK:** *(Now completely integrated with the set)* I can feel every room. Every person is a feeling in my walls. You're all inside me now.

**CHEN:** The flood finally stopped.

**ANYA:** Because you're drowning in air now. Evolution.

**CHEN:** Or death.

**ANYA:** Same thing, different speed.

### Scene 2: The Recognition

*(GRANDMOTHER appears to all, not just BAIK.)*

**GRANDMOTHER:** Time to choose.

**THE UNCHANGED:** We choose to remain!

**GRANDMOTHER:** That's a choice to change slowly. Still change.

**UNCHANGED 7:** Then what's the point?

**GRANDMOTHER:** The point is the choosing. The only tragedy is having change chosen for you.

**BAIK:** I chose to become everything.

**GRANDMOTHER:** No, child. You chose to stop choosing. Different thing.

**BAIK:** I am the walls. I am the settlement. I am home and exile, question and answer.

**GRANDMOTHER:** You're a child who learned to swim by becoming the ocean.

### Scene 3: The Weather Report

*(KAI stands with his equipment sparking and failing.)*

**KAI:** Final forecast: Everything simultaneously. Sun and rain, winter and summer, birth and death, all at once, forever, starting now.

**ZIEGLER:** That's not weather. That's existence.

**KAI:** Same thing. I've been reading existence this whole time. The barometer was just... translation.

*(The equipment explodes softly, releasing butterflies made of light.)*

**ANYA:** In tomorrow, those butterflies cause hurricanes.

**KAI:** In yesterday, they're still caterpillars.

**WOOZY PUPIL:** Right now, they're both. And neither. And beautiful.

### Scene 4: The Last Change

*(The stage is now completely fluid—walls moving, breathing, speaking. All characters are present.)*

**ZIEGLER:** *(Writing furiously)* I can't keep up. Everything's changing faster than words.

**BAIK:** Then stop writing. Start witnessing.

**ZIEGLER:** Same thing.

**BAIK:** Not anymore.

**CHEN:** The water in my lungs turned to air. The air turned to light. The light turned to... *(Begins glowing)* Oh.

**ANYA:** He's becoming unstuck from tragedy.

**THE UNCHANGED:** *(Beginning to shift and change despite themselves)* We... we remain... we... are... remaining... differently...

**WOOZY PUPIL:** *(To the walls)* Wake up! Wake up! Remember you were mountains! Remember you were stars!

**CHORUS OF WALLS:** We... remember... everything... nothing... the difference... disappears...

**GRANDMOTHER:** This is how worlds end. Not with destruction, but transformation so complete that ending and beginning become the same word.

**BAIK:** Ziegler, you're the last unchanged.

**ZIEGLER:** *(Still writing)* Someone has to document the ending.

**BAIK:** What if it's not ending? What if it's just changing so much it looks like ending?

**ZIEGLER:** *(Pen beginning to shift into something else)* Then someone has to... to...

*(His pen becomes a flower. He stares at it.)*

**ZIEGLER:** Three songbirds remain.

**ALL:** Three songbirds remain.

**WOOZY PUPIL:** *(Singing)* Or five. Or none. Or the number that can't be counted because counting means separation and we're not separate anymore.

*(The lights begin to pulse with heartbeat rhythm. The walls glow. Everything is transforming.)*

**BAIK:** *(Voice everywhere and nowhere)* Does god exist?

**ALL:** We are becoming the answer.

**BAIK:** What's the answer?

**ALL:** The question.

*(Lights fade to black except for three points of light where the songbirds were/are/will be.)*

**ZIEGLER:** *(In darkness)* Tomorrow's Edge. Today's Middle. Yesterday's Beginning. All the same place. All different. All changing.

*(The three lights merge into one, then explode into countless points of light like stars.)*

**WOOZY PUPIL:** *(Singing in darkness)*
*We were sound*
*We were silence*
*We were then*
*We are now*
*We are changing*
*We are changed*
*We are change itself*
*Somehow*

*(Blackout.)*

---

## Epilogue

*(A single light on ZIEGLER, no longer unchanged—he flickers between forms.)*

**ZIEGLER:** *(To audience)* These fragments were found in my throat. Or my memory. Or the walls of Tomorrow's Edge. Or all three. Or none. The story changes depending on who's telling it, who's hearing it, what form they're in when they encounter it.

Three songbirds remain. 

Or everything is songbirds.

Or nothing ever was.

Welcome to Tomorrow's Edge, where the only constant is the question, and the only question is: What are we becoming?

*(He transforms into light, then darkness, then silence.)*

**END**

### Production Notes

**Set Design:** The set should be organic and transformable—walls that breathe, platforms that shift, suggesting both architecture and organism. Projections can show the settlement's depths and the characters' transformations.

**Lighting:** Should suggest depth, transformation, and time fluidity. Each character might have their own light quality—Ziegler in stark white, Baik in shifting colors, Chen in blue-green suggesting water, etc.

**Sound:** Ambient sounds of breathing walls, distant songbirds, water, and transformation. Woozy Pupil's songs should feel both ancient and futuristic.

**Costumes:** Should suggest transformation—layers that can be removed or added, fabrics that suggest both human and other, pieces that can be shared between actors to show collective identity.

**Movement:** Choreographed transformation sequences, especially for Baik. The Unchanged should move as a unit until they begin to individuate. The Chorus of Walls might be dancers integrated into the set itself.

**Casting Note:** Baik should be played by an actor comfortable with extreme physical transformation. The Chorus of Walls could be voiced by the entire ensemble. Grandmother might appear as projection, shadow, or memory depending on production capabilities.

**Runtime:** Approximately 2 hours with one intermission after Act Two.

### Themes for Director's Consideration

- Transformation as both liberation and loss
- The price of refusing change vs. the price of embracing it  
- Memory and forgetting as survival mechanisms
- Individual vs. collective consciousness
- The nature of documentation and witness
- Time as non-linear experience
- Identity as fluid rather than fixed
- The question of what makes us human when humanity itself transforms

### Final Note

This play exists in the space between states—between human and other, between question and answer, between ending and beginning. Like Tomorrow's Edge itself, it should feel like it's breathing, changing slightly with each performance, never quite the same twice.

The central question—"What are we becoming?"—should resonate not just with the characters but with the audience, who are themselves in constant transformation, whether they acknowledge it or not.
