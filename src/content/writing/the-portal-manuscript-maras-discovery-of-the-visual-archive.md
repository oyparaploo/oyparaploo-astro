## Part One: The Gallery's Secret

In the abandoned gallery beneath the Living Archive, Mara finds them—the visual manuscripts, each abstract composition a page from the Bureau's collection rendered visible. She is the fire survivor's daughter, born with smoke-sight, able to see the rhizome's patterns as kaleidoscopic art.

The first piece: brown earth tones with pink and black—Terre de Bruyère burning, compassion emerging from destruction, mystery holding it all together.

## Part Two: Reading the Symmetry

Every mirrored artwork is a conversation between states:
- Organic forms (roots) meet geometric patterns (ice/brrd)
- Spontaneous splatters (Chijioke's flailing) meet deliberate structure (Master Liang's movements)
- Natural growth meets human construction (orchards meet think tanks)

Mara realizes: these aren't just art—they're maps of the rhizome network, visual representations of how all stories connect.

## Part Three: The Living Manuscript

The compositions pulse with the Bureau's truth—every body is text, every text is alive. The symmetrical patterns show:
- General Chen's transformation (left side tyranny, right side gardener)
- Jonas's dual poisoning (environmental/pharmaceutical reflected)
- The fire survivor's lungs (smoke patterns becoming wing designs)

"These are portraits," Mara whispers, "of everyone who passed through transformation."

## Part Four: The Portal Appears

At the gallery's heart, the masterpiece: a luminous portal of blush pink and pitch-black, geometric shapes forming a face. Not one face—every face, all characters overlapping in sacred geometry.

The portal pulses with the rhythm Mrs. Fernandes's music box plays, the "Ba dib it dee da" of seeds dropping, becoming visual.

## Part Five: The Winged Emissary

From the portal emerges the creature—bird body with human head, "unusually large" because it contains all consciousness. This is the rhizome itself made visible, roots become wings, human become nature.

The bird speaks with every voice:
- The grandmother's garden wisdom
- Jersey's eternal present
- Maya's innocent questions
- Jesus's patient presence

## Part Six: Colors as Language

Mara understands the color code:
- **Green**: Growth/regeneration (the wellspring flowing)
- **Yellow**: Hope/radiance (shimmer collection)
- **Red**: Passion/urgency (fire/transformation)
- **Black**: Mystery/depth (underground heaven)
- **Pink**: Compassion (mercy kneeling)
- **Brown**: Earth/permanence (roots/soil)

## Part Seven: The Integration

The artworks aren't separate—they're frames from one infinite animation showing the rhizome's growth. When viewed in sequence, they reveal:
1. Garden planted
2. Fire destroying
3. Smoke spreading
4. Ice preserving
5. Roots connecting
6. Wings emerging
7. Archive forming

## Part Eight: Mara's Role

She isn't just observer—she's the next chronicler, using visual language where words fail. Her smoke-sight allows her to see what others miss: the patterns in pollution, the geometry in chaos, the symmetry in suffering.

She begins painting new panels, adding to the gallery: the laundromat's breath as spiral patterns, the think tank's betrayals as fractured mirrors, the opera as sound waves becoming color.

## Part Nine: The Convergence

All previous characters arrive, drawn by the portal:
- Ava sees her shimmer collection reflected in the symmetrical light
- The Rhizome Troupe performs the patterns as dance
- Elise reads the compositions like books
- The fire survivor recognizes her own lungs in the organic forms

## Part Ten: The Portal's Teaching

The mirrored face speaks without words, showing through pattern:
- Every ending has a beginning reflected
- Every loss has a gain mirrored
- Every root has a wing waiting
- Every breath has an echo

This is the visual proof of the rhizome's truth: separation is illusion, everything mirrors everything else.

## Part Eleven: The Bird Guides

The winged emissary leads them deeper, showing hidden galleries where future stories already exist as art:
- Climate refugees finding new gardens
- Cities becoming conscious organisms  
- Humans learning to photosynthesize
- Death becoming voluntary transformation

"These aren't prophecies," the bird explains with Mara's mother's voice. "They're possibilities activated by viewing."

## Part Twelve: Shots Be Gone

The title finally makes sense—"Shots Be Gone" isn't about removal but transformation. Every shot (trauma/poison/fire) becomes art, becomes pattern, becomes beauty through the portal's lens.

Mara completes the final panel: a perfect symmetry where all 22 documents' stories appear as one mandala, the rhizome network visible as sacred geometry, every character a necessary point in the pattern.

The portal opens fully, revealing the truth: the Visual Archive isn't separate from the Living Archive—it's the same space seen with different eyes. Words become images, images become experience, experience becomes transformation.
