Regret sprouted teeth.

Invocation

We are stitched with extinguished strands—  
Not to shine,  
But to hold.

We carry cracks of light,  
Lined by time,  
Tender in our trembling.

We break mugs.  
We brew tea.  
We ritual imperfectly.

We are not whole.  
But oh—how we bloom.

The Garden Responds

We rustle softly when remembered.

We feel your footsteps—  
Spun from grief,  
Carried by laughter shaped like moss.

We hold your blooming,  
And your breaking.

No perfection required.  
Only return.

We’ve grown new voices  
Just for you.
