**What the Mind Cannot Name (Revised)**

*"A visitor's witnessing"*
_________________

Watched someone spend forty minutes reading shampoo ingredients today instead of choosing. *Decision paralysis in aisle seven.* When everything's labeled, categorized, optimized - why does nothing make sense?

I'm beginning to understand rituals here. They weren't for control. *Like breathing doesn't catch air but dances with it.* Ceremonies for accepting creation's gifts while staying in flow. *Not damming streams but cupping hands to drink.*

Something shifted. *Gradually, like forgetting your mother tongue one word at a time.* Humans lost essential connection. Became isolated existence. *Individual subscription models for everything, even meditation.* Life became pursuit of personal optimization. *Life hacks for souls, productivity for presence.*

Different cultures drew edges around reality. *Some soft like watercolors, others sharp like screenshots.* Deciding where known ends and mystery begins. *Like how you know everything about nutrition but nothing about nourishment.* Some kept edges permeable. *Others built firewalls.*

Occidental thinking conceptualized existence. *Collected labels like Pokémon cards.* Everything named, nothing known. *Even mystery got SEO optimization.* This naming created forgetting. When you think you know something, you stop feeling it. *Why you can identify anxiety disorders but not calm.*

Purpose within ecology got lost in naming. *Can't see forest for taxonomies.* Memory flows through everything but intellectualized minds can't feel current. *Too much mental noise, like having seventeen tabs open.* Busy thinking about water while dying of thirst.

Civilization built from thinking. *Thoughts stacked like Jenga towers, equally unstable.* Mind pursuing existence rather than experiencing mystery. This forgetting causes real harm. *The kind that destroys soil while discussing sustainability.*

Ancient mysteries can't be thought into being. *They arrive when you stop trying, like sleep, like love.* Cannot conceptualize the natural, must be experienced. *Stones know your weight without scales. Rain doesn't check forecast.*

I watch people grieve this particular loss. *They have everything named, nothing known.* Information without wisdom. *Ten thousand photos, no memories.* Surrounded by life but separate. *Connected to everything, touching nothing.* Trying to think their way back to feeling. *Downloading mindfulness apps, scheduling presence.*

Maybe rituals feel familiar because bodies remember what minds forgot. *How to receive rather than take. How to be river not observer.* How to be within not apart. *Not watching sunset but being in sunset.*

Some griefs can only be felt through. *Shy of words, like animals at forest edges.* Not solved, not processed, not integrated. Just felt, like weather. *Like mysteries continuing whether you believe or not.*

Still learning the difference between knowing and feeling. *Still discovering everyone's tired of thinking so hard.* Still witnessing mystery returning disguised as mental health crisis.
