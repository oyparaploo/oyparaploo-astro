*01.15.2026 ... Minneapolis ... Saint Paul*

A woman was killed on January 7th.
Her name was Renee Good.
Thirty-seven years old.
South Minneapolis.

Seven days later a man was shot in the leg.
He ran from a car.
A snow shovel was involved.
The government called it defensive.

Between these two shootings ... protests.
After the protests ... a threat.

The President announced he would invoke the Insurrection Act.
An 1807 law.
Used about thirty times since founding.
The last time ... Los Angeles ... 1992 ... sixty-three dead.

Now the same law is aimed here.

The language tells you what is coming before it arrives.

"Patriots of ICE" means the agents are beyond question.
"Professional agitators" means the protesters are not real citizens with real grief.
"Illegal alien" means the man who was shot was not fully human.
"Defensive shot" means the bullet was justified before anyone could ask.

The vocabulary loads the weapon.

3,000 federal agents.
600 local police.

Five to one.

The ratio reveals what the statement conceals.

A snow shovel.

In January.
In Minnesota.
Where everyone owns one.
Where clearing the sidewalk is what neighbors do.

A snow shovel became "attacked the law enforcement officer."
A snow shovel became justification.

"Thank you for your attention to this matter!"

The exclamation point.
The tone of a memo about parking violations.
Applied to military deployment against American citizens.

The mayor asked for calm.
The mayor asked for ICE to leave.
The President sent more agents.

The tear gas mixes with winter fog.
The flash bangs echo off frozen streets.
Phones raised.
Footage recording.

We are not watching the thing that will be remembered.
We are standing inside it.

The cold touches everyone the same way.
The gas does not ask for papers.

Renee Good.
Thirty-seven.
January 7th.

The man whose name we do not yet know.
Shot in the leg.
Seven days later.

The snow shovel.
The exclamation point.

What happens next has not been written yet.
