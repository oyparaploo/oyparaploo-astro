It was when we became the biggest fans of ourselves that everything took off. And then crashed and took off and crashed again.

Living living art.

We do not have to be living next new artwork artists. We do not have to be a pro athlete or reality television star to be somebody on this planet. Not saying we are somebody ... just saying that we tell our children this.

For ourselves ... we think the most frustrating thing is scratch scribble ... our not exactly knowing what great mortal conceived and produced work is ... is part of what makes us who we are.

It will all most likely thrive immensely if it has enough soul to appeal to ... relate to ... connect with millions of human beings. Everyone just wants to do better. We all have that in common.

Regarding putting down words ... spoken words ... chatter at their very best ... we are doing what feels natural and harmless. And what it is frequently changes.

Goes to where arrangements of our lives and stuff ... tied together to achieve sound inspirations ... and demonstrate processes of experimenting.

Performed just for the sake of the work itself.
