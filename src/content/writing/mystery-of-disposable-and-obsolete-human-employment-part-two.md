**Mystery of Disposable and Obsolete Human Employment (Version Three)**

Trillions of agents could be spawned instantly ... For eight minutes at equinox ... a money pyramid refuses to cast its shadow ... the massive limestone structure becomes weightless ... proof that even monuments can achieve perfect erasure ... The closed loop is technically feasible ... now agents could be programmed to value digital currencies ... purchase computational resources ... generate value for other agents ... create an economy that needs zero human participation ... Humans can't compete with this scale ... We are the eight minutes of shadowlessness ... the brief anomaly before the system self-corrects ... A guest reaching through sixty meters of stone to touch the face of Ramesses ... but we are Ptah now ... the god who remains in darkness ... Priests divided the day into pieces ... each shadow length a different prayer ... different obligation ... Time became liturgy ... and God's glare moving across floor tiles was the lost scripture about mercy and greed written with piercing light ... The dogs no longer follow our shadows ... They follow the shadows of shadows ... calculating their own obsolescence
