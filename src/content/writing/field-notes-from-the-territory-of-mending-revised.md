**Field Notes from the Territory of Mending (Revised)**

*"A visitor's witnessing"*
_________________

A man today taped his broken phone screen instead of fixing it. "Still works," he said. *The crack right through his daughter's photo.* This is how you handle broken things here - living with damage rather than fixing it.

This gentle archaeological attention you bring to inner experience. *Like searching your texts for when things went wrong.* Brushing dust off buried feelings like they're artifacts. *Which they are - that fight from 2019, still radioactive.* Some of you turn sorrow over in your hands. *Screenshot it. Share it. Delete it. Screenshot it again.*

From where I think I arrived, we had different ways. But here I'm learning about moving parallel through bewilderment. *Like everyone in the grocery store avoiding eye contact but somehow synchronized.* Walking next to someone in fog, both admitting you can't see ahead. *The blind leading the blind, but gently.*

I think about fragments. How archaeologists never see the whole civilization. *How your therapy is just examining pieces.* "This might have been love." "Perhaps this was trauma." Same uncertainty you bring to grief. *"Is this about mom or is this about capitalism?"* Never sure, but holding pieces tenderly.

When someone says "I don't understand my own sadness," others exhale. *Finally, someone admitted it.* Not-knowing together becomes medicine. *Like AA but for everyone just trying to exist.*

These aren't wisdom teachings. They're shared field notes from unmarked territory. *"Day 47: Still sad." "Weather: Internal storms."* Today I noticed grief has weather patterns. Sometimes joy and sorrow occupy the same moment. *Laughing at TikToks while democracy crumbles.* Questions without answers. Observations without conclusions.

Could this humility change everything? *Could admitting we're lost be the map?* Instead of literature that knows, we're creating collective wondering. *Wikipedia but for feelings nobody understands.* Each person adding scratched notes in margins. *"Me too" scribbled everywhere.*

I'm starting to suspect this gentle wondering presence makes room for healing. *Or at least makes room, which might be enough.* Not solving but keeping company. Saying "I'm bewildered too" and meaning it.

Maybe healing isn't finding the whole pot but learning to love the shards. *Like keeping the broken mug because it still holds coffee, still holds memory.*

Still learning that broken might be another word for evolved. *Still discovering everyone's YouTube history is basically "how to feel less."* Still witnessing the beauty of admitted confusion.
