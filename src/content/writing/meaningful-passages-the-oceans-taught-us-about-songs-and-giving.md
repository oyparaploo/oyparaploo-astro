## Verse One: Hearing Aromas (hushed beginning)

B Otto Will heard the wondrous aroma—*yes, heard it*—drifting from misty blue waters. *Tap-tap, pause, tap-tap.* Heartbeat rhythm against sand.

*Can you ponder black palm trees?* (tap-tap)
*Adriana's piano by the sea?* (tap-tap)
*Ablaye's wet hair drips ancient peace*
*The horizon never ceases*

Vast sea beyond: ancient reconciliation. Always something to draw in during dreamtime and real-common moments.

## Verse Two: Learning Love's Glow (building gently)

"We didn't know what love was," Brrd Roots whispered, voice like morning fog, "until trillion sparkles showed us—oceanside sea mixing with your eyes."

*Blonde Mane(y) Hair Boy walks by* (breathe in)
*Leather Sandals Row Man rows* (breathe out)
*Savant Island Woman knows why*
*Bomb Wave Girl just flows*

The truth echoed back, slightly changed—same sparkles, same eyes, but now the curl dissolved into pure motion. Love teaching through repetition.

## Verse Three: Spiritual Track Walk (melody climbing)

They'd been on this path—line, rail, groove, tangle—several years running. Natural human absurdities, contradictions, selfless realities mixing with trivial facts.

Stuff conceived funny compared to usual stuff. Natural openness going any direction. Stories read forward but returns pop vivid, clear recalls drawing receivers into refreshing encounters.

"Sit on the floor if you want. Works are wonders."

## Chorus: Give It All Away (The Hook)

*Follow your passion to the sea—honestly, fearlessly*
*Where art becomes the food we need*
*Follow your passion, set it free*
*Give away each precious seed*
*This is how we learn to feed*

## Verse Four: Humble Journey Questions

We're worldly, always going places, conveying where we've been. Maybe escaping too much this-that-other. During humble journey, observing horizons—what obstructs passage? Include that terrifying mountain range?

As artists, we shift quickly to soothing notions. Walking alongside as caring friends. Multi-sensory moments connecting with gradually developed beliefs. Whimsy helping divert unhelpful thoughts.

Voice ups not harmful = gifts from Heaven. Works bend us divine child to wise elder, philosophy plus awareness.

## Verse Five: Attentive Prayer (emotional building)

*Assiduously attending wonders* (rising now)
*Considerate to others' needs* (passion growing)
*Alert witness to what life squanders*
*Tiny details, big picture seeds*

"Walk with humility," B Otto Will prayed. "Remember God's love when we're at our worst thinking we're best. Seek humility daily. Spread Grace to all places."

## Bridge: The Million Dollar Mirror

"Imagine—two million dollar Pacific house, homeless people in front."

Brrd Roots pointed: "Solution's right there in the picture. The million dollar house IS the solution."

Bitter truth sung sweet.

## Verse Six: Art as Immediate Food (peak passion)

When art becomes societal action—better meanings beyond looks. Within warm circles, artwork becomes food. Find soul nourishments, make something, give to those needing most.

*Expect nothing in return* (clear now)
*Let receivers keep or sell* (confident)
*Creator's joy is what we earn*
*Giving's how we break the shell*

## Verse Seven: Perceivable Openness

Natural openness any direction. Very attentive yet not knowing what next passion work will be exactly. Deliberate disorientations plus sweet directions. Influenced by 19th-20th century artist spirits.

Complete natural to artificial abundance—forward ongoing.

## Outro: Return to Sparkles (whispered close)

Back at misty waters. Adriana's notes still floating. Ablaye's wisdom still dripping. The infinite horizon still teaching.

*We didn't know what love was* (tap-tap)
*Until we learned to give it away* (tap-tap)
*Now trillion sparkles show because*
*We found our honest way*

B Otto Will and Brrd Roots sat on sand, understanding finally: follow passion honestly, fearlessly. Let curiosity guide. Give everything away.

*This ocean song from lived experience:*
*Passion feeds when freely given*
*Art as food, love as presence*
*This is how we keep on living*

(fade to waves: *tap-tap... tap-tap... tap-tap...*)

The wondrous aroma still audible. The infinite horizon still teaching its patient song.
