We said to our father ... Dad we are going to start a loving garden ... We had this elaborate collection of Mayan seeds ... Saved for years ... Waiting in jars like little prophecies ... Sometimes we would hear them during the night ...

Without saying anything ... Dad went ahead and built this huge corn farm on a bridge ... A bridge ... Maybe something told him to ... Something older than our ancestors ...

Dads are like that sometimes ... They do not say much and then build something humongous ...

Grandma walked eleven miles for commodity canned ham ... and said it was not even good cheese ... but she made do ... She enhanced it with other collected ingredients ... We remember the veins on her hands ... and traced them ...

Our lands fed us without asking us for anything ... Except saying thank you in a very respectful way ... Then that food got taken ... This is still happening ...

Tables take a long time to become what they are ... We have to wait for the tree first ... tree has to wait for the seed ... and somebody waits for all this to become a place where we set food down and converse with each other ...

Liffey auntie says marriage is just two people agreeing to confuse each other forever ... but kindly ... haha ...

Two daughters together watch everything even if you think they do not ... My mom thought we were not paying attention and now we cook almost exactly like her ... including how we hold a carved wooden spoon ...

We are knucklehead nutritionists ... The best kind ... Ones who are aware they do not know everything ...

Children used to know where food came from by just going outside ... Now we have to teach foraging again like it is new ...

We asked our nephew where eggs come from ... He said the store ... How would he know? ... That is on us to correct ...

Dad had not said anything about the corn farm on the bridge ... We had to remind him to harvest it ... He just stood there looking at it ... Like he could not believe it ...

Our cousin is learning to harvest wild rice the old way ... Manoomin ... It is not just learning what to pick ... it is learning how to be the kind of ready person who gets to pick it ...

Rice knows if you are not ready ... The rice has been here longer than any of us and has seen things ... Remembers people coming wrong ...

Lazy Gardener ... Haha ... That is what we call our other cousin ... He just sits by his garden and talks to himself ... But his garden does fine ...

Our pumpkin heads never survive drops from a porch ... We keep growing them because the splat is a tradition ...

Every summer somebody asks ... Who wants to be the blueberries boy? ... Somebody has got to do it ... We told our daughter we would grow kale and she said she would be willing to wear it as a new kind of fashion ... She was not joking ...

It is not scarcity ... it is allocation ... who deserves to eat and who does not ... According to individuals who have never gone hungry a day in their life ...

Auntie who works at a food shelf says summer is when she sees the same kids every week ... Looking thinner each time ... That is only a twenty-minute drive from the closest shopping mall ...

We can tell what a society cares about by what it counts ... We count money and votes very carefully ... We count hungry people like it is a rough estimate ...

Our grandma had a cellar ... We could survive for three months down there if we had to ... She was not fearless ... She was ready ... There is a big difference ...

She used to say ... These jars tell me what to make ... and what was missing ...

Her cellar had slightly chipped jars older than us ... She would hold one up to sunlight streaming through a cellar window and say ... This jar remembers how to get to the heart of everything here ...

We believed her voice and most of what she said ...

Our relative will not tell anyone her berry spot ... She will drive you there ... She is not being mean just careful ... That area fed her family through hard years ...

The way people used to just know when to come over ... Before phones ... They would sense it and start walking ...

In talking circles everybody gets to speak ... Words go around and come back changed ... Sometimes they even come back around saying things that nobody said ...

We do not have to do everything at once ... That is the thing nobody tells us ...

Start with one seed and see what happens ...

There is something growing in us we did not plant ...

Returning to the wealth that we have known means to give food away ... That is the return ... The economics of it ... Not hoarding ... Giving ... More we give the more there is ...

That unexpected One appearing ... wheeling a shopping cart of bright squash around a village block ... following the sun's direction ... not caring what her close neighbors think ...

Quiet now ... that is where abundance lives ... In speechless hands that recall building a corn farm on a bridge ... Like taking instructions from someone we are not able to see with our eyes ...

Seeded prophecies are waiting ... with a softness that holds laughter and tears ... longer than our uncle has been saving ketchup packets ... and he has been saving them since before we were born ...

Dad is still standing there ... looking at what he built ... A corn farm on a bridge ...

Something is always waiting for us ...

- Oyparaploo
