do not tell me how to be ... do not tell us how to be ... we will decide how to be ... for our works ...

professional personas ... for the sake of our work ... as those beloved amateur home movies that can be also other places than just here ...

human bird artists say nope ... as a chirp regarding social stuff ... things can be countless different ways ... long as nobody is hurt because of it
