We like to smell what is left behind. On a garment ... perfume of former living now deceased.

Elder beings do not care. We do not care what the perfume is by name. Just the trigger of our imagining who they may have been when they were alive.

Were they choosy about the perfume. We are not sure. Really choosy about the perfume. We are not sure.

We imagine also ... most of our fabrics ... light to heavier textiles ... colorful textiles ... were at some time in the past brought brought brought to us by goodwill ... sons ... daughters ... sisters ... brothers ... close friends.
