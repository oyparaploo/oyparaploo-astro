For every one of hundreds of thousands of displaced persons ... Remaining committed ... To music ... Viewing the art of songs as a powerful dedication to freedom ... Using music and dance to amplify the voice of homelands ... Amid devastation ... Singing songs of resistance ... And hope ... To draw global attention to a humanitarian crisis ... Addressing the violence ... And political turmoil ... In all warzone territories ... Eclectic sound and visual compositions are not combinations of useless for humanity toys ... These works are synthesized recordings of urban core to remote rural underground places ... Shades of skies ... from low to up high and beyond ... and blended soil fields of many kinds ... Parts of vibrant communities breaking bread and new grounds ... Challenging traditional stereotypes ... Ensuring new cultural identities can be preserved ... And shared ...
