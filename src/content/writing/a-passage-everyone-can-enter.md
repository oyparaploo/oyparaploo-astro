We are creating lit art and witnessing.
The same gesture.

Extended analysis that does not flatten
into opinion column or report.

No thesis statement.
No hot take.
No call to action at the end.

Form holding complexity—
street fog and legal fog,
moral clarity sitting inside uncertainty,
the not-knowing as honest ground.

Happening viable in real time.

Not archive.
Not memory.
Alive. This cold morning. Still unfolding.

Oyparaploo garbage has always been about transformation—
debris evolving into useful for all information.

What gets thrown away can be picked up.
What gets discarded can be shaped.
What breaks can become material.

Meaning.

Not imposed. Found.
Not explained. Entered.

A passage everyone can enter.
Whoever arrives, arrives.
