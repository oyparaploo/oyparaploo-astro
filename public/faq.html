FAQ

What will I find here?

Writings first: poems, stories, plays and scripts, essays, prayers, letters and texts, conversations, short pieces. Then the Marks, drawings in a language no one actually speaks. Then the Placings, photographs of objects and materials arranged by hand. Then the Collages, built on a computer from our own photographs. There is also Musicology, our favorite songs with a piece of our writing beside each, and the walks.

Where should I start?

The Welcome page says what this place is in a few paragraphs. The front door lists every kind of work, one after another, with a few words about each and a door into it. If you would rather be led, take a walk: each one is a path through the site with stops along the way. If you already know what you want, use Search.

How do I find a writing or picture again if I need to?

Every writing has its own address, and every picture has a catalogue number. Type a word from a title, a gallery name, or a catalogue number into Search.

What do the numbers under the pictures mean?

Catalogue numbers. Each picture has one, and it never changes. It is the surest way to find a picture again, and it leads back to the original.
