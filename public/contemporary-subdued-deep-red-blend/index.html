Contemporary Subdued Deep Red Blend

( harmless moth )

It is just too painful to show red blood. So the white stains and marks represent red human and animal blood.

I killed a pretty awake invention. Pretty woke invention. In our art studio garage.

It is foolish most of the time. Most of the time I felt sorry for myself ... was frustrated.

I could be that harmless moth that came to me ... the controller operator of warm to cool artificial light.

*

Crazy quilt Basel recycle. I step on it. Recycle recycle after use recycle.

Thou shall not steal.

Lightly sterilize. Pollution products. Status statement. Stateless enemies.

Made in the USA. Made in China. Made in Vietnam. Fabric product. Bad water.

Recycle the pan. The end.

*

Are you having a midlife crisis at soon to be 60 years old. I am way past that. No response. Oh okay.

It is now my time. It is now my time to shine ... until death and leaving this planet Earth life existence. And or other outer ring space.

Chop shop chopping block. Incredible worldly hero worry free. Worry doll wigs. Where can I buy one or more than one.

I will be incubating them ... distilling for a few weeks ... then presenting examples to a handful of closest trusted audience members ... hands ... and hand it off.

Who are they. Presently at just the born in concept stage.

*

As an adult mortal human bird Earth laying ... if I had to make a choice ... to be down to earth and prejudice or not at all prejudice and up in the air ... I would every time choose to be up in the air.

With your own unconditional kindness towards all living beings and things ... manifesting good energy is more important than many sense on a continual basis.

*

New objects and installations that in the end help to make a mortal being feel better.

Are you specifically referring to humans or Earthlings.

It can also extend to the spirits over a river if you want. Still to streaming river.

Hugs and sometimes even kisses are wasted on the young person performing something new with support.

My far nourishing and uplifting best music on Earth. Sensitive humble slightly bumbling approach.

Can you add this ... also good cosmic vibrations. Sure. With Earth soiled to clean hands approach.

Just so ... a new member has joined bird. Their name is simply means. And listen to plexus chakras during the evening hours.

And favor eating from the yellow foods group.

Wow. Skull face big eyes. Wow cool.

Now calm down. Now calm down.
