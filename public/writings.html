Writing

The writing practice treats found text — product warning labels, transcribed voice recordings, and other discarded language — as raw material for finished literary work. Each piece receives its own atmospheric design, derived from the register of the specific writing. 34 pieces locked and ready, 19 needing one shaping pass, across five categories and several hundred pages of source material.
