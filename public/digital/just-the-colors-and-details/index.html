Find the pink comb

Open the big picture at the top and look low on the left. There is a star with a face in there too.

No person chose where they went. A computer program cut our pictures into scraps and sorted the scraps by color, and by how loud they are. It has never seen a comb. Our rule for it: no shout beside a whisper. Nothing was stretched, and nothing was painted in.

There is probably enough red in your kitchen to make one.
