The Methodology

The second row carries section anchors for a deep reference page. Philosophy, voice, craft, making, witnesses, reference — each tab scrolls to its section.

Philosophy
The Two Laws
Embodied over conceptual. Trust the reader. Everything follows from these.
Philosophy
The Core Trinity
Wonderful Garbage, Pressure Ghosts, Middle Eye. Art made from what is discarded, what remains after crushing, what is between what is visible.
Philosophy
The Four Virtues
Tenderness, humility, mercy, vulnerability. Before craft, before intelligence, before originality.
Voice
Fragmentary Prose Conventions
First person plural. Present tense. Ellipsis as breath. No contractions. Sentence length 5–30 words. The "we" that cracks once.
Voice
The Seven Words
Wistful, tender, longing, wayward, plaintive, bittersweet, rueful. Each word located in the body.
Craft
The 33 Domains
Material testimony, strategic subtraction, experiential rendering, behavioral incrementation — through to kindness as design and scaffolding awareness.
Craft
The 30 Quality Controls
Word-level, structural, paragraph-level, and document-level controls. Choose the stranger word. Trust adjacency. No morals drawn.
Making
The Three Modes
Surgical, ecological, running and growing. Each mode asks a different question of the work.
Making
The Three-Layer System
Lovely Quietness, AI Detection, Deep Revision. The passes that take work from alive to finished.
Witnesses
Marie (Skagit Valley)
The one who touches. The clay knows before she knows. Reach for her when writing needs to start in the body.
Witnesses
Hahnu Youn
Eighty-three years old. Seeds in her pocket. She does not give advice. She asks questions.
Modes
The Eight Signature Modes
Minimalist witness fiction, objects as reliquaries, language and loss, transformation/magical realism, found documents, climate/collapse, intersection collective, war/displacement witness.
Reference
The Essential Ten
Brown Paint, Seventeen Chairs, Kukura, Sandhill Crane, Like Thursday, She Had a Hammer, Defiant Mangoes, When the Counting Stopped, Boot Message, Forty-Seven Toy Guns.
