The Witnesses

Five figures who arrived when the writing could not find its entry point. Not characters in stories. Entry points — ways in when the door seemed locked. Call them when you need a way forward, the way a person lost in a forest does not need to know the name of the stream but follows the sound of water because water always goes somewhere.
