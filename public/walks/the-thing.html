About 15 minutes

On this walk, objects stand around as if they were alive. Puppets are buried in the rain, a fishing net becomes a home for light, and a blue glass ball, two unlabeled packages and a crowd of lamps wait in the photographs. The writings in between try to say what this kind of thing is, and why it will not leave you alone.
