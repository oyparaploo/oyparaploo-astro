So Much Loss and News of Healing

living with damage, taking in what was left behind, and softening, which then turns out to be good news

Begin ↓04391

About 23 minutes

A walk through grief and the slow news of feeling better. A mother paints with her fingers, a garment still smells of someone gone, and a crane lands where it should not while a father is dying. The pictures are of broken mirror, mourning flowers and tears drawn in ink. It ends gently, with healthy courage and a poem about being soft.
