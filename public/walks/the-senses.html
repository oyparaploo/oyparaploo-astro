The Senses

drawn thousands of times, being looked into

Begin ↓09953

About 13 minutes

A walk through seeing, touching, tasting, smelling and hearing. You will read about slowing down in front of art, a cup of coffee taken in with nine senses, how different surfaces feel under the fingers, and how the mind finds faces in rain and wind. Between the readings are eyes made of plaster, brush and collage, and pen drawings that keep a sound or a touch in their titles.
