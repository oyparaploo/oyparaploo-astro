Decades of Umbrellas

we the para and the ploo, and all that we shelter

Begin ↓05083

About 14 minutes

The name Paraploo has the French word for umbrella folded inside it. This walk opens that umbrella: what para means, what ploo means, and everything sheltered underneath. You will see tents from the inside, a home made of gathered things, and pen drawings about oceans and heads. It ends with a longer piece about an umbrella dome that will not fold up.
