Wonderful Garbage

discards resurrected for healing or soothing in a different way, for those in need of this

Begin ↓04558

About 17 minutes

Everything on this walk was thrown away once. A park bench that used to be a water bottle, a bottle from a thrift shop, a rusty ladder from a demolition site, and the slow rot of what we throw out all become something else. The pictures are of found objects laid out in sand and on tables. It ends with a short letter about picking up broken things.
