Running Jokes

under stones, in uplifting light, and not always aware of everything

Begin ↓09980

About 18 minutes

This walk starts on a bench with Hahnu Youn, who counts things and gives no advice. From there it follows the jokes that keep coming back in these writings: a love letter to a pair of boots, the three dots, a jacket that bursts into feathers, and a list of disasters that never happened. Pen drawings and found toys sit between them. Most stops take a minute or two.
