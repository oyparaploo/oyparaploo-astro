On this walk the news of the world comes indoors: melting ice, broken toy soldiers, a list of worries that ends in what to do, and the quiet harm of neglect. Pen drawings about storms and a letter to a man who might start a war sit between them, with pictures from fire and storm. It closes with hope rising like a quiet dawn.
