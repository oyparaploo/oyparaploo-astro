Machine That Doesn't Eat Ties

made by language-ing that feels

Begin ↓08202

About 24 minutes

The last walk is about writing with a machine and keeping the feeling in it. It starts on a quiet morning, asks what a machine can and cannot be, plays with strange combinations, nurses a cold, and asks for a request to be turned into a lamb. It ends with a long piece about nine months of this work.
