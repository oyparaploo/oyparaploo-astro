Marks

that can be written and read in more than one way

Begin ↓02189

About 14 minutes

This walk is about marks that can be read more than one way: a looping 8, lines that never quite become words, a sentence written wrong on purpose, and poems made of sounds. Between them are pen drawings, a handwritten page, a brush drawing and a page of words set as a picture. It closes with a run of good mornings and good evenings.
