The Witnesses

We, Them, and Gene Jean

Begin ↓19631

About 15 minutes

This walk is about watching and being watched. It begins in a room where five people are frozen, then stands with people who raised their hands in a hearing room, neighbors holding candles in the cold, and phones held up before daylight. The pictures are of eyes, and of what is left after something has happened. It ends with a visitor learning how people here speak sorrow aloud.
