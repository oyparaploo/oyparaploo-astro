Breathe
Between every piece of finished work there is a space where nothing is being made. The practice does not fear this space. The practice lives in it.
stillness
Lovely Quietness is not a technique. It is the acknowledgment that the work arrives when the maker stops performing the act of working and simply waits. The waiting is the work. The breath between the notes is what makes them music.
patience
Three hours of garbage. Then the sentence that opens the portrait arrives in the fourth hour, not because you worked harder in the fourth hour but because the first three hours were the breath before the voice. The garbage was the inhale. The sentence was the exhale. You cannot have one without the other.
presence
The Middle Eye opens in the pause. Not in the making. Not in the analyzing. In the moment between them — when the hand is still and the mind has not yet decided what to do next. That is where you see the work as it actually is.
Thirty years of making. The practice that survived was the one that knew when to stop. Not stop forever. Stop for the length of the breath. Then begin again. The breath is the spine. Remove the pauses and the music collapses into noise.
