Aroha films her granddaughter making angels in the black sand, holding the phone sideways because she's sixty-three and never learned the vertical way, and honestly, this feels right—getting the whole frame, the child and the surf and the sky.

"Did you get it? Did you see my soul?"

"I got it, love."
