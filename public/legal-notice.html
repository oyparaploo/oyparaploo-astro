Legal notice

Paraploo is a multidisciplinary practice in the Twin Cities, Minnesota. paraploo.com is its online archive: writing, pen drawings, placed objects, collage, film and sound made over thirty years.

Outside works

The site refers to works by other artists, writers and musicians. Some of it is interpretive — ideas, methods and forms taken up in our own way, in found text, fiction and mixed-voice pieces. Some of it is shown outright, with the maker named, in the rooms where we keep the work we love. Songs on the Favorite Songs pages play through Spotify under Spotify's own terms; we host no recordings. These uses are made in good faith, for commentary, study and appreciation, and nothing of anyone else's is offered for sale here.

If your work is here

If you made something shown on this site and want it credited differently, linked differently, or taken down, write to hello@paraploo.com. Tell us where it is and what you want done. We will answer, and we will act on it.

Found materials

Some pieces are built from found things — printed matter, objects, scraps of text, pictures that came to us without a name. Where a maker cannot be found, none is named. If one of them is you, the paragraph above applies.
