About this archive

This working collection spans the full history of the Wonderful Garbage practice. Some earlier material may reflect earlier stages of development. The archive is a living document — pieces move, new rooms open, and the practice continues to shape itself.

Immerse yourself in the full span of the practice — writing, sound, visual, and performance work.

The Oyparaploo collection maps three hundred and thirty pages across eleven rooms. Each room has its own temperature, its own rules, and its own relationship to the found-text methodology that governs the practice.

Begin anywhere. Enter through the Writing room, explore the Good Grounds novel project, or browse the curated collections below, which reveal the connections between rooms.
