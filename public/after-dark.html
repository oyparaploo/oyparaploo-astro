After Dark ...

What does it mean to have rays of natural light glow and radiate from sweet human and lovely animal hearts? What does it mean to extend the breath of forest canopies? What does it mean to masterfully serve dark and bright Creation? Best God of Goodness we have and all great prophets who have passed on to another place a long time ago ... assert collected wisdom essences of human and wonderfully innocent wild animal unity. May we all now move flow and sit very settled and meditate ... in harmony ... each one of us with our slightly different to extremely different mindful and physical makeups. Let us now lucidly sense unified faithful love existing everywhere. What does it mean to strongly desire only good things for this world ~ Miriam ... Elijah ... Isaiah ... Jeremiah ... Micah ... Zechariah?
