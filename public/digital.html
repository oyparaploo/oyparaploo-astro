Symmetrical and Kaleidoscopic

A mirrored picture goes another way. It usually starts from a single photograph. Part of it is copied and flipped, and the copy is laid against the original so the halves answer each other across a middle line, the way an inkblot does when the paper is folded.

Photomosaics

A photomosaic runs the other direction. Small photographs are set edge to edge, and from far enough back they add up to a single large picture. It is the best way we know to show how much of something there is.
