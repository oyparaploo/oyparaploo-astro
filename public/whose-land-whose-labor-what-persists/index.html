Declaration

Nothing amputated. Everything belongs.

The blood knows better. The body remembers. The river keeps moving. Every part included in what this practice asks of itself and its witnesses.

Paraploo transforms what others discard. Thirty years of finding profound meaning in overlooked fragments — writing, visual art, installations, performance, sound.
