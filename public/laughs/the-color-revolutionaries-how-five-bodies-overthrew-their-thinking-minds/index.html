From THE COLOR REVOLUTIONARIES

"You have thought 67,432 thoughts today. 67,398 were repeats."

A small vial of gray powder labeled "Crushed Dreams of Your Thinking Mind" (actually edible glitter that turns bright colors when mixed with liquid)

the museum transformed into a "Department of Serious Thinking."

Thought number 43,281: What if everyone realizes I'm pretending to understand?
Thought number 43,282: The same but louder.
Thought number 43,283: The same but with more panic.

I have optimized my walking pattern for maximum efficiency.
I save 3.7 seconds per day.
I spend 97 minutes worrying about those 3.7 seconds.
This is productivity.

I am having thoughts about my thoughts about my thoughts.
It's thoughts all the way down.
I haven't noticed my body in seventeen days.
What's a body?

I am preparing for a problem that hasn't happened.
When it doesn't happen, I'll prepare harder.
Anxiety is my cardio.
Worry is my weight training.

Everything I've ever said is wrong.
Everyone remembers and judges.
They have spreadsheets.
They compare notes at secret meetings.

Walking (purpose: transportation only)
- Sitting (purpose: productivity only)
- Standing (purpose: waiting only)
- Lying down (purpose: sleep only, sleep not guaranteed)

This is how bodies should be.
Quiet. Contained. Grayscale.
Notice how nothing flows.
Excellence.

Did... did anyone see that?
My finger just... moved wrong.
It moved without permission.
Without purpose.
It just... wiggled.

Contain it.
Think about why it wiggled.
Analyze the wiggle.
Create a wiggle prevention protocol.

CONTAINMENT BREACH!
UNAUTHORIZED COLOR!
PURPOSELESS MOVEMENT DETECTED!

It's spreading.
The wiggle is spreading.
I can feel it climbing my arm.
It wants to... spin?

Think gray thoughts!
Quarterly reports!
Traffic patterns!
Mortgage rates!

It's not working!
The yellow is... laughing?
Can a color laugh?
OH NO, NOW MY LAUGH IS YELLOW!

It will make you want to... to...
TERRD:
To what?
MAZA:
To MOVE FOR NO REASON!

No no no no no!
I'm backsliding!
I'm literally backsliding!
And it's... orange?

I forgot to breathe shallowly!
I took a full breath!
Now my lungs are jazz blue!
They're improvising without permission!

I reached for something!
Not because I needed it!
Just because my arm wanted to extend!
My spine is spring green!
It thinks it's a plant!
It wants to grow toward light!

No! I am efficiency!
I am optimization!
I am... I am...
(Her hips start swaying)
I am apparently mambo?
When did I learn mambo?
WHY DO MY HIPS KNOW MAMBO?

Citizens of Lyon!
Your bodies are not machines!
They are ridiculous meat kaleidoscopes!
They know dances you've never learned!

We wash our clothes but not our thoughts!
Spin cycle your anxiety!
Tumble dry your rumination!
Add fabric softener to your harsh inner critic!

This fountain has been here since 1892!
It has never worried about anything!
Be like fountain!
Spray purposelessly!

TERRD (green from spine outward, doing involuntary tree impressions):
Sign here if you've ever wanted to skip instead of walk!
Initial here if you've secretly wanted to wear yellow!
Check this box if your body knows dances your mind denies!

We're not asking you to quit your jobs!
We're asking you to wiggle while you work!
Spreadsheets in salsa rhythm!
Emails in iambic pentameter!
Conference calls in cape!

We believe in rotational truth!
Spinning makes thoughts dizzy!
Dizzy thoughts can't hurt you!
They're too busy trying not to fall down!

Your heart is the original drummer!
Everything else is remix!

We believe oxygen is a party drug!
Provided free by trees!
Side effects include living!

We believe bodies are plants with anxiety!
Grow toward light!
Away from fluorescent thought!

We believe spines are not rulers!
They're snakes pretending to be straight!
Let them curve!

NAH (fighting it):
But...
But I haven't thought about that thing from 2019 in three hours...
MAZA:
I forgot to optimize anything...
JON:
My breathing just... happened...
TERRD:
Nobody actually cared that I was green...
FREDRIKA:
The emails... will still be there... even if I mambo...

In gray, I knew exactly who I was.
Worried. Controlled. Appropriate.
In orange, I don't know who I am.
But my hips do.
And they're voting for this.

Walk like your feet know something your head doesn't!

Björk was spotted in the Green Zone growing toward streetlamps

Cafes offer "Revolutionary Rates" - 20% off if you're wearing bright colors

Office buildings designate 3 PM as "Wiggle Hour"

Footage is only released if participants vote (while spinning) to share it

"Rumination Interruption Kits" - bells to ring when you catch yourself overthinking

Scientists call it "helical flow dynamics." Your body calls it "wheeee!"

This is why children spin until they fall down - they're manually operating their joy fountains.

The platysma (neck dancer) finally gets to perform
- Your toe muscles remember they exist individually

You become a walking space heater of delight

Ascent: "I'm leaving!"
- Peak: "I'm free!"
- Descent: "I'm choosing to return!"
- Landing: "But I might leave again!"

Worries can't maintain position in rotating reference frame
- Anxiety gets motion sickness

Each cell becomes a tiny disco

Dopamine: "Yes, more of this!"
- Serotonin: "Everything is actually okay!"
- Endorphins: "Pain is optional!"
- Oxytocin: "We're all in this together!"
- GABA: "The worry department is closed for dancing!"

Your endocrine system votes unanimously for revolution

Make the mayor spin (publicly, in yellow)

Wear it to work at least once
- Spin in at least three inappropriate places
- Teach someone else to wiggle

37 trillion cells voting to move
- 206 bones agreeing to dance
- 639 muscles conspiring to wiggle
- 45 miles of nerves transmitting joy
- One body refusing to think straight

their colored capes creating a human rainbow visible from the International Space Station. Because joy should be visible from space. That's the whole point.
