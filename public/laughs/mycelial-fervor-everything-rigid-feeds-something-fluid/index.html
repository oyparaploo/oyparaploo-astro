From Mycelial Fervor

A dying cedar dumps its whole life savings of nutrients into some baby fir tree it's never met, just passes it through the fungal internet like "here kid, make something of yourself."

identity isn't some fixed point on a map. It's jazz, baby. It's everyone soloing at the same time but somehow making music instead of noise.

We're walking apartment complexes for ten trillion bacteria, each one voting on what we eat, how we feel, whether we sleep tonight or lie there thinking about that stupid thing we said in third grade.

Sky Golden Feathers shows up to teach rain how to fall upward (gravity's just peer pressure from the planet, fight me). Cool Hot Dot Soul makes sound visible, paints frequencies in neon architectures that prove—PROVE—music has color. And Walk From Shadows? They illuminate darkness without killing it, like holding a flashlight with love instead of fear.

See, ceramic's just organized mud with ambition, and it remembers.

It'll hold tea but not tears. Coffee but not wine. Like it learned to say no after thirty years of swallowing everyone else's feelings.

Time down there is spiral, recursive, more like a drunk snake eating its own tail while humming. This backwards watch that shows all hours at once? It's not broken. It got fixed.

The fish swimming backward through music, carrying messages from futures that ain't happened yet? They're not prophets. They're memories with ambitious travel plans, swimming upstream through causality to spawn in the past.

And the transparent one, the one where you can see its bones? That's just showing off, reminding us that structure is temporary, scaffolding at best.

There's this step—the seventh one on a staircase that goes nowhere. It whispers. All day, all night, in the voices of everyone who gave up climbing. Not bitter whispers, nah. Forgiving ones. Like a chorus of "it's okay, we tried, that counts for something."

Its neck reaches through dimensions like it's peeking over the fence between realities. Each vertebra is a different level of getting it: dirt-level consciousness at the bottom, then awareness, perception, comprehension, wisdom, and finally at the top where the head floats in its own weather system, transcendence.

The giraffe wears a velvet hat. Little thing, burgundy maybe, or the color of forgotten wine. Found it in a thrift store between seasons—too small for winter, too warm for spring. The hat was nobody's, living in that liminal space where things wait to be needed. The giraffe saw kinship. Both of them: too much of one thing, not enough of another, perfect in their perfect wrongness.

Your tongue is just fancy skin, and skin is just brain that wanted to travel, and brain is just organized lightning having an identity crisis.

There's a bat. Invisible to most. It whispers stock tips but not the Wall Street kind. Real investments: put your money where your transformation is. Divest from grudges (deprecating assets). Pour everything into forgiveness (growth industry). Diversify across species lines. Keep some joy liquid for surprise opportunities to celebrate.

So this kid in some museum, they lick the wall. Just straight up tongue to plaster. And they announce: "Tastes purple." The adults are mortified but the kid's not wrong.

Their grandma, colorblind for seventy years, hears this and BANG—suddenly sees purple. Not gradually. Like a switch flipped.

Dolphins show up uninvited. They don't knock 'cause in the ocean everything flows into everything.

Their sonar reads our bones like braille. Finds the salt. Proves we're still mostly ocean cosplaying as solid.

B Otto Will and Brrd Roots takes this inherited violin—generations of "hold it like this," "play it proper"—and says nah. Plays it with teeth. Uses the body as a drum. Bows it backwards. The violin screams, then laughs, then finds voices between the notes that the notes were trying to hide.

The colander-rubber band instruments B Otto Will and Brrd Roots builds? They play themselves at 3 AM 'cause that's when the border between dream and wake gets thin as soap bubbles. The colander stops separating water from pasta and starts resonating possibility.

When bugs attack a tree, the fungal network carries the warning to trees miles away. They start making poison before the threat arrives. This is solidarity at the molecular level. This is "I got you" in chemical.

There's this café. Only exists Wednesdays that feel like Thursday.

Plastic flowers honest about being eternal.
