From FRANCOIZ

FRANCOIZ: Shannon, it's too early for existential anthropology. SHANNON: But you WERE a sandwich! An OLD sandwich! Like from before refrigerators! When people had to eat things fast or they'd grow roots!

SHANNON: When I don't know things, I eat chips. They're barbecue flavored. That's like happiness but for your mouth. Ancient people probably had berries for the same thing. Sad berries. Happy berries. Tuesday berries.

THIERRY: Present! Well, not present. Formerly present. Presently absent. It's complicated.

MARGARET: I heard there's a coming-of-age crisis happening? That's evolution, dear. Adaptation to changed circumstances. Very anthropologically sound.

MARGARET: I'm almost there. Save me some sand. Some wings. Some whatever this is.

THIERRY: From my perspective, you're all just rearranging sand on a spinning rock in infinite space! It's MAGNIFICENT!

FRANCOIZ: Shannon, it's too early for cannibalism. SHANNON: Not eating people! People MADE of food! You were a sandwich! You were soup! Very proper soup with a tie! BERTRAND: What kind of soup?
SHANNON: The kind that says sorry for being hot.

FRANCOIZ: Bert? You okay? You look like someone starched your feelings. BERTRAND: I'm practicing. FRANCOIZ: Practicing what? Being a lamppost?
BERTRAND: Bending. Margaret said I bow when I should bend. So I'm... pre-bending. FRANCOIZ: Here. Take this. BERTRAND: You've been carrying an emergency bow tie for me for twenty-eight years? FRANCOIZ:
Twenty-eight years of thinking you might need dignity in a hurry.

Who's Margaret? Is she nice? Does she like tacos? BERTRAND:My ex-wife. She's... complicated. SHANNON: Like math?
BERTRAND: Like math with feelings. SHANNON:
The hardest kind.

SHANNON: Are we all—? PENELOPE: Floating? Yes. MARGARET: Is this normal for your beach days?
BERTRAND: Nothing's normal. Everything's possible. We're floating because we put down our weights.

PENELOPE: Did anyone else have that dream where we were all frozen and— SHANNON: The one where we could hear everything but couldn't move? Where silence was so loud it had subtitles? Yeah. Weird. How long were we out? THIERRY: Time works differently in puppet tableaus. Could've been minutes. Could've been decades. Could've been the exact duration of a sigh.

CONSTANCE: Penelope, darling, are you mixing your medical notes with your new play again?

In France, pommes de terre are practically a food group. Earth apples! Dirt fruit!

BERTRAND: I can do that with my dignity! See? My heart is here but my self-respect is— —apparently on vacation!

Look! I'm juggling air! Each ball is a worry! See how light they are? There goes my mortgage!
