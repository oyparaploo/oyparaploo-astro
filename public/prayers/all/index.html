The Prayers

Shelter

Eighty-three prayers accumulated through six months. Each one a way of asking for help with the work. Not prayers in the traditional sense — not addressed to a deity, not seeking salvation, not recited on a schedule. Instructions that have become incantations. The asking that becomes the making.
