Twenty-Eight Domains

The practice works across every medium that will hold still long enough. Here is the territory map — the domains where fragments become finished work.

Explore the Map
↓
Domain

Writing

Fiction, essays, dispatches, declarations, origin stories. The words arrive as fragments first — sentence-length, sometimes just a phrase. The practice holds them until they find their companions. Good Grounds, the current novel, has been building itself from coffee residue and overheard conversations for three years.

Domain

Visual Art

Mixed media, found objects, photography, installations. Beautiful Garbage begins here — the discarded thing subjected to sustained attention until it reveals what it has been holding. Every visual piece passes through the seven-pass protocol before anyone else sees it.

Enter this domain →
Domain

Sound Work

Field recordings, ambient compositions, voice pieces, radio static. The Noir Song frequencies hold the room where they were made — not the sound of the room, but the feeling of it. Each track is a pressure ghost in audio form.

Domain

Methodology

Beautiful Garbage. Pressure Ghosts. The Middle Eye. The Receiver Turn. These methods did not arrive as theory — they arrived as description, after the fact, of what the hands were already doing. The practice names its moves only after they have proven they work.
