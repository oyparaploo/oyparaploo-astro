On this site a visitor meets the Placings as photographs, so the fingers have to be imagined. Everything was made and photographed at home: in the basement studio, in the garage that stands apart from the house, and in other spots around the property. That is why a workbench, a floor drain or a patch of grass sometimes turns up at the edge of a picture. We left those in. They are where the thing was placed.

These things are made for hands as much as for eyes.

What goes in: cedar and local wood, tin and flashing, steel rod, concrete and pipe, pieces of mirror, rope and knots, wheelchair spokes, ping-pong balls, expanding foam, cloth from Goodwill and cloth from family. An electric blue cord gets woven through. Nails are left half driven, and the old ones are the ones we reach for first.

From Placings, in About →

For a long time our word for all of this was placemaking. Here the section is called Placings, after the last thing we do with a piece: we set it down somewhere it might be found.
