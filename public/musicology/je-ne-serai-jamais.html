JE NE SERAI JAMAIS.

Zélie

Lille, then Paris ... twenty hours a week of contemporary dance at the conservatoire, a body trained to say things without words ... Then, at seventeen, somebody gave her a ukulele, and the words came ... Zélie Claeyssen was born in 2001 and is a dancer who sings, which is why the songs move the way they do ...

She calls what she makes pop chanson, and it borrows the flow of French rap and the textures of electronic music, laid over old-fashioned French songwriting about the heart ... Her second album is called LE CŒUR ET SA DICTATURE — the heart and its dictatorship — with the full stop in the title, and one after this song's title too, as if each were a verdict ... She has said this is the record where she stopped trying to be nice, stopped being easy to like, the way young women in French pop are asked to be ...

JE NE SERAI JAMAIS. means I will never be ... and the sentence stops there, on the period ... Whatever comes after it is the listener's to fill in ... By January 2026 it had been played a million times ...

It's about the dancer ... Twenty hours a week for years teaches a person exactly where a phrase begins and ends, and the songs have that ... they land on their marks ... The full stop after the title is the same thing, a foot coming down ...
