Ichiko Aoba and NATURE

air and NATURE ... Ichiko Aoba, and beside her, credited as a musician, NATURE ... Not a metaphor ... A series called Sounds Right, made with Brian Eno's people, lists nature as a co-artist on the record, and nature's share of the money goes back to the ground ... So the birds on this song are on the payroll haha ...

She has always sung close to the microphone — nylon strings, fingers, a voice kept up high and quiet, as if the whole room were fast asleep ... This one began on a stage, the Royal Albert Hall, and was finished in April with Taro Umebayashi, who has worked beside her for years ... Her account of the session: birds singing, white light scattering like steam ... Ichiko turned to him and asked what he was thinking about right now, and what he told her, a little at a time, became the art ... and world ...

She said she wanted the song to float softly through the air and never settle in one place ... That's the whole instruction, and the record obeys it ... Field recordings from outdoors are mixed in as a layer of the music, not as a backdrop — the air is an instrument here, playing itself ...

Their earlier work together was heavier ... This one, she said, was made in a more relaxed and natural way than anything before it — unguarded, tender ... Out on September 18, 2026, eight days before these words came about ...
