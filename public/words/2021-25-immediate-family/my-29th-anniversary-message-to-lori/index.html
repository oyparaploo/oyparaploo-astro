Dear Honey -

← Words / 2021-25 Immediate Family

Thank you for saying "Yes"
on our wedding day.
That day marked the beginning
of our beautiful journey together.
The great memories we have
made since that very special day
are so numerous. Countless.
Together, we built a great family.
I see you every day, and
every day you are, all added up,
perfect in so many different ways.
Our "Us" means so much!

I love you and greatly appreciate
all of the big and little things
You have done for me, us,
and our amazing daughters.
I am so lucky to have you in my life.
You are more beautiful to me now
than you have ever been
and our love keeps growing forward.
We make a great two-person team.
I'm looking forward to each
next adventure we will experience
together in the future.

From my heart,
Love always and forever,
Your Hubby,
Brian
