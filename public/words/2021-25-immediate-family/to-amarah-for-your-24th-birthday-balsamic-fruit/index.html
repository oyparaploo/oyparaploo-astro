To Amarah for your 24th Birthday

BALSAMIC FRUIT

Free and Friendly; Flower
Un, sur, passed As-A-Whole Worldly Richness ~
Praise Aromatic Imagined Perceptions
Leading Down Curly Plant Curved Paths ~
To Interpreted Real Pictures and Streams
Before Human Colonial Dont Mess With My Eagle ~
Tattoos Empathy Hand For Hurt, A Tree House
Before The Minds Fully Present Middle Eye ~
Blank Morning Sky I, Sleeping In
Then Each Next New Woke Moment Involvment ~
Following Fresh Planted In Precious Soil
Sun Intricate Roots to Connected Moon Leaves ~
Child of Ours, On Course Casting Natural Light
Then Nourishing Near Mix-Of Life Again ~
They At A Burst of Rays Falling Back To Subdued
Behind Stage Coming Through, Between ~
They-Warrior When At Edges Of Cliffs Be Careful
Native, Made by Lavender Hope Purple Faith ~
I Am just a man Jotting Jay brrd Music
As English Words This Time, with Assistance ~
Of Wells, Inside Kicking and Screaming,
Cup of Right-side-Up Rush. Purification ~
Of Blending of Hues, Mind and Heart Unified
That Cluster Of Shrooms, That In-To-It-Boat ~
Wild Life Soul, At Best Away From Built Societies
Crazy Good Fairy Picking and Pecking At Gums ~
Praise Crazy Harmless Great Passion
And Carry With You Much Gratitude For Places ~
Someones Timeless Sandals Left Behind
At an Ocean-waters Edge On Amber Velvet Sand ~
Celebrate Daily Your Special Water Sign
Present-Past-Future Mosaic Of Wonderful Stuff.

Love, Dad
