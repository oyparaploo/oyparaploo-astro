Consoling Ella with a Text and a Phone Call

← Writings / 2021-25 Immediate Family

Ella — your mom and I love you so much for exactly how you are. For how you have always been, and for how you are now. This is a hard time, but with the strength and goodness of Christ you will get through it. You are doing nothing wrong. It’s just circumstances — one of many you will experience in your life.

When I was around your age, I asked a very good friend, much older than me, whether it was worth my time to be making a certain kind of art. I had a studio space next to his office on Raymond Avenue in St. Paul. He said, “If you weren’t meant to create this art, you wouldn’t be creating it. You’d be doing something else.” I have agreed with him ever since.

We see the same truth in you. The way you are, and the way you are living your life — your athletic training, your volunteer work, the Church of the City dance program, the friends you stay close to — is exactly the plan God has for you, or your journey wouldn’t be happening this way. You have so many gifts. It is your gentle journey to keep fulfilling them, guided by the Holy Spirit.

Matthew is carrying something real and heavy. Losing his job after being so sick would be hard on anyone. You can love him with your whole heart and still not be able to carry it for him — that was never yours to carry, and you are not the only one he can lean on. And he has the same church and the same God you do.

We are always just a call away. Always know that we love you so much, and are extremely proud of you for being you.
