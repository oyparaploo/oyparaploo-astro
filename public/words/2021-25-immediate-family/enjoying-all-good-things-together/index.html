Enjoying All Good Things Together

for Amarah on her Birthday 2024

Common things like ~
the buzz of human social activity
… tears and hugs of family and friends
… elements of Mother Nature
that become nourishing food for the mind
and other aspects of the body as a whole.
Representing gift of unconditional
service-to-others-love
that gives meaning and hope.

As all things on earth and the universe
gather and create support
… they enter on foot land where those who
give significance for life reliably exist.
They occupy space of thoughts during the day
and are often or sometimes part of dreams.

Wild Mother Nature Child
curates outside and indoor special places
that become or became adorned with
( so intuitive! ) lovely plants of many types
… best art there is.
They also … as much as possible
… like to be the explorer of
cared for wide open left for wild landscapes
… and existing with mortal soul city gardens
framed with intricate umbrella-like trees.

From seed to flowering
… they exude and display patience
When alone … they gently offer advice
as near silent whispers
on ways to evolve a yard that has no history
or well documented horizon to horizon
sacred held property.

They often lets their hair go wild
in creation’s showering styling sunlight
that with the passage of seconds or hours
produces physical evidence of highlights
along the edges of everything important
… seen and remembered during moments.
Forever Wild Mother Nature Child
lives with compassion
and great ability to enhance all lives.

Happy Birthday Amarah!

Love always,

Dad
