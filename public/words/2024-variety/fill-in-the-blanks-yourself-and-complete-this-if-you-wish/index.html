What human empathy Looks like, if it were an

arrow symbol.

Overalls. aesthetics.

Try something with a primed flat shoe.

blended oranges in the background, and dots to join.

To yore field a yard.

stark wooden chairs captured and framed numerous times.

heavenly rest of a scene

~ fluffy pure white snow.

(head resting on one ear,

one nostril hole over the other)

Sideways smile says ~ Can you really accurately

imagine what deceased heaven looks like?

They are a transplant and descendent of a healthy tribe

that has their community center over

that there hill.

they spent their past building after a loss years

at the edge of a non-reservation theme park.

gathering of sighs.

mo si sections with many fault events.

on a white man's stolen blend of soils.

several comings and reluctant witness accountings.

they also associate their anxiety with too many moves.
