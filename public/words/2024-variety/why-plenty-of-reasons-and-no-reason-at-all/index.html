Why Plenty Of Reasons

← Writings / 2024

and No Reason At All

channelers gotta reminisce, to work around

lots of stuff.

red, white, black, and colorful paths and

roadways. neighborhood long days.

Vietnam Kindred. spirits professional.

industry collar jobs are extremely competitive

right now.

drop and pick time pressures.

represention of this and that hot urgency.

Gotta go. and use the bathroom before

getting back on.

race cars are not rusty old pontiac Pintos.

for sure.

also rose stems perceived as safe science

and international ~ We are not full of beans!!

...No matter what you think!

Kee Ote, we do not have your pain. And pills.

Sorry, but we do understand.

Most definitely.

Data bud ~ Keep searching until you find

the kind of soul you need Again.

look on screen...

the cole bird with snapped neck sold.

We are reasonable people.
