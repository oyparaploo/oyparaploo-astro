Sea Ice Is Melting A Little Bit Faster Every Day

← Words / 2024 Variety

We smell ice melt.

Earth is currently facing a critical situation that demands action. We are on thin ice, and poor actions and inaction have dire consequences. Scientists have warned that we are on the brink of a critical situation. We have a chance to prevent the worst effects of climate change, but we must act.

"Humanity is on Thin Ice, and That Ice Is melting."

We need to set targets to achieve a sustainable future. Our choices in this decade will have long-term consequences, and climate change poses a significant threat to human well-being and planetary health. Let's show compassion and sympathy towards Mother Earth and All Living Beings by taking action to protect our home and ensure a sustainable future.
