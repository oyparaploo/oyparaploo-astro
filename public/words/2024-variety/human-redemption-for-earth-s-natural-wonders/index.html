Human redemption for earth's natural wonders ... All of earth's pure wild places are magnificent wonders ... Regarding how we exist as a very good partner with Mother Earth redemption is still a valuable possibility worth entertaining during the contexts of these unusual times ... Just to warn you there might be tears welled in eyes ... It is a far forward reality that many of the things in natural places that taught us about

dormancies inactive cycles ... comebacks ... and rebirths are extinct now because of human's evolving interests and actions resulting from those interests ... With respect to this we have to try our best to heal our own hearts and ask Mother Earth for forgiveness during our mournful praying ... Enter yourself into that tender realm ( that cannot be literally-actually seen but can exist impressively through faith ) ... With soul-mind-heart combined ... We have only human redemption now ... Making something or any situation better ... By each new measurable action freeing ourselves from what we humans did ... Freeing ourselves from feeling guilt ( worrying or unhappiness that you have caused harm to something and or someone ) that is there in the background and can come forward in mind and cause different intensities of anxiety ... or even debilitating melancholia ... All peops ( from babies to elders ) are our relatives and ancestors because they are all human beings ... We are all together as one big whole as humans and harmful actions during the past to now can be a weight on our heart ... Immortal God and Great Sacred Spirits know that within every earthling are seeds as spiritual subconscious helping us to flow out of not-good habits and awaken the motivation to perceive the capacity of what humble-emboldened hearts can do ... For environmental amends and social justice or abiding by an excellent internal standard for fair behavior
