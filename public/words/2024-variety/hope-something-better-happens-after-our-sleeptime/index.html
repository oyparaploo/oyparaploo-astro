Hope Something More Happens After Sleeptime

← Writings / 2024

Returning home can sometimes be challenging.

Moving overseas also.

re-doing repatriations.

upside down H patre x-Asch Crescent commas

leading to triple beings as unfortunately property.

valuable cultural assetsas also Returns.

refugees temporarily at a safe place on their

seemingly never ending nomad journey.

send that money back!!

...to its legal bald square head owner, The thief,

Tender Hearts.

pit tree with roots on top. Do not send

the woman and child back to their home country

at this fatal time!

back to that massive killings conflict zone

against the mother's wishes.

also, Acknowledge unjust ways black and brown

skin people were treated in the past,

going way back.

This is also our fond farewell to a

private infant, After a public life. Hold hope.

Something most definately will happen. We believe.

After an artificial inteligence rapid eye opens

for sure the data projects will get done ontime.

wonderous accounts somehow outside of

danger's play.

Mercy mercy. half of born longings were still

too little and young to matter now.

adults ~ become aware of it.

realize that flexible defenses never melt. Down.

and thee hooz oohs and ahhs lower their heads

with all do respect each and every case study.

Got it?

light and lovely absolutes. viewing better even

with deeper vestibule eyes shut.

No arguments or explosions, just a few fallouts.
