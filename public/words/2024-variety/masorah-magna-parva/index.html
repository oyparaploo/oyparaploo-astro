Masorah Magna Parva

← Words / 2024 Variety

Decorated and adorned with semi-abstract grotesques. Natural world motifs were contemporized. Scenes depict an "Odd Paradise" brimming with food, which will surely salivate the observer's mind. In one situation, a spirit plays a viol near a wild ox. The message could be more explicit overall, representing the last day of Judgment or simply being a composed and levitating repast. The artwork has various objects, such as rocks, manuscript paper, rusty scissors, a one-eyed deer, a dusty lion, and a discolored donkey. In some ways, much is distorted, and it is also important to mention that a couple of birds look like an evangelist ziz conversing with hovering saints.

( shorter version )
Anointed with semi-abstract grotesques and contemporized motifs. Brimming with food. A spirit plays a viol near a wild ox. Messages could be more explicit, representing a day of Judgment. Levitating and rushing repast. Spiritual rocks, manuscript paper, rusty scissors, royal deer, a dusty lion, and a discolored donkey. A couple of birds look slightly like an evangelist ziz conversing with hovering saints. And, of course, we have the original line art concept included.
