Goals Addiction

← Words / 2024 Variety

Are Personal Goals Addictive for Other Planet Aliens and Earth's Most Recent Immigrants?

At the heart of every living being lies the desire to pursue personal goals, no matter where they come from. The search for meaning and growth is fundamental to existence. Goals are a vital source of motivation, driving us towards our ultimate purpose.

Extraterrestrial life's goals may vary depending on its level of sophistication. While very primitive life forms may focus on basic survival needs, highly intelligent or brilliant aliens may set more complex goals, such as extensive discovery of their surrounding environment and close examination of society.

Immigrants' goals are also shaped by their experiences and surroundings. They may seek safety, opportunity, and belonging in a new environment, but their goals more often shift as they adapt to their new home. They enrich everyone's experience.

Goals come with risks and challenges. Pursuing them can trigger unnecessary extra desire and excitement, and goals can become adversely addictive if they happen to dominate our lives. Achieving a good balance is essential for well-being.

Despite their differences, all living beings share common goals like connection and sustainability. Our goals should foster cooperation, understanding, and empathy if we encounter someone very different from ourselves. Personal goals are a feature of life transcending borders. Our everyday pursuits unite us in a quest for meaning.
