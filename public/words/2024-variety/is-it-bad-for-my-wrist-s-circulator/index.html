Is it Bad for The Wrist's Circulatory Flow to Wear a Heavy Steel Bracelet Rolex Watch?

We want to share some vital information regarding the potential implications of wearing a heavy watch on your wrist's circulatory flow. Whether it's a genuine or a fake Rolex, it's essential to understand the potential effects of wearing a heavy watch
on your wrist.

Your wrist comprises veins and arteries that carry blood back to the heart and away from the heart, respectively. When you wear a heavy watch that tugs at your wrist, it can compress these blood vessels, adversely affecting blood flow. This can also result in bulging or larger veins on the back of your hands, which indicates restricted venous blood flow. Not good. Watches like this can lead to painful sensations from the reduction of arterial flow.

If you wear instead something like a smartwatch or a fitness tracker, it can help you monitor your heart rate and provide information about your heart. While wearing a heavy watch may not seem to cause significant harm, prolonged use with excessive tightness and tugging could negatively impact your wrist's blood flow. It's always a good idea to weigh your choices when buying or choosing, in your bedroom, which piece to wear.
