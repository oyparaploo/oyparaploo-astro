Fun With Word Sounds

Inspiration Board Zero Zero One

Is it okay to combine t's brrd A.I. sandpipers with brrd roots twice

and mint green on emerald green vertical bar? Sure.

Is it okay to combine an A.I prancing on soft velvet shore sandpiper

with an A.I. sweet-as-candy baby owl? Sure.

Is it okay to combine aqua scribbles of a scientist's theories with colorful

roots, a soft yellow rectangle, and a double oo? Sure.

Is it okay to combine an A.I. sweet as honey spice paradise innocent

chipmunk with a sideways brrd and toot? Say again.

Is it okay to combine oot oot prr code, a solid teal vertical bar, two pairs

of ancient Roman sandals, and the corpse of a mummified toddler

fully dressed? Sure.

Is it okay to combine photos of Egyptian desert ruins, a fragile papyrus

Sanskrit manuscript, and a neon pink, purple, orange, yellow, red,

roots brrd composition? Yes.

Is it okay to combine a gray capital letter S, the word goofy,

an A.I. lovely, sweet, lucid flower bunny that has a surreal oversized

eye with an all-in-white Ethiopian black Mary holding a

brown baby Jesus? Sure.

Is it okay to combine a cool hues seascape wallpaper pattern, two

time tinted brittle ancient psychic astronomy diagrams, and an

A.I. cute little possum-like Greenland porcupine? Sure.
