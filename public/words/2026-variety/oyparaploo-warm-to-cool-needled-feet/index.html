Warm to Cool Needled Feet

← Words / 2026 Variety

.

This IS unmistakably Our voice ... The sound of the writing ... the o mouth flattened into Tesseh Tooreh ... and wonders purged or pulled from the heart and soul ... That's something pure identifying ... directing itself ...

.

The words ... here ... belong to a "diva pantheon foil" ... the thing we set up to avoid dethroning ... thwart side up ... Built from anti conservatory measure vocabulary ... Unless it's reclaiming ... it points at Accept and Harmonize ... melting into sensing warm to cool needled feet ... If that's our intent ... well ... the transparency over plain opacity eases our concern ... It took a very bluesy rhythm of beats to encourage us to hunt for the "ultimate centering source read" ... that has buried and ... out in the open ... c(h)ords woven ... so that the receiver can arrive at the music without too much emphasis of cryptic meaning ... As sound it sings ...

.

But still ... there's a contradiction lurking around ... and we think it's living ... Hand over the "here's how" qualities ... tension is the thing that has to resolve ... without sitting on one plane ... Eight are technical feats ... vocal agility, whistle notes, tessitura, breath control, octave range, operatic flair, improvisation, impeccable pitch ... the conservatory and competition measure ... the concept of a "supremely wondrous" voice ... The other four ... emotional expression, timbre, influential phrasing, hypnotic depth ... are aspects tasted that can't be scored ... Beauty isn't the feats ... it's the felt ... The Foil left standing.
