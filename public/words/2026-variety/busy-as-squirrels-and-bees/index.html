Busy as squirrels and bees human beings

← Words / 2026 Variety

Our vomit writings ... sight ... smell stuff. Before I cried. Then I died ... became fully alive again.

Violence over there. We can make a body ... immortal spirit smile ... wonderfully tired and still sort of able.

Best wishes are all sensory ... all senses ... ear ... nose ... mouth ... throat. Touch to the fingers.

Anything and everything trying to salute the flag but having a very hard time. This is America. Crazy stuff ... all the crazy stuff. Putting up walls. Gates.

We mortals are the foolish and obsessive train ... over and over ... energy spent.

When we cause a human to come out of their dance and skin ... that is when you got it right. Trying to get it right. That is what perfection is ... trying to get right.

Oui oui oui oui oui oui.

Busy busy busy bodies ... trained ... practice practice practice for your legacy and give your work more legs. Lots of insect legs.

We were the fools in the obsessive train. Spent. Woke up the next day bright and early ... the sun up ... ready to go ... lunch pail under the arm.

February 4, 2021 (voice) / February 4, 2026 (edit)
