Oh Coherent Weakness

But charged mood reading as an axis that splits fields Mid-journey ... Sweet O'cinematic territory ... homeland warmth ... spot on instinct for very quiet color shadows and light ... that models grit and lack ... aimed at a vibe appropriately placed ... Which is exactly how we show mysterious prospects ... Mood bored? ... No worries ... raw consistency can hold coherent weakness ... Indistinguishable from a baked-in dreamlike capture ...
