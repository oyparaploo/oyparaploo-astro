We Look Around Every Day At Things We Did Not Make

We look around every day at things we did not make ... could not make ... That will not still be here after they fall apart ... And the daily noticing of this ... the shape of the sunlight on a wall ... the bird on a wire that just flew a very long route we can't imagine ... the neighbor going to a landfill party we were not invited to ... slowly disassembles the ponderings of belonging ... until what is left is just the witnessing itself ... which turns out to be the only thing that was ever ours to begin with ...
