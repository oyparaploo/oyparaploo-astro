Prayer and Soil

Big Problems Take Longer To Fix

( blackfin icefish )

So like when you accidentally put your t-shirt on backwards ... then as a result lost it.

You need to do something about this. What do you recommend. Hot dish.

Full morning of lamenting ... making prayers for all unnecessary violence caused by mostly men. Males. Is there not enough increasing climate change caused natural violence. Do you need to add to it. Men ... do you need to add to it.

You can not change the world immediately. It is going to take a lot longer than we think.

*

The best ways to reduce chronic inflammation are exercise regularly ... eat healthy ... and get at least seven hours or more of sleep every night. Is seven hours enough.

*

Earth frigid ocean waters outlier. Bottom dweller ... ultra super duper edge Arctic ocean waters fish species.

Thrives with no fish scales ... no red blood ... but instead blood that when examined looks as clear as clean water ... and a skin covered skull so thin its brain can be semi seen by any naked mortal human eye.

This species of fish absorbs oxygen through its skin and has a heart that is four times larger than all red blooded species of fish.

Blackfin icefish.

*

Solar radiation snowballs. To see ice that was there and is now gone for good.

Squiggle squiggle I miss you. Shimmy squiggle. I miss you ... I miss you.

Warming uncertainties. Normal ice shelf ... a raft is on the horizon of the sea floor with projections of a human bird's eye observing a crowd ... or better yet ... enormous school of silver always on the move.

*

Open to possibilities after our early morning daily strong mug of coffee.

R-o-d ... r-o-w ... r-o-d ... o ... k.

Keen bird helpful for many Earth mortals ... full sensory impressive expressive.
