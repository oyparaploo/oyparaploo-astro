notes: |
Ninth piece of the May 2 session. Companion to Who Is The
One.

Built through a different vector than the standard
fun-process flow. After Who Is The One was metabolized,
Clod offered eight diagnostic options across two diagnostic
questions about the piece (the imperial bias line and
the critical engagement line). Reach took the diagnostic-
options conversation itself and metabolized it into a new
piece — not as choices to apply to Who Is The One, but as
raw material for a companion piece teaching about how the
practice handles body versus theory in writing.

This is the o'metabolism vector — the conversation about
the piece becoming the next piece. Same vector as Evidence
of Agitated Caps Struggling (4/30), Yes Chair (4/30), and
the eleventh-and-twelfth movements of Twah Trust the Kess
Cachoo Reader (5/02). Each of these turned a meta-
conversation about the work into the work.

Title — Traces of Refusal Might Be the One — pulls *Trace
of Refusal and The One* together. The diagnostic-image
and the figure from Who Is The One threaded into one
title. Might be held open as possibility, not
declaration. The piece's argument lives inside the title's
might. Plural Traces — more than one trace, the body
keeping multiple records of refusal.

New compound vocabulary and figures:

- Flip the agency / Flip the bias — the diagnostic
moves named as flip-actions, doing what correct or
fix couldn't.
- Bias as the shaper / The One as the shaped — the
paired position-claim. Renames the relationship
between bias and One in two breaths.
- *The body shifting odd as resistance, not as
compliance* — the corrected reading of the line
that pulled in Who Is The One. The strangeness of
the body's movement is the trace, not the symptom.
- Trace of refusal — the body's record. Singular
here, plural in the title. The body keeping a mark
of what it refused.
- *Let the body do the work the theoretical words
were doing* — instruction line. Names the practice's
preference for body-figures over theory-vocabulary.
- Register rupture — what *intersections of
perception and power* does inside Who Is The One.
The diagnostic-vocabulary version of what the
practice elsewhere calls a register-break.
- Sparse vocabulary the imagery can't carry — what
the diagnostic itself requires. Some material can't
be carried by figures. Some material requires its
own vocabulary.
- *Some readers in our constellation would receive
their own native vocabulary with excitement* —
who the register-rupture is reaching toward. The
academics, the organizers, the readers whose first
language is theory-vocabulary.
- Who knows — the held uncertainty. The piece
refusing to settle whether the rupture is craft or
necessity. Both are true close pattern threading
from Yes Chair (4/30).

Punctuation moves:

- Ellipsis-spacing throughout.
- Who knows held as its own breath inside the
argument. Refusal of resolution.
- Single-paragraph flow under the essence-arriving
rule.

Image threading:

- The One threading from Who Is The One.
- Trace of refusal extends the May 2 cluster's
witnessing-and-inventory thread (We Look Around
Every Day, We Happen To Be At Our Most Payless
Time). The body keeps inventory of what it refused.
- Both are true close pattern threading from Yes
Chair (4/30).

Companion to: Who Is The One (same session). Pair-thinking
running across the two — original metabolization and
metabolization-of-the-diagnostic.
