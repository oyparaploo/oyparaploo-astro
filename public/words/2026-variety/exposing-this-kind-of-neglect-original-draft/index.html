Exposing This Kind of Neglect ( original draft )

Same words ... two different directions ... "Failure to protect" is the heaviest charge in family court — a parent can lose her children to it ... Did you know that government has no constitutional duty to protect a person from a harm that it knows about? ... Even a restraining order carries no enforceable right to be enforced ...

Right now every state in our nation defines a parent's neglect with this list: failure to provide adequate food, shelter, medical care, supervision, education ... Further up the chain — nutrition programs are being cut, housing waitlists are years deep, millions more uninsured — and the definer as words fails its own definition ...

Conditions become the charge ... The empty fridge ... a child is home alone because a shift can't be dropped ... Neglect does not include failure caused by financial inability — and yet more new cases arrive anyway ...

Abuse hides ... neglect files itself ... A budget is the one place a shrug gets written down — every cut has a date, vote, signature ... Another passive voice does the escaping ... but the record exists ...

Government is the only caretaker that collects its fee in advance ... by law ... with penalties for missing a payment, then decides afterward how much care the fee bought ...

Every state orders teachers ... nurses ... and doctors to report a parent's suspected neglect — it adds up to millions of calls a year ... each opening a file ... There is no mandated reporter for the government's neglect: a teacher has to call about the hunger she sees ... and has no number for a lunch program that was cut ... Same child ... same hunger ...

Refusals costs more than the care ... Prevention is the cheaper purchase — neglect cannot shelter under scarcity ... The government always pays afterward ... larger ... when the payment buys nothing back ... It's their insane preference ...

The best-documented neglect there has ever been ... Clear risk ... ignored ... is at its purest with Mother Nature ... The warnings sat in writing in the oil companies' own internal research ... Decades of documented risk before the escalation now has arrived as extreme ... drought, wildfires, floods ... The weather now in more places reads like a neglected child's bids for attention ... each louder than the last ...

Witnesses were the protection plan ... Checks and balances were the founders' own case plan written against power itself — oversight is protection ... and dismantling it is the oldest move a neglectful hand makes — closing doors so nobody can sees in ...

Posterity ... Our nation's founding list — justice, tranquility, general welfare — and it names the children outright ... The children are in the first sentence of the constitution ... Neglect is a breach of the oldest promise ...

The US is now split ... When caretaker and ward are the same body ... neglect turns inward — a nation neglecting its people is a body declining to feed itself ... an immune system reading its own cells as the threat ... Self-neglect as the national diagnosis ...

Social Security cut elderly poverty by more than half ... clean-air law counts saved lives in the hundreds of thousands ... the wire reached the last farm because government carried it — so this is neglect by a capable caretaker ... which every moral tradition weighs heavier ... and "government simply can't" is foreclosed by its own record ...

And the protection system's front line is itself neglected — caseworkers carrying double loads at low wages — the removal machine is wearing the rescuer's uniform ... So the want flowing underneath may not be more intervention ... It may be care much more so than custody ...
