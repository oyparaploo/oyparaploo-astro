Yes, We're All In

← Words / 2026 Variety

&nbsp;

At our softest ... we are least dangerous ... to say plainly ... Iz paktos bea da ee deh ayer ... Confession Sweetness ... hides ... Free from risk is not harmless ... Admits the dial during every setting ... where the needle rests ... We come with Non Zero Fragment ... fitting talk for armor ... conversation about club fitting bodies before they harden ... into its persistent errands ... Anger ... like a hermit ... hangs out inside menacing mammals ... and easing drains the crossness ... Soft answers turneth away wrath ... circling looping ... Our good ink asks ... Am I a safe lamb or or harmless lion? ... Neither ... just least ...

&nbsp;

Mear ichess di-eann ... Elder to child ... no need for missiles ... Cooing crib mirror and unknown kind tool created our wardrobe ... Pet without teeth showing its belly is the oldest trust there is ... Disarmament followed by laying down of arms ... literally ... Oddest wearables now rule Paris and NYC soft smiling charitable runways ... The softest thing and lifeform in the world is water carving a valley as a song ... lowering to meet us where we are ... God of goodness ... patient ... all knowing ... from the beginning to now ... land, lake, river, sea ... Creator ...

&nbsp;

Deh peah ... ya rilie maye dah ... du bay-eh ... eh reye eh zin-veeeee aye ... veee aye eh ... hah binn ah deah dah don da ... Each forward step of kindness ... sinks in heavenly clouds of hearts ... Reh lehkay fealah ... Walking rising soul ... Maaa aee hahhh ...

&nbsp;

This all is one ... stillness ... Nothing to argue about ... Lost into it ... We swore this was once before recited quietly out loud ... backstage ... from the anatomy of a human back ... Speaking to a severely damaged crown and stars choir ... In a pew body ... of work ... where the inventor remains indefinite and the cast goes home ...

&nbsp;

Salt water so sour and sweet ... Eeye dit en noe et leh hhh-aye ah-eir heir ... Teacher ... turned inside out front-woman ... for the people ... who refuse to smile for selfies ... Grieving nihilists are no fun ... And now for something completely different ... a wink verbatim holds hands with an ache ... Mun deh shee sen bah daye ...

&nbsp;

Honest least distinctive accents cost very little ... keep plain living things more visible amongst today's untouchable noise ... Flattened and pulled use it for the art anyway ... Rubbery silly meaninglessness is yours to set as a lodge ... Boards turned into or became lovely beach reads with plenty of knots ... oval imperfections formed where branches are embedded in heavy suitcases ...

&nbsp;

More often ... really sore backs are not able to survive a stabbing ... shake off the switchblade ... Mej wheen eh tabbo lein ah zak lea ... Heart is love held with account and accountability ... Not tilted woundedness ... Close the coronet and batter on a melancholy piano song ... that streams from posteriors of humanness ... Flagged as ours to share and never ignore ...
