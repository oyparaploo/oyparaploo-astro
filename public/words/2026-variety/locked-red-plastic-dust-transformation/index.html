When There Is Red Plastic Dust Over The Sky

← Words / 2026 Variety

When There Is Red Plastic Dust Over The Sky ... Reign Pertains To Endings ... A dented can of beans ... A threadbare sweater ... A stray coffee lid ... Being With The Worldly Ones You Love ... Grandma fragrances so sweet buried in night flower garden polyester shirts ... And Tolerating The Dictating Ones You Disagree With ... Her father busies himself with faucets as though plumbing could mend hearts ... Letting Faith Guide Us Through Storms And Other Dark And Heavy Events ... Glistening golden throat songs sung by a holy desert ruin prisoner ... Cotton bolls ...

Brrd Roots ... Iconic ... Legendary ... Powerful All Senses Involved Musicality ... A boy is detained for stealing a cardamom cookie ... Evoking Different Moods ... Shifting Of Themes ... The scarf that smells faintly like a memory of someone you miss ... Mirroring Reconveying Consumerism Or Detachment From Earth's Natural Environments ...

All Types Of Evidence Of Human Efforts Remade ... She fell from her 34th-floor apartment window ... Countless Iterations Of The Same Things ... Every Small Simple Or Large Complex Work Has A New Tidal Wave ... Disposable diapers cradling the softest beginnings of life yet clinging stubbornly to the land for 400 years ...

Artful Displays Impactfully Symbolizing An Overwhelming Presence Of Plastics And Other Types Of Artificial Pollution ... Fibers stir with invisible radiation ... Allergy-laden folds brushing the skin ... Lyrical Manufactured Chemical Solids ... Scotch whisky legos ... A dread wig clumped with adhesives worn thin by time holds on stubbornly imperfect and sacred ...

Exploring And Explaining Current Ways Of Living ... Where Moments Are Often Fleeting And Things That Are A Part Of All Of Those Incidences Are Disposable ... Doughnuts disappeared into diners ... Little League uniforms stitched with names ...

Within Any Walled Or Wall Less Border Society An All Senses Involved Song Can Also Be A Critique Of A Trend ... Whole in the brokenness ... Glowing in aloneness ...
