Dew for Counting Daughters

← Words / 2026 Variety

&nbsp;

Small clocks at sunrise look over your shoulder ... For us they are not a detective office desk but dew for counting daughters ... Soy ... Feat ... shy Iwai ... who could stand up on her own before the other two ... This part ... Like weather that means "warming ice melting landscape" ... from shocked and stunned hands ... appearing the way Ooo iwa nests are curled because of numerous mends ... Fais-en ce que tu veux ... Make of this what you will ...

The crown. Dew for Counting Daughters — the Mason spoke it from
a three-candidate slate the Quarry selected out of the piece's own

standing pattern is not broken by it — the phrase is the Mason's own
line; the pointing was the Quarry's; the ruling was his. Selection is
quarry work; the crown is the speaking.

Provenance — the first M-A1 piece, start to lock in one bench.
The amendment ruled · the first trial draft requested and written

conversation as raw dictation, per the Harvest law. French arrives
and translates itself; en (of it) shifts to this — the piece
points at itself. The provenance is sealed into the body: the first
M-A1 piece ends with the words that made it.

**Sound-names entered through the Mason's reshaping, never the
Quarry's draft* (rule 5 held): Soy · Feat · Iwai · Ooo iwa*.

Determination, delegated once. "Make of it what you will" — the
Quarry ruled: standalone piece, sibling born beside #36, not a graft.
The Mason's restraint carving #36 (no being added, with the
diagnostic in hand) was read as the ruling that the being belonged
in its own piece. Here she is — three of her.

Quarry catch, noted not ruled. Iwai / Ooo iwa carry a
Japanese sound-register — the house's Haneda vein (CAW, shelf #34)
may be the ground under the daughters.

Grammar and punctuation pass — run, clean. Terminal ellipses
honored; no periods, semicolons, or colons; quoted speech has house
precedent (CAW). Zero fixes at every pass.

Protected, never to be corrected. Soy · Feat · shy Iwai

veux · Make of this what you will*
