We Acknowledge Humans Have Ten Primary Senses

← Writings / 2026

Traditional External and Internal/Somatic

Sight/Vision (Opthalmoception): Through eyes perception of light, color, depth.
Hearing (Audioception): Through ears perception of sound waves and vibrations.
Smell (Olfacoception): Through nose chemical perception of molecules.
Taste (Gustaoception): On tongue and in mouth chemical perception of soluble sweet, salty, sour, bitter, umami, molecules.
Touch (Tactioception): Through skin receptors perception of pressure, texture, and other types of physical contact.
Body Awareness (Proprioception): Unconscious awareness of where our body parts are in relation to each other.
Balance (Vestibular Sense): Perception of balance, spatial orientation, acceleration managed by fluid in our inner ear.
Temperature (Thermoception): Ability to sense heat and cold, on our skin and in our core.
Pain (Nociception): Sensation of nerve irritation or tissue damage, critical warning system.
Internal State (Interoception): Awareness of our internal organs pertaining to hunger, thirst, racing heartbeat, need to use the restroom.
