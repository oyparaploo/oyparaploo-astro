# The Big River Is Something Very Deep That Runs Inside of Us

This is not the river we crossed once ... while on a bridge in Provence ... not the Columbia River in the photograph ... not the Spree River we read about in a cast iron gated park in Manhattan ... Stop fishing ... The slow moving water that has been there since before we knew it had a ... current ... that doesn't turn over stones ... near the shadows of a cynical and morally ambitious crime ...

Rough nails Nile carries everything that has ever been put into it ... including the things we tried to innocently hoard ... and the things that ... in a very peculiar way ... rolled in by accident ... And the stuff that came in upstream from raw players we will never meet during this lifetime ... The strange thing about this St. Lawrence River is that we only notice it in our mind's eye when we really slow down and permit ourselves to rest without that nicked up phone ...

There it is again at the edge of the dining room table screen up ... Little Roe River flowing from Giant Springs moves the way it always sounds in a mystery novel ... an orange yellow hair Low and continuous Something Else ... that turns every which way ... to be the floor underneath a streambed ... everything cobbled as wet silt and clay.
