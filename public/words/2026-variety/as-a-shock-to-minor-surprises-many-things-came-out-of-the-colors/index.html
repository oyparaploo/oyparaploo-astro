Prayer and Soil

as a shock to minor surprises many things came out of the colors

present ... present three ... what you are experiencing now is a major renewal of your spirit ... the kind that is positive ... and not only for human life on planet Earth ... but also fairness for all ... long overdue ...

it has been stained by earth soil ... Holy Ghost knows ... it all has ... in certain ways ... been stained by earth soil ... and therefore ... it is all good conflict ... and then wonderful rainbow ...

do not just tell me about it ... do not just talk about it ... show us ...

the works are meant for changing previous perceptions ... reconciling reverence for other types of nourishing life ... enjoy life ... and also be a keeper of life ...

on me ... meteor stone burned a hole through our dining room table ... and burned the floor ... and embedded into the concrete basement floor

February 11, 2021 (voice) / February 2026 (edit)
