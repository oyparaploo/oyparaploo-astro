Working With the Machine

Not A Perfect Warm Togetherness Score Thank You

Namaste poster unity ... the kind suspended slightly off kilter above a tea tray ... Skipped ... because the differences are still erased ... as a polite kind of arson ... with matches that won't strike ... Smoothing friction becoming uneasy ... sandpaper surfaces vanishing when a harsh disagreeing numbskull walks in and brings with him the opposite of "patience is important now" ... Wei ... the short of it ... effort drops when we act on something without internalized our most helpful phrases ... Remembering before a rear-view catches up ... the We Have Some Courage frame is too often a political divisive shut-in ... door pulled by friendly hands ... Togetherness as only an outfit ... or trap with a triangular flag at the end of a thin stick planted in balsa wood ... FOUR TAILS ... swooshing left and right ... This is the one we think might have the best features for our practice ... Appendages that know all the routes of our journey ... connected also to rattle river and the greatest Fennec Fox ear of your choosing ... High frequency lug that can hear fine sand move under the roots of a plant ... A patient's street clothes or nurse's uniform are within the same absurd field or phenomenon ... showing up in a pencil written register ... Where no torn corners need to be taped to the main section ... in order to understand what it's saying ... Neither replaces the other ... Combining destroys ... and the destruction looks like neatness ... So ... this stance has to mean something other than a minor collapse that doesn't lean or twist.
