The Biography Stayed While the Insights Went Out Searching

← Writings / 2026

Same child same black soil ... same cross at six Grandma framed and hung where she slept ... Same Ukrainian dolls holding receivers to their ears ... Same garage same wood ... same cold to warming ...

Every automatic biography we ever wrote agrees on this ... the story never moved ... It was the book that couldn't sit still ...

2010 ... Brian Otto Defluxos with a web ... Twenty days later the same biology ... Defluxos A Gain ... because something in it was wrong ... And writing it twice would find the error ... It didn't ... 2008 ... Otto Desoulos and Bomayka Sens ... nom de plume near a migration route ... 2022 ... ottootto Life also locally known as Brrd Roots ... Cool Hot Dot Soul ... Sky Golden Feathers ... Walk from Shadows six names ... like a raincoat rack in a shelter where nothing stays for long ...

Then we made the whole wearables wardrobe ... out of colors for trust and urgency ... How a brand smells ... a bouquet is rearranged several times ... once by a great aunt who was only a figment ... That's a lot of tailoring for a body that kept crawling out of a fitting room ...

Then every hat thrown to the ceiling at once, floating for weeks ... Bo Tempo ... Tone Riot ... B. Oscillate ... Bri Ant Bantó ... Brio Natto ... Botto ... Baby Ahht Zee ... B. Doo Du Solus ... Of all of them ... Metallic Lime Green Huffy ... captures by far the most intrigue ...

It was Oyparaploo then Oy fell off and became Paraploo, The Elder ... Umbrella rain everybody wanted ... And this September ... Brian Otto and Paraploo ... was etched or inscribed on many different types of surfaces ... as The Most Beautiful Garbage on Earth ... After the rest of the underground names went through the wash the Trash kept or held ...

Un nom n'est pas une maison ... A name is not a house ... it's a smoking jacket we wear to the front door when someone rings ... So ... here's the decades long automatic biography ... and it fits ... because we stopped trying to fit in ... Fais-en ce que tu veux ... Make of it what you will ...
