Terms for a Person Who Is a Fan
A person who is a fan is most commonly called a fan, but there are many synonyms and slang terms depending on the context and level of devotion.

Common and general terms

Fan – A person with strong interest or admiration for something or someone, such as a celebrity, sports team, book, or TV show Wikipedia.

Supporter – Often used in sports or politics to describe someone who backs a team or cause Wikipedia.

Enthusiast – Someone with strong interest and active engagement in a subject Merriam Webster.

Admirer – A person who respects and looks up to someone Merriam Webster.

Devotee – A deeply committed and loyal supporter of a person, cause, or belief FluentSlang+1.

Slang and more specific terms

Stan – An extremely devoted and often obsessive fan, especially of celebrities or fictional characters FluentSlang+1.

Groupie – A dedicated fan, often of a musician or band, who follows them closely and may seek personal interaction FluentSlang+1.

Superfan – A fan who goes above and beyond in support, often with deep knowledge and active participation FluentSlang.

Buff – A knowledgeable and passionate enthusiast, often in niche areas WordHippo.

Freak or Fangirl/Fanboy – Informal terms for a passionate fan, often with a strong personal connection WordHippo.

Fundi – A slang term for a fan, especially in online or gaming communities WordHippo.

Usage tips

Use fan for general, non-slang contexts.

Use stan, superfan, or devotee for more intense or dedicated fans.

Use supporter in sports or political contexts.

Use groupie or buff for niche or informal settings.

In short, the most straightforward answer is fan, but depending on the level of passion and the context, you might choose stan, superfan, devotee, or other synonyms to better describe the person.
