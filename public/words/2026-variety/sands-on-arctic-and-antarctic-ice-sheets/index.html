Prayer and Soil

Sands on Arctic and Antarctic Ice Sheets

Tragic Human Consequences of Climate Change ... How can we help not just ourselves ... but also others who are suffering a lot? ... God instruct us to more so face outward ... If the sky was nothing but drools of gruel ... If oligarchs and everyone else knew, they wouldn't ignore the fact that in America's southwest region lakes and rivers ( that provide fresh and clean drinking water ) are shrinking at a much faster rate today ... Our feet that can't follow are ... for some insane or absurd reason ... causing our cheeks to swallow tomorrow's larynx apples whole ...

Don't you worry about a thing ... Say Key Sailor ... shaye cah plea ... dah play la ... le tome ... Don't you worry about a thing ... Dee Chew ... doan tree ah Men'Nah ... De Klahh Vess ... Don't you worry about a thing ... Soan Sah Dawn Chorus ... before the boat arrives ... Don't you worry about a thing ... There's a rat mill that has its mitts on ... What should we do, Baby? ... Don't you worry about a thing ... There's a light switch embedded in the bark of a sequoia ... there's a disturbing hush of a whisper gash on my shoulder ... Don't you worry about a thing ... child ... The rain will come soon and fill up our bone dry lakebeds and riverbeds ...
