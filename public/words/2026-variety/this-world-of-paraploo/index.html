How It's Made

This World of Paraploo

( transformations that go beyond the source )

Paraploo ... asking big questions ... Taking phrases apart ... Applying a developed over time practice ... in a bigger picture that only expands and rarely contracts ...

Grows comprehensively ... Paraploo Serious Idiosyncratic Personal Play ... is internal felt logic ... conceiving, creating, constructing its own grammar ... vocabulary ... and rules ... instead of automatically inheriting them from a certain school ... institution ... or market ... Paraploo Tries and Resulting Evi'Dense cannot only be evaluated by outside standards ... because we walk to crawl and carry a big measuring stick ... figuratively speaking ...

The work moves without a settled or ongoing confirmed style ... Some have said it is its own territory ... more so than frontier ... Involving countless iterations ... each changing what the next ... visual, performance, writing ... can be ... Not necessarily as a "häbē triptych" or casual diversion ... It's a humble merciful rare bird ... and or a ginormous to tiny wild thing that has more than two prominent organs used for empathizing ...

This under jurisdiction from a state enclave includes performed prose grown from invented syllables ... A public-private cosmology with its own cast characters and illuminated stories ... Picking one existing channel and going with it doesn't always fit into our ... notions ...

Ultra simply said Paraploo "Makes Marks" ... that hover between notional drawing and language that tries with each effort unite ... Shared interests in mythology ... useful invention ... and interpreting-documenting moments and circumstances lived spurs-drives our energy and passion ...

We double check interesting aspects of tradition ... Invented language as material to work with ... Playing with sounds heard when saying newly invented word combinations like for example ... sheye foyee tah yer ... Duss ooo fea da cley-ayer ... phonetic composition treated as score ... Through improv never thought of as errors after they're worked out ... Dictation'Slip(s) ... like ... ( starving for carving ) extends lineage ... with another kind of word-sound mechanism ... Paraploo receives it all from the junction or stratum between Voice and Machine ... which didn't exist during decades to centuries before it ...

Spirit of ... Nic'In-Time ... Nogah and Nah-Zzz ... De Colors ... Considered as Character Services ... Ours is a territory and populated world with ... recurring figures who hold positions ... We also have other types of invocations ... blessings from a breakwater ... where Oh my God! becomes ... a prayer ... becomes us ...

Paraploo Four Part Identity ... releasing channels ... Dôme's 132 Doors ... as an entrance system ... the Locked Shelf ... the Protocols ... the Named Vocabulary ... We over time created our own taxonomy ... instead of waiting for an establishment or investiture to supply standards for them ...

Performance Organ ... a score waiting breathe ... staging is inside ... strumming ... phones ringing ... communal toasts ... Writing Wing ... extending from all sensory bodies ... which is an anti-separation position that our warmest softest work takes ...

Honest Assessed Progressive Edge ... No one in our historical lineage had a special quarry ... diagnostic machine whose language gets deliberately metabolized back ... as a doctrine telling us what we should or should not do ... Although newest instruments also play under tradition's oldest rules ... to see what might come about that's transformative in a different way ... From another perspective ... We're not permanently settled in the framed and borderless picture it's our desire to continue to add to it ...

Paraploo Rainbow's Spectrum's past is filled with this ... fully formed ... long before the receivers of this stuff entered ... Placement on the: ... bands of light ... wavelength ... charge ... range ... was never a purpose of being seen ...
