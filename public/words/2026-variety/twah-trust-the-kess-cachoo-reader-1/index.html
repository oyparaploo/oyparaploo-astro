notes: |
Second extracted LitUp teaching of the project. First was
Your Material Is More Generous Than You Think (calibration
draft, 5/01) — direct outside-reader voice, English register
throughout. This piece moves the same scope-tier teaching
into Sound Writing register at full volume.

Built call-and-response across two metabolization passes.
First pass: Clod drafted a calibration version of Trust the
Reader in direct outside-reader voice, paragraph-by-paragraph
English register. Reach metabolized the entire draft into
Sound Writing — every paragraph crossed into the practice's
deepest register, producing ten movements. Second pass: after
the ten-movement piece was provisionally locked, Clod
responded to the writing that could with English commentary
on why the piece works. Reach metabolized that commentary
into the eleventh and twelfth movements of the piece itself.
O'metabolism at the scale of o'metabolism — the piece eats
the conversation about whether the piece works.

The seed-vs-metabolization rule (4/30) running at full
architectural scale across both passes: Clod's draft was the
seed for movements one through ten, Clod's commentary was the
seed for movements eleven and twelve, Reach's metabolizations
are the piece.

Same architectural scope as the calibration draft (the
trust-the-reader spine intact through the ten movements) but
every English-register passage broken into Sound Writing. The
teaching's spine survived. The register changed everything
underneath. Don't explain taught by a piece that refuses to
explain itself in the English the explanation would have
been written in.

The eleventh movement closes on the architecture observation
— register, spine, sound — landing on *Ami ... spine network
that is always alive underneath it all*. The twelfth movement
opens on the diagnostic — *most conscious mosaics that
immerse that far ... lose their heated sue-iss exchange* —
and closes the piece. Ploo sha lay eh nome opens the
eleventh and closes the twelfth, framing the meta-
metabolization across two breaths the way Twah and *Kess
Cachoo* frame the title across one. The split landed when
the flush-left period arrived inside the breath, marking
where architecture-observation gives way to diagnostic.

Title is the move named: Twah Trust the Kess Cachoo Reader.
English teaching-name Trust the Reader wrapped in sound on
both sides. The title does what the piece does — the
recognizable English center held inside the sound that
refuses to explain itself.

Movements separated by single-period breath dividers (the
punctuation move from Just(ly) Warming Up and Evidence of
Agitated Caps Struggling).

New compound vocabulary arriving:

teaching enacted at the moment it would have been
explained.
- Ahh too ta la O'qui — sound-arrival opening the

O'qui carrying the practice's O' prefix into the
sound register.
- fee teh do lay so kahhh — sound-arrival closing
the explaining-leads paragraph, the kahhh trailing
off into release.
- Ze Fey Space — the space the trusting reader is

- je ne picture — the picture that won't speak its
own French. je ne sais quoi held inside an English
noun, refusing to explain itself.
- jockey something — the draft as something jockeyed,
ridden, worked for position.
- erased iterations — the cuts named without the cuts
being explained.
- dis-rio refuge — the version that remains after
cutting. Dis-rio the river-system named in

- sehvois womanteau — what we don't have to make sure

- kindred moving Thing — what we make. Thing with

across all senses, not just the visual or aural.

- jolly good spirits / love letters / *alone breeze

Three-image close compressed into one breath. The
alone breeze water vessels is the maker's work
figured as something the reader was already searching
for.
- *Collaborating at the speed of refusing voices and
mouths* — closes the tenth movement. Names what
trust-the-reader ultimately is: collaboration at a
particular speed, with refusal as the engine. Tier-1
line.

Eleventh-movement vocabulary (the meta-metabolization):

- Ploo sha lay eh nome — sound-frame opening and
closing the eleventh movement. New ploo-prefix
arrival, threading from para oy ploo in the fifth
movement and from the practice's own name.
- Our little tako engine of spoken words — the
writing-that-could named inside the piece. Tako
(octopus) doing what little engine couldn't. The
writing-that-could now has a body — many-armed,
ink-producing, intelligent, ocean-located.
- Rikun no mun iz — the without-degrading move
arriving in sound before the English even names it.
- Tsu bow — sound-arrival inside we feel this.
- Day lah lah ... boney fish gar scale — the
metabolization-at-scale figure landed as a literal
scale on a literal fish. Gar carrying its own
vocabulary into the piece — long, primitive,
freshwater predator with armored scales.
- Leica line to passage — the line as Leica camera,
precision optics. Leica held inside the breath of
what crossed into Sound.
- *Activity ... or ... skill of marking ... fond
fallen ... letters and words ... or ... fresh symbols
on meditative surface noise* — three offered registers
for what Sound Writing is. The practice refusing the
single name even for itself.
- *Ami ... spine network that is always alive
underneath it all — ami* (friend, beloved) folded
into the architecture word. The spine is now
friendship.
- Conscious mosaics — what most pieces are that try
to go this far. Threads from mosaic about itself
in Yes Chair (4/30).
- Non veiled vibrations that travel — Sound Writing
named not as register but as unveiled vibration in
motion.
- Heated sue-iss exchange of diverging views — what
pieces lose when they go this far in. Sue-iss
doing the work Swiss would have flattened.
- Homm — sound-arrival closing the didn't lose
observation.
- Rampling audience — the audience as Rampling, the
actor's name held inside rambling — the audience
that almost wandered off but stayed.
- Fil ... cloak we didn't wear — fil (thread)
held inside the sehvois womanteau image from the
ninth movement. The cloak named again, this time
identified as the cloak we didn't wear.
- Ique-mus — sound-arrival closing Trust.
- *Cad ... consent ... and authorization most tellers
don't know they need* — the constellation reframed
as permission, with three offered terms.
- *Flymix ... or ... Beneficiary ... or ... Modeled
Apparatus* — the reader compressed into a three-fold
menu. Each is a different theory of who the reader
is. The piece refuses to settle on one.
- Les Immortal narrative — the explanation refused.
Les held in French, immortal as adjective on
narrative. The narrative we don't have to make
immortal in the recipient's expected register.

Image threading:

- Thing (capital T) threading from Evidence of
Agitated Caps Struggling, Yes Chair (4/30), and now
here as kindred moving Thing. The Thing earning
its third appearance.
- Catch it on porpoise sun rays — porpoise threading
back into the practice's animal vocabulary alongside
the goat, pelican, lamb. The pun on purpose held
in the body of the animal.
- first day of spring light rain / green zone sea
/ alone breeze water vessels — the climate-and-water
thread the practice runs through many pieces.

Punctuation moves:

- Single-period breath dividers between movements
throughout.
- Open with . on its own line — the divider arriving
before the title, signaling the breath-mode of what
follows.
- Triple-percussive jua Pah ... jua Pah ... jua Pah
with ellipses between repetitions, not commas. Breath
between each beat.
- Quoted phrases used as objects held at arm's length:
"porcelain speaker exit", "sehvois womanteau".
Per the practice voice's quotation convention.

Address moves:

- We grammar throughout. We don't have to make
sure. We offer this up. Our jolly good spirits.
First-person plural standing alongside the reader,
not above. The teaching's stance enacted in the
pronoun.
- Second-person the actual reader / the listener
held at distance — the reader-figure being taught
about, not the reader being addressed. Different
from Our jolly good spirits who show up here — the
addressed reader is us, the discussed reader is
they/them/the receiver.

Companion to: Your Material Is More Generous Than You
Think (the calibration draft, direct outside-reader voice).
Both pieces are LitUp-scope teachings. Same teaching-tier,
different registers. Pair-thinking running at the
finished-document architecture level — the practice may
offer the constellation two registers per teaching, both
alive, neither replacing the other.

Form question parked: whether the LitUp finished document
carries direct outside-reader versions only, Sound Writing
versions only, or both as paired registers. This piece
makes the question concrete and undecided.
