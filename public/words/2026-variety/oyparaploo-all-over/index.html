Presence After a Loss

All Over

All Over ...
us over a downed figure ...
Done with ...
U Know Flah Be ...
All Over the holes of our reheated arguments ...
Wretched smiling Spirit ...
WHY ... does the bleeding monumental arch touch nothing ...
So ... Joie discrete ... make your point ...
Suspended still weak and through glistening skin ...
colorful tears fall down a face ...
Then comes another insignificant briefing ...
that's confined to a chin working its weather or not ...
Should we order a latte? ... Maybe ...
All Over ...
our familiar cool and quiet waggles that keep returning for the thing it singles out ...
In the gut of your "listless will" create with Renewed Purpose ...
Still Full ... and Over us ...
While falling following another "here we are" worded set of jangles ...
Spinning Palace Rings ...
All Over ...
the sound of sepia noir bedroom wall moving palm leaf shadows ...
Partout Partout Partout.
