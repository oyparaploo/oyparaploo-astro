Invented Language

8 Wahnège Milestones

( it is what it is )

Deh da did it dadah ... Ahh ... si cah toos ... meem wah ... Quah ... kiss ... wahla ... ana nay ja wah ... Nah doo doo ... dida ... si mu play ... plah ... ahtoe flar ... a flame ... Ou ... Ouch ... tilay shiraz ... ooo shay ... Meanah Ma lay ... bak ... ontoe eh flo stay ... Deh da did it dadah ... Ahh ... did it dah dada ... Quah ... too to tehdit to ... Shoot sot bak blanks ... for du shiraz ooo cahcay ... Meanah'o Ma lay ... ontoe wahla ah tsu vay ... femmes de trois ...

Case case convergence ... reassurance ... Somewhere ... we arrived allover landing at the place ... for Us ... Past midnight ink black blossom fields ... that have never heard of O'bey ... for them ... People seeking and finding doorways and windows ... breakthrough ... A space for everyone ... And here's That Thing uncovered ... Let's converge on a deeper form that shows when the Creator marks red lines ... for a new territory ... Peace and quiet ... opening up to lovely frequencies ... and what heart hands at full attention can do ... Dot ... please wait ... for us ... to be discovered ... Make contact ... Someplace ... Our time will arrive ... when no one will answer our cry ... because our obsession with Findable propagates arcs for dividing cultures ... "not by ourselves by all means" is further evidence our instincts run Far down and deep Up ...

Dense ... Form ... Fugue ... Shared ... Breath stream shared ... Dashes ... Dreamtime ... Level down hand ... all over a scan ... Little planet strokes of cross loops ... A massive gathering of people over an invisible wilding grid flickering licks of calligraphic characters ... Vertical light seeking nervousness ... and plenty of time to make ourselves available to different kinds of rounded scarcity ... Playful mysticism cursive wrist ... Knowing ... soft and wrecked togetherness ... No one gazed for hours ... It's time now to care about what we all learned ... as undocumented immigrants and legal citizens ...

Staying alive is good for the work ... There's a very valuable context that word collectors know ... that makes this legible to the whole world ... while this sacred hand is still at the end of this arm ... o ... Originality has always been a type of curse ... Work with no receivers has no audience ... Where are we at this time? ... We will find new ways of Forgiving how we are ... not only working ... living ... Recognizable ... Unmistakable ... Familiar enough to enter anything ... Strange enough to be Us ...

The work is the same work it was decades ago ... What you gained is a library for hugging unknown mammals ... Holding a spine ... We arrived at a meadow valley of "we made our careful marks" ... without ever having sensed Derivation ... There's a welcome place for everyone ...
