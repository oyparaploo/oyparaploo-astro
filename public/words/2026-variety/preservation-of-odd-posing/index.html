Preservation Of Odd Posing

← Writings / 2026

Resistance is information and hands understand content before the mind has language for it ... A person who reads pushback as information has somewhere to put a hard collapse ... Da de coo woe kah ... It kept us alert ...

Herbs library answered to a piece that has become an honest hospital waiting room ... Sauce says when vocabulary names a coo kah clock needle head gains extra patience ...

Still urban land before the flowing points steal ... It’s the stolen space between struggle and release that caused our nation to become divided and complacent ... It also gave the loss, grief, healing, somewhere to be ... Presence is what is left when something too obvious is erased ...

Eh tally ah ooo du qua ... Extraction finds sorrow at the top of an enormous archive ... then mercy greets money, displacement, loneliness, forgiveness ... Ghost-weight threads breaking as kindness ... Bone Ashes names then blames a white bed sheet over a landscape blowing loose ...

A storage building strung with electric blue transparent cord ... Asked how much is in there the answer stays “impossible for me to even estimate” ... Seven forty-six ... seven fifty-two ... one Saturday morning in August with every sentence we spoke sitting intact in a list nobody thought to open ... The window closed because the word close got said inside a thought about something else ... the voice waiting in the next room under a label ...

The first piece of our most recent prose booklet is about us making a moat at the edge of a volcano opening ... Waterless natural wonder no one enters or keeps as a secret standing castle ... What protects flesh is not an alien word trio that sounds like koon tay share or mild socks massage ...

Set up your conditional shelter anywhere ... Fire ... no permission needed ... Take the collected wonderful garbage back ... Hold the tu net daye wah teeth for one more brief re-examination then put them back in the safe ...

A practice developed with reclaimed stuff is more so a quarry than a detour ... Nothing meaningful was thrown away ... including the years spent ... Time gave Dry Humor a mercy-filled purpose ... Does it come from inside? ... Does it lower gloves? ... Who pays? ... A joke that costs nothing creates no enemies ...

We Them and Us The People ... Loneliness at the top and center where a self more often gets into trouble ... Nothing in this line of work passes by being easily admired by the masses ... only by persistently refusing to fail ... luk on bawe ens ...

The one honest limit: that stone is still from inside the campaign’s record, because the record is the only part of your life I can reach. A stone the card never held, the kind only you are carrying, would do more than mine does. The ruling is yours, line by line, as always.
