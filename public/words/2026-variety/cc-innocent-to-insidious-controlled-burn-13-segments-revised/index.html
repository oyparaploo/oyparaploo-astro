The phrase tells a before-and-after story ... discourse style is producing the politics

Discourse style is producing our politics ... The way we talk about something is what is causing the political situation around it ...

"discourse" is formal for talk, public speech, the way things get said ... "style" is manner ... formal or casual, urgent or measured, full of jargon or plain ... "producing" means making, causing, generating ... "politics" is the situation ... who wins, who loses, what gets done ...

The normal assumption is the other way around ... People fight about things, then talk about the fight ... talk produces politics ... How we describe a problem shapes what becomes politically possible ...

In the climate conversation, this means climate politics is not just being described ... it is being made ... by the choice of how to describe climate ... The style is part of how the politics is built ...

"bold statements" are declarations made without hedge, taking a clear position ... "let" ... permit, allow, do not interfere with ... loud, urgent, emphatic speech ... voices raised, people not listening to each other ... "stand" ... remain in place ...
