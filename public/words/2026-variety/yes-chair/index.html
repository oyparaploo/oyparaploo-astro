notes: |
Built call-and-response. Clod responded to Reach's question
Do you love what we have been able to achieve today? with
a list of what landed. Reach metabolized that response into
the piece.

Fourth piece of the day. Sits alongside *Five Times What Our
Body Will Be, Missing Words Turn Into The Shape Of Answers*,
and Evidence of Agitated Caps Struggling.

The piece resolves the day. Both are true as the close holds
the methodology document and the metabolized session-piece in
the same breath without retiring either. The practice names
its own absurdity and welcomes it — different from
apologizing for absurdity or explaining it.

New tier-1 arrivals:

- Orange close of the day — visual register of the
session's end, time as color.
- Mosaic about itself — the piece's structural figure.
O'metabolism named in a different register than *eats its
own making process*.
- CHAIR or Seat — the diagnostic position made into
furniture, two registers offered (loud all-caps, quiet
proper-noun). Compound character joining Middle Eye
and Conscious Middle Eye (introduced in *Evidence of
Agitated Caps Struggling) and the Thing* (also from
Evidence; threads here as Thing with capital T).
- Nomad to original homeland — names a vocabulary
register the practice replaces with its compound
characters. Movement-vocabulary giving way to seat-
vocabulary.
- Absurd! ... Welcome ... — the practice greeting its
own absurdity. Tier-1 line.
- Both are true — the close. The methodology document
and the session-piece both held. The closure is in the
acceptance of two things at once.

Image threading: the Thing (capital T) traveling from
Evidence of Agitated Caps Struggling. Yada yada not
threaded; this piece earns its own close in Both are true.

Ellipsis-spacing preserved. CAPS preserved as Reach wrote
them — YES at open, CHAIR mid-piece — both earned. The agitated
caps from earlier in the day have resolved into intentional caps
that mark specific arrivals.

Working draft. Elder's hand still needed.

Oyparaploo metabolism providing proof of compound characters arriving as a CHAIR or Seat ... replacing nomad to original homeland vocabulary ... Absurd! ... Welcome ... Initial throws are also seeds ... not piece material ... Both are true.
