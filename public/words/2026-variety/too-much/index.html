Scraps From the Workbench

Too Much

Yet-Eee ... another one of these ... We keep writing them, and every time we mean it ... this one a little more than the last ...

We tell stories with marks that are also pictures ... in writing, some of it spoken out loud in syllables before it is words ... in pen drawings, in photographs, in pictures cut apart and put back together, in things nailed together with the oldest nails in the jar, in puppets, in film, in sound, and in whatever else is nearest the hand ... we are not particular ... We make all of it for whoever walks in, and we read and look for a long time first, longer than anybody asked us to, so the floor is swept and the light is on when they come, and there is something on the table to pick up ...

We sit with a thing until it does something ... a coneflower from the yard gone brown, or an electric blue cord ... Sometimes the thing changes ... more often it is us that changes, and the thing just sits there ...

We take the leftover pieces of a hard history and brush them against the grain and with it, to see what lifts ... We dig for the layer underneath that tells it the other way, in art and in the plain facts ... We follow the roads and the crossings people take when one life is ending and the next has not begun, and the dreams they carry on the way ... What comes out of that is a change, and every person who stands in front of it reads the change a little differently ... That is fine with us ... The made-up and the real sit at the same table here, and we let them play ...

What the body remembers, we turn into something that can be held in the hand ... We hold loss ... We hold the pull between two things that will not let go of each other ... We hold the hurt we do on purpose to a thing ... and we hold comfort, and the slow ways of getting better ...

We keep coming back to belonging ... to where we belong, and to the ground where remembering happens ... a basement floor with a drain in it ... a patch of grass at the edge of a photograph that was meant to be about something else ...

A hand held out, and a hand that takes it ... the leading and the following ... every one of those motions we bring back to life as marks set down with care, one ballpoint pen at a time ... There is joy in that ... drawing life this way ...

And when something is on its way to the ocean or the landfill, we take it in and turn it into another possibility ... familiar, and a little strange ... like a cousin at the door we know but cannot quite place ...

This page was on its way to the landfill too ... We fished it out this morning, or one of the mornings ... There is a coffee ring on it, and under the coffee ring, in pencil, in a hand we recognize, the words too much ...

*

I kept the coffee ring ...

Good night ...
