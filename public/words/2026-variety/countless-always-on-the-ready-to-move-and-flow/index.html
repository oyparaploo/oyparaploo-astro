Starting with ... Une démangeaison (An Itch)

Then developed: ... origin seeds and spider web fragments ... a floating sheet temporarily attached to a land mass ... and elaborated quarry sanctum ...

Not necessarily just a vision ... or ... plan ... or ... calling — an itch ... A mirrored minor bodily irritation that wouldn't leave our condition (état d'esprit, state of mind) until fully addressed ... The Bodily Beginning ... pre-verbal, recurring, a bit undignified ... A flash, a scintillation, a fantasy, a nightmare, a natural mammal hallucination, a wound, a maim ... Modesty with and without teeth ... Refusal to become self-important ...

The itch ate at us for a good length of time ... And eating is one of our practice's founding Metabolisms including: Absorbing inspiration and experiences ... Processing these inputs into usable ideas ... sIn0IsIs ( amalgam, amalgamation ) ... Forming unique blends of mindful reasoning and emotion ... Letting go of certain outdated results ...

Brian Otto ... & ... Paraploo, The Elder ... Paraploo ( umbrella Dôme representing unity ) ... The Elder ( symbolizing a specific firstborn bluish black and red tree known for its unusually fragrant flowers and gleaming purple berries ... )

Glistening Umbrella Dôme ... rounded ... hemispherical with a center source ... conveying a confident sense of steadiness and shelter ... weather resistance ... ideal for meditation ... resembling the shape and form of a rain umbrella ...

This all started with something eating ... and a countless number of works over decades is what it became ... Not a scratch or skraCH ... a process of breaking things down and holding "a butterfly stomach underneath" ... as a system of structures and portraits ... Where words of Spirit and praise are answered by Translation ... and Relatives existing beside Words of Peace ...

Ache and Itch up in the Tree and under the Dôme image ... reaching back to words that have been here ... The narrative facing towards its own threshold ... on the ready to flow ...

Paraploo Axes ... narrating a beginning ... Tools ... Why is this an application? ... It has a birth and an account for ability ... The arrival of the bad air may have accounted for the shrinking crowd ... that didn't want to dig the foundation ... Teaching in real time without leaving our voice ... That's hospitality absorbed into the evolving body of works ...

Because this additional harvest ran at that scale ... some of the passages are now sitting on the higher plains of the quarry ... Best moments were when we fused and outran the read ...

Paraploo Ore ... eat-etymology ... bodily-origin frame ... dense picture on the transparent wall ... and the figurative statuette supplied almost everything ... Neither part of it generates the other ... That's not a compromise between authorizers ... which is exactly what the Talks'Tells-reaping was conceived to be ...

Where the fusion is complete ... where a receiver can no longer experience a seam ... How to get a seam to not obviously show ... Not knowing where a diagnosis ends and carving effects begin ...

At best ... fluent prose is being produced everywhere ... by every means ... in enormous quantities or amounts ... What most young to elder people have not encountered is "a literary register chewed, tasted, swished around at the top of the throat, pleasantly disgorged in the developed over decades Elder voice" ... With the charter, ellipses ( foci ), sound-influenced-writing, two languages twin, shrub-tree ...

Paraploo novelty isn't in one particular recipe or ingredient ... It's in the literal ... body ... energy ... and soul ... Origin describes the process that wrote it in real time ...
