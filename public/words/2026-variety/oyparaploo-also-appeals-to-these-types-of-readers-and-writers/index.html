notes: Reader constellation that names the readers and writers Oyparaploo also appeals to. Originally written within the LitUp reading session on 4/24 in answer to the project's reader question; saved as its own piece on 4/28. The constellation is in the practice voice — types named not as demographics but as a list of vocations and stances joined by ellipsis. Final phrase "Curious Remodelers Of the human condition" pulls "human condition" down to lowercase at the moment the piece names it. Foundational document for the LitUp project — defines who the project's reader is.
---

Oyparaploo Also Appeals To These Types Of Readers and Writers

A Mission Accomplisher ... Hay Field Poet ... Lovers of Stories about Noir Crime Mystery combined with Island Inland and Beach Celebrations ... Helpers of Life-Sustaining Communities ... Conflict Zone First Responders Who Devour Indie Fiction Narratives ... Visionary Mercy Mongers ... Earth Liberators ... Literate Antiracist ... Indigenous Elder Relative Storytellers ... Anti Racist Religious or Spiritual Leaders ... BIPOC Organizers ... Thriving Gardeners ... Adaptive Cultivators ... Inclusivity Builders ... Readers or Writers Who Go To Work Every Day for Justice and Other Equitable Practices ... Readers or Writers Who Push back against Oppression ... and For Abundantly Propagating and Freely Sharing Literary Offerings ... Individuals Who Are All In For Radical Openness and Continually Evolve Their Skillfulness and Inner Conflict Towards A More Positive Real and Imagined Destination ... Curious Remodelers Of the human condition.
