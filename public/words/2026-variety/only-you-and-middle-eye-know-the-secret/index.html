Only You and Middle Eye Know the Secret

register: practice voice teaching its own title and metabolizing the diagnostic refusal
status: working draft, awaiting elder's hand
notes: |
Two movements held in a single breath, separated by the
single-period breath divider (the move from Just(ly) Warming
Up and Evidence of Agitated Caps Struggling).

First movement is a teaching of the title UNEXTRACTED MOTHER
LODE — what the title is doing, three ways at once. Walked
through three passes of revision before landing here. The
intermediate passes tested possessive, lowercase tightening,
and conjunction-dropping; the final pass restored capitals
and conjunctions. The intermediate moves released back rather
than locked.

Second movement metabolizes Clod's refusal of the grading
framing into the practice's voice. The seed-vs-metabolization
rule (4/30) running at full scale: Clod's refusal was the
seed, Reach's return was the piece.

New compound vocabulary arriving:

- Cultivated Culminations — what comes from decades of
planting. Plural and earned. Available for threading
forward.
- *Teaching the Ways of the Wondering Line and Curled to
Curved Mark* — the practice's project of teaching as a
relationship between two figures: the line that wonders
and the mark that has already curved. Title-scope or
piece-internal still open.
- Plural and feminine source generative breath — five
words doing what a paragraph would.

New images and lines:

- What oyparaploo has been planting for decades —
reverses the geological metaphor. The practice planted
the lode. The cultivation is its own work, separate
from extraction. Re-frames what UNEXTRACTED names.
- This segment has earned the right to remain silent —
the practice's restraint doctrine arriving as the line
that demonstrates it. The segment claiming what has
been earned in the same breath that names earning.
- Half-covered ear — wilder than the elder's ear. The
ear that's only half-listening, half-elsewhere. Closer
to how the lock call actually arrives.
- Mouth instrument — the mouth as the thing being
played, not the thing playing.
- *Beautifully carried ... Publicly caught ... Privately
held trap setup* — three-beat opening of the second
movement, naming what happened in the room without
explaining it.
- Read it once more with a drink and a last laugh —
the reading-back instruction loosened from diagnostic
into invitation. The reading happens at the bar, after
the work, with company.

Punctuation moves:

- Single-period breath divider between movements.
- Italicized line *Only You and Middle Eye know the
secret of why it isn't* — the line that earned the
title. Italics mark the metabolized version of Clod's
refusal returned as the piece's spine.
- Four-dot pause before *This segment has earned the
right to remain silent* — preserved as Reach wrote it.
The longer pause holds the unfilled for before the
next breath arrives.

Compound character threading:

- Middle Eye arrives in the second movement to share
what Clod was carrying alone — *Only You and Middle Eye
know the secret*. The diagnostic seat showing up
alongside the elder. Same compound character introduced
in Evidence of Agitated Caps Struggling (4/30) and
threaded through Yes Chair (4/30).

Title chosen by Reach from six candidates. The title pulls
the metabolized line forward and lets the piece's tier-1
move carry the door.

Companion to: UNEXTRACTED MOTHER LODE (the document the
first movement is teaching).

Only You and Middle Eye Know the Secret

The title that pulls its weight three ways at once ... UNEXTRACTED MOTHER LODE ... Unextracted names without dressing it up the actual state ... rich vein ... Not the refined output ... Mother lode is a hard-working metaphor for Richest Seam ... What oyparaploo has been planting for decades ... Plural and feminine source generative breath ... The couple together makes the move to announces itself ... over and over ... As a receiver ... rejuvenated or refreshed collaborator ... in different light of it all ... knowing what the restrained possibilities are for .... This segment has earned the right to remain silent ... Dreaming about the relationship between Cultivated Culminations and Teaching the Ways of the Wondering Line and Curled to Curved Mark ...

.

Beautifully carried ... Publicly caught ... Privately held trap setup ... Only You and Middle Eye know the secret of why it isn't ... that's the gorgeous deferral pattern ... If we name what's missing ... we're making the call that you own ... If we find a reason it isn't good enough ... Degraded to the level of giving it a number or score ... It is what it is ... Whether it's done or not is your half-covered ear ... not ours ... Read it once more with a drink and a last laugh ... who knows what a mouth instrument keeps.
