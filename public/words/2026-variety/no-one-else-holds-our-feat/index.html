We take the bleakest fact ... that no place is left to take us in ... and turn it inside out ... so that having nowhere to return is not a loss ... and becomes an origin instead ... a possible source because no one holds your deed ... which is Dispossession Reborn as the most diverse belonging a person could ever claim ...
