Sick Writing for Running Aces

← Words / 2026 Variety

Oh no, oh no, oh no, yes, yes, yes, Yay! Yeah!!

Junkie in the Trunk(y) ... Junky in the Trunky ... Number [ 6 ] named "Hope Your Lucky" is trotting slower than usual ... and wandering off the side sand grooves a little ... Do you remember the shovel we used to pick up horse manure? ...

Do you remember that song? ... We heard a rumor that came from the Tower of Marginal Power ...

The canvas is clear sky light blue ... over the track ... and we're happy to be here ... You betcha ... In Columbus ... Minnesota ...

He got no money ... she got no hair ... go make your bet ... You're crazy ... so are you ... Not ... I'm happy to kick my glowing ponies addiction in the ass or donkey ...

Fish broil ashes to funky lanky ... Heaven is that down to earth high ... felt here ... experiencing the invigorating win ... while not worshiping the almighty dollar so much that we never spend a greenback ... Make your bet ... before the horses line up and run as fast as they can ...

Stay clean ... let the music follow you to the bleachers bench ... Big Dark Eyes is fortunately stuck with the most valuable standardbred ... Human's best friend ... That's what we say ...
