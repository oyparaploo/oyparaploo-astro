Who Is The One

← Writings / 2026

The One who didn't know about the perspective assumptions ... of male colonial inherited sediment ... The One whose human landscape body shifted odd ... because something inside it wanted to represent an imperial bias ... The One watching ... while we discover that the exploited child at work is still there eating their lunch underneath the sewing table ... The One not gone or lost ... just covered over by intersections of perception and power combined ... And the practice of returning to surface beauty is not about recovering our lost self ... but accurately interpreting buried critical engagement ... The One waiting in the shape of who we were before the outer world taught us how to discharge ourselves.
