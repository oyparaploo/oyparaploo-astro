Oh Yay Para Pourr (sounded)

← Writings / 2026

Throwing a wise bole meta seed ... for our companion ... sitting as the second member of a pair ... Oy und O ... ? ... of the First Sound holding one curiosity ... Y ... versus ... open mouth ... O ... What does the sound of the name do when transcribed into English approximation? ... The inseparable pair could nest where neither is alone ... but the peaceful relationship between them is itself a type of artful way of being ... Notation ... oh yay para pourr ... does sonic mirror work gesturing at oyparaploo ... That's a different operation from etymology ... which breaks open existing words to find their roots ... mirror gimmick ... mirror enemy ... catches a manufactured sound ... The form fits our tilde as oO'open ... doubled letter sound roughing like brrd does to bird ... Here we decided to turn pour into pourr ... The discovered Oh Yeah affirmation from Oy and O ? ... arriving as ... yay ... rather than ... yeah ... which moves the affective register from grown-up confirmation toward a child's wordless delight ... oyparaploo ... manufactured sound ... not a dictionary word with meaning to translate ... a reader who sounds out ... oh yay para pourr ... enters at the level of an alternative trajectory to ... chute slash sprout ... pourr is instructive ... Pourring out one's heart ... Pourring our arranged marks near ploo ... Pourr roughed pour into this signature accent ... This iteration could also be felt as material the way ... Just(ly) Warming Up ... made a warm up the work ... Pourr is one approximation among many others ... oyparaploo isn't a final shape but an earned through use body ... landing exactly where it needs to be ...
