notes: Short version of the laisse pair. Pure invented sound — no English. Two passes of the same phoneme sequence, identical, side by side as the form's repetition device. Pair-mate of Coming_Upon_Laisse_to_Doelo.md (4/22), which is the same laisse with English commentary woven through ellipsis by ellipsis. The pair operates the way Belly Fat / Belly Fat River does — neither replaces the other, both alive. Closest formal ancestor: the laisse of the chanson de geste tradition (named explicitly in Almost_Recognizable_Dialect_LOCKED.md). Phonemic shadows of French and English brush against each other ("done done ... doan doan," "luk keh for so long," "beh dey sow veedey swa va") without committing to either language.
