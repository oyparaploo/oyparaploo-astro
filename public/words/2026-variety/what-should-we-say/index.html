What Should We Say? (Que devrions-nous dire ?)

← Writings / 2026

What should we say about them ... the ones who drew one frame and walked off whistling ...

We went looking for the funniest ... a hundred years of a country we once stood in without knowing any of them ... and came back with a handful of names and a different mind ... top tear cartooning ... we are keeping the spelling ... because it tore something open ...

They said in a few lines what we keep trying to say with a thousand marks ... a column of men marching in step ... fire coming up through a floor and a fireman strolling off ... a man flat on the floor at the foot of a pair of high-heel shoes ... serious absurdity ... the type of gravity a crowd could read ... spare ... incisive ... parole puns and flesh stains ... worldly precursors who thought by drawing ...

We learned the words that go with them ... the chained duck, we keep calling geese ... live cow handed over as a prize ... magazine that intentionally called itself stupid ... tribute redrawn for a postage stamp ... honor roll of missteps that turned out to be the nearest thing to a trophy case ... even their traps are gone now ... the doors with their names on them lead to a suspended site, lowering to the level of Mars Sediment mixed with Earth Soil ...

.

Pay the price ... this and much more is our everything ... four seasons built the form and we arrived in the fifth, hungry ...

So what should we say ... we drew to see what might rise inside and out ... our marks have no words ... their lines made a room laugh and ours cause a room to quietly contemplate, and we are not sure that is a different thing ... stumbling electricity quoted through lawful means ... keeping the work fizzing as a million little twilight spots ... protected by courting no rules whatsoever ~ ah de bee ondah more ...

Who will be the next never shy standing butterfly ... roll the dice ... both are true ... we laughed until it hurt ...

*

Ass or donkey met six trees ...
