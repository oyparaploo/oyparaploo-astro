How It's Made

From Altars to Sacred Soil, what came up Mostly Green

Say Welcome ... The sacred answers to that, the same as most of us would ... Any space will do once the human exchanges in it are kind and sweet ... a fire cave set up between one sentence spoken and the next ...

Straw and hay, and any bovine can tell them apart ... honey seeds to syrups ... We ask visitors in, and then “closer than” feels comfortable ...

Enter where many paths flow into one ... toward gatehouses and huts shaped like open umbrellas ... Clothes and bluff stones are kept for cleansing what was found ... and what was lost, which takes longer ...

Origin passage ways are here ... the ones all of us came in on ... and at every gathering space our continued learning is simply about Unity following Rhythms ... Tootsies or soles agree first ... mouths to feed come last ...

From the altars ... to the places so still and so close that breathing wonderfully adds up to tender conversation ... to our precious soil ...

Our practice shows on slow moving water ... We lean over and are caught looking ... Role as Life looks back up at us ... and behind everything here are the places we arrive at ... and return to ...

We draw from water and iron wood ... with a bucket, with a pen ... The works come out rough to the touch and they take a side ...

Warmth granted personhoods are coming under government control ... More mines open, dug by hands like ours, and cut themselves loose from the laws set down to protect Earth Mother ... every mouth saying “mine” ...

Our marks carry the whole variety of living things ... and our fighting, in the same stroke ... Our lines are rooted in community ... in the water it gets ... and in everything that has happened to it ... We remember ... and remain at attention where we are standing ...

Dense soil also bears the traces of our struggle and survival ... of grief ... and what came up mostly green ...

This is absolute staying alive service, for the many of us and for the spirit ... The desire to use all kinds of stuff again does not leave us ...

Warmth and energy are still ours to grant ... We treat the materials as innocent forces ... This straw has done nothing wrong ... We the makers stand far in the distance, or out of the picture ...

We the wild human worker bees keep our bond with our Mother Nature ... and the honey is delivered unsigned ...
