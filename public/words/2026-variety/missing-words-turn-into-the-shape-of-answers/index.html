Body of this piece is Reach's metabolized response after Clod's
initial diagnostic throw. Two changes from prior version:

1. Paragraph breaks dividing the metabolization into five
movements for breath and flow.

2. One small revision: Two big questions can be the spine
changed to Two big questions held silent in the spine.
Per Reach's option to revise — can be (possibility,
structural call) became held silent in (felt-presence,
gesture to the unsaid). Three-word change addressing the
dangling reference to questions that live in the original
throw and aren't in the piece.

The five movements:

1. The practice's voice and the form noticing itself —
Oyparaploo aliveness, smooth missing words as another
answer or tilde, addressing a receiver without
interpersonally knowing them.
2. The felt-presence questions as spine — held silent, one
broken, one whole, the rough deliberate pull into the
Other dialect.
3. The receiver and the new character — that look on your
face, what do you hear returning, the Seth Thomas wall
clock slowing down time.
4. Missing-words as form, the question that surfaces — the
shape of answers, honest middle instruction to her who
doesn't stay, *How can you after everything we've been
through ... be like this?*
5. The close — grounding ritual is itself ruminating on a
rounded point, not behind it or under it, On it.

Title not given by Reach. Placeholder slug until elder names

Rounded Point, On It, She Who Doesn't Stay, How Do Still
