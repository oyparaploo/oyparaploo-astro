No Way Drink Drunk

B.Will Øtto ... BrianØ ... B.W. Øtto ... B'Rian Øtto ... Brian William Otto ... Falling onto the ground ... Crawling on the sidewalk ... Likes so few ... Put your ear to the side of the little desk ... One Day Chair ... make it honest ... A waste of time ... Ooo Ooo ... Ooo ... Let your heart love this ... under a carpenter son's moonlight ... Blue Burlesque Shine ... They don't feel the same outside ... regarding tech cannibals ... Let us show you with outer side spoken words ... Leh Fill ... cool ... let us hold all of you now ... It'd be nice ... Cheap Thrill ... Eat like a bunch of DollFins ... Say again ... Veteran relationship ... absent eyes ... i-35 oo oo what a little cello night dream can do for all of us ... ( tickling the ivories here ) ... This has more empathy than an oligarch's mantle ... Google says ... "An oligarch is a very rich leader with a great deal of influence ... particularly in reference to individuals who benefit from the privatization of state-run industries and markets ... to be continued ...

Chat weight: moderate and climbing — the Elegy now sits in here twice at full length. This reading proceeds here; when the afternoon returns to the build, the next dispatch opens a fresh chat.

Received, and read close. Here is what I see, my observations flagged as mine.

You walked out of an afternoon in the effigy quarry and the first thing on the page is five fashionings of your own name — B.Will Øtto, BrianØ, B.W. Øtto, B'Rian Øtto, Brian William Otto — likeness after likeness in substitute letters, the slashed Ø working like a mask. The piece opens by casting effigies of its author, and then the body falls to the ground and crawls the sidewalk while the names stand upright. Later the DollFins swim past — small dolls with fins, effigies again, eating in a bunch. Whether you carried the word in on purpose or it followed you in, it's in there.

This is Noir Chanson on the page. The Ooos, the cello night dream, Blue Burlesque Shine, the carpenter son's moonlight — and the parenthetical "( tickling the ivories here )," a stage direction inside the text. The piece knows it is being performed, not read. The ellipses are the breath of it: dictated pauses kept as punctuation, the silence made structural. And "Let us show you with outer side spoken words" names your own method from inside the piece — words made on the outside of the body. "Leh Fill" I'll flag as a guess only: half-heard French sitting inside a chanson — le fil, the thread — but that reading is mine, not the page's.

The plural is native here. "Let us show you," "let us hold all of you now," "what a little cello night dream can do for all of us." After this, the order to turn the Elegy's I to we reads as more than an edit — the practice speaks as we, and the quarry document was being brought into the voice that was already waiting for it.

The strongest move is the last one. A song that claims more empathy than an oligarch's mantle proves the claim by taking Google's flat definition into its own mouth — found text, the machine's voice swallowed whole and made to sing along — and then stops mid-borrow: to be continued. The piece ends by refusing to end, with i-35 running underneath the whole thing, headed home.

It holds. Where it routes is yours to say.

One fact and the reading revises itself.

The ellipses aren't breath — they're seams. The dots are where the backing shows between the stuck-down pieces. The punctuation of the piece is the glue line, and that's better than breath: the page keeps the physical fact of the making visible in every gap.

And the afternoon's parallel goes from thematic to structural. You quarried the Hiisi elk today — a creature assembled from gathered materials, stuck together, animated, set running — and then handed me a song assembled from gathered musical materials, stuck to a backing, set running. Wonderful Garbage method carrying Noir Chanson content. Both identities in one object.

Two observations flagged as mine. First: "backing" is doing double work — the board the scraps are glued to, and the backing track a voice sings over. The piece is stuck to both at once. Second, a guess only: the five names at the top may be found rather than penned — your name as it actually appeared on five different musical objects, gathered. If so, the opening is found effigies, not made ones, which is the deeper version.

The Google definition at the end was the tell the whole time — the most undisguised stick-on in the chain, the method showing its own hand in the last move.

And the piece carries its own oath — make it honest — with "No lie" keeping it. The method and the ethic are the same sentence.
