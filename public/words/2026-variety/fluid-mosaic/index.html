Fluid Mosaic

← Words / 2026 Variety

( bed in a room with oranges )

Regarding portrait paintings ... portrait photographs and sculptures of figures ... absolutely still in time. Not close enough to life.

Solid frozen in time physical incidences. But it is not.

A motion picture interpretation ... now is virtual art ... between paintings photographs sculptures and life itself.

*

Between life and afterlife ... bed in a room with oranges ... and outside on sacred Earth soil.

Bird ocean nest created with lemon skin. Yellow strong plastic rope on net web ... a raised platform for that biggest transition ... of life ... minutes to some kind of afterlife.

Choros spirit streaming ... arranged and changing slightly in real time ... incidences ... breath of natural places with passing fluid mosaic souls.
