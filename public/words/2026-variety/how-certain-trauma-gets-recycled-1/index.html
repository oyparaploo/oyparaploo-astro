How Certain Trauma Gets Recycled

← Words / 2026 Variety

Trauma gets recycled ... the way the Mississippi River carries sediment downstream ... What was put into the water generations before ... arrives at our bare feet ... as if it belonged to now ... and we look at the rest of the world through a lens ... of what our ancestors possibly did to the ones who came before us ... mistaking a way of regarding or analyzing a situation ... as a stained piece of curved substance ... Or ... an iris by which light is focused for the world ...

projecting old wounds onto walls of rooms that never held it ... Onto strangers who never used a belt to raise an issue ... on the grounds we came from ... And the strangest part of re-experiencing the same patterns in new situations ... placing us in environments that trigger past pain ... the unresolved waste is passed on unconsciously ... The more we are involved with it the more we sever ourselves from the source ...

Because the people who held and hugged us ... the rooms and patios that fed us ... the soil that grew the food we ate ... all of it gets redone in the disturbing coatings of the original injury ... Until we cannot see what is actually there ...
