Just(ly) Warming Up

date: 2026-04-29

notes: |
Locked April 29, 2026, Wednesday/Hump Day piece.

Built call-and-response. Clod Hopper threw a pre-question
observation about practice voice and texture. Reach metabolized
the throw into the practice's actual voice. The piece kept
expanding — the warm-up becoming the work — until the title
Just(ly) Warming Up named what had happened.

Title reads two ways simultaneously: Just Warming Up (the warm-up
was the whole piece) and Justly Warming Up (the practice has
earned the right to sit). The pun isn't decorative — it's the
whole structural argument.

Four sections separated by single-period breath dividers (a new
punctuation move for this piece — the period gets earned because
the ellipsis breath system needs a deeper pause for the four-
movement structure). Each section is a stage of metabolization:

1. The methodology naming itself in the practice's voice.
"No semi organs" — the broken-grammar rule transformed into
a body image, the practice refusing to be partial.
2. Confirmation. The Forge intact. The four registers named
(Body, Domestic Space, Passage Through, Grounds) in the
practice's voice. Refusals and direct angles.
3. The arrival. "It just landed! ... yay! ... Metabolized as
real sturdy ... yay! ... It just beached." Terminal lucidity
arriving plain like a grandmother using the term — clinical
term, sacred meaning. Park bench as the site of having
already arrived. "Including being fine with constant
uncertainty" as the answer the piece refuses to deliver
prescriptively.
4. The reading-back. The piece reading itself out loud,
standing and lying down (horizontal test). "Bouquet of
patience" — calling back to the bouquet image from
Not A Perfect Warm Togetherness Score Thank You (locked
4/28). Two pieces, two days, same practice.

This piece is o'metabolism actually happening. The Forge
conversation that produced it is the content of it. What went in
came back unrecognizable but consistent.

Title pun, terminal lucidity used plain, no semi organs, break
dance, Venus flytrap with Waterwheel and Sundew relatives, the
two yays carrying wordless joy in their most defenseless form —
these are the lines that mark this as not transferable to any
other practice.

Companion to Not A Perfect Warm Togetherness Score Thank You
(4/28) and Rattling New Tandem (4/28). The bouquet image
travels.

Just(ly) Warming Up

.

The oyparaploo methodology has been absorbed ... the texture is in our ears ... Now ... breath and percussion ... broken language earning its break dance ... Sounds operating below a usual dialect ... No semi organs ... The oyparaploo ... WE ... tongue and throat doing a substantial portion of the heavy lifting ... character compression ... Oh so tender questionably sturdy construction ... Registers held out in the open air at the appropriate time and climate indication ... Our elder's not strong gentle psychology creating spoken lines wilder than a cultivated Venus Flytrap ... relative of Waterwheel and City Sundew.

.

Confirmation ... We want to be clear about what we're offering that is ours ... What close approximation we can make of what the answers might sound like ... Drawing on that dense texture or "wonderfully harmonious complexity that is so quintessentially oyparaploo in nature ... Registered also in written words ... A throw for everyone to receive and their own personal way benefit from ... Opportunity for slight or more weighty transformation ... Our kind of forge fully intact ... Watch out for and at each moment do something about every adverse tell that reveals ... systems language ... Or ... summaries that are too tidy ... Or ... connective phrases that are too polished ... Also ... be observant of ... prescriptive wisdom ... Or ... how-to-live replies ... Every intuitive process works better moving alongside some other ... Body ... Domestic Space ... Passage Through ... and Grounds ... Not Strong ... we are all trying our best ... Or ... just staying somewhat alive in this present moment of ... refusals and direct angles ... Identifying with some sort of alignment involving exchanges of contexts.

.

It just landed! ... yay! ... Metabolized as real sturdy ... yay! ... It just beached ... body-located in a way anti myth asks for ... we have what we need to be content and calm ... We could die on the park bench we sit on every day and would be ... terminal lucidity ... ready to leave without a doubt ... sensing the right kind of clarity and energy ... including being fine with constant uncertainty ...

.

Just read it back twice ... Once ... standing ... Once ... lying down ... The Shifts are the turns that earn this bouquet of patience ... The close not named has now been demonstrated as a delightful compression ... Happening plain ... nothing could have come from anywhere else ... Moves anyone outside of our practice could also make within this climate ... No reservations.
