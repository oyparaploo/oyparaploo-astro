Before The Effective Gaze Revealed Itself

← Writings / 2026

How slowly it happened ... not a sudden hardening but a gradual cooling ... the way an oven goes from warm to cold when no one tends to that ... Oh so ... familiar face ... arriving with a new edge and vintage tilt ... that wasn't there before ... the way "did you sleep" is asked with a different type of weight ... Until the day we cause ourselves to shrink in the presence of a chosen whisper ... withdrawing us ... And our recognition arrives not as anger but as quiet inventory ... The noticing of how much of ourselves we had already given up ... before the effective gaze revealed itself ...
