Scraps From the Workbench

oO'open

Ghostly y ... honest detours ... zzz ... oO'open for ... PASS ... back again ... Polished until that Thing becomes both ... rough and raw ... No hiding in plain sight ... Rye ... clean up your lean ... Ready for whatever will happen next ...

F ... F ... Form First ... since "for what it is" matters ... This is a drowsy coda or half-awake murmur ... with callbacks compressed in ... The looseness is just one tool in our toolbox ... Over-polishing kills the kat ... So ... most of what we desire to offer is light hanging over a recreated assemblage ... flows into the most spirited line ... and allows a branded character to walk on an all-black glistening stage setup ... o'Mosaic becomes a cryptic address to an Island colorful figurative profile of three transgender figures... So memorable ... Maybe too much weight on who hasn't earned our truest aesthetic pulls ...

What's the gravity of this imagined scene ... exactly? ... That extra relaxed moment ... Or was it a casual incident? ... Late coda genre ... Less interesting touch-shaped ... A new tilde wave pause could land somewhere on the beach as ... Terror-tory ... No! ... Would Bee ... should be a dreamtime candidate for deeper REM ... Fine ... go ahead over-formalize it ... ruin it for us... Your inclination might be too heavy for this unscheduled celebration ... first surviving last month's storm ...
