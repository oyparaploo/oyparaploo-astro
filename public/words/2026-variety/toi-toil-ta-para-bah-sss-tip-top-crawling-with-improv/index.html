Tip Top Crawling with Improv

← Writings / 2026

Toi Toil tá-para bah sss ...

Then the phone rings and Colors is at the other end with Character holding down a good job ... as a mechanic ... One minds the home shelter while the other gazes at the orange horizon ... Establishing a "Stable Feel ... Walking through a French garden wearing a valuable and worthless coat of mammals ... So sweet! ... A home shelter can be portable and resolve its own doubts ... amongst childhood tools ... and invitations ...

Silva arguing with Ach'Tun tender jewels ... that have English and a little bit of French showing through their sparkles ... We like to coin almost every travel memory ... by Volume ... Going free ... in an empty chalk white space ... with cold puppets right there close at hand ... plenty of room to roam ... Letting all weather patterns in ... to decorate insane wants and needs ...

This is where the best kind of intentional crawling begins ... Living with realities of obsolescence ... uncertainty ... and extinction(s) ... How we live with the harsh fact that everything we like and love is temporary ...

Spending lots of time shaping wholes ... believing in the returns ... Not judging or pointing a finger ... With a hand mirror living courageously with impermanence ... Softening without removing crusty puckers and creases ... that occur naturally ...

New beginnings will always arrive ... make of it what you will ... First self acceptance ... then approval of plain speech ... doing sacred work ... Every correspondence is a paragraph for the ... WE ... who show up when we didn't have to ... Who will come back again and again? ... Every tip top shape addressee ... maybe ... Bottoms up ... let's all finish the drink ... together ...
