How It's Made

Starring Aromas of Less Drafty Soil

We go back and check with the ground ... yes ... it is still holding our place ... Soil sits at the core of being a human mammal ... the same soil we were taught to wipe off at the door ...

We sit the way the ground sits ... in layers ... leaf on top ... clay under the leaf ... stone under the clay ... and from the bottom of all that sitting we can tell where our seat is in the whole sky ...

All of our senses show up ... the nose first ... then the tongue, which was not asked ... Every smell and taste is turned up louder than we left it ... A back step comes up with the smell ... a bent spoon ... a pie made of mud that nobody ate twice ... then the memories that come up all in one pull ... tangled, roots and all ...

When did the water become land? ... and vice versa ... We were there for both and wrote nothing down ...

Whatever falls here gets used ... a leaf ... a sparrow ... one day us ... no hurry ... The ground makes itself again out of all of it ...

Tug on one root and a tree three yards off leans in to listen ...

In we go, hands first ... we take up with the shapes ... the light ... the weather as it comes ... the sound, and all the room the sound needs ... We say a thing by doing it and the place answers the same way ...

Our kindreds wear the place ... wearable environments ... the turtle for good ... the hermit crab only until Thursday ...

We are in it over our heads and still strangers in the oldest home we have ... and it smells wonderful ... the fragrance of immersive estrangement ... Eh , strange , meant ...

Call us dirt rich ... soil ... dry grass ... cinnamon ... clove ... cocoa ... every one a different brown ... and under all of it white threads thinner than a hair, carrying sugar and news from tree to tree ... the mycelial networks ... a root-government older than any map ...

Into this we weave what we have ... ink marks that stop just short of words ... materials heavy enough to need both hands ... and awareness kept keen, the way we keep the spade ...

To be or not to be shown ... and if shown, set side by side with the counter positions ... the vice versa of everything said here ... The soil has not said ...
