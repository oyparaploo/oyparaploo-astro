What is the Rationale for No Hands

← Words / 2026 Variety

( paper records at neither hand )

Frames crop hands on rifles ... hands reaching through busted doors like they're going to do something. No merciful hand arranged peace.

First generation hand holding a pigeon as a myth and ... cluster of contrived shadows.

Structures set it all against a collection ... while Note's gesture is to pull the trigger. Cell O'o painted her grandmother with finger ties insanely threaded.

Mommy's face was pressed to the icy concrete of a sidewalk slab! Between smashed cracks of war. Paper records at neither hand.

Showing an unusual hand is displacement. A skeleton showing through the first layer of skin of prose. Absence of hands is life-defining evidence.
