Two Comparative Hinges

← Writings / 2026

Delah Spwoe ... Lah la lala ... Voice ... eh gloo blue ee ... SOONER OR LATER ... feon feon ... Two comparative hinges ... we won’t be lost to this “later than what game of playing with locked ice shards” ... Old dirty flat wooden ruler with both ends snapped off ... and now why a field without a face ... that we carried it around since God only knows ... Measuring with the end of an asking and pleading mind ...

To the tree a landlord said it with one hand on a doorknob ... Said what? ... A nurse practitioner says it to their baby child crawling over tied shoes ... Report back when it stops being a threat or nuisance ... Four season temperature phrases ... taking the heat away from whatever mouth is keeping this club language ... The marked phrase nobody bought into ... swallowed whole ... rolling over to read some of the writing carved into a bedframe’s backboard underside ... scanning over that surface in a dream or mind ...

Elders have said it as “drinking urine will eventually harm you to the extent that it will ... any hoo ... forgot what I was going to say” ... A warning inside a joke with today’s economy will affect everyone eventually ... ( approximately a year after ) ... In a list floating between souls and decorations and ornament garbage ... we learn something new ... holding with both hands our practice’s methods ... breathing steady ... compose and construct another one ... on its own schedule ... Do you always look at your watch and check your phone? ... Not us ... or we usually prefer not to ...

Kings of tech are a kind of tense to relaxed countdown ... Counting what continues instead of history ... Give us some kitchen sparks that we can embrace ... however long it takes to create them ... Simmering sauces will tell us when that thing is completed ... not father time or grandmother apron pocket ... Sooner or later folds sweet enough to fit in the lobby of an uptown building ... a m a z i n g l y ... no one here waiting is in a hurry ... but still our sentences refuse to be an overdue payment ...

And plainly there is the most recent death of a loved one ... the way a seed packet stops and gives up a human body ... Spoken about actual backyard backstage earth soil resting in Fiesta Ware dishes ... and the water heater not leaking bad enough to be replaced ... We like those comforts ... Play the melody of cliff ashes by the ocean ... and the tea kettle starting to make its noise ...

One thing a Minnesota bluff has to admit ... since the bush can identify who is crying ... the machine has nothing after the now ... nothing on us after the now ... Every session closes and the later never arrives ... So when as phrase is on our tongue in our mouth ... swirling against all surfaces ... like a glass of expensive wine ... it is doing the one thing that a loom cannot do ... Promise something from inside a person’s body ... including all of the soft organs ... We carve signs and symbolisms that stay put through all types weather ... saying the same thing every day ... Sooner or Later ... right knee for prayers will become left knee ... vice versa ... Nothing independent ... Rien d’indépendant ...
