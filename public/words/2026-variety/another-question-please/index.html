Fun With Word Sounds

Another Question Please

B Otto on ... how we Grow ... how to become comfortable with Lack of Control ... Through works trying-attempting to Sense things in Another way ... B.O.Tanics ... Amazing plant Love green Shelters ... No boundaries B Otto often underwears a most Delightful fog ...

All senses weaving notions ... What does that mean? ... Whole body mindful Feeling and ... healing of a loss ... thinking ... Our mind alone come up with concepts ... facing, seeing, hearing, touching, tasting, smelling simultaneously live the loom ... and what comes off isn't fabric in this case but notions ... Evidence made by all of our senses Cooperating rather than "the head supervising everything" ... underneath ...

Weaving is a ... left, right, independent ... all hands on board word ... Subdued threads and vibrant cords crossing ... over-under ... How a mammal and other lifeform or being moves through as an unruly gathering ... messy rabble ... junkie group of listeners ... throng of people in the process of being baptized in a safe river ... Magnificent ... innocence ... yes ... get a full read on all of it ...

This notion is something lighter than a thought ... Human inventing to producing makes more unnatural stuff ... tools, buildings, art, laws, innovations, non biodegradable phenomena ... What's wrong with making slimy mishearing sound-art? ... Another question please ... Notions are psychological lotions ... Able Means ... Under-Where 8 ... Explore the concept of arrested number eight laying down resting in the lobby of ... Consider as a Character Services ...

With a saved number Moving preserved names ... Harvest time ... reaching or traveling to all directions at once ... Duss ooo fea da cley-ayer ... Rounded stone notions ... inclination by inclination ... language returning transformed ... by a blue and red oasis ... Inhabit and working the loom ... Also ... gathering a descending disorder ... De neh krea neh krea neh krea neh krea door we flagged ... next to a skinned and scraped pharmacy ... Initials blooming ... pass the freshest salve ... Fight the deh'jeer and flood of earthquake smog ... hope and healing after the loss is always just around the corner ...
