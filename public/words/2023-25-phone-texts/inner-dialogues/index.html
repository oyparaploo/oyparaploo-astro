A person's life is a type of not known exactly how long journey. Continuous process of learning and experiencing. Each person has their own path ... ups and downs ... unexpected things that happen ... challenges to overcome ... achievement milestones ... necessary times of rest ... and moments of reflection.

Practical ways to capture life's meaningful moments:
1) Keep a journal for documenting experiences - including emotions and sensory details. 2) Take photos to capture genuine moments. Instances that are authentic and free from ulterior motives. True to oneself connections that leave a lasting impression because they align with your values and beliefs rather than automatically conforming to external expectations. 3) Create and collect physical mementos and put them in memory bins for future displays or revisiting. 4) Record voice notes and videos to further preserve the authentic feeling. 5) Fully experience special places without grabbing your phone or any other devices. 6) Write poetic letters to your future self that are interesting and easy to understand. 7) Create visual art works or songs that express or pertain to significant moments in your life.
The key is building habits that help you notice and document the things that matter most, moving forward.
