June: Isn't every thought an "Inner Thought."

Every human brain has valleys and ridges that fold over each other. The deep grooves are called Sulci and the raised areas are called Gyri. All of our body's important information is stored in the grooves. Our brain's countless number of curved folds can pack in more neurons enabling an increased amount of cognitive abilities. The Amygdala part of our brain is responsible for inner dialogue. Every time you have an inner thought ... your amygdala moves around and becomes juicier.

Having good character in life frequently prompts and leads to not realized before self-revelations. To ... on another path ... develop a wicked character one has to completely leave behind what was intended for them by The Creator. This is subjective and requires a lot of ongoing discussion. To become an infinite representative of Goodness a person has to accomplish what was initially perceived as impossible ... then becoming something else. Something much better. What about all the humans on earth who were not lucky enough to live very long? Do they come back and get another try?

A question is an open window that occasionally shows some soothing contemplative activity ... An answer is a sheer curtain that if one is not careful while looking at it can become a completely opaque curtain ... blocking out all nicely brightened up good possibilities.

Jesus has that parable where he talks about the servant who invested his talents rather than bearing the talents. I like interpreting that as investing gifts. And I feel like our most notable jazz artists and poets are exemplars of righteousness according to that parable.

For myself ~ Jesus, Yahweh, Allah, Buddha, The Great Spirit, and Ancestor Spirits spiritually provide everything if I do some lifting or put some necessary effort in myself.

My encouragement for dark times:
Jesus claims to be the Way, the Truth and the Life.
Therefore, whether you are looking for direction, understanding or hope, Jesus is more than capable of providing. 💛

Thank you for your beautifully written gratitude list. I have taken on a practice each night of writing down my daily GRACE (gratitude, realizations, actions, challenges, enjoyment). It has been a great way to end the day and rest into slumber.

Celebrate every small achievement ... to maintain your motivation. Find things that help you rest and relax ... especially during your busiest days.

Challenge mental demons or negative thoughts, feelings, and beliefs that cause distress and can disrupt your peace of mind with an abundance of positive affirmations.

In several ways it may seem like dark ( unpleasant, frightening ) times right now. Our nation might be in a phase of stagnation or period of decline. To navigate out of dark times, focus on seeking added support. Engage in activities like ~ exercise and meditation, identify the small moments of joy, actively seek experiences that foster a sense of hope, be kind to yourself, and acknowledge the challenges you face.

Autonomous sensory meridian response ( MSMR ) is a tingling sensation combined with subjective euphoria usually starting at the neck and then spreading to the rest of the body ... triggered by auditory stimuli.

Goosebumps are powerful emotions affecting human skin causing infectious excitement.

Seeing awe and grace in others makes life worth living.

Celebration of deep love is important in times like these.

I furthered this larger than life message by asking myself How can I begin now to not take myself so seriously? How can I lighten up a lot more going forward?

A good wolf replied Smile in the midst of all types of misery. Will to yourself a cheerful nature during every self trial you enter. You do not always have to know what you are doing or find the answer to every question after you have effectively lightened up. Love your journey's rising experiences to weighted down phases. Try your best to also see the humor as part of each and every moment.

I'm so grateful for the advice of this Good Wolf!
