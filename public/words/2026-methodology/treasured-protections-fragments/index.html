What We Carry

Treasured Protections (Fragments)

What a beautiful and inspiring dimension ... Perfect accordion ... Excellent case ... All the lead sheets soft from over handling ... The exotic cabaret song transplanted to a common community center ... Grandpa's watch dissolving into stained newspaper headlines ... and two useless photo portraits of a former head of state. These are oblique shelters we can try to fold up and store for when a case opens again ... The Englsh word "Our" makes it communal ... assuming we're all in this together ... assuming we're all carrying small and inadequate protections ... against the often harsh weather of life ... The words "Most Treasured" captures paradox where the things that matter most are more often than not the most fragile and temporary ... We now tend toward simple lessons that have to do with breathing and undoing wrapped tension ... In a moment of revelation ... near an end ... trying to explain something ... impermanence rarely diminishes importance ... We also work musically with other artist's rhythm ... Work with those waltz-like clustered syllables all around ...TREES shared ... POOR table ... TEAM poo-scary ... SHELL tears ... Suggesting agency not at the mercy of things that appear and vanish ... Pick this up ... Carry that ... What Ambrosia does with her trunk and her grandmother's memory ... What we all do with our protections
