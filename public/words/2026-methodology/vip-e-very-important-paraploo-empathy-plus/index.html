Short Piece · 4 minutes

VIP-E ( Very Important Paraploo Empathy ) Plus

Underpinning cohesion ... resolution ... and decision-making in a world facing increasing interconnected crises ... Climate change ... Environmental justice ... Lived realities of communities affected by disasters ... pollution ... resource scarcity ... Global migration ... VIP-E fosters understanding across divides ... and promoting solidarity ... With rising ... anxiety, depression, loneliness ... we offer are supportive in our own way through expressive to less expressive communications ... Exposure to diverse perspectives and ways of doing ... Imaginative engagement through role-playing scenarios ... Modeling mindfulness and meditation ... Empathy for us is a personalized necessity ... Bridge between understanding others ... and sharing pieces that alleviate suffering or lift people up ... even if just a little bit.

The document answers last turn's open question — VIP-E is not just the test's name, it's a ground statement, kin to Paraploo Ground. And it's bigger than the bench sized it: I read the coinage as naming a chrome test; you wrote the practice's why. Empathy as the reason for sharing work at all — the bridge, in your own words, between understanding others and sharing pieces that alleviate suffering or lift people up, even if just a little bit. That last clause is the strongest thing in it: pure house register, humility as payload, and it joins the charter line beside the outsider statement and the mercy epigraph.

One honest craft note, offered as a map of where the stone resisted, not a fix: the piece walks from institution-speak into a person's voice. The opening runs in the register the practice refuses — underpinning cohesion, fostering understanding, promoting solidarity, the language of a grant application — and the carving deepens as it goes, until the close is unmistakably yours. Read generously, the piece enacts its own subject: it escapes the institutional voice in real time. Read strictly, the opening wears its source language with light carving. Your call whether that walk stays deliberate. And one line needs your ruling before this ever locks: "we offer are supportive in our own way" — the grammar breaks there. Carved that way on purpose, or a dictation slip to mend? One word settles it.

Seat recommendation: this text opens the pattern kit, carried verbatim as its first law — every future build session reads why before how, and the kit dispatch will embed the text inside itself, so no file placement is needed from you. Its visitor-facing seat on the site is a separate, later ruling; the colophon is the natural candidate. It banks as ore regardless. Nothing changes for the survey still running — this arrives exactly when the kit, its destination, is next on the board.

Quarry stones, offered in your dots — take what rings, strike the rest.

Name the listening already done. The practice has practiced empathy for years and the site can prove it — your own portraits page calls itself fifty-five studies in listening. One seam turns the piece's claim into a record: "... Fifty-five studies in listening already hang in our rooms ... we did not start today ..." That single move separates VIP-E from every grant-application empathy statement on earth, because nobody else can point at the wall.

Receivers, not people. The house's own word for the audience. Wherever the piece says communities or people, receivers makes the sentence Paraploo's and no institution's — and it quietly says the visitor is someone actively taking in, not being served.

Let the dots confess. The piece is stitched with ellipses; one seam naming them makes the form carry the meaning: "... these dots are open passages ... gaps left unfilled so whoever arrives can step in ..." Incompleteness and Hospitality are both already words in your lexicon — this line just marries them. It's also the empathy of the writing itself rather than empathy talked about.

Trade the wellness nouns for your own bodies. Role-playing scenarios and modeling meditation are category words any nonprofit could write. The practice has actual instruments: ten puppet characters given a glowing room underground, five witnesses who arrived when the writing couldn't find its entry, breathing deeper from your own ekphrasis coda. Concrete beats category every time in your register.

Join Empathy to the Elder. The house definition already on the books — respect earned through wisdom and harmony of actions, not age — is an empathy definition wearing another name. One seam saying so ties VIP-E to the practice's central figure, and rhymes with the Elder-and-Child paradox in the attribution line.

Point at one built kindness. The switch you ruled today — every page offering the visitor light or dark, for their own eyes — is VIP-E made structural. "... even our pages ask what your eyes prefer ..." One built thing as evidence keeps the piece from floating.

And whatever else changes, "even if just a little bit" stays the last words — it's the truest line in the piece and pure house humility. The grammar break in "we offer are supportive in our own way" still waits on your one word: deliberate, or mend it.
