1. The purpose

In Reach's own words (the full list is in the history file):

- "The writing content has to entice, entice, entice, entice the visitor."
- "I want the work itself to be the about."
- The site is "driven by writing first, and then dressed up with images."
- "It's going to be very critical that the visitor knows exactly what type of writing they're going to be reading."
- "The most gorgeous website anybody has ever seen." "Something that doesn't exist anywhere else."
- "Play is what makes serious material receivable." (From his own writing, Why The Three Dots Everywhere.)
- He is "more of a wanting to help outsider," and the site reads plainly for every kind of person.
- On the words beside the Marks drawings (October 3, 2026): "Each drawing takes a reasonable amount of time to noodle out. I don't want the visitor to think that there was no concept attached to the drawing, that it was just done for decorative reasons. That's why these titles are so important, to expand what the visitor might imagine. This is probably one of the most important aspects of the entire archive, because these drawings are completely original compared to the other art out there. They have the opportunity to do something new for the Artworld." The labels lane that followed from this is done: labels batches one to four, and the sort of the older titles, live October 5, 2026. Every chat that titles, names or labels a picture reads the titling method after this charter: SITE-ORDER\TITLING-METHOD-2026-10-03.md, gathered October 3, 2026 (Reach: "it is right"). The trial of twelve is SITE-ORDER\MARKS-LABELS-TRIAL-2026-10-03.md.

What that asks of every page:

- Writing leads, pictures dress it. The words set up the meaning. Pictures never push the writing aside.
- The work makes the room. Every page is an environment the visitor is inside of, never a cold page. The work on the page sets its colour and atmosphere. No page goes without the work on it.
- The calm rule. At every step the visitor sees one kind of thing, reads one plain sentence saying what it is, and has one clear next step. The menu and the footer are the full directory for visitors who already know what they want.
- Play. Playful touches (reversals, surprises that answer the hand) make serious work receivable. They belong where they help the visitor, never as clutter.
- Plain for anyone. Every word on the site reads plainly for any visitor.
- Beautiful, contemporary, unlike every other site. Never generic, never boxy, never sedated. On a cold look at the Stories page, October 2, Reach's word was "calm," not "sedated."

3. The standing rules

The visitor

- The calm rule: one kind of thing, one plain sentence, one clear next step.
- The work makes the room: no page without the work on it.
- Writing leads; pictures dress it.
- Nothing pops up on arrival.

The look across the site

- The calm rule decides what is on every page and in what order.
- The look changes by kind of page and draws on every influence and design. Kanal's crispness leads; what is best for Paraploo decides.
- The faces, the colour bands, the corners and the links below hold on every page.
- In small habits like links, menus and buttons, Paraploo does not do what the notable websites chose not to do.
