Short Piece · 1 minute

There must have been some type of personal folly that brought you here. Was it something funny looking? Was it a human grazer or nice bits from a mechanical or natural system? Which wild things came first? A friendly pig with tusks or lovable and stoic blood stained bull? (delightful haha giggle giggle sound goes here) Oink oink. Boots is the undisputed queen of the patches here. We love these daisies. So peaceful. Someday we'll all be covered with bellis perennis. Lovely common marguerite. We would love to know how you embark upon building these relationships and working along side these individuals who are in this rich heritage of networks of growers. We make an effort to meet them where they are and understand their families and colonies
