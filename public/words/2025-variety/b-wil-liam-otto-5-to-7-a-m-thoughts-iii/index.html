b. Wil Liam ~ otto ( 5 to 7 a.m. Thoughts III )

We have been in tiny spaces to vast circles, chasing
something. Thousand figurative to abstract glimpses ...
trillions of fragments fixed - like and floating in mind.
Contemplative series of different complex patterns we
call prayers. We’re becoming much better at honing
our ability to understand and share, in our own way,
the feelings of others. Visitors to our quirks filled
fabricated experiences to audience members who have
not yet attended any of our reveals. Temporary events. Longer - lasting shows ... that also have injections of
various rushing streams of easily recognizable image
bits. That’s how it is today for many eye yay oh ah
visual ( and other ) artist manufacturers of this jazz.
