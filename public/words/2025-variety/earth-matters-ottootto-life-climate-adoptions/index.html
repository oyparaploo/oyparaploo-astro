Earth Matters (2)

ottootto Life ( Climate Adoptions )

← Writings / 2025

Accelerate adoptions of climate solutions. Do you feel
sad, gloomy, miserable, and melancholy ? Or perhaps you feel immobilized and numb. Climate Change reality can cause these six human emotions above + plus guilt and
anger. They are understandable given the present type of match or ball game we are playing. Every daily choice
we make can effect the score and future results. We are
happy to say actions are already improving and that we are going in the right direction. Each small individual
action is not inconsequential or absurd. Adding your
efforts to countless other efforts picks up the pace -
speed of overall climate improvements. The remedy and
antitoxin of excessive - worrying - causing - apprehension
is move - to - action. Get into the climate solutions game and play at your best, use your wonderful human gifts.
