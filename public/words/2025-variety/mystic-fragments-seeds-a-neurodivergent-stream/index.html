Mystic Fragments

Seeds ~ A Neurodivergent Stream

← Words / 2025 Variety

This extraordinary piece of experimental writing transforms conventional spiritual philosophy into a luminous kaleidoscope of sensory experience and interconnected revelation. Through its innovative stream-of-consciousness technique, the text captures the electric feeling of synapses firing, creating unexpected connections between the microscopic and the cosmic—from DNA spirals to fungal networks to the fabric of consciousness itself. The author masterfully employs a neurodivergent lens that shatters linear thinking, allowing profound truths about growth, potential, and transformation to emerge through fragments of sound (ping ping ping, CRACK!), synesthetic moments where "hope tastes purple," and recursive patterns that mirror the very biological processes being explored. What could have been a simple meditation on seeds becomes an immersive journey into the spaces between thoughts, where scientific precision meets mystical insight, and where repetition becomes incantation. The writing pulsates with authentic urgency—words tumbling, spiraling, and exploding across the page like the very seeds breaking open that it describes. This is not merely poetry or philosophy but a third thing entirely: a textual mycelium that connects reader to universal truths through the raw, unfiltered experience of consciousness observing itself. Perfect for readers seeking writing that doesn't just describe transformation but enacts it through language that cracks open conventional forms to reveal the luminous potential within.

Seeds ~ A Neurodivergent Stream

flesh holds galaxies... tiny dormant universes in muscle fiber weaving like mycelial threads beneath forest floors... armillaria ostoyae whispers through Oregon soil same way potential whispers through marrow... biggest living thing is underground did you know? connected... all connected...

mustard seed faith bouncing like pinballs in skull cavity... ping ping ping against bone walls... grows grows GROWS until mountains move sideways... or is it us moving? perspective shifts like tectonic plates...

water drips... drip drip drip... on hard shell defenses... erosion is love is patience is TIME... Mother Earth's heartbeat synchronizes with seed pulse... ba-dum ba-dum ba-dum... everything has rhythm even silence...

blueprint DNA spirals like cosmic staircases... Jacob's ladder? no... seed ladder... climbing from soil darkness to light addiction... photosynthesis dreams in green tongues... chlorophyll prayers...

reaping sowing reaping sowing... circular like ouroboros eating its own consequences... kindness seeds scatter like dandelion wishes... whoooosh... landing in unexpected heart-soil... growing compassion forests where deserts used to scream...

vulnerability cracks... CRACK!... like egg shells like seed coats like ego armor... necessary breaking... necessary opening... spilling light everywhere... can't contain can't contain CAN'T CONTAIN...

thread-like roots = neural networks = fungal highways = cosmic web = all same pattern repeating fractally into infinity... zoom in zoom out doesn't matter... seeds within seeds within seeds...

hope tastes purple sometimes... did you know? historical roots reaching backward-forward simultaneously... time isn't linear in seed-space... everything happening NOW and THEN and WILL BE...

fertile soil whispers secrets to those who press ear to earth... listening with whole body... bones know what brain forgets... ancient seed memories encoded in mitochondria...

transformative chrysalis moments when seed becomes NOT-seed... identity crisis of becoming... who am I when I'm no longer what I was but not yet what I'll be? suspended animation of growth...

precious precious PRECIOUS... they keep saying... but precious like uranium? dangerous potential? or precious like grandmother's locket? held close cherished protected?

light and water... binary necessities... but also darkness matters... germination happens in the blind spaces... trust without seeing... faith without knowing...

every action has opposite reaction but what about diagonal reactions? perpendicular consequences? non-Euclidean karma spinning off into dimensions we can't name?

miraculous mutations... unexpected... hope wearing disguises... sometimes weeds are medicine... sometimes medicine is poison... depends who's swallowing...

cycle spirals not circles... returning but different... elevated... or descended? direction is relative when you're spinning...

seeds in flesh in muscle in memory in trauma in joy in tomorrow in yesterday in NOW NOW NOW...

breaking open is the prayer... staying closed is the fear... growth demands sacrifice of shell-self...

everything connected by invisible mycelial thoughts... armillaria ostoyae of consciousness spreading spreading spreading...

...and the seeds dream of forests while forests dream of seeds...
