THE JOY PANTHEON

A Mythological Navigation System

← Words / 2025 Variety

Les Subsistances, Lyon

PRE-SHOW: GPS Calibration
Audiences receive "Joy Deity Assessment" identifying their dominant joy god (Dionysian/Apollonian/Trickster/etc.) via quick questionnaire.

ACT ONE: The Lost Gods

Stage: Modern office space gradually revealing ancient temple underneath

ANGELIQUE (mythologist, excavating through office floor):
Every spreadsheet covers an altar.
Every PowerPoint hides a prayer.
Pulls ancient mask from under desk
We buried the gods but kept their hungers.

SUSO:
Buried? Or evolved? This mask—what deity?

ANGELIQUE:
Dionysus. Found him under quarterly reports.
Makes sense. Corporate culture fears ecstasy.
But look—brushes dust—the hunger for chaos remains.
See these scratch marks? Someone tried to dig him out.

NILS (approaching with instruments):
The gods never left. We just stopped listening.
Plays chaotic drumming
Dionysus sounds like this—wild, uncontrolled.
Switches to ordered harmony
Apollo like this—structured, beautiful.
Your body knows which god is calling.

MOSES:
My body knows? Prove it.

NILS:
Close your eyes. I'll play both.
Alternates between chaotic and ordered
Which makes you want to move?

MOSES (swaying to chaos):
The first. Definitely the first.

CECILE (entering with broken compass):
That's because you're a trickster deity.
Chaos is your prayer language.

MOSES:
And you? What's your language?

CECILE:
Cycles. Winter and spring. Loss and return.
We ranked emotions like Olympics.
Gold: Happiness. Silver: Calm. Bronze: Gratitude.
But joy isn't competing—

SUSO:
—It's coaching all the others. Yes!
300,000 years of joy evolution taught us that.
Projects human brain evolution
Crawling joy became walking joy became flying joy.

ANGELIQUE:
But we forgot we have wings?

SUSO:
Worse. We convinced ourselves flying is impractical.
"Stay grounded," we say. "Be realistic."
Meanwhile, our neural pathways for transcendence atrophy.

ACT TWO: The Pantheon Awakening

Audience divided by their dominant joy deity

NILS (to Team Dionysus):
You chose chaos. Good. Chaos is honesty.
Order lies. Says everything fits.
But you know better. You know mess is truth.
Ready to dissolve some boundaries?

TEAM DIONYSUS MEMBER:
What kind of boundaries?

NILS:
The one between you and music.
You don't listen to rhythm. You become it.
Drumming intensifies
Stop thinking. Start being sound.

ANGELIQUE (to Team Apollo):
You chose order. Beautiful.
But not rigid order—living order.
The kind that makes galaxies spiral.
Want to create beauty from chaos?

TEAM APOLLO MEMBER:
How do we start?

ANGELIQUE:
Take this chaos—dumps box of random objects
Find the pattern hiding inside.
Every constellation was once just scattered stars.
Someone saw the story. See yours.

MOSES (to Team Trickster):
You magnificent rule-breakers!
Which rules are you done with?

TEAM TRICKSTER MEMBER:
The rule that says joy must be earned.

MOSES:
YES! Who made that rule? Not your ancestors.
They knew joy was birthright, not salary.
Break that rule. Take unearned joy. Now.
Leads them in spontaneous dance

CECILE (to Team Persephone):
You understand seasons. Internal seasons.
Tell me—what season are you in?

TEAM PERSEPHONE MEMBER:
Winter. Been winter for months.

CECILE:
Winter joy is different from summer joy.
Not less. Different.
Winter joy is the bulb knowing it will bloom.
Can you feel your bulbs underground?

TEAM MEMBER:
Actually... yes. Waiting.

CECILE:
Waiting is not passive. It's gathering.
Every winter you've survived has led to spring.
This one will too. The cycle is promise.

SUSO (to Team Prometheus):
You're the fire-stealers. The innovators.
What fire does the world need?

TEAM PROMETHEUS MEMBER:
The fire of giving a damn again.

SUSO:
Apathy is Zeus keeping fire from humans.
You're going to steal it back.
But here's the secret—you already have matches.
Every small rebellion lights one.
What will you rebel against today?

ACT THREE: The Evolution Chamber

SUSO:
Your joy is evolving NOW.
Shows audience's biometric data changing
Watch—I'll prove it.
Rings bell

AUDIENCE MEMBER:
Pleasant.

SUSO:
Rings again
Still pleasant?

AUDIENCE MEMBER:
Less. Expected now.

SUSO:
Rings repeatedly
And now?

AUDIENCE MEMBER:
Annoying actually.

SUSO:
Changes rhythm, makes pattern
Now?

AUDIENCE MEMBER:
Oh! That's... interesting. Musical even.

SUSO:
THAT'S evolution! Your joy mutated in real-time.
From stimulus to irritation to pattern recognition.
You're not broken when old joys stop working.
You're updating. Evolving. Growing new joy receptors.

NILS:
Like how children's songs bore adults—

ANGELIQUE:
—But complexity we couldn't understand as children now delights us.

MOSES:
So my inability to enjoy what I used to—?

CECILE:
Is graduation, not failure.
Your joy has a PhD now.
It needs more sophisticated stimulation.

ACT FOUR: The Navigation Test

Maze created on stage

MOSES:
Forget the exit signs. They lie.
Your body knows the way.
Follow what lights up your chest.

PARTICIPANT (navigating):
But the sign says exit is right.

MOSES:
And your chest says?

PARTICIPANT:
Left. Definitely left.

MOSES:
Trust it.

Participant goes left, finds hidden treasure

PARTICIPANT:
How did my body know?

ANGELIQUE:
Every hero's journey is joy architecture.
Joseph Campbell mapped it, but your cells live it.
Leave comfort—that's separation.
Face the unknown—initiation.
Return with gifts—integration.
Your body recognizes the pattern.

CECILE:
We've been taught to trust maps over instincts.
But maps show what was.
Instincts know what's becoming.

PARTICIPANT 2:
My instincts have been wrong before.

MOSES:
Were they wrong? Or were you not listening?
There's a difference between instinct saying no
And fear saying no.
Instinct whispers. Fear screams.

SUSO:
Exactly. Joy GPS doesn't yell.
It pulls gently. Like gravity.
You feel drawn, not pushed.

FINALE: The Modern Mythology

ALL (gathering audience):
Let's write our mythology. Now. Together.

ANGELIQUE:
Once, humans forgot their gods lived inside...

AUDIENCE MEMBER 1:
They searched in temples, in books, in others...

NILS:
But the gods were hiding in their nervous systems...

AUDIENCE MEMBER 2:
Dionysus in their need to dance...

MOSES:
Trickster in their urge to break stupid rules...

AUDIENCE MEMBER 3:
Persephone in their survival of winters...

CECILE:
Apollo in their love of patterns...

AUDIENCE MEMBER 4:
Prometheus in their refusal to accept darkness...

SUSO:
And one day, they remembered:
They weren't worshipping gods.
They were recognizing themselves.

ANGELIQUE:
Your pantheon is complete.
Every deity accounted for.
Not outside. Inside.

NILS:
Your Dionysus and Apollo can coexist.
They're not fighting. They're dancing.

MOSES:
Your GPS works. Always has.
You just learned to trust maps over instincts.
Time to trust yourself again.

SUSO:
Your joy evolved for THIS moment.
This exact challenge. This precise need.
Million years of evolution prepared you.

CECILE:
Winter joy becomes spring. Always.
Not because gods promise.
Because cycles are truth.
And you ARE the cycle.

ANGELIQUE:
You're not finding mythology.
You're not even creating it.
You ARE the mythology.
Living. Breathing. Evolving.
Right now.

Final measurement: Each deity team shows different but equal joy increases

MOSES (to audience):
Which god is strongest in you now?

VARIOUS AUDIENCE MEMBERS:
Trickster! Apollo! Dionysus! Persephone!

NILS:
Good. Let them all speak.
Your inner pantheon is democracy, not monarchy.

CECILE:
Go home different. Go home multiple.
Go home knowing you contain gods.

SUSO:
And tomorrow, when joy GPS signals turn—

ALL:
TURN!
