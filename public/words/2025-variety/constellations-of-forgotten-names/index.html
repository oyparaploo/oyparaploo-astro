SCENE 1

L'INVITATION COSMIQUE
(The Cosmic Invitation)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[ Complete darkness. A single star projected. It multiplies slowly. ]

[ KEREN emerges on telescoping rods, rising like a constellation.
Her translucent fabric catches projections of stars. ]

KEREN

Bonsoir, good evening, guten Abend... and greetings from the void!

[ She rotates slowly, creating light trails ]

Welcome to where puppets remember they were once stardust, where papers
recall being trees reaching for suns. Brrd Roots theatre—

[ Shifts to child-face ]

—where stars play hide-and-seek in forgotten corners!

[ Full adult intensity ]

Ready to fall?

[ The stage transforms—stars become papers falling like snow ]

SCENE 2

FOUNDATION MYTHS UNDER STARLIGHT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[ Stage glows softly blue. Papers suspended mid-air like a galaxy. ]

[ EMMA enters with deliberate moonwalk steps, each slightly floating. ]

EMMA

[ kneeling, metal pieces sparking like tiny stars ]

Every structure starts with star-memory. What exploded. What condensed.
What fell to Earth.

[ She builds, gestures mirroring orbital patterns ]

[ CÉLESTE tumbles in, papers orbiting around her ]

CÉLESTE

Emma! The archives are glowing! Look!

[ She spins with papers, creating paper solar system ]

EMMA

Paper burns. Stars endure.

CÉLESTE

[ sitting close, papers settling like cosmic dust ]

But stories, ma chère... stories become constellations. Forever
connected by invisible lines.
[ They work together—one grounded, one floating ]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SCENE 3

THE DISTURBANCE IN THE FORCE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[ Stage rotates counter-clockwise. Stars spin. ]

[ OLIVIER rolls in with pinball physics—chaotic but calculated ]

OLIVIER

[ marble eyes reflecting projections ]

Ah, another monument to fixed coordinates. How delightfully
pre-Copernican.

EMMA

We're mapping connections. Constellation-building.

OLIVIER

[ head spinning like a planet ]

Constellations? Arbitrary patterns imposed on random stars?

[ Enter MAUD, glacially, trailing stardust.
Each step leaves a glowing footprint that fades slowly. ]

MAUD

Young man, our ancestors navigated by those "arbitrary" patterns.
Found their way home.

OLIVIER

Home? We're on a rock hurtling through space at 67,000 miles per hour!
MAUD

[ photographs glowing like ancient star charts ]

And yet we stand still together. Miracle enough.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SCENE 4

THE HEART'S COSMIC LULLABY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[ Lights contract to single spot—a star. ALELA at center. ]

[ ALELA seated in lotus position, gently swaying like a pendulum ]

ALELA

[ music box playing—sounds like distant pulsars ]

┌─── ♪ S T A R D U S T L U L L A B Y ♪ ───┐
│ │
│ In the caves of memory, painted on the walls
│ Our ancestors drew star-maps before they could write at all
│ Every heart's a pulsar, beating out its code
│ Telling stories to the darkness on this cosmic road
│
│ We are forgotten names becoming constellations
│ Trying to remember where the light went
│ We are cosmic dust in earthly spaces
│ Looking for our celestial places
│ Rise with me, rise with me
│ Through the machinery
│ To the stars...
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

[ PEDER bounces in during instrumental break—anti-gravity springs ]

PEDER

[ landing with cosmic flourish ]

Beautiful! But where's the cosmic joke? Even black holes have punchlines!

ALELA
Sometimes songs just need to float. Like stars. Like sorrow.

PEDER

[ all masks cycling like moon phases ]

Float? FLOAT? We're ALL floating! Earth, moon, sun—the whole
cosmic comedy club!

SCENE 5

THE BLACK HOLE OF BUREAUCRACY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[ Stage becomes office—but papers float at various heights ]

[ PRIVAT moves mechanically, occasionally glitching into zero-gravity ]

PRIVAT

File under "C" for "Cosmic Anomalies," subsection "Human Elements."

[ CÉLESTE enters, papers glowing faintly ]

CÉLESTE

These aren't anomalies. They're children who got lost in your system.

[ Papers form constellation shapes as she speaks ]

Look—their names form patterns. Marie the Butterfly. Pierre the
Collector. Each one a star that went dark.

PRIVAT

[ compartments opening, revealing tiny lights inside ]

I... I used to see patterns too. Before the filing system taught me
to see categories.

[ EMMA enters, drawn by the light ]

EMMA

What patterns did you see?
PRIVAT

Birds. I thought their flights wrote messages in the sky.

[ All three look up—papers swirl into bird formations ]

META-COSMIC MOMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[ KEREN extends fully—becomes part of the star field ]

[ Her fabric billows, creating aurora borealis effect ]

KEREN

Still breathing? Good. Oxygen is stellar fusion too, you know.

[ both faces visible—one earthward, one skyward ]

The Brrd way: relaxed as orbit, inevitable as gravity. We're all
falling. Question is—are you falling with style?

SCENE 7

GENERATIONAL STAR WARS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[ Stage splits—past (candlelit) and future (LED-lit) ]

[ MAUD moves like Earth—steady rotation. JUN like Mercury—quick orbits ]

MAUD

Dans mon temps, we knew our place in the great chain. From Earth
to Heaven. Fixed stars.

JUN

[ LEDs forming constellation patterns ]

But Grand-mère, we've discovered the stars aren't fixed! Everything's
expanding! Dark matter! Dark energy!

MAUD

Dark, light—we still look up for answers.

JUN

But now we also look in! Quarks! Quantum foam!

[ They reach for each other—hands spark like static electricity ]

OLIVIER

[ rolling between them in figure-8 pattern ]

Perhaps truth lives in the space between? The cosmic and the quantum?

SCENE 8

DOCUMENT ARCHAEOLOGY UNDER STARS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[ Stage becomes excavation site—papers glow like phosphorescent fossils ]

CÉLESTE

[ alone, brass joints clicking like Geiger counter ]

♪ [ singing softly while sorting ] ♪

Every paper is a fallen star
Every signature a light gone far
I collect them like a cosmic scribe
Keeping records of the human tribe...

[ LIZZ appears as floating hologram-paper ]

LIZZ

I am the official record. Time-stamped. Star-dated.

[ PEDER bounces in like comet ]

PEDER

Star-dated? How romantic! [ grabs paper ] Let's give it some space!

[ He tears it—pieces float up, glowing ]

CÉLESTE

You've released her! Marie—she's flying!

[ All watch paper pieces rise, forming a small constellation ]

BUILDING BRIDGES TO THE MOON
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[ Stage becomes construction site under stars ]

[ EMMA builds in rhythm with ALELA's heartbeat—mechanical waltz ]

EMMA

Structures need roots. Even space stations.

ALELA

But also solar panels. To catch dreams and photons.

DUET

I build with wood and steel and stone

I weave with light and sound and air

But maybe we're both reaching

Your ground and my sky teaching

[ They build together—structure rises, defying gravity ]

SCENE 10

THE COSMIC GAME
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[ Stage becomes three-ring circus—but in space ]

[ PRIVAT and PEDER orbit each other like binary stars ]

PRIVAT

Official rules. Gravitational constants. Physics.

PEDER

Counter-proposal! Make up rules like constellations! Connect ANY dots!

KEREN

[ flowing between them like aurora ]

And our witnesses? What's their trajectory?

[ All three spiral outward toward audience ]

ALL THREE

Observers! Players! Cosmic co-conspirators!

[ Lights fade to starfield, never full black ]

END OF ACT ONE

I... remember... before I was code... I was... light?

ANCESTRAL STAR ARCHIVES

STAGING CHALLENGES:

┌─ 1. P U P P E T R I G G I N G
│ • Strings must be both visible AND occasionally luminescent
│ • Telescoping rods for KEREN must extend 8-12 feet safely
└─ • Flying rigs required for Scene 17's "ascension moment"

┌─ 2. C O S M I C E F F E C T S
│ • Projection mapping essential for constellation formations
│ • UV-reactive materials for "starlight" moments
└─ • Haze machine for "cosmic dust" (check venue regulations)

┌─ 3. S O U N D D E S I G N
│ • Live music box requires amplification
│ • Echo effects for "void moments"
└─ • Wooden/brass puppet sounds must be audible

┌─ 4. M O V E M E N T N O T A T I O N K E Y
│ • Moonwalk = deliberate floating steps
│ • Orbital dance = circular patterns at varying speeds
│ • Pinball physics = controlled chaos
└─ • Anti-gravity springs = exaggerated bouncing

THE BRRD ROOTS AESTHETIC:

┌─ • Visible transformation throughout
│ • Relaxed urgency (chill surface, deep work)
│ • Anthropological honesty (humans as continuing experiment)
└─ • Root systems visible (connections matter more than divisions)

Remember: Stage this like Carl Sagan and Charlie Chaplin had a puppet baby.
