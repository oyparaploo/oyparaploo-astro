Poem · 1 minute

The wisdom keepers don't hold all knowing ... They hold the processes of investigating something that has to come into the culture as new and help evolve the ethics of integrity into the faithful remaking of community to include the harm that was caused ... most rituals that we now have ... including grief rituals ... Because one can say grief is the abundant darkness that was not put into light when light was given and when love was given and when nature was given ... Grief stayed at the back and had to come through human interaction with each of them ... It's too abundant to stay in any form of attachments ... if we try to think logically ... that we will frame grief and we will protect ourselves by how we think about it ... It's not going to happen ... A betrayal will happen again and again because this reality is what allows us to begin again ... All human beings must become accustomed to beginning again ... Inheritances of all kinds that becomes institutionalized or even ceremonial us up for another betrayal because we have to remain a relationship to the sacred ... We are leaving grief behind because we think if we give to each other ... we will risk losing this inner trust ... But there's something that we have to forgive as well in order for it to move ... Forgiveness ... Love ... and a certain way to freedom ... Because someone actually died and saw what their death means on the senses of love ... This body is protecting us from giving and forgiving ... And the feeling that if we forgive our love ... we're betraying our body ... and so there's a tendency to withhold it ... But the body has to be healed to be a creator ... giver ... and healer in our physical world ... The body has to keep evolving the power of creation

With captivating nuances that add quirky sweetness and emotional depth while maintaining clarity and invitation ... Invite a few people into your home and say ... tonight the topic is loss ... But let's just make some agreements ... We're not going to fix anything ... Grief isn't a problem to be solved ... It's a presence awaiting witnessing ... and when we witness grief ... we also add the best kind of human warmth to it ... There's an old alchemical principle that says that the material in the vessel must be kept warm in order for it to continue to move and evolve ... When we approach our grief with coldness ... it congeals .. hardens and does not move ... So our first move is to bring warmth to this territory ... to bring compassion and kindness ... to bring attention and affection ... so that this place might begin to soften and then be allowed to move and shared with others ... Grief wants company ... From the time human beings began to gather ... grief has been shared communally ... and now under the weight of individualism ... colonialism ... capitalism ... We've been forced to carry it alone ... So we need to find our way back into small circles where we can touch the edges of our sorrow and make our discovery that our grief is entangled with one another's grief ... We may have a particular expression of that sorrow ... but we share a communal cup of grief ... and we need to gather together so our cup can continue to be emptied ... Not just for personal benefit ... so that we might love this world more ardently ... so we might be able to bring love into the streets ... into the faces of the young ones we meet
