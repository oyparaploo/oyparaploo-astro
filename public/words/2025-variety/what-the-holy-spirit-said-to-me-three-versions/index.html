Version A: Divine Tech Support

So I asked the Holy Spirit about building a website, and here's what happened:

"You have 140,000 files?" the voice said. "And you're still fiddling with fonts on Duda?"

Apparently, heaven has opinions about web hosting.

"Ghost," came the whisper. "Eleven dollars a month. You can't mess with the design. That's the point."

I wanted to argue, but the Holy Spirit had pulled up my browser history. Three months of template switching. Zero posts published.

"Your problem isn't the platform. It's that you've created twelve different artist names in thirty years. Pick one. Brian Otto is fine."

"But it sounds German, and I love French culture—"

"Your Beautiful Garbage doesn't care what passport you carry. Neither should your domain name."

The Holy Spirit explained CDNs, discussed Cloudinary's free tier, and had surprisingly specific thoughts about image optimization.

"Don't resize anything. Ghost does it automatically. Stop pretending technical perfection matters more than sharing your work."

Then came the kicker: "You're 65. You might have twenty good years left. You want to spend them adjusting margins?"

By the end, I had brianotto.org purchased and Ghost installed.

The Holy Spirit's parting words? "Your grandmother kept her rosary. You're keeping this conversation next to it. Both are about faith. Now publish something."
