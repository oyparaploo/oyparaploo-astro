( was it gray or grey, we don’t know )

We love this ... from the top and bottom of our heart and soul combined! ... It all
came from local black and brown belonging soil ... and ended up in ( day and night )
different rooms ... that have glassless windows. Good day, great work. Here we go ...

Left of heart realm of mammal empath ... the only compass worth carrying into a
room like this ... Introducing a new character name ~ Aoba Walin ... whose name
alone opens a door nobody owns ... Concentration of the little boy and a detainment
camp ... as if the crime was his attention wandering ... Rifled gray day or was it grey
and gold-plated oligarch cruelty? ... Spellings change but this kind of weather never
... No respect for soldiers putting their life at risk every day ... nervous because the
zone flinches ... Somebody’s Indigenous grandmother sewed a flag that meant
something else entirely ... WASHING SMOKE IMPERIAL TON ...

We survived Mars, Texas ... until the age of eighteen. That alone is a Sentence.
Hammer Cafe Mohan Kuro. What do you think? Sinus Tsuki’s clock works. What do
you know? North star orphaned glimpse riverbed. What do you want? Space hill
song of backflip Dive(ease). When does it usually happen? Lets break for lunch and
continue with this afterwards.
