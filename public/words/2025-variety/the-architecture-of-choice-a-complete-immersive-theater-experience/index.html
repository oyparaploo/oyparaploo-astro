THE ARCHITECTURE OF CHOICE ~ A Complete Immersive Theater Experience

Words / 2025 Variety

### Based on "Be Careful What You Wish For"

---

# PRODUCTION SPECIFICATIONS

## Audience Flow
- Maximum 60 audience members per performance
- 4 groups of 15 (Groups A, B, C, D)
- Staggered entry every 5 minutes
- Convergence points: Scenes 3, 6, 9, 11
- Total duration: 90-120 minutes

## Scene Leadership
- Scenes 1-2: CARO leads
- Scenes 3-4: OLIVIER leads
- Scenes 5-6: THIERRY leads
- Scenes 7-8: PASCAL leads
- Scenes 9-10: All share equally
- Scene 11: SKY emerges as guide
- Scene 12: Rotating (changes each performance)

## Safety Protocols
- Touch only with consent signals (green wristbands)
- Exit paths always illuminated
- Safe words: "Pause" (temporary) / "Exit" (removal)
- Staff stationed at each territory boundary

# PERFORMANCE SCRIPT

## ACT ONE: THE MARKETPLACE OF EXISTENCE

### Scene 1: The Grocery Store of Life
*[TRANSITION IN: House lights fade. Sound of fluorescent buzzing. Temperature drops to 65°F]*
*[Groups A&B enter north corridor, Groups C&D enter south corridor]*

**CARO (Scene Leader):** "Welcome to the marketplace where you're expected to know what you want before you've tasted anything."

**OLIVIER:** "Just take what's convenient. What's closest."

**PASCAL:** "I've been walking these aisles for years, asking 'What purpose do you have for me?'"

**THIERRY:** "My mother shopped here. She chose based on what her mother chose."

*[If audience doesn't move toward shelves, BACKUP DIALOGUE:]*
**CARO:** "Not choosing is also a choice. Standing still is movement through time."

**[WORDLESS SEQUENCE 1: 30 seconds]**
*[Characters move through space, touching empty shelves. Sound design: heartbeats at different speeds. Audience naturally follows or stays frozen]*

**CARO:** "The paradox - remaining at the intersection of all possible paths isn't preserving your options. It's choosing to live at an intersection."

**OLIVIER:** "You can't build anything meaningful at a crossing point."

**SKY** (appearing suddenly): "What are you hungry for that you've never tasted?"

*[TRANSITION OUT: Shelves retract into walls. Garden elements descend. Temperature rises to 70°F]*

### Scene 2: The Prayer Garden + Digital Illusion Chamber (COMBINED)
*[Groups A&B experience prayer first, C&D experience digital]*

#### Prayer Side (Led by PASCAL)

**PASCAL:** "When I kneel and ask for divine purpose, I'm surrendering the need to know."

**CARO:** "How do you distinguish divine guidance from projected desires?"

**THIERRY:** "My grandmother knelt here every morning. She died still listening."

*[If no one kneels, BACKUP:]*
**PASCAL:** "Standing is also prayer. Your posture is your theology."

#### Digital Side (Led by OLIVIER)

**OLIVIER:** "Think of your attention as water in a river. Now imagine thousands of tiny pumps diverting streams in different directions."

**CARO:** "Every notification, a pump. Every scroll, a diversion."

**THIERRY:** "Research shows twenty-three minutes to refocus after interruption. But you never get those twenty-three minutes."

*[SENSORY EXPERIENCE: Screens flicker with notifications while prayer space remains dark]*

*[GROUPS SWITCH SIDES after 5 minutes]*

*[TRANSITION: Screens and garden elements rise. Floor becomes gentle slope]*

---

## ACT TWO: THE ARCHITECTURE OF DRIFT

### Scene 3: The Sliding Scale (All Groups Converge)
*[Floor tilts gradually without audience awareness]*

**OLIVIER (Scene Leader):** "When we speak of sliding into situations without intention..."

**CARO:** "Picture yourself walking on this gently sloping floor."

**THIERRY:** "Someone leaves a toothbrush. Then clothes. One lease ends."

**PASCAL:** "Family keeps asking about marriage."

*[Audience realizes they've descended during dialogue]*

**SKY:** "You've been sliding this whole scene. Did you notice when it began?"

**[MOVEMENT SEQUENCE 2: 45 seconds]**
*[Characters create physical patterns showing drift - circular walking, gradual pulling apart. No words, just movement and breath]*

### Scene 4: The Practice Room (NEW SCENE)
*[Audience paired with strangers for synchronization exercise]*

**CARO:** "Before you can choose partnership, practice coordination."

*[Physical exercise: Pairs must move platforms together]*

**OLIVIER:** "Notice who leads, who follows, who negotiates."

**THIERRY:** "My parents never learned to move together. Forty years of stepping on feet."

**PASCAL:** "Synchronization isn't matching. It's complementing."

**SKY:** "Some pairs are already fighting. Some have found rhythm. Both are data."

*[If pairs struggle, BACKUP:]*
**CARO:** "Failure to synchronize is also information. Not all combinations work."

*[TRANSITION: Platforms separate. Mirrors descend with lighting that creates infinite reflections]*

---

## ACT THREE: THE QUESTION OF KNOWING

### Scenes 5-6: Mirror Maze + Measurement Chamber (COMBINED)
*[Groups A&B start in mirrors, C&D in measurement. Switch after 7 minutes]*

#### Mirror Side (Led by THIERRY)

**THIERRY:** "Being seen as attractive isn't required for dignity, but it IS necessary for certain specific goals."

**CARO:** "The hidden tax: those deemed less attractive must develop superior skills for equal treatment."

**OLIVIER:** "Sometimes being too attractive creates barriers. There's a sweet spot - attractive enough but approachable."

#### Measurement Side (Led by PASCAL)

**PASCAL:** "We live trying to capture smoke with numbers. How much am I wanted?"

**SKY:** "Career failure is a problem to solve. Social rejection is a verdict on your soul."

**CARO:** "Ten thousand followers, desperate loneliness. Twelve close friends, deep satisfaction."

*[PURE SENSORY MOMENT: 60 seconds of just temperature changes and shifting lights. No dialogue. Bodies in space]*

### Scene 7: The Alone Room
*[Individual spotlights isolate each audience member]*

**SKY (Scene Leader):** "Your fear about tomorrow is your mind attempting to solve what can't be solved."

**PASCAL:** "Being alone now doesn't mean the universe has rejected you."

**THIERRY:** "The fear you feel is ancient biology. Ancestors survived in groups."

**CARO:** "The paradox: desperate need for partnership often prevents it."

*[If audience members reach for each other, BACKUP:]*
**OLIVIER:** "Reaching is also aloneness. You reach because you're separate."

---

## ACT FOUR: THE CHOICE CATHEDRAL

### Scene 8: Dating App Wasteland (TRIMMED)
*[Quick montage of swiping projections - 3 minutes max]*

**CARO:** "Five-second clips of songs, asking if each could be your favorite forever."

**OLIVIER:** "Every swipe triggers dopamine. Variable ratio reinforcement - addiction's pattern."

**THIERRY:** "The alternative: meet through what you love. Authentic habitat."

**PASCAL:** "Shared activities create misattribution of arousal. This is how bonding naturally works."

**ALL:** "Binary outcomes - match or rejection - replaced by spectrum of connection."

### Scene 9: Family Formation Laboratory
*[All groups converge]*

**CARO:** "Can everyone have joyful family? Theoretically yes. Practically, no."

*[Rapid-fire exchange, characters overlapping:]*
**THIERRY:** "Refugee camps-"
**PASCAL:** "Mental illness-"
**OLIVIER:** "Abuse-"
**SKY:** "Real obstacles beyond choice."

**CARO:** "Yet humans show remarkable creativity forming family-like bonds."

*[MOVEMENT SEQUENCE 3: Characters physically demonstrate different family structures through positioning]*

### Scene 10: The Synchronization Chamber
*[Two large platforms requiring coordinated movement]*

**CARO:** "Picture two people bound together. Initially appears as limitation."

**OLIVIER:** "Yet with right understanding, transforms into power neither could achieve alone."

**THIERRY:** "Partnership becomes the lens through which all else is processed."

**PASCAL:** "When synchronization works, every challenge becomes manageable."

**SKY:** "When it fails, every movement contested."

*[Audience must work together to move platforms]*

---

## ACT FIVE: THE RESOLUTION

### Scene 11: The Choice Cathedral (REFINED)
*[All elements from previous scenes visible but fading]*

**CARO:** "When you pick a life partner, you're choosing your whole future family."

**THIERRY:** "The family for generations after you."

**[CHARACTER TRANSFORMATIONS BEGIN]**

**CARO** (accepting uncertainty): "I thought choice meant control. But choosing is surrendering to what we create together."

**OLIVIER** (acknowledging cost): "I championed drift to avoid pain. But avoiding choice is choosing emptiness."

**PASCAL** (embracing questions): "I sought answers in the garden. The garden taught me to love questions."

**THIERRY** (releasing patterns): "I carried my parents' marriage like stones. Tonight, I set them down. Here. Now."

**SKY** (gentle intervention): "I've watched long enough. Time to choose. Not for you. With you."

**ALL** (building):
"No matter where you come from..."
"You have the power to create your own family future..."
"The partner you choose becomes your teammate..."
"This is the deepest human power..."
"Creating the ground your children will walk on..."

### Scene 12: The Release
*[House lights at 25%. Performance lighting remains]*

**REMAINING CHARACTER** (rotates each show):
"The experiment continues outside. Every conversation after this carries tonight. The measurement of being wanted you'll check later - that's part of this. The appearance you'll adjust tomorrow - that's part of this. The family you're building or avoiding - that's part of this.

We've given you no answers, only better questions. The path forward isn't to not be afraid. Fear is human. The path is to be afraid and continue anyway.

You entered through a door. You can exit through the same door. Or different door. Or make a door. Or stay. Staying is also choice."

*[Final wordless sequence: Characters slowly freeze in tableau as audience decides when/how to leave]*

# TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

## Transition Protocols
- Scene changes: 45-90 seconds maximum
- Sound bridges all transitions (no silence)
- Temperature shifts begin 30 seconds before scene end
- Lighting follows 5-4-3-2-1 second warning pattern

## Backup Protocols
- If <5 audience members engage: switch to intimate mode
- If technical failure: characters acknowledge and incorporate
- If audience distress: immediate comfort protocol
- If overtime: skip Scene 8, combine 9&10

## Fast Track Version (75 minutes)
- Combine Scenes 1-2 (7 min)
- Combine Scenes 5-6 (10 min)
- Cut Scene 8 entirely
- Trim Scene 11 to essential transformation only
- Reduce wordless sequences to 30 seconds each

*Character transformations: 5*
