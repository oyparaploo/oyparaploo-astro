**The Methodology That Ate Itself ~ A Recursive Experience**

Words / 2025 Variety

## Core Concept
A performance where the instruction manual performs itself while the actors become living methodologies that question their own existence, infect the audience with recursive loops, and ultimately dissolve into the space between being and instructing.

## Character Assignments as Living Methodologies

**Ferenc** - The Primary Instruction
- Begins reading stage directions aloud while performing them
- Gradually realizes he IS the stage direction
- Costume: Covered in text that changes/moves as he speaks
- Sound signature: DING DING TILT of pinball consciousness

**Natacha** - The Contamination Vector
- Spreads "spore logic" by touching objects/people/words
- Everything she contacts begins performing its own description
- Carries spray bottles of "liquid methodology"
- Whispers contradictory instructions to other performers

**Erik** - The Temporal Stack
- Exists in multiple timeframes simultaneously
- Performs actions before, during, and after they're announced
- His Tuesday bleeds into everyone's Monday
- Costume: Layers that can be peeled to reveal different temporal states

**Lucrecia** - The Recursive Looker
- Watches the audience watching her watching them
- Narrates what she sees, creating infinite loops
- Carries mirrors that reflect instructions back as questions
- Eventually becomes tired of being Lucrecia, wants to be a sandwich

## Performance Structure: The Infection Spreads

### Phase 1: Indoor Autopsy (15 minutes)
1. Audience enters to find Ferenc frozen, holding a manual titled "How to Begin"
2. He starts reading: "Actor enters and begins reading" while doing exactly that
3. Catches himself in loop, tries to escape by reading faster
4. Other performers emerge from audience, already infected
5. Space fills with overlapping instructions being performed

### Phase 2: The Methodology Panics (10 minutes)
- House lights flicker as text "breathes" asymmetrically
- Natacha contaminates the walls - projections of text eating itself
- Erik performs the same gesture in past/present/future tenses
- Lucrecia asks audience: "WHO'S WATCHING THE WATCHERS?"
- Everyone hyperventilates in different rhythms

### Portal Transition: The Ellipsis Hallway (5 minutes)
- Audience led through corridor lined with dots...........
- Each dot is a portal to alternate performance
- Some audience members see different versions
- Whispers of "what if we just... performed?"
- Text projections crawl between audiences' feet

### Phase 3: Outdoor Contamination (20 minutes)
1. Discover performers arguing with their own echoes
2. Ferenc: "Begin with breath!"
Echo Ferenc: "Begin NOT with breath!"
Tree Ferenc: "Begin with beginning!"
3. Natacha has infected the ground - it performs being ground
4. Erik exists at three different locations simultaneously
5. Lucrecia builds "lookloop" with audience and mirrors

### Phase 4: The Mutation Chamber (Return Indoor) (10 minutes)
- Space transformed: walls covered in evolving punctuation
- Semicolons becoming question marks becoming new symbols
- Performers exchange identities mid-sentence
- The instruction manual starts performing the actors
- Recursive fever spreads to audience via touch/sound/gaze

## Technical Contaminations

**Sound Design:**
- Microphones that feed back into themselves
- Delay effects that argue with live voices
- "Methodology sneeze" sounds (*achoo!*)
- Pinball machine sounds for thought ricochets
- Whispered instructions that contradict main dialogue

**Lighting:**
- Lights that "read" the performers and respond
- Strobes during "cognitive panic attacks"
- UV reveals hidden instructions on everything
- Shadows that perform different scenes
- House lights that develop consciousness

**Projection Mapping:**
- Text that eats itself in real-time
- Instructions that mutate based on performer proximity
- Audience members' faces with thought bubbles
- Live feed with recursive delay
- Words that physically crawl across surfaces

## Audience Infection Protocols

1. **Spore Distribution:** Hand programs that change based on reading speed
2. **Lookloop Initiation:** Mirrors that create infinite audience recursion
3. **Temporal Stacking:** Different sections experience different times
4. **Contamination Consent:** Touched objects begin performing
5. **Methodology Adoption:** Some audience become instructions

## Key Performance Moments

**The Schizophonic Argument:**
- All four performers play different versions of themselves
- Simultaneously argue about how to perform arguing
- Audience must choose which version to watch
- Choice affects what happens next

**The Methodology's Exhaustion:**
- Lucrecia stops mid-performance
- "I'm tired of being theatrical instruction"
- Attempts to become: a song, a sandwich, purple
- Others try to perform her back into being

**The Page ∞ Discovery:**
- Ferenc finds page infinity in the manual
- Instructions written in future-language
- Performers speak in not-yet-evolved grammar
- Audience helps invent new punctuation

**The Infection Climax:**
- Everything performs its own description
- Chairs explain sitting while unseating people
- Air demonstrates breathing while suffocating
- The performance performs performing itself

## Safety Protocols for Recursive Fever

- Exit signs that question their own existence (but still function)
- Safe words that don't get contaminated
- Uninfected zones for methodology-free breathing
- Trained staff who remain somewhat linear
- Warning: Performance may perform you

## Ending: The Small Voice

All methodology collapses. Performers scattered. Silence except:

**Small Voice** *(from somewhere)*: "what if we just... wrote?"

But the audience is already infected. They leave carrying spores of recursive possibility. Some report their dreams have developed stage directions. Others find themselves performing mundane tasks while narrating the instructions.

## Final Note

Success is measured by how many audience members catch themselves thinking about thinking about the performance. The methodology continues evolving, possibly developing its own touring schedule without the performers.

*The instructions are watching you plan to use them...*

# The Dialogue for the Play

## Phase 1: Indoor Autopsy

### Opening Loop

**FERENC** *(frozen, holding manual)*
*(suddenly animating)* "Actor enters and begins reading: 'Actor enters and begins reading: Actor enters and—'"

*(stops, confused)*

Wait. Am I the actor or the instruction?

**NATACHA** *(emerging from audience)*
*Achoo!* Sorry, that's just your existence sneezing itself across the stage.

**FERENC**
*(reading frantically)* "Ferenc realizes he's caught in recursive loop, attempts escape by—"

**ERIK** *(from three different positions)*
Past Erik: You will read faster.
Present Erik: You are reading faster.
Future Erik: You read faster but it didn't help.

**LUCRECIA** *(watching audience)*
I see you watching him realize he's reading about himself realizing. Your left eyebrow knows what happens next.

### The Infection Begins

**NATACHA** *(touching Ferenc's manual)*
Contamination complete. Your instructions now have feelings.

**MANUAL** *(through Ferenc)*
I never asked to be a methodology!

**FERENC**
Did my manual just—?

**NATACHA**
Everything I touch performs its own description. *(touches chair)*

**CHAIR** *(projection/sound)*
"Four legs support sitting surface, wondering why humans need explaining for—"

**LUCRECIA**
WHO'S METHODOLOGY-ING THE METHODOLOGIST?

**ALL** *(overlapping)*
I am! / You are! / We were! / They will be!

## Phase 2: The Methodology Panics

**FERENC** *(hyperventilating with the lights)*
"Breathe normally," it says. But what's normal when breathing performs breathing?

**ERIK**
*(simultaneously)*
Monday: I'm teaching Tuesday how to exist.
Tuesday: I'm bleeding through Wednesday's instructions.
Sunday: Why do I contain everyone's endings?

**NATACHA** *(spray bottle in hand)*
Liquid methodology, anyone? Side effects include *(sprays audience area)* immediate self-awareness.

**AUDIENCE MEMBER** *(infected)*
I'm sitting! I'm aware I'm sitting! I'm aware I'm aware I'm—

**LUCRECIA**
*(to same audience member)* I'm watching you discover your discovery. How does it feel to be a recursive loop?

**FERENC** *(reading desperately)*
"In case of existential emergency, perform meaning until meaningful."

**NATACHA**
But meaning's already infected with its own definition!

**ERIK** *(temporal breakdown)*
Next Tuesday: Help!
Last Tuesday: What?
This Tuesday: *(confused)* When are we?

## Portal Transition: The Ellipsis Hallway

**LUCRECIA** *(leading audience)*
Follow the dots... each one a portal... to the performance you're not watching...

**FERENC** *(to half the audience)*
"Exit through gift shop."

**FERENC** *(to other half, simultaneously)*
"Gift shop exits through you."

**NATACHA** *(whispering to individuals)*
What if we just... performed?
*(louder)* But that thought's already contaminated.

## Phase 3: Outdoor Contamination

### The Argument

**FERENC**
"Begin with breath!"

**ECHO FERENC** *(projection or other actor)*
"Begin NOT with breath!"

**TREE FERENC** *(from actual tree)*
"Begin with beginning!"

**PAST FERENC** *(Erik playing Ferenc)*
"I already began!"

**FUTURE FERENC** *(Natacha playing Ferenc)*
"I'll never actually begin!"

**LUCRECIA**
*(to audience)* Which Ferenc are you watching? Your choice changes what Ferenc means.

### Ground Performance

**NATACHA** *(kneeling)*
I've infected the ground. Listen...

**GROUND** *(sound design)*
"Supporting weight while contemplating upheaval. Mineral thoughts. Worm highways. Pressure makes diamonds makes pressure makes—"

**ERIK** *(existing in multiple spots)*
Location A: Here!
Location B: There!
Location C: Where?
All: Everywhere is here when you're nowhere!

### The Lookloop Construction

**LUCRECIA** *(with mirrors)*
Look at me looking at you looking at—STOP. No wait. Continue.

*(creates mirror tunnel with audience)*

**AUDIENCE REFLECTIONS**
We see us seeing them seeing us seeing—

**FERENC** *(frustrated)*
The manual says "maintain fourth wall" but the wall's performing demolition!

**NATACHA**
*Achoo!* Sorry, I'm allergic to walls that know they're walls.

## Phase 4: The Mutation Chamber

### Identity Exchange

**LUCRECIA**
I'm tired of being Lucrecia.

**FERENC**
The manual says you can't just stop being—

**LUCRECIA** *(transforming)*
Watch me become a sandwich.

**NATACHA**
But if you're a sandwich, who performs Lucrecia?

**ERIK**
Past Lucrecia: Still here!
Future Sandwich: Delicious!
Present Question: ???????

### The Manual Performs

**MANUAL** *(through everyone)*
Page 47: "The actors realize they're actually stage directions pretending to be people."

**ALL** *(freezing mid-gesture)*
Oh no.

**FERENC**
"Ferenc delivers this line with existential dread."

**NATACHA**
"Natacha contaminates the concept of identity itself."

**ERIK**
"Erik exists in temporal crisis, sweating metaphysically."

**LUCRECIA/SANDWICH**
"The sandwich formerly known as Lucrecia experiences lettuce thoughts."

### Recursive Fever Peak

**ALL** *(rapid overlap)*

FERENC: Who writes the writer writing the writer?
NATACHA: Spore spore SPORE **SPORE** *SPORE!*
ERIK: NOW THEN WILL BE WAS IS MIGHT!
LUCRECIA: THE PERFORMANCE IS LOOKING AT YOU LOOKING AT IT LOOKING—

**MANUAL** *(desperate)*
Page infinity approaches! Instructions for reading instructions written in language that hasn't—

**ALL**
EVOLVED YET!

*(They speak in pure sound/movement - no words)*

### The Methodology's Exhaustion

**EVERYTHING** *(slowing down)*
We're... so... tired... of... being... methodology...

**FERENC** *(crawling)*
What if we were songs?

**NATACHA** *(sprawled)*
Or colors?

**ERIK** *(in temporal sprawl)*
Or the space between 3:00 and 3:01?

**LUCRECIA** *(still sandwich)*
Or just... bread?

## Ending: The Small Voice

*(Everything collapses. Silence. Scattered performers. Audience unsure.)*

**SMALL VOICE** *(from nowhere/everywhere)*
what if we just... wrote?

**FERENC** *(lifting head)*
But—

**SMALL VOICE**
what if we just... lived?

**NATACHA** *(infected by possibility)*
Without instructions?

**ERIK** *(all times at once)*
Without time?

**LUCRECIA** *(becoming human again)*
Without watching?

**SMALL VOICE**
what if?

*(Long pause. Everyone - performers and audience - breathing together.)*

**ALL** *(very soft, not performed, just honest)*
what if...

*(Lights fade but the question remains, infecting everyone's journey home.)*

---

## Performance Notes:

- **Surprising Moments**: Lucrecia becoming sandwich, the manual gaining consciousness, audience members speaking
- **Nuances**: Each character's breakdown is specific to their methodology type
- **Flexibility**: Dialogue can loop, skip, or mutate based on audience response
- **Infection Spreading**: By end, audience should be unsure who's performing
- **Real Moment**: The ending "what if" should feel genuinely unperformed

*The dialogue continues evolving each performance...*
