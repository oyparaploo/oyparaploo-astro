Steel, Sweat and Tariffs

Shattered Ceilings, Rising Horizons

Uniting for Equality's Symphony

Systemic gender discrimination,
And the broader biases that wound marginalized groups,
Are woven deep in the nation’s roots.
They linger in the shadows of history’s gaze,
In the culture we’ve built, in societal haze.
These are not merely lines on a ledger;
They are lives shaped by countless pressure.

For too long, society locked brilliance away,
Half its power, its heart, its sway.
Laws and norms decreed, “Stay unseen,
Stay silent, stay on the margins between.”
Women, sidelined. LGBTQ+ denied.
At the intersections, hope had to hide.
But these scars, visible and hidden within,
Cry not for pity but justice to begin.

To accept injustices without resolve,
Is to fail the humanity we should evolve.
We cannot call ourselves complete,
While voices are silenced, while dreams retreat.

The Legacy of Constraints
For decades, laws created walls,
Cultural bias fortified their halls.
Women were barred from wealth, from rise,
Their visions dismissed, their worth denied.

Even when laws began to change,
Bias whispered in echoes strange.
Lowered wages, ceilings unseen,
Opportunities scarce, futures lean.

Consider Mary, whose brilliance burned,
For leadership roles, she was spurned.
Her male peers climbed with ease and grace,
While Mary waited, finding no space.

Yet the lab hummed, the molecules stirred,
Whispering truths her brilliance conferred.
“Your voice will echo, it’s vital, it’s whole,
Beyond these shadows, beyond this toll.”

To silence Mary’s work’s refrain,
Is to bury solutions, humanity’s gain.

Modern Mechanisms of Bias
Today, these barriers still remain,
Wearing new faces yet leaving pain.

In wages: a gap persists, unfair,
A chasm dividing effort and care.
Advancement sees ceilings too high to breach,
Networks tilted out of reach.

In classrooms, resources falter and fade,
Girls run marathons in shoes ill-made.
Society whispers: “It’s not your lane.”
Dreams diminish; voices wane.

Imagine Emily, engineering dreams in hand,
Drawing bridges that could span the land.
Yet doubts drown her voice in hushed refrain,
“You don’t belong here,” they explain.

Still, blueprints glimmer in constellations above,
Lines of hope, threads of love.
Her brilliance waits to burst the chains,
A future shaped by what remains.

In finance, scrutiny sharper than knives,
Doors closing, preventing lives.
Generational wealth fades like mist,
A future of fairness long dismissed.

The Economic Costs of Inequality
Imagine orchestras with silence abound,
Music lost, progress unwound.
Communities dim where potential stays,
Bound by bias that blocks the rays.

Generations pass; poverty grows,
Branches spread where despair flows.
Each child inherits fewer dreams,
Each future carries faded schemes.

A society ignoring voices unspoken,
Is a bond with humanity, cruelly broken.

Efforts to Dismantle Barriers
Yet this tale turns, hope prevails,
Against the winds, progress sails.

Laws of courage, like Title IX,
Promise change, bright designs.
Affirmative programs carve pathways wide,
Though debates linger at their side.

Movements rise, stereotypes fracture,
Narratives shift, inclusion captures.
Grace speaks of ceilings kissed by mist,
Pierced by progress, by the sun’s twist.

Breaking the Cycle
To end bias, all roads must meet,
Race, ability, class complete.
From classrooms up to boardroom seats,
Empathy calls for a world replete.

This isn’t just about what's fair,
It’s unlocking minds, talent rare.
Creative forces, vibrant dreams,
Futures bound by harmonic streams.

Justice leads as our compass guide,
Equality shines, starry-eyed.

A Future Defined by Potential
This is not a tale of despair;
It’s a song of hope sung everywhere.
Communities rise, voices ascend,
Barriers crumble, their reign at end.

Prosperity blooms in a world set free,
Built on fairness, creativity.
When stories are honored and dreams take flight,
We find a path bathed in light.

Together we rise, together we reach,
For equality’s harmony, beauty’s speech.
Hand in hand with the shimmering glow,
Humanity’s promise continues to grow.
