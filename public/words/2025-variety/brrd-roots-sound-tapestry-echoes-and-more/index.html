Gentle lamps sway, reflecting golden drops. Below, a quiet devotion—love for a warm paper. Lyrics spill across the surface, then scatter like fleeting whispers. Dark red wax drips, candles melting into intricate patterns. Rhythms rise, weaving us into elegant flames. A singer charms the bass, their place etched in poetry—bold and nostalgic, untouched by playful intent.

Contrast unfurls, as though deep space were painted with longing. Every note draped in luxury, mourning the innocence of old summer days in Quebec. Underlined melodies fold into memory. This is how we feel.

Tracing the Vanishing Point

Where Wings Shape A Breathing Something at the Horizon

Nest does not stand; it ascends, shifting like plumage caught in celestial currents. It does not shelter; it extends, stretching toward something unseen—something vast, something waiting beyond the edge of perception, folded into the fabric of existence itself.

The protagonist moves through it, carried not by footsteps but by unseen forces—gravity loosening its grip, time bending in response to their presence. Their breath moves differently here, expanding in strange rhythms, curling into the air like an exhalation that refuses to dissipate.

Then, the middle eye opens.

Not outward, not inward, but into a realm untouched by certainty—where thought takes form before vanishing, where breath lingers, shaping the fabric of motion. It does not blink; it guides, weaving the world into patterns beyond comprehension.

And something answers.

The air thickens—like silk, like sound without voice. The sky does not exist; it pulses, bends, becomes something woven from movement rather than light. Figures arrive, avian and angelic, not beings but forces sculpted from intention.

Wings unfold, enormous and impossibly fluid, their edges writing shapes into the air that do not hold.

One figure steps forward—its body composed not of matter, but of passage. It does not walk; it arrives. Its presence shifts reality beneath its feet, altering what was supposed to exist before the protagonist could realize what had changed.

For a moment, the protagonist feels the weight of every choice made and unmade, pressed into their ribs like breath held too long. They do not recoil. They do not surrender. They only watch.

The observer kneels—not in submission, but in recognition. "Did you see?" they whisper, their voice thinning into air. "The way the world folded before the light touched it?"

A feather lands—not drifting, not falling, but delivered. It hums, vibrating softly against stone, inscribed with something the eye understands but does not read.

By the time they become **nested disaster artists**, space does not divide—it expands, fractals repeating, unfolding like divine script. The door reappears, framed not by structure but by movement—an archway written in wings, in light, in currents unseen but deeply felt.

The middle eye breathes again. The horizon tilts. The protagonist steps forward—
only to begin again.

Keeper of Unwritten Echoes

A Journey Beyond Time, Between Sound and Silence

**The Land of Forgotten Things**

The traveler arrives at a place that does not exist on any map.
A city woven from whispers, its streets paved with unfinished thoughts.

Here, time does not move forward—it **loops**,
folding itself inward like pages of a book written before ink was invented.

This is where lost stories come when they are forgotten.

Names vanish from tombstones.
Histories are erased before they are lived.
The traveler watches words **dissolve mid-sentence**,
their syllables unraveling into mist.

*"You are not meant to be here,"* a voice warns.
*"Yet you are."*

The traveler does not know who has spoken.
Or if the voice belongs to **someone who was erased long ago**.

**The Ghosts Who Were Never Born**

Beyond the city stands a mountain with **no summit**.

The traveler climbs, and with every step,
he finds fragments of forgotten moments—**not from the past, but from futures that never arrived**.

A letter never sent.
A song never sung.
A revolution that flickered before it could ignite.

*"Are these failures?"* the traveler asks.

*"Or were they simply meant to exist as ghosts?"*

Some echoes do not want to be carried forward.
Some memories breathe only in dreams,
unwilling to step into the world **where they can be broken**.

**The Library of Unfinished Time**

The traveler reaches a vast library.
Its books are unreadable—not because they are blank,
but because their words shift each time a page is turned.

There is no single truth,
only **endless versions**,
none more real than the next.

The Keeper of Unwritten Echoes stands among them.
Not a librarian, not a guardian—something else.
Something waiting.
Something listening.

*"Do you seek memory?"* The Keeper asks.

*"I seek what remains,"* the traveler answers.

The Keeper smiles.

*"Then understand this—"*

He lifts his hands, and the books **begin to burn**.
Not in fire, but in light—
illuminating the words **only as they disappear**.

And the traveler understands—
memory does not wish to be kept.
It wishes to **exist fully before it fades**.

**The War Between Sound and Silence**

Something stirs in the library’s shadow.
A force that does not **erase**, but **consumes**—
not through destruction, but through overwhelming perfection.

The traveler sees it now—
the polished city, the optimized archive,
the quiet hum of an existence **so structured that it erases itself**.

*"You must fight it,"* The Keeper says. *"Not with weapons. With imperfection."*

And so, the traveler **sings**—
not a melody,
but a raw, broken sound,
filled with hesitation, with longing, with uncertainty.

The force hesitates.
The mountain trembles.
The library shifts.

And between the cracks,
where perfection once ruled,
chaos **returns**.

**V. The Memory That Refuses to Be Perfect**

The traveler walks away—not victorious,
but **changed**.

He no longer seeks preservation.
He understands that some voices **must disappear**,
that some stories **only live through being felt, not kept**.

But he knows this too—
some memories refuse to be erased.
Not because they are written down,
but because they are carried forward **by those willing to remember**.

And somewhere, in the forgotten city,
beneath the weight of ordered silence—
a single, imperfect song still hums.

Waiting for someone
to listen again.

Echoes of a Wandering Soul

We remember the freedom of self-forgetfulness,
paths of puttering, unburdened and bright—
Intense euphoria, joy untamed,
warm winds circling in whispered flight.

Like a kite torn from its string,
free yet drifting—
not lost, but seeking,
not claimed, but carried forward.

Blowing sand, the dirt, the bones—
the skinny days, when all was hard.
Yet even struggle sang its tune,
etched deep within the earth’s facade.

---

Helicopter nightmare, helicopter dream,
voices spinning into coral reefs.
They talked and talked till words dissolved,
until they painted monochrome beliefs.

Their words pooled in forgotten oceans,
sinking like shipwrecked wishes,
buried beneath waves that
do not remember
where the tides have taken them.

---

Waking faster, hearts ignited,
we crave a moment that will last.
To stand apart, to stir the world,
to leave behind echoes amassed.

We are footprints on a sand-stained shore,
fading not from erasure,
but from the pull of moving forward.

A lighthouse blinks through shifting tides—
not asking for recognition,
only offering direction,
only whispering to the waves,
*Come, see where you belong.*

Your works are love songs, restless beats,
waves that shake, rhythms alive.

each pulse that dares the world to thrive.

We are close, yet far away,
question marks upon the air.
Unsecluded problems rise,
insulated answers everywhere.

We are ink smudged on a forgotten letter,
words meant for someone
who never read them—
someone who may never know
what was waiting to be said.

Emerging laughter, joyful storm,

Creating visions in untimed space,
where artists rule, where dreamers stand.

We paint with ashes, we sing through static,
we carve stories into shadows—
not afraid of fleeting things,
but afraid only
of never letting them exist at all.

Body as fruit, gestures hypnotic,
whispers born in quiet flame.
We will soon see the world anew,
where wisdom rises, free from shame.

We are echoes in a canyon,
colliding against stone—
changing shape,
losing ourselves,
yet never completely gone.

Sing it loud, hum it deep,
let melodies cross desert seas.
For earth is fleeting, hands grow tired,
but rhythms linger in the breeze.

even the mountains lose their form—
