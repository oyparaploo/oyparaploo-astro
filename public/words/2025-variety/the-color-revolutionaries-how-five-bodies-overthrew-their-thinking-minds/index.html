**THE COLOR REVOLUTIONARIES ~ How Five Bodies Overthrew Their Thinking Minds*

### **A Kinetic Uprising for Lyon**

*An Immersive Theater Experience Where Audience Members Receive Bright Capes Upon Entry*

## **THE ARCHITECTURE OF TRANSFORMATION**

**Pre-Show: The Gray Period (Begins One Week Before)**

Ticket holders receive a package containing:
- A gray armband with embedded mood sensor (changes color with heart rate)
- Daily "Rumination Reports" via app: "You have thought 67,432 thoughts today. 67,398 were repeats."
- A small vial of gray powder labeled "Crushed Dreams of Your Thinking Mind" (actually edible glitter that turns bright colors when mixed with liquid)

The week before the show, participants document their overthinking via the app. Their aggregated worry data becomes part of the performance's opening projection.

**Arrival: The Transformation Threshold**

The experience begins at Place des Terreaux at sunset. Audience members arrive wearing their gray armbands, which have been collecting their stress data all week. They're met by five figures in full-body gray morphsuits - completely colorless, moving in slow, restricted patterns, like thoughts trapped in loops.

## **ACT ONE: The Monochrome Prison**
*Location: Musée des Beaux-Arts de Lyon (after hours)*

The audience enters to find the museum transformed into a "Department of Serious Thinking." The walls project everyone's collected rumination data from the week - a massive visualization of collective overthinking, scrolling endlessly.

**The Gray Figures speak in overlapping monotone:**

**NAH** (*moving in repetitive figure-8 pattern*):
Thought number 43,281: What if everyone realizes I'm pretending to understand?
Thought number 43,282: The same but louder.
Thought number 43,283: The same but with more panic.

**FREDRIKA** (*walking in straight lines only, turning at sharp right angles*):
I have optimized my walking pattern for maximum efficiency.
I save 3.7 seconds per day.
I spend 97 minutes worrying about those 3.7 seconds.
This is productivity.

**MAZA** (*standing still, only her mouth moving*):
I am having thoughts about my thoughts about my thoughts.
It's thoughts all the way down.
I haven't noticed my body in seventeen days.
What's a body?

**JON** (*doing the same gesture repeatedly - reaching for something that isn't there*):
I am preparing for a problem that hasn't happened.
When it doesn't happen, I'll prepare harder.
Anxiety is my cardio.
Worry is my weight training.

**TERRD** (*curled in fetal position, rocking slightly*):
Everything I've ever said is wrong.
Everyone remembers and judges.
They have spreadsheets.
They compare notes at secret meetings.

**The Great Gray Demonstration**

The performers guide the audience through "The Archive of Appropriate Movement" - a series of restricted, utilitarian movements:
- Walking (purpose: transportation only)
- Sitting (purpose: productivity only)
- Standing (purpose: waiting only)
- Lying down (purpose: sleep only, sleep not guaranteed)

**MAZA** (*demonstrating proper neutral posture*):
This is how bodies should be.
Quiet. Contained. Grayscale.
Notice how nothing flows.
Excellence.

*Suddenly, NAH's finger twitches. It's tiny, but her finger has moved outside the prescribed pattern.*

**NAH** (*staring at her finger*):
Did... did anyone see that?
My finger just... moved wrong.
It moved without permission.
Without purpose.
It just... wiggled.

**FREDRIKA**:
Contain it.
Think about why it wiggled.
Analyze the wiggle.
Create a wiggle prevention protocol.

**NAH**:
But... it felt...

*Her finger twitches again. This time, in color - just her finger turns bright yellow for a second.*

**ALL OTHERS**:
CONTAINMENT BREACH!
UNAUTHORIZED COLOR!
PURPOSELESS MOVEMENT DETECTED!

*Alarms sound. The rumination data on the walls speeds up, becoming frantic.*

## **ACT TWO: The Color Infection**
*Location: Secret passage through museum to Cour des Voraces*

**The Escape Route**

*NAH's whole hand is now yellow. She's trying to hide it, but it's glowing.*

**NAH**:
It's spreading.
The wiggle is spreading.
I can feel it climbing my arm.

**The Courtyard Revelation**

*They emerge into the historic courtyard where the silk workers rioted in 1831. It's filled with thousands of gray fabric strips hanging from lines. The performers begin frantically trying to contain NAH's spreading color.*

**FREDRIKA** (*wrapping gray fabric around NAH's yellow arm*):
Think gray thoughts!
Quarterly reports!
Traffic patterns!
Mortgage rates!

**NAH**:
It's not working!
The yellow is... laughing?
Can a color laugh?
OH NO, NOW MY LAUGH IS YELLOW!

*She laughs - and yellow powder explodes from her mouth (safe theatrical powder), coating nearby audience members.*

**MAZA**:
Don't breathe it in!
Don't let the yellow into your lungs!
It will make you want to... to...

**TERRD**:
To what?

**MAZA**:
To MOVE FOR NO REASON!

*MAZA's horror at saying this makes her step backward - but it's not a normal step. It's almost a dance move. Her foot turns orange.*

**MAZA**:
No no no no no!
I'm backsliding!
I'm literally backsliding!
And it's... orange?

*She tries to think her foot back to gray, but instead starts unconsciously salsa stepping. The orange spreads up her leg.*

**The Contagion Dance**

*One by one, the performers are "infected" with color and movement:*

**JON** (*his chest turns bright blue as he accidentally takes a deep breath*):
I forgot to breathe shallowly!
I took a full breath!
Now my lungs are jazz blue!
They're improvising without permission!

*He starts breath-beat-boxing, unable to stop*

**TERRD** (*her spine turns green as she stretches*):
I reached for something!
Not because I needed it!
Just because my arm wanted to extend!
My spine is spring green!
It thinks it's a plant!
It wants to grow toward light!

**FREDRIKA** (*fighting hardest, but her hips betray her and turn red*):
No! I am efficiency!
I am optimization!
I am... I am...
(*Her hips start swaying*)
I am apparently mambo?
When did I learn mambo?
WHY DO MY HIPS KNOW MAMBO?
