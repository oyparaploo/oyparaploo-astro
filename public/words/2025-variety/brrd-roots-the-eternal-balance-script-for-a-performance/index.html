Brrd Roots ~ The Eternal Balance ~ Script For A Performance

Words / 2025 Variety

Title: **The Eternal Balance**

---

Act I: The Fractured World

*(The stage is divided, with half of the space resembling the ruins of a grand cathedral in shadow, while the other half evokes the chaos of a modern city—neon lights blinking in smog, echoes of political speeches overlapping with faint cries for help. Marcus sits at the dividing line, a broken pocket watch in one hand and a sealed envelope in the other. He begins speaking in **free verse**.)*

**Marcus (free verse):**
They told me I held the keys—
To power, to prosperity,
To immortality, perhaps.
I built towers that scraped the heavens.
But the stars wouldn’t look down.
And now, nothing remains.
Ashes fill the cracks,
And silence mocks me from the depths.

*(Sophia enters, pacing angrily. Her speech begins in **blank verse**, rising with rhythmic intensity.)*

**Sophia (blank verse):**
You call this silence mocking?
These ruins are screams.
The forests tremble, dying inch by inch.
The seas rise against your gilded towers.
You think the stars turn away—
No. They burn with fury,
Condemning what you’ve done.

**Marcus:**
For you! For your future, Sophia!

**Sophia (sharp prose):**
What future, Father? A monument to greed?
I wanted something worth saving,
Not a world of smoke and sorrow.

*(She exits. Eleanor enters, her appearance ethereal, holding a fragmented book with torn pages. She speaks in fragmented **couplets**.)*

**Eleanor (couplets):**
To seek is noble, to know divine,
Yet wisdom fades with the shadow of time.
Truth evades me, as stars evade grasp,
Knowledge shatters like brittle glass.

*(Marcus looks at her, tired but intrigued. The wind rises, carrying faint **chanted tones** as Francis appears from the shadows.)*

---

Act II: The Mirror of Conscience

*(Francis speaks first, his tone alternating between **prose** and **lyric poetry**, resonating like a hymn.)*

**Francis (prose):** Marcus, Sophia, Eleanor—your worlds collide, yet your hearts remain apart. You grasp at ambition, rage, and intellect, forgetting the simple truth of love. You cannot find answers where connection is absent.

**Francis (lyric poetry):**
What lies beyond ambition’s veil?
A heart unchained, a soul to sail.
What wisdom echoes in the night?
The quiet flame, the guiding light.

*(Francis gestures toward Alastair, who emerges trembling, dressed in tattered robes. Alastair speaks in halting, broken **stanza**.)*

**Alastair (stanza):**
I traded faith for fleeting fire,
Sold my soul for shadowed desire.
Now, my name is whispered in shame,
And guilt consumes like endless flame.

*(Francis kneels before him, offering bread. He turns to Marcus, speaking in **metered prose**.)*

**Francis:**
Marcus, this bread is not absolution.
It is an invitation—to see beyond yourself.
Eleanor, take this compass.
It does not point North.
It guides hearts to places unseen.
Alastair, let go of your pain—
For grace is not earned, but given freely.

*(As Francis speaks, Amanda enters silently, her candle flickering. She places the candle between them and begins humming faint **melodies**, almost divine.)*

---

Act III: Redemption in Unity

*(The square transforms gradually—flowers bloom through cracks, light refracts through unseen prisms, and the modern city merges with the ancient ruins. Alastair speaks in **haunting lament**, his voice trembling.)*

**Alastair (lament):**
Is grace for one who betrayed the light?
For one who abandoned humanity’s fight?
I kneel not to beg, but to hope—
Can love endure where shadows grope?

*(Amanda kneels beside him. Her tone begins softly, rising into **lyrical crescendo**.)*

**Amanda:**
Redemption is water for the parched soul.
It is rain that blesses without restraint.
Open your hands, Alastair.
Grace flows where hearts surrender.

*(Marcus approaches with the bread, his speech shifting into **pentameter**.)*

**Marcus:**
I give not to erase what I have done,
But to build something new beneath the sun.
Take this—
Let the smallest act sow compassion’s seeds.
May love rise where ambition deceives.

*(Eleanor clutches the compass and begins her own reflection in **free verse**.)*

**Eleanor:**
Perhaps wisdom is not knowing—
It is kneeling.
It is giving.
It is surrendering the need for answers,
And trusting the currents to carry us home.

*(Sophia steps beside Marcus, speaking in **high lyric**.)*

**Sophia:**
We rise together, hand in hand,
Service, not greed, will heal the land.
Let power crumble,
Let love endure—
What burns as fire
Can blossom as cure.

*(Francis lifts his hands, blessing them in rhythmic **liturgical tones**.)*

**Francis:**
You have glimpsed salvation’s path,
Not through conquest, but through care.
Let love break every chain,
Let grace redeem every soul.

*(The stage fills with light as the characters stand united. Amanda’s candle ascends, glowing faintly before extinguishing—a symbol of rebirth. The cathedral blooms with life, and the cosmos hums faintly. The chorus of **diverse meters**—lament, lyric, hymn—merges into harmony.)*

**Voices (choral finale):**
Grace redeems... Love restores...
Passion ignites... Truth endures...

*(Curtain closes.)*
