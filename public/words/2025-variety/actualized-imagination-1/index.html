Anthro~Archeo physical artifacts awaken…

Words / 2025 Variety

Anthro~Archeo physical artifacts awaken, paired with expressive wallpaper—joyful and somber in tone—and wearables infused with artistry: intricate face masks, full headpieces, hats, caps, footwear, and body wraps. Jewelry enhanced with photo effects—necklaces, bracelets—embellishes the narratives woven into these creations. Museum rooms transform into immersive realms, showcasing high-resolution rear projections that dissolve gracefully, ranging from expansive large-scale imagery to intimate, medium-sized works of art.
The artistry transcends convention with living sculptures—elaborate, untamed, unconventional, sometimes upside-down—unveiling visions never before seen. Performers move in fluid, deliberate rhythm, their forms cloaked in strands of natural and artificial non-biodegradable materials. The interplay of textures, from unyielding hardness to ethereal softness, crafts a mesmerizing spectacle, leaving an indelible impression on the imagination.
