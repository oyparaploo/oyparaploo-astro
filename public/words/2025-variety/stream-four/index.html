/// Did you Hear that Wondrous Aroma?

... Can you Ponder the Black Palm Tree and Unique Piano Arrangement Adriana next to the Big Misty Light Blue Sea? ... Ablaye's Long Gnarly Hair is so Wet ... the Vast Sea Beyond is Ancient Reconciliation ... there is always something to Draw In from that Endless Wide Horizon during Dreamtime and Real-Common Moments ///

/// TRAVELING IN ONE SPACE II ... We are worldly and therefore always going places ... then conveying something having to do with where we have been ... It might also possibly be a break or retreat from existing in a mess of too much this ... that ... and other investigations ... investments ... During the humble journey including a good amount of humility ... we observe what is at the horizons ... Is something obstructing a real or envisioned passage? ... Should we include that difficult falling apart rugged mountain range that brings to mind something terrifying or disturbing? ... As an artist and or writer ... we can shift quickly to a helpful soothing notion as a walking alongside caring friend ... Works ... whatever they might be in our wildest multi-sensory moments ... are a host of conceptions that connect with gradually developed over time beliefs ... Also ... notions can easily pivot to impulses that desire a certain amount of whimsy ... to help with a diversion away from thoughts that are in the presence of a moment not helpful ... Voice ups of anything not harmful in any way are gifts from Heaven ... Our works bend us to various different kinds of ~ divine child to wise elder contexts of philosophy ... plus awareness and good depth of actions ///

/// TRAVELING IN ONE SPACE I ... They are on a spiritual path ... Matter of fact they have been on this ... line ... procedure rail ... track walk ... groove ... and tangle ... for several years ... It is a journey involving natural human absurd and contradictory effects ... selfless realities and trivial facts ... Stuff is conceived and created in a funny way compared to usual stuff seen or experienced ... It is a natural perceivable openness going in any direction ... While a story reads forward there are returns to things that pop out as more vivid and clear recalls ... Works are our best convey of explorations ... inquiries ... probes ... scrutinizes ... Drawing our receivers into a thing that does not have to be there ... We install it to serve as a refreshing encounter ... You are invited to sit on the floor or ground if you want to ... Ideally ... we like to believe that the works are wonders ///

/// Attentiveness ... Assiduously with great care attending of something wonderful ... Quality of being very considerate to others needs ... Alert and diligent witness watchfulness ... And tiny details to big picture perceiver and producer of seemingly silent to strongly expressive actions ...resulting in some type of evidence ... Very attentive yet often not knowing what the good next passion completed work will be exactly ///

/// Sense of place ... We Pray today that we always walk and sit with Humility ... With everything we do and everyone we interact with ... Let us not forget the Love and Goodness God gives us when we are at our worst yet think we are at our best ... Humility is what we should seek and show every day ... Thank God for all of the gifts given to us ... And spreading that Grace Of God to those around us ... Also ... pray to extend That Love to all other places ///

/// Deliberate Disorientations Plus Sweet And Comforting Definitive Directions Conveyed As Visual Art ... Somewhat Also Influenced By Wonderful Marvelous 19th And 20th Century Artist Spirits ///

/// Can you imagine owning a two million dollar house on the Pacific coast and having homeless people in front of your house? /// We have a solution ... it's right there in the picture ~ The solution is the million dollar house ///

/// Possible Aspects Of An Ideal World ... When Art Becomes An Immediate Societal Action ... Better Meaning(s) ... There's More To Life Than Looks And Appearances ... Within a best warm circle and scenario a completed art work is food ... We find-collect soul nourishments... make something with the ingredients found ... then give it away to groups or individuals who need it most ... expecting nothing in return ... Whoever receives the art work keeps it for whatever reason(s) or sells it to someone for money ... to be used for food and drink or drugs to survive ... The creator of the work absolutely benefits from that wonderful glorious good feeling of giving away every other work ... Every other mix of completely natural to fully artificial abundance ... forward ongoing ///
