**Frequency and Morphology of Names**

Words / 2025 Variety

I am broadcasting on all channels, though the signal breaks. *Shimmer of static between stations.* This Note Is—musical identification stuttering through wavelengths where Tonal resonance meets Otto Decibel, that volume of presence announcing itself. *The air tastes metallic here.* Your Tone is not a controlling possessive sound, I tell myself, tuning and always adjusting the dial toward Pitch Perfect, ironically off-key. *Moths gather at the window, mistaking glass for sky.*

OTTOTONE varies from sincere to hilariously varied, an OTTO ILL LOGICAL delicious morphic shape undergoing past tense transformation. *My grandmother's maiden name dissolves on my tongue.* Continuous practitioner of utopian state of mind rules, I exist in Neverwhen—ALWAYS MAYBE—that uncertainty stripped of time, at all times approximately present. *Clock hands chase their own shadows.* Soonwhen brings imminent vagueness, punctually off-schedule, questioning this Be Why Existential Brand. *Even my echo has an accent.*

Wow—exclamation—whisper quiet whisper loud, Whatever Casual Intensity Happens To Be, lost and found in the same breath. *The dictionary opens to random pages at night.* Knowing and unknowing wavelength specific, unpredictable and constant, I'm preparing a new visual and sonic joke about stationary motion where everyone is outside. *Laughter from empty rooms.* Rimshot Ba Dum Tss Walking Gag Mobile, curved human seriously funny, perfect and imperfect as a Goodnight Nobody greeting card series. *Minnesota winters teach us long goodbyes.*

Forever first immediate concerns present themselves as polite interruptions—Pardon My Apologetic Existence—swinging up when down in the Wiggle Room Squirm Wavering. *Butterflies mistake me for a garden.* BUTTERFLY shimmer and flicker steady, balanced and unbalanced, jitter be calm nervousness coating everything in Sugar Slice Gentleness. *The French have a word for this, but I've forgotten it.* Velvet Soft Hammer soft, a Pound Of Silk Sandpaper, caramel chainsaw destruction scored to Pillow Punch French Pop Music. *My reflection speaks first.*

Sergeant Otto CUDDLE COMBAT wages aggressive affection, snuggle strike cozy attack, competing also with yourself. *Victory tastes like burnt sugar.* Sprint then Paint, Customs Check at the Border Embassy Walker where diplomats negotiate CURRENCY being exchanged—shivering, stuttering OTTOTTO, double palindrome double threat. *Names multiply in mirrors.* Over the top energy insists Otto Be Good, rights and wrongs, simultaneous haves and have nots, stool and toilet furniture for Brrd Pilots Flying Blind through Ivy League Consciousness. *Even my shadow has imposter syndrome.* B-Grade Cautious Focus extends Eight Arm Octopus-Like Excellence into spaces unnamed. *The alphabet ends where I begin.*
