THE GEOMETRIC REVOLUTIONARIES

How Six Bodies Discovered They Were Made of Colors, Dots, Dashes, and Curves

← Words / 2025 Variety

A Kinetic Uprising for Lyon

An Immersive Theater Experience Where Audience Members Receive Both Colored Capes and Geometric Cards Upon Entry

THE ARCHITECTURE OF TRANSFORMATION

Pre-Show: The Gray-Geometric Period (Begins One Week Before)

Ticket holders receive a package containing:
- A gray armband with embedded sensors that change color with heart rate
- A clear card with one geometric symbol (dot, dash, OR curve)
- A small vial of gray powder labeled "Crushed Dreams of Your Thinking Mind"
- Daily prompts: "You thought 67,432 thoughts today. You also passed 1,847 dots without connecting them."

Arrival: The Double Sorting

The experience begins at Place des Terreaux at sunset. Audience members arrive wearing gray armbands and carrying their geometric cards. They're met by five figures in gray morphsuits and one peculiar presence - MOE, a seven-foot black line created by a performer in black manipulating a thick black fabric tube that varies from one to seven feet in length.

ACT ONE: The Monochrome-Geometric Prison
Location: Musée des Beaux-Arts de Lyon (after hours)

The Separation Begins

NAH (in gray, moving in repetitive dot-like pulses):
Thought number 43,281: Am I a dot pretending to be a person?
Thought number 43,282: Or a person pretending to be complete?

FREDRIKA (in gray, making dash movements):
I have optimized my walking pattern for maximum efficiency.
I move in straight lines only.
Curves waste 3.7 seconds per day.
I spend 97 minutes worrying about those seconds.

MAZA (in gray, attempting curves but failing):
I tried to flow once.
It felt like drowning in possibility.
Better to think in straight lines.
Even if they lead nowhere.

JON (switching between geometric positions):
Dot. Dash. Curve. Dot. Dash. Curve.
I can't decide what I am.
The indecision is killing me.
But at least it's efficient killing.

TERRD (curled up as a dot):
Everything I've ever done is wrong.
Every movement misunderstood.
I should just stay still.
Still can't be wrong, right?

MOE (the seven-foot black line, constantly morphing length):
Wrong. Wrong. Wrong.
You're all wrong.
Not because you're dots or dashes or curves.
But because you think you have to choose.

Moe contracts to one foot, then extends to seven feet, creating different shapes

MOE:
I am one foot of doubt.
Three feet of reaching.
Seven feet of sprawling possibility.
I am the line that refuses to be straight.
The connector that connects nothing.
The divider that divides everything.
And I am perfectly fine with that.

The Geometric Chambers

The audience is separated based on their cards into three chambers, but Moe flows between all spaces, disrupting each:

In the Dot Chamber with NAH:
Moe circles the dots, creating boundaries

MOE:
You think you're complete?
You're just points in space.
Without lines, you're not even a pattern.
Just scattered loneliness.

In the Dash Chamber with FREDRIKA:
Moe parallels the dashes, then crosses them

MOE:
You think you're efficient?
You're just fragments.
Broken lines going nowhere.
I may meander, but at least I continue.

In the Curve Chamber with MAZA:
Moe mimics curves, then straightens abruptly

MOE:
You think you're flowing?
You're just avoiding decisions.
Curves without purpose are just scribbles.
Pretty, meaningless scribbles.

The Color Revelation Begins

NAH's finger twitches and turns yellow

NAH:
Did... did anyone see that?
My finger just... colored itself?

MOE (suddenly interested, coiling around NAH's hand):
Finally!
Color!
The antidote to gray thinking!
But color without shape is just noise.
And shape without color is just death.

Moe changes - the black line now has an iridescent quality

MOE:
I am black that contains all colors.
Too many to see at once.
Like you, if you'd stop choosing sides.

ACT TWO: The Color-Geometric Infection
Location: Streets of Croix-Rousse

The Escape and Spread

As the performers break free from the museum, colors spread through their bodies while they maintain their geometric natures:

NAH (yellow spreading, still making dot movements):
I'm a yellow dot!
Complete AND bright!
Still but visible!

FREDRIKA (orange spreading, dash movements becoming rhythmic):
Orange dashes!
Efficient AND joyful!
Direct but playful!

MAZA (blue spreading, curves becoming more fluid):
Blue curves!
Flowing AND purposeful!
Indirect but beautiful!

JON (green spreading, alternating between all geometries):
Green everything!
Dots when I rest!
Dashes when I reach!
Curves when I dream!

TERRD (red spreading from spine outward):
Red expansion!
My dot is pulsing!
My hidden dashes emerging!
My secret curves unfurling!

MOE (still black but now clearly iridescent):
And I remain black.
The line between your colors.
The border that defines your brightness.
Without darkness, light has no meaning.
I am your necessary shadow.

Street Activation Stations

The performers lead the audience through Lyon, creating color-geometric interventions:

Station 1: The Worry Laundromat
Audience can wash their gray clothes, which emerge colored

MOE (wrapping around the washing machines):
I am the line between dirty and clean.
Between gray and colorful.
Between who you were and who you're becoming.
Cross me. I dare you.

Station 2: The Fountain of Foolishness
Colored lights and geometric projections on water

MOE (creating shapes in the fountain spray):
Water refuses to be straight.
It dots as drops.
Dashes as streams.
Curves as waves.
Be like water.
Be like me.
Be everything.

Station 3: The Reasonable Person's Rebellion Corner
Audience creates a collective geometric-color artwork

MOE (becoming a frame around their creation):
I frame your revolution.
Every picture needs a border.
Every freedom needs definition.
I am the necessary limit
That makes your limitlessness visible.

ACT THREE: The Synthesis
Location: Les Subsistances

The Integration Discovery

JACOB (appearing in full color with all geometric symbols):
Wait! We're not choosing between color OR geometry!
We're not choosing between types of shapes!
We ARE all of it!

He demonstrates on his body

JACOB:
My eyes are colored dots!
My bones are colored dashes!
My blood vessels are colored curves!
And connecting them all...

MOE (wrapping around Jacob):
Is me.
The dark line that holds light together.
The boundary that makes color possible.
The limit that creates freedom.

DINA's Practice (emerging from audience):
Everyone stand.
Be a gray dot. Feel your incompleteness.
Add color. Feel your visibility.
Become a dash. Reach with color.
Flow into curve. Move with brightness.
Now notice...

MOE (extending to full seven feet, creating a horizon line):
Notice the transitions.
I am every transition.
The moment between dot and dash.
The shift between dash and curve.
The space between curve and dot.
I am the between itself.

The Body Geometry-Color Revelation

Each performer demonstrates the full integration:

NAH:
Yellow dots of joy at my pulse points!
Every heartbeat a small sun!

FREDRIKA:
Orange dashes in my gestures!
Every reach a flame reaching!

MAZA:
Blue curves in my movement!
Every flow an ocean flowing!

JON:
Green everything in my breathing!
Life growing with each breath!

TERRD:
Red expansion in my courage!
Every fear becoming fire!

MOE:
And black lines connecting everything.
Your shadows. Your borders. Your definitions.
Without me, you're just chaos.
With me, you're magnificent chaos.

ACT FOUR: The Living Canvas
Location: Parc de la Tête d'Or

The Human Mandala

The audience creates a massive pattern:
- Colored dots (based on their cape colors)
- Creating dashes (through reaching gestures)
- Flowing in curves (through movement)
- All bordered by Moe, who extends and contracts to frame different sections

MOE (creating words with its seven-foot form):
BETWEEN
BECOMING
BOUNDARY
BEAUTIFUL

Each word dissolves as Moe shape-shifts

The Individual Recognition Moment

Audience members step into the center, declaring their full geometric-color nature:

Audience Member 1: "I'm a purple dot when sad, yellow dashes when excited, rainbow curves when in love!"

Audience Member 2: "My anxiety is gray dashes, my joy is golden dots, my dreams are silver curves!"

MOE (circling each speaker):
And I am the line that contains all your contradictions.
The frame that holds your chaos.
Beautiful, necessary chaos.

ACT FIVE: The Dawn Integration
Location: The Confluence of Rhône and Saône

The River Teaching

MOE (stretching across where the rivers meet):
I am seven feet of meeting.
Not long enough to bridge the rivers.
Just long enough to show the gap.
The gap is where the magic happens.

ALL PERFORMERS:
We are dots of different colors!
Connected by dashes of reaching!
Flowing in curves of movement!
Bordered by the beautiful darkness of Moe!

The Final Practice

The Complete Human Geometry-Color Practice:

1. Start as a gray dot (stillness, colorless)
2. Add your color (identity, visibility)
3. Extend into dashes (connection, reaching)
4. Flow into curves (movement, journey)
5. Return to dot (rest, completion)
6. Notice the transitions (Moe's domain)

MOE (forming a question mark):
What are you?
Not dot OR dash OR curve.
Not one color or another.
You are the conversation between all possibilities.
The beautiful MEss.
The meandering through meaning.
The seven-foot attempt to bridge infinity.
That's enough.
That's everything.

THE INTERNATIONAL ELEMENTS

The Double Revolution

This show is unmissable because it addresses both:
- The gray plague of overthinking (color revolution)
- The isolation of categorization (geometric integration)
- The necessity of boundaries (Moe's gift)

The Souvenirs

Participants receive:
- Their colored cape (to wear in daily life)
- Their geometric card (now decorated with all symbols)
- A seven-foot black ribbon (to remember Moe)
- Instructions: "You are everything. Border yourself beautifully."

The City Integration

Lyon transforms:
- Businesses display dots, dashes, curves in windows
- Cafés offer "Moe Moments" (seven-foot coffees)
- The rivers are temporarily outlined in black light
- Every boundary in the city is celebrated for one week

THE IMAGISTIC BODY AMPLIFICATIONS

The Moe Body Wisdom

Your Fascia is Moe
The connective tissue that wraps every muscle, organ, and bone - that's Moe. Not quite muscle, not quite skin, but the thing that holds everything together. Seven feet wouldn't cover all your fascia, but it represents the principle: connection through separation.

Your Outline is Moe
Stand against light and see your silhouette - that edge is Moe. The line that says "this is where I end and world begins." But it's permeable, always exchanging heat, moisture, energy. You are bordered but not imprisoned.

Your Lifetime is Moe
Birth to death, maximum seven feet on the cosmic scale. A short black line between infinities. But in that seven feet, every color, every shape, every possibility.

The Practice Integrations

Morning: Wake as gray dot, add color, extend to dash, flow to curve, notice Moe in every transition

Noon: Stand in sun, see your Moe-shadow, recognize it as your geometric proof of existence

Evening: Trace your day's path - never straight, never longer than necessary, always Moe-andering

Night: In darkness, become Moe - the black line that contains all colors waiting for tomorrow's light

THE FINAL MOMENT

The performance ends with all performers and audience creating one massive geometric mandala:
- Dots of color (individuals)
- Dashes connecting them (relationships)
- Curves flowing through (journeys)
- All contained within Moe's seven-foot sections (necessary boundaries)

Seen from above, it spells: "WE ARE THE BEAUTIFUL MESS"

But Moe has the last word:

MOE:
Seven feet is enough.
To show you're not infinite.
But also not nothing.
You're exactly enough.
A perfect black line
In a colorful world.
Necessary. Beautiful. Complete.

● — ～ MOE ～ — ●

[That's Moe, never longer than needed, always exactly enough]
