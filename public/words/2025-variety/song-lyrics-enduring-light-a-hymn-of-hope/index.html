Two Francises

Enduring Light

A Hymn of Hope

(Verse 1)
The candle flickers, its glow is faint,
Saint Francis sings though burdened by pain.
The scent of wildflowers fills the air,
A hymn of defiance, a soulful prayer.

His fragile frame may weaken and break,
But his spirit soars, no joy can forsake.
Even in shadows, his light shines clear,
A melody rising, his God draws near.

(Chorus)
Oh, joy that burns through the darkest night,
A beacon of hope, unwavering light.
Through trials, through sorrow, it calls, it stays,
A radiant anchor, to guide our days.

(Verse 2)
See Pope Francis with laughter in his eyes,
The weight he carries, the world’s silent cries.
A child’s small fingers held in his grasp,
The burdens of love, and hope that will last.

The chaos swirls, the world feels heavy,
But his joy deepens, so steady, so ready.
“This,” he reflects, “is why I remain,
To cradle the hurting, to soothe their pain.”

(Chorus)
Oh, joy that burns through the darkest night,
A beacon of hope, unwavering light.
Through trials, through sorrow, it calls, it stays,
A radiant anchor, to guide our days.

(Bridge)
Wildflowers blooming, their fragrance sweet,
Faith’s steady rhythm, hope’s quiet beat.
Francis calls through flickering glow,
“Even in struggle, let your spirit show.”

Laughter ripples, the child’s bright sound,
A world in chaos, yet joy is found.
Pope Francis whispers, with love’s refrain,
“Even in hardship, hope will remain.”

(Verse 3)
Chaos and serenity meet as one,
The dance of pain and hope begun.
Saint Francis weak, yet his hymn defies,
Despair and darkness that cloud the skies.

Pope Francis carries the weight of grace,
His laughter etched on a weary face.
Both men remind us of light’s true place—
In hearts that endure, in love’s warm embrace.

(Chorus)
Oh, joy that burns through the darkest night,
A beacon of hope, unwavering light.
Through trials, through sorrow, it calls, it stays,
A radiant anchor, to guide our days.

(Outro)
Oh, joy that rises, resilient, pure,
A flame of grace, a light secure.
Saint Francis sings, Pope Francis smiles,
Their faith transcends the weight of trials.

The storms may come, but joy will sustain,
A love that heals, a hope that remains.
Could this joy sustain us, too?
Could this light guide us, through and through?
