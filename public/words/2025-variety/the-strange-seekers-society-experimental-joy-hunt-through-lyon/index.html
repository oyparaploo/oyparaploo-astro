THE STRANGE SEEKERS SOCIETY

Experimental Joy Hunt Through Lyon

← Words / 2025 Variety

Les Subsistances

PRE-SHOW: The Joy Drought Assessment

Audiences receive "Strange-Seeking Licenses" and document:
- Days since last genuine laugh
- Current absurdity detection level (1-10)
- Strangest thing noticed this week

ACT ONE: The Collectors

Stage: Lyon street recreation with hidden absurdities

HOLLIE (humor archaeologist, photographing invisible things):
10,000 photos of strange nothing.
Misspelled signs, dogs in coats, philosophical graffiti.
My Joy Bank.
Shows projection of actual Lyon absurdities
Today's deposit: that pigeon wearing a baguette like a hat.

JORGE (survivor, chemo patient):
Hospital at 3 AM.
Nurse's shoe squeaks Beethoven's 9th.
Not funny because cancer's funny.
Funny because I still notice.
Demonstrates squeak-symphony
Unconquered territory in my consciousness.

ROSALIA (third-eye developer):
I share harmless observations.
"Doorknobs are wall handles."
"Puddles are temporary mirrors."
To audience
Your neurons just backflipped.
No victims, only perspective shifts.

NITIN (reformed joy-drought sufferer):
47 days without joy.
Tried forcing happiness. Failed.
Then started texture inventory.
Touches everything
Day 1: Noticed velvet exists.
Day 47: Everything is hilarious velvet.

JOEL (reticular activating system programmer):
Prime your brain: "Show me weird."
Lights reveal hidden absurdities in set
Reality reconfigures.
Same street. Different perception.
You're not finding more strange.
You're finding the strange in more.

ACT TWO: The Hunt

Audience divided into Strange-Seeking Teams, given iPads

Live Lyon Feed: Cameras throughout city streaming real-time

HOLLIE:
Find one absurdity in next 60 seconds.
Audience spots real Lyon oddities on screens
That businessman skipping!
That dog judging architecture!

JORGE (teaching survival humor):
Find the squeak in the serious.
Orchestra plays, one instrument squeaks
There! Proof you're not consumed.

Interactive Absurdity Museum: Audience submissions projected

ACT THREE: The Third Eye Training

ROSALIA (leading harmless humor workshop):
Watch someone's joy potential.
Points to audience member
They haven't smiled yet.
What harmless observation might work?
"Your shoelaces are having a conversation."
Person smiles
Third eye opening confirmed.

NITIN (demonstrating micro-dosing):
For joy-drought people:
Touch five textures. Now.
Audience touches seats, clothes, hair
Not for joy. For noticing you notice.

ACT FOUR: The Joy Bank

All performers' collections displayed

JOEL:
Every strange thing collected = future withdrawal.
Bad day? Access accumulated absurdity.

Giant projection of audience's found absurdities from Act Two

ALL:
This is your Joy Bank.
Opened tonight.
Infinite deposits allowed.
Withdrawal anytime.

FINALE: The Strange Revolution

JORGE:
Finding squeaky shoes funny at 3 AM
Isn't denying difficulty.
It's proving you're unconquered.

HOLLIE (giving audience strange-seeking homework):
Document three absurdities tomorrow.
Text them to strangers.
Start joy epidemic.

ROSALIA:
Your third eye is open.
You see others' joy potential.
Harmless humor is your superpower.

NITIN:
If joy drought returns:
Week 1: Texture inventory
Week 2: Micro-absurdities
Week 3: Gentle sharing
Week 4: Collection beginning

JOEL:
You've reprogrammed your perception filter.
Lyon looks different now.
Everything looks different now.

Final moment: Live feed shows audience members throughout Lyon, all simultaneously pointing at something absurd and laughing

IMAGISTIC AMPLIFICATIONS

The Strange-Seeking Neural Architecture
Your reticular activating system contains millions of neurons filtering reality. When hunting absurdity, these neurons reorganize within minutes, creating new perceptual patterns. Brain scans show increased activation in humor-processing regions even when nothing funny is happening—just the search creates the neural party.

The Joy Drought Recovery Map
During joy drought, dopamine receptors downregulate like flowers closing. Micro-dosing novelty (textures, sounds) sends gentle chemical signals that coax receptors open incrementally. Each tiny noticing creates one new dendritic spine—physical proof of returning capacity for delight.

The Third Eye Neurology
Sharing harmless humor activates your temporal-parietal junction (perspective-taking) and anterior cingulate cortex (social awareness) simultaneously. These regions physically thicken with practice. You're literally building brain infrastructure for seeing others' joy potential.

The Collector's Hippocampus
Humor collectors show 15% larger hippocampi than non-collectors. Each collected absurdity creates a unique memory trace tagged with positive valence. These traces interconnect, forming what neuroscientists call "joy networks"—highways for future happiness.

The Survival Humor Phenomenon
Finding absurdity during trauma activates the prefrontal cortex (executive function) even while the amygdala (fear center) fires. This dual activation prevents total overwhelm. The squeaky shoe at 3 AM literally keeps part of your brain free from fear's monopoly.

The revolution begins with noticing that doorknobs are wall handles, that puddles are temporary mirrors, that survival includes finding the absurd. Lyon becomes the city where strange-seekers congregate, where joy banks open, where third eyes develop, where the ability to notice becomes the ability to survive.
