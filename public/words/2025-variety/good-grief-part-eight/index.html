This intellectualization in which the mind pursued existence and not the experience of the mysteries is the result of the kind of civilization we're in and the harms that are caused by our forgetting ... Because thinking cannot create the reality that it's to be known what the ancients call the mysteries ... And it cannot think nature ... Nature has to be felt
