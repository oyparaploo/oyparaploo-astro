Sacred deep shared with plants…

Words / 2025 Variety

Sacred deep shared with plants ... We step across a threshold that most modern discourse ignores ... boundary between maintenance and metamorphosis ...Accumulated dust sills and spider plants ... Variegated leaves stretch toward a window's sunlight ... Particles should be swept up now ... to represent time made visible ... Cells of our former selves ... Pollen from the last season ... detritus of interior spaces in repeated ritual decomposition and renewal ... sometimes layers indicate neglect that mirror internal landscapes ... A philodendron in the corner ... its aerial roots reaching ... has witnessed days of partial attention ... Hurried waterings ... our failure to notice its broad leaves are like a veil ... Mere presence of plants can alter the spiritual and other dimensions of deep cleaning ... Unlike the passive couch or a silent wall ... plants are participants in every space's system ... They breathe with us and thank goodness process our exhaled carbon dioxide into oxygen ... creating an invisible circulation that binds us in reciprocal relationship ... When we clean around and for them something special happens ... The act of cleaning a plant becomes a meditation ... Our daughter's fingers traced the split patterns that evolution carved into these leaves over millennia ... A plant cannot shake off dust that dims its capacity for effective photosynthesis ... Careful attention restores its ability to transform sunlight into life ... This creates a caretaking consciousness ... State of awareness where the boundaries between self and environment become a portal to transcendence ... Deep cleaning reveals the history of habitation ... Behind a wide shelf vines creep and you discover archaeology of your own existence ... a receipt from a restaurant ... a photo of an past friend ... dried skeleton of a big leaf ... Each discovered item carries what some Japanese philosophers call "mono no aware" or the bittersweet awareness of the impermanence of all things ... Soil scattered tells its own story ... Some of it there since the last repotting ... Some sandier soil fell yesterday during watering ... This mixture of temporal moments becomes a tangible metaphor for how our lives accumulate in non-linear ways ... Newer earth mingles with older earth ... just as our present selves carry forward past versions of who we have been ... Water in deep cleaning takes on ceremonial significance In a living space that includes plants ... Fresh water becomes the medium through which purification and nourishment flow ... Water serves as the bridge between mundane and sacred realms ... Water beads on waxy leaves ... soaks immediately into terracotta ... pools in saucers beneath pots ... Each interaction teaches something about porosity, resistance, and absorption ... Qualities that exist not just in physical materials but in our own spiritual and emotional lives. The lily drooping needs watering ... its leaves are flagging like prayer flags ... After providing that water the leaves lift over hours and you a witness a resurrection ... Plants resist the human impulse toward perfect order. Leaves drop according to their seasonal rhythms ... This tension between our desire for cleanliness and the plants' inherent wildness is a teacher ... During deep cleaning ... we temporarily impose order ... knowing that entropy and growth will immediately begin to reassert themselves ... This acceptance of impermanence within the practice transforms it from a struggle into something more like tending a garden ... The deepest spiritual aspect of cleaning among plants lies in its transformative power ... Not just of space but of consciousness itself ... As you progress from chaos toward order an internal transformation parallels the external one ... The mind ... cluttered with the detritus of daily concerns ... begins to clear ... The attention required to move heavy pots without damaging roots develops into a quality of mindfulness that continues beyond every deep clean process ... The plants ... thriving in their cleaned environment ... become more fully themselves ... and seeing this ... we understand something essential about care ... attention ... and the cultivation of life ... The space you have cleaned holds you more gently and supports your activities more fully ... The practice of deep cleaning becomes what mystics have said is a collapse of the subject-object duality ... And holds the imprint of this care like a blessing ... Attention itself is transformative ... The windows are clear now ... Sunlight streams through onto plant that now glow and shine with renewed vitality ... You have participated in the great work of maintaining the conditions for life to flourish ... In this humble labor ... you have touched something that is infinite
