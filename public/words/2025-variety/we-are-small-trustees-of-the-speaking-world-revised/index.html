**We are Small Trustees of the Speaking World (Revised)**

*"A visitor's witnessing"*
_________________

A woman today apologized to a tree before the city cut it down. She'd watched it from her window for twenty years. "I'm sorry we failed you," she said. *The workers pretended not to notice her crying.* This too is expression - how we speak to what cannot speak back.

Everything communicates, I'm learning. Not just obvious voices but the quiet languages. *Like how your houseplant droops when you've been crying for days, as if keeping you company.* The way stones hold yesterday's heat. How sorrow travels through a body like water finding every hidden channel. *The way your chest physically aches from headlines you can't stop reading.*

In grief, the self scatters. *Like when you find yourself standing in Target, forgetting what could have possibly mattered enough to bring you here.* Through reverence, we might collect our scattered pieces. But it's never solitary. We're all checking "Are you okay?" texts at 3 AM, crafting responses we'll never send. The separate self is illusion - we exist in webs of connection, even when we're pretending to be fine on video calls.

Sacred attention means looking past surfaces to where everything connects. *It's why you save voicemails from people who are gone, why you can't throw away their handwriting.* There's weight in taking life to sustain life. The grocery store anxiety isn't just about choices - it's about everything that had to die for these shelves.

All things have life, though we've forgotten this. *Your car knows when you're crying. Your phone holds every desperate midnight search.* "Our world" isn't ownership but membership. We're temporary caretakers of an ongoing ceremony. *Like inheriting your grandmother's plants and feeling the weight of keeping them alive.*

Time circles rather than arrows. *It's why Thursdays feel like drowning, why 3 PM is always somehow when the news hit.* Past and future exist in this moment - your ancestor's trauma in your shoulders, your children's future in your climate grief.

We need witnesses, not solutions. *The friend who just sits while you ugly-cry. The stranger in the bathroom who hands you tissues without asking.* Creating space where grief can exist without being fixed, without someone saying "everything happens for a reason."

Small - we're admitting our minor role. *Like finally understanding you can't save everyone, can't even save yourself some days.* Trustees, not owners. The relief of knowing we're not in charge, just participants. *Like when you realize it's okay to let the garden die this year.*

Living within truth rather than measuring against it. *Not "healing journey" but "still here."* Trees know their inner pattern. Every atom seeks its authentic vibration. *Your body knows exactly when to cry in the car before going inside.*

Still learning that witnessing might be enough. *Still discovering everyone else is googling "how to keep going" too.* Still finding each other in the speaking world.
