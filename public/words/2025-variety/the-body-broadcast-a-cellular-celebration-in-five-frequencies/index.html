**THE BODY BROADCAST**

## *A Cellular Celebration in Five Frequencies*
### **An Experimental Symphony of Forgotten Joy for Lyon**

---

## **THE ARCHITECTURE**

**Pre-Show: The Body Audit (24 Hours Before)**

Ticket holders receive biometric sensors that track:
- Heartbeats, breaths, steps, blinks
- Data becomes part of the performance: "The audience has collectively breathed 14 million times since yesterday"

**Arrival: The Sensation Checkpoint**

Théâtre des Célestins transformed into a "Body Broadcasting Station." Five rooms, each hosted by a performer teaching audiences to tune into different joy frequencies.

---

## **ACT ONE: The Frequencies of Forgotten Joy**

### **Station 1: The Heartbeat Channel (LE PHANT)**

*Audience enters a room where their collective heartbeat is amplified*

82 years.
82 years of continuous beating.
2.5 billion beats without stopping.
Not once did I thank my heart until it stuttered.

*Places audience member's hand on his chest*

Your heart has been applauding your existence since before you were born.
Can you hear it? That's not just circulation.
That's cellular jazz.
Every lub-dub is your body saying "Yes, more of this living please."

*Room fills with visualization of everyone's heartbeats as points of light*

### **Station 2: The Breath Orchestra (PAOLO)**

*Room filled with hanging ribbons that move with air currents*

**PAOLO** (*conducting the ribbons*):
20,000 breaths per day.
20,000 negotiations with the atmosphere.
Watch children breathe - their whole body participates.
Watch adults - we've relegated breathing to a small corner of our chest.

*Teaches audience to breathe with different body parts*

Breathe with your back.
Now your knees.
Your fingertips.
Your cells remember when breathing was a full-body celebration.

### **Station 3: The Proprioception Laboratory (NAGISA)**

*Mirrors and motion sensors track audience movement*

**NAGISA** (*dancer, constantly shifting position*):
Close your eyes.
Touch your nose.
HOW DID YOU FIND IT?
Your body has GPS that never needs updating.
100 million position sensors reporting constantly.

*Leads blind navigation exercises*

You know where you are without looking.
That knowledge? That's joy.
The joy of being successfully assembled.
Children feel this. Adults forgot to notice.

### **Station 4: The Digestion Gallery (ALTHEA)**

*Sounds of digestive processes amplified*

Your gut has 500 million neurons.

90% of your serotonin - your happiness chemical - made here, not in your head.

*Audience tastes something while listening to digestive sounds*

That satisfaction after eating?
Not psychological. Chemical.
Your intestines are literally manufacturing joy.
Right now. As we speak.

### **Station 5: The Cellular Disco (SALIF)**

*Microscopic footage of cellular processes projected everywhere*

**SALIF** (*scientist-DJ mixing cellular sounds*):
37 trillion cells.
37 trillion tiny construction sites.
Listen...

*Plays sounds of cellular division, ATP production*

That's your mitochondria spinning at 1000 RPM.
That's joy at the subcellular level.
Children feel this. They call it "being hyper."
It's not hyper. It's appropriate response to being impossibly alive.

## **IMAGISTIC AMPLIFICATIONS**

### **The Heartbeat Architecture**
Your heart is a four-chambered drummer living in a bone cage, playing roughly 72 beats per minute for decades without sheet music. Each ventricle contracts in sequence - atria then ventricles - creating a wave that pushes life through 60,000 miles of blood vessels. Children feel this as excitement. Adults pathologize it as "palpitations."

### **The Breathing Topology**
Your lungs have 300 million alveoli with the surface area of a tennis court, folded into the space of two footballs. Each breath is your tennis court exchanging gases with the universe. Children marvel at visible breath in winter. Adults see only inconvenience.

### **The Balance Symphony**
Three semicircular canals in each ear, filled with fluid and lined with hair cells that bend with movement, constantly reporting your relationship with gravity. Add proprioceptors in every joint, pressure sensors in your feet, visual horizon detection - you're a walking gyroscope that never needed calibration. Children spin to confuse this system for joy. Adults take dramamine.

### **The Digestive Orchestra**
Your gut performs peristalsis - waves of muscle contraction moving food through 30 feet of intestine at 0.2 inches per second. The gurgling sounds are your second brain singing. Children giggle at stomach noises. Adults are embarrassed by evidence of successful digestion.

### **The Cellular Light Show**
Mitochondria produce ATP through electron transport chains that would make CERN jealous - protons pumped across membranes, creating electrical gradients that power cellular turbines. You're literally electric. Children who say they feel "buzzy" are accurately reporting cellular activity. Adults call it anxiety.

### **The Neural Lightning Storm**
100 billion neurons firing up to 200 times per second, creating patterns more complex than galaxy formations. Each thought is a thunderstorm of electricity. Children's "racing thoughts" are neural celebration. Adults medicate the storm instead of dancing in the rain.

### **The Temperature Ballet**
Your hypothalamus maintains 37°C through vasodilation, vasoconstriction, sweating, shivering, metabolic adjustment - a dance with entropy that never stops. Children play with temperature (ice on skin, sun on face). Adults just adjust thermostats.

### **The Repair Workshop**
You replace 330 billion cells daily - your body is simultaneously destruction and construction site. Your skeleton completely replaces itself every 10 years. You're not the same matter you were, but the pattern persists. Children grow and feel it. Adults age and mourn it. Both are renewal.

The body broadcasts joy continuously on all channels. Children are born with all receivers on. Adults learned to tune out. But the broadcast never stops. The play is just reminding you where the dial is, and that you're allowed to turn the volume up.

Every heartbeat is applause.
Every breath is negotiation with the universe.
Every successful step is a small victory over entropy.
The joy was never missing.
Just forgotten.
Now remembered.
Now broadcast.
Now celebrated.
