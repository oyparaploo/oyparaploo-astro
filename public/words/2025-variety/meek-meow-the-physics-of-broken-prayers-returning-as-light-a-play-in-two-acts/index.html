MEEK MEOW: The Physics of Broken Prayers Returning as Light

Words / 2025 Variety

**A Play in Two Acts**

By [ B. Otto Will ]

**Premiere Production:**
[Brrd Roots]
[Date]

---

## Characters

**JON** - 30s-40s, botanist/physicist. Measures grief like data. Wears a lab coat that gradually transforms throughout the play.

**TSEGE** - 30s-40s, former emergency room nurse. Transforms witnessing into action. Carries invisible weight.

**KEMET** - Ageless, exists in multiple times simultaneously. A philosopher of temporal fragments. Their gender is fluid.

**SUSUMU** - 20s-60s (age shifts), prophet of incomplete thoughts. Sometimes child, sometimes ancient.

**ENSEMBLE** - 3-5 performers who embody: The Pigeon Uncle, Grammy, The Boy, Helicopter Children, Cave Echoes

---

## Setting

A liminal space suggesting multiple realities. The stage contains:
- Sand/dirt floor sections that actors manipulate
- Multiple playing levels connected by ladders/ramps
- Projection surfaces (scrims, cyclorama)
- Mobile hanging elements (cards, molecular structures, text fragments)
- A cave mouth (upstage center)
- Laboratory table with specimens (stage left)
- Grammy's rocking chair filled with yarn (stage right)

Time: All times. No time. The moment between heartbeats.

---

## Production Notes

**Lighting**: Designed to fragment and unite throughout. UV reveals hidden text on costumes/set. "Broken prayers" literally become light beams in Act II.

**Sound**: Layered whispers of "meek meow" as sonic foundation. Cave acoustics. Static/divine interference. Live vocal processing.

**Costumes**: Begin naturalistic, gradually abstract. Jon's lab coat accumulates stains/writing. Tsege's nurse scrubs transform to flowing fabric. Kemet wears all seasons. Susumu's clothes age/youth with them.

**Movement**: Shifts between scientific precision and spiritual flow. Repetitive gestures accumulate meaning. Final image: all creating one breathing organism.

# ACT ONE

## Scene 1: The Question Forms

*Pre-show: Ensemble scattered in audience, whispering fragments of text. As lights fade, they move to stage.*

*Lights up on JON at lab table, examining pressed flowers under microscope. TSEGE sits with invisible patient. KEMET stands in multiple positions simultaneously (lighting effect). SUSUMU draws in sand.*

**JON**
*(To audience, not looking up)*
Love makes kindness, so kindness has to make something else—maybe molecules doing the cha-cha between heartbeats?

*(Looks up suddenly)*

Yesterday I gave my sandwich to a pigeon who looked like my dead uncle.

*ENSEMBLE MEMBER becomes the Pigeon Uncle, pecking at invisible crumbs*

**PIGEON UNCLE**
*(In pigeon coos that somehow form words)*
Photosynthesis... continues... cooo... after... cooo... death...

**JON**
*(Disturbed, returns to microscope)*
But we get lost trying to math our feelings, solving for X when X keeps morphing into why the hell.

**TSEGE**
*(To invisible patient)*
Days slide by like botched experiments. Shhh. I know. Each morning we test if honor and trust can be our anthem.

**KEMET**
*(From three positions, voices overlapping)*
Past-Kemet: Our cells hummed offkey—
Present-Kemet: Our cells hum offkey beneath everything—
Future-Kemet: Our cells will hum offkey until—

**SUSUMU**
*(Drawing spirals in sand)*
The scientific method says: stay put equals data collection. Like catching invisible butterflies with torn nets. But the butterflies are thoughts. But the thoughts are prayers. But the prayers are broken.

**ALL**
*(Sudden unison)*
Run from grief equals contaminated results. Footprints everywhere.

*They begin moving in patterns, leaving actual footprints in sand*

**JON**
But here's the flip—some unmeasurable future awakening is coming BECAUSE we can't time it.

**TSEGE**
Can't cage it in calendars.

**KEMET**
Can't trap it in clocks.

**SUSUMU**
Can't can't can't—

*(Stops, tastes the word)*

Can't tastes like aluminum. Like disappointment. Like—

**ALL**
Hope.

## Scene 2: Cards Fall Backwards

*Hanging mobile of cards begins rotating. Each card shows a different image: roses, hammocks, molecular structures, prayer hands.*

**JON**
*(Back to cards)*
My back's to the cards. Those paper fortune-tellers lined up like tiny soldiers. Not reading futures that already happened yesterday.

**KEMET**
In another kitchen—watch—

*Lighting shift. We see shadow-versions of all four characters facing the cards, reading them hungrily*

**SHADOW-JON**
The cards say my mother returns as photosynthesis.

**SHADOW-TSEGE**
The cards say every patient I lost is teaching flowers to bloom.

**SHADOW-KEMET**
The cards say time is a spiral staircase and we're walking up and down simultaneously.

**SHADOW-SUSUMU**
The cards say meow meow meow meow meow meow.

*Shadows vanish. Original positions restore.*

**JON**
When we finally drop our boxing gloves—

*All four raise fists, then slowly lower them. As they lower, their hands transform into flower shapes*

**JON** *(CONT'D)*
These stupid fists, these flowers that forgot blooming—something clicks.

**TSEGE**
*(Demonstrating with whole body now)*
Giving up resistance makes us stronger than holding tight. Watch—

*She becomes water, flowing around invisible obstacles, the ensemble creating rocks in her path*

**TSEGE** *(CONT'D)*
Like water that goes around every rock but still carves the Grand Canyon. Still cuts through time itself.

**KEMET**
Time itself—yes. I've seen the Grand Canyon before it existed. Will see it after it's gone. The water remembers forward.

**SUSUMU**
*(Reading from invisible book)*
"Be calm in Soul and Mind," the books say all serious.

*(Throws invisible book)*

Then the universe butts in—

## Scene 3: The Teaching of Meek Meow

*Stage transforms. Ensemble creates a circle. SUSUMU enters the center.*

**SUSUMU**
First there was the word. But before the word was the sound. But before the sound was the—

**SUSUMU & ENSEMBLE**
*(Whispered)*
Meek meow...

**SUSUMU**
A cat spoke this to me in a dream. No. A cat spoke this to me while I was awake but dreaming. No. I am the cat.

*SUSUMU transforms movement into cat-like meditation*

**SUSUMU** *(CONT'D)*
Meek meow...

*TSEGE joins the center*

**TSEGE**
My grandmother's cat. Lived twenty-three years. Outlived Grammy. At the funeral, it said—

**SUSUMU & TSEGE**
Meek meow...

*KEMET joins, moving through multiple time positions*

**KEMET**
Every cat that ever lived is saying it now. Egyptian temple cats. Bodega cats. The first cat to purr at fire. All saying—

**SUSUMU, TSEGE & KEMET**
Meek meow...

*JON resists, stays at his table*

**JON**
This is ridiculous. Sound waves don't carry meaning without linguistic structure—

**ALL OTHERS**
Meek meow... meek meow... meek meow...

*The repetition builds. Ensemble joins. It becomes rhythmic, hypnotic, almost unbearable*

**JON**
*(Breaking, joining)*
MEEK MEOW!

*Sudden silence*

**JON** *(CONT'D)*
Makes zero sense till it makes ALL the sense.

**KEMET**
Cat buddha teaching us submission has its own power trip.

**TSEGE**
*(Tasting the air, synesthesia visible)*
The meows taste purple in my mouth. Purple like bruises healing. Purple like royalty earned through suffering. Purple like—

**ENSEMBLE**
*(Overlapping whispers)*
Eggplant enlightenment / Violet violence / Indigo insight / Lavender longing

**SUSUMU**
Space rockets go up by pushing down—Newton getting freaky with metaphysics.

*Ensemble creates rocket shape, pushes against floor, begins to rise*

## Scene 4: Before Sight Comes Seeing

*Lights shift to golden. Ensemble becomes OWL BABIES, eyes closed, huddled together*

**KEMET**
Sun Shots stab our eyes like golden needles—

*Sharp light beams pierce the space*

**KEMET** *(CONT'D)*
Blinding us into seeing. Those light fuzzy owl babies keeping eyes shut see more than grown owls with 20/20.

**OWL BABIES**
*(Eyes closed, speaking as one)*
We see: Tomorrow's rainfall. Yesterday's sorrow. The color of kindness (it's periwinkel). The weight of photosynthesis (seven pounds, three ounces). The distance between heartbeats (infinite) (intimate) (infinitimate).

**JON**
Before we can see it—

**ALL**
God of Goodness creates it.

**TSEGE**
My grandmother knew this. Knitted blankets for graves before anyone died. "Preparation," she said, "is prayer."

*ENSEMBLE MEMBER becomes GRAMMY, knitting in the rocking chair*

**GRAMMY**
*(Knitting furiously)*
One stitch for the stranger who'll die Tuesday. Purl two for the child not yet born who'll need warmth in winter 2047. Cable knit for the complicated grief coming. Drop stitch for the words we'll drop when sorrow makes us stutter.

**KEMET**
Creation first, perception second. Textbooks got their chronology backwards.

*KEMET moves to cave mouth*

**KEMET** *(CONT'D)*
Listen—I'll prove it.

*(Cups hands around mouth)*

HOLLER FROM A CAVE AMONGST MYSTERIOUS SHADOWS!

*The holler echoes—but returns as LIGHT, not sound. Beams shoot from the cave mouth*

**ENSEMBLE**
*(Awestruck whispers)*
The echo bounces back as light / Sound becoming brightness / In the cave's weird chemistry / Alchemy / All-chemistry

**JON**
*(Scientist breaking)*
Physics calls bullshit but physics never figured in—

*TSEGE and SUSUMU find each other, hold hands. The space between their palms glows*

**TSEGE**
That chance inflation of an elder hand holding another elder hand.

**SUSUMU**
*(To TSEGE)*
Your hand remembers forward. Mine remembers backward. Together we remember now.

**KEMET**
Touch making atoms spread out. Love fattening up the universe. The Big Bang was just God holding hands with Themselves.

## Scene 5: Urgency Lullaby

*Ensemble becomes THE BOY, limp in TSEGE's arms*

**TSEGE**
*(Urgent, cradling THE BOY)*
Fill that boy up with just arrived food so that he doesn't die while we are holding him.

**THE BOY**
*(Weakly)*
The food tastes like tomorrow. Like survival. Like my mother's hands before she—

**JON**
Biology screams: calories in, death out. Brutal survival math.

**TSEGE**
*(Feeding invisible food)*
But each spoonful is also prayer. Also rebellion against entropy. Also—

**KEMET**
The way they do political leadership makes us want to go away to—

**ALL**
*(Building to primal scream)*
Ahh— Ahhh— AHHHH— EEEEEEEEE—

*The scream transforms into musical note*

**SUSUMU**
Where words just break. Crack like bones that heal wrong. But wrong healing is still healing. Crooked miracles.

*THE BOY suddenly sits up, strong*

**THE BOY**
I dreamed I was a pigeon. I dreamed I was your uncle. I dreamed I was a sandwich being given.

**TSEGE**
Yesterday I gave my sandwich to a pigeon who looked like my dead uncle.

**PIGEON UNCLE**
*(Returning)*
Coooo. The sandwich tasted like recognition. Like being seen. Like—coooo—love with mustard.

*Pause. Everyone processes this impossible exchange*

**TSEGE**
The pigeon knew. The pigeon remembered.

**JON**
That's not how memory works—

**KEMET**
That's EXACTLY how memory works. Ask Grammy.

## Scene 6: The Grammy/Grime Equation

*Lights shift. The rocking chair becomes central. GRAMMY still knitting but now covered in dirt*

**ALL**
Police say the hammock is covered with Grammy—

**GRAMMY**
*(Still knitting)*
And grimy. Yes. I am grime now. So?

**TSEGE**
Grammy IS grime? Grandma IS dirt?

**GRAMMY**
Honey, I been dirt since 1987. Just took dying to make it official.

**KEMET**
Oh wait we all become dirt anyway.

**TSEGE**
*(To GRAMMY)*
She knitted blankets for strangers' graves.

**GRAMMY**
Still do. Death didn't stop these hands. Look—

*Her knitting needles move impossibly fast, blankets flowing out, covering the stage*

**GRAMMY** *(CONT'D)*
One for the tsunami victims. One for the children in cages. One for the species going extinct tomorrow at 3:47 PM. One for your broken heart, Jon.

**JON**
My heart's not—

**GRAMMY**
Will be. When you finally let it.

**SUSUMU**
That's it. That's it. That's t—

*Cannot finish. The sentence self-amputates. SUSUMU's mouth moves but no sound*

**KEMET**
The sentence amputates itself because completion would lie. Truth lives in the gap. In the gasp. In the—

**ALL**
T—

*They hold the unfinished sound. It becomes musical. Becomes holy*

## Scene 7: The Boring Middle Eye

*Everyone turns to look at center stage. Nothing there but intense focus*

**JON**
Middle-eye is boring.

**TSEGE**
Centered vision only sees straight when everything spirals.

**KEMET**
I am boring when I forget—

**SUSUMU**
They are irresistible.

**ENSEMBLE**
*(Becoming THEY—beautiful, strange, magnetizing)*
We are: The unnameable. The uncontainable. The uncle-pigeons. The grammy-grime. The photosynthesis after death. The meow before meaning.

**ALL**
Be that go.

*Movement sequence: everyone attempts to "be that go"—abstract, failed, funny, profound*

**ALL** *(CONT'D)*
Anyway.

*They wave white flags made of neural tissue (fabric that looks like brain matter)*

**JON**
Logic surrenders.

**TSEGE**
Finally.

*End Act One*

# ACT TWO

## Scene 1: The Partnership Ends

*Stage scattered with dead plants. JON examining them with increasing desperation*

**JON**
We took one look beside your Asking Why—

*Giant question mark appears (projection or physical)*

**JON** *(CONT'D)*
That wrecking ball punctuation—and that was the end of my partnership with photosynthesis.

**ENSEMBLE**
*(As plants, dying dramatically)*
The questions killed us / Your need to know / Your measuring / Your microscope eyes / We prefer mystery / We choose darkness / We divorce you, Jon

**SUSUMU**
Plants show us questions murder connection.

**TSEGE**
Sometimes knowing kills the real knowing.

**KEMET**
But things need to die for other things to live so—

*The dead plants suddenly transform into letters: U U U U U*

**ALL**
The beginning of U changed my life.

**JON**
*(Tracing the letter in air)*
U—the letter is a vessel. A cup. A—

**TSEGE**
Uterus. Universe. Understanding. You.

**SUSUMU**
You contains Universe.

**KEMET**
Understanding starts with standing under mystery's weight.

*They all stand under the giant U, letting it press down on them*

## Scene 2: Pictures of Heaven

*Ensemble creates tableau of "moving pictures"—Instagram-style frozen moments of perfection*

**SUSUMU**
Human cruelty now is never more obvious than when we're touching moving pictures of heaven.

**MOVING PICTURES**
*(Frozen, smiling)*
#Blessed #Living #MyBestLife #NoFilter #HeavenOnEarth

**JON**
*(Swiping through air)*
Swiping through paradise while actual paradise rots outside.

*The Moving Pictures begin to decay while still smiling*

**TSEGE**
We crave for more—junkies in nice clothes.

**KEMET**
Every swipe a prayer to the wrong god. The god of More. The god of Not Enough. The god of Everyone Else's Better Heaven.

**ENSEMBLE**
*(Breaking from pictures, becoming desperate addicts)*
More meow! More light! More Uncle Pigeons! More Grammy Grime! More! More! M—

**ALL**
*(Cutting them off)*
They look at our wonders and that was the end of today's continuum.

*Clock sounds. Everyone freezes mid-gesture*

**SUSUMU**
Time clocks out, beat.

## Scene 3: Braille Will Be Never Ore

*Darkness. Only touch and sound*

**SUSUMU**
*(In darkness)*
Braille Will be never ore—

**JON**
Future blindness makes metal not sight.

*Single light on TSEGE*

**TSEGE**
And then you called me with a quiet and fragile voice.

**VOICE**
*(From everywhere)*
Help. (Beat) Please. (Beat) I'm strong but—

**TSEGE**
Your words smelled like rain on hot sidewalks. Like promises about to break. Like—

**JON**
That was the afternoon that Everybody thought I was so strong.

*Lights reveal everyone wearing "STRONG" signs, but trembling*

**EVERYBODY**
*(Forced smiles)*
Jon's so strong! Never cries! Never breaks! Made of steel! Made of numbers! Made of—

**JON**
*(Breaking)*
Tissue paper. Spider web. Breath.

**TSEGE**
After their other side broke down the doors—

*Ensemble becomes doors, breaking*

**KEMET**
Which side is OTHER when all sides are other?

**SUSUMU**
When we're all foreigners in our own houses?

**ALL**
Coming back to your sucking arms home place where—

**ENSEMBLE**
*(Trying to lift invisible weights)*
No one / Can lift / A damn thing / Any more / It seems

**TSEGE**
Gravity increases proportionally to need.

**JON**
Einstein's theory of emotional relativity.

## Scene 4: Gray Wedding

*Wedding march plays in minor key. Everyone in gray*

**KEMET**
Gray wedding.

**ALL**
The color of joy mixed with sad till you can't separate them.

*SUSUMU becomes minister*

**SUSUMU**
Do you, Happiness, take Sorrow to be your lawfully wedded—

**HAPPINESS & SORROW**
*(Ensemble members, identical)*
I do I do I do I don't I do

**TSEGE**
It ought to bring us Your Will.

**KEMET**
Breathing it on to us.

*Sound of breathing fills space. Becomes wind. Becomes song*

**VOICE FROM CELL**
*(Ensemble creates prison bars with bodies)*
Song sung from a cell of a hot and sunny desert ruin prison—

**GRAMMY**
*(From within the cell)*
Even here, I knit. Even here, the blankets grow. Imprisonment makes the yarn more precious. Makes each stitch a revolution.

**JON**
Heirs to kings and queens give instructions—

*Ensemble becomes royal court with charts and graphs*

**ROYAL HEIRS**
Full of charts! (*showing pie chart of sorrow*)
Facts! (*bar graph of kindness*)
And figurative pictures! (*scatter plot of prayers*)
Dividing blank pages in a Book of Love!

**TSEGE**
Data visualizes what cannot be quantified.

**SUSUMU**
Desperate, lay your head down.

*Everyone lays down. Constellation formation*

## Scene 5: Mother Logic

**TSEGE**
Your mother's smile has more sense than many living for dynasty.

**ENSEMBLE**
*(As dynasty-seekers)*
Our shorts are long! (*showing long shorts*)
His see-through pants is scary! (*transparent costume*)
Fashion is dynasty! Dynasty is fashion! Transparent scary fashion dynasty!

**KEMET**
You can bet that words will disappoint you.

**ALL**
Yet we keep betting.

**JON**
Gambling addicts at the conversation casino.

*Sound of helicopter approaching*

## Scene 6: The Helicopter Children

*Ensemble becomes children. Massive wind effect. Dust everywhere*

**KEMET**
When the children run towards the helicopter lowering and blowing blinding dust in all directions—

**CHILDREN**
*(Running in slow motion)*
Rescue! Danger! Rescue! Danger! Which one? Both one! Run toward! Run away! Run! Run!

**SUSUMU**
Innocence rushing toward danger bringing rescue bringing danger.

**TSEGE**
Snake eating its tail eating its tale eating its—

**JON**
Complete heat lurks in your iris.

*Everyone's eyes begin glowing with heat*

**ALL**
Fever in the seeing-ball.

**KEMET**
Resolutions of all the fruitless subjects—

**ENSEMBLE**
*(As subjects)*
Math without numbers! Science without facts! Love without object! Prayer without words! Fruit without seeds!

**ALL**
Stay unresolved.

**SUSUMU**
That's the only real resolution.

## Scene 7: Final Fragmentation/Unity

*The stage begins to physically fragment. Platforms separate. Lights fracture*

**SUSUMU**
Fragments gonna fragment.

**KEMET**
Like light hitting a prism—

*They become the prism. Light shoots through them, creating rainbow fragments*

**KEMET** *(CONT'D)*
Each piece has all colors.

**TSEGE**
Logic breaks—

*Sound of breaking glass/breaking hearts/breaking atoms*

**TSEGE** *(CONT'D)*
And breaking shows what it really was.

**JON**
The spiritual floats upside-down—

*SUSUMU inverts, floating*

**SUSUMU**
Astronaut in a flooded church where—

**ALL**
MEEK MEOW ECHOES THROUGH SPACE ROCKETS!

*The meek meow builds again, but different—triumphant, transformative*

**ENSEMBLE & ALL**
*(Building cacophony of meows, until—)*

*Sudden silence*

**TSEGE**
*(Softly)*
Sound travels in vacuum when vacuum's stuffed with love—

**KEMET**
With kindness—

**SUSUMU**
And our gorgeous need—

**JON**
To stay lost.

*Pause. They breathe together*

**ALL**
*(To audience)*
Meek.

*Beat*

**ALL** *(CONT'D)*
Meow.

*Lights fragment into countless broken prayers. Each fragment rises, becomes a star, a point of light. The stage fills with ascending light until—*

*Total illumination. Then—*

**BLACKOUT**

---

## Curtain Call Instructions

As lights return, each actor gives audience members small cards with fragments of text. Audience reads them aloud together, creating final cacophony of broken prayers returning as sound, returning as light.

**END OF PLAY**

---

## Technical Specifications

**Running Time**: Approximately 100 minutes without intermission

**Minimum Cast**: 4 principals + 3 ensemble (doubling possible)

**Orchestra**: Optional live musicians for underscoring

**Special Requirements**:
- Flying rig for floating sequences
- UV light system
- Projection mapping capability
- Haze for light beams
- Sand/dirt floor sections

**Note to Directors**: Embrace the impossibility. Where logic breaks, theatre begins.
