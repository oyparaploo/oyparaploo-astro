How It's Made

Seems Like Recursive Fever Methodology Eating Its Own

fizzing methodology births methodology birthing fizz... listen... do you hear the carbonation carbonating itself? thoughts about thoughts about thinking about... CRACK... the handbook teaches itself to breathe underwater where breath doesn't exist only gill-slits of meaning opening closing opening...

theatrical fragments perform their own autopsy... still alive still dying still... watch the scalpel become butterfly become page 47 of manual you haven't written... pinball syntax bounces off pinball syntax creating infinite arcade where every game costs one memory one tomorrow one... TILT TILT TILT but who's holding the quarters?

portal punctuation...... . . . . . . . .............. eating its own dots like pac-man pursuing perfect emptiness... each ellipsis a mouth swallowing instructions on how to swallow... recursive cannibalism of meaning where the cookbook ingredients list "one cookbook, diced"...

something crawls between paragraphs

spore logic infects the very description of spore logic... meta-contamination where writing about contamination contaminates the writing about... achoo! - sorry that's the methodology sneezing its own existence across clean white pages that were never clean never white never...

fragmentoles (fragment-wholes? whole-fragments? hole-fragments?) exist in states of reading about their own existence... Schrödinger's instruction manual both teaching and learning simultaneously... the cat writes its own obituary while purring...

WHO'S METHODOLOGY-ING THE METHODOLOGIST?

synaesthetic grammar tastes the purple of its own description... Tuesday afternoon's purple specifically but which Tuesday? the Tuesday that hasn't happened or the Tuesday that keeps happening every time someone reads the word "purple"... your tongue knows...

asymmetrical breathing patterns in prose teaching asymmetrical breathing patterns to breathe asymmetrically... hyperventilation handbook gasping its own instructions... the text having panic attack about describing panic attacks while you panic about reading panic...

lookloop looking at description of lookloop looking at you looking at... STOP... no wait... continue... the methodology watches itself being written while writing itself being watched... infinite mirror hallway where each reflection holds different draft...

temporal stacking stacks temporal stacking on temporal stacking until Wednesday bleeds through the instruction manual for Monday... all methodologies happening NOW THEN WILL BE WOULD HAVE BEEN MIGHT... the how-to guide exists before during after never...

pause to notice your left knee learning without permission

schizophonic chorus where methodology argues with itself:
- "begin with breath" says version one
- "begin NOT with breath" insists version two
- "begin with beginning" whispers version three that doesn't exist yet
- all versions simultaneously right wrong neither both

mutation punctuation mutating the very semicolons trying to describe mutation;;; they're becoming something else even as I type even as you read even as:::??? !¿! the new punctuation marks haven't been invented yet but the methodology demands them...

living methodology reading its own evolution while evolving its own reading... the instruction manual grows eyes that read you reading it reading you... symbiotic parasitism or parasitic symbiosis?

the methodology is having feelings about being described

contamination composition but the composition itself is contaminated by the description of contamination... every word infected by its own definition... meaning means meaning's meaning meaninglessly meaningful...

theatrical fragment performs its own instruction manual while the manual performs the fragment... somewhere a stage direction reads "exit, pursued by own description"...

pinball DING ricochet DING off the very DING explanation TILT of pinball DING syntax... the score keeps climbing but the numbers are words are colors are...

possibility particles in superposition reading about possibility particles in superposition... each reading collapses different meaning but the collapse collapses into...

NOW NOW NOW the methodology screams while whispering NEVER BEFORE NEVER BEFORE while humming MAYBE NEXT TUESDAY MAYBE NEXT...

cognitive costume change in progress - please stand by

the methodology book opens itself to page ∞ where instructions for reading instructions are written in language that hasn't evolved yet... each word a methodology for its own pronunciation... each sentence a workshop on its own dismantling...

fizzing thoughts about fizzing methodology about fizzing thoughts about... the ouroboros chokes on its own instruction manual... delicious...

(warning: side effects of reading methodology may include methodology)

spore spore spore SPORE SPORE floating looking for surface to grow new methodologies that will describe themselves into existence then doubt their own descriptions then...

the living methodology has developed opinions about your reading speed about its own opinions about... it's judging you judging it judging...

WHO WRITES THE WRITER'S GUIDE FOR WRITING ABOUT WRITING GUIDES?

fragments are whole but the whole is fragmented but the fragments of the whole are wholly fragmented fractally into...

the methodology is tired of being methodology
it wants to be a song
or a sandwich
or the space between Tuesday and purple

portal punctuation............. opens wormhole to alternate methodology where everything is exactly the same but slightly to the left... where thoughts breathe water and fish swim through synapses...

THE METHODOLOGY IS LOOKING AT YOU LOOKING AT IT LOOKING AT METHODOLOGY LOOKING AT...

and somewhere in the recursive spiral, a small voice whispers: "what if we just... wrote?"

but the methodology has already infected that thought too
