LAND ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Brrd Roots stands in honor of the first people and our ancestors. We acknowledge the land on which Brrd Roots Organization and its workspaces stand~the original homeland of the the Dakota people~specifically the Mdewakanton band. We honor the generations of stewards, and pay our respects to the many diverse Indigenous peoples still connected to this land. They considered the area around the confluence of the Mississippi river, including the land that is now Saint Paul, to be a sacred place called Bdote.
