Fight-or-Flight and Rest-and-Repair IV

← Words / 2025 Variety

While the fight-or-flight response equips us to deal with
immediate threats, the rest-and-repair response is essential
for long-term health and well-being by allowing the body to
recover and maintain its functions. Finding ways to engage
in rest-and-repair activities is key to sustaining a balance.
