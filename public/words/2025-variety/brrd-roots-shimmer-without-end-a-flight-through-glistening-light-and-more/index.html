Brrd Roots ~ Shimmer Without End ~ A Flight Through Glistening Light and More

A thread hums in dawn light, silver and trembling, stretched between branches where the wind refuses to hold still. The bird moves before thought arrives, wings adjusting, gaze locking onto the gleam that flickers in the air. There is no hesitation, only the pull, only the certainty that shimmer belongs to motion. It lands, watching, knowing better than to mistake reflection for permanence. Everything gleams before it fades. Everything bends before it vanishes. But for now, it remains, and that is enough.

Water does not stay still, folding light into itself, pressing reflections beneath ripples that stretch perception into something half-recognizable. It lands at the edge, staring as sky bends into shifting waves, pressing gold where shadow should linger, stretching presence where emptiness should settle. It dips its beak, the surface fractures, and everything vanishes—the shimmer, the illusion, the depth that pretended to hold steady. But as the ripples fade, the light returns, a cycle wrapped in breath, a motion pressed into memory that does not ask to be kept.

The nest is not built for comfort. It is built for light. Mirror shards angled to bend, not to reflect, twisting recognition past its usual limits, catching glow in fractures, pressing silver into places where movement never fully settles. Beads rest between feathers, small and worn, catching gold between the space of yesterday and now. Metal curls into tight spaces, not holding weight, only presence, only the hum of shimmer collected before it had the chance to slip away.

If one shine is lost, another arrives. If one thread snaps, another trembles in morning air. If one reflection bends, another twists, pressing itself deeper into the rhythm that never hesitates, never settles, never asks permission to begin again. The pull remains, the flight presses forward, and when the next gleam catches, twisting between surfaces, curling beneath motion, stretching through air thick with morning hush—it moves just like it did before.

Fractured Reflection ~ A Reckoning Before, Within, and After Collapse

They don’t arrive here.

They were always here.

Before breath. Before silence. Before history decided forgetting was easier than holding memory close.

A city once stood. A river still moves. The walls? They listen. They remember.

But time?

Time pretends it hasn’t already lost control.

It grips at the edges of its own certainty, turning over moments like loose stones, searching for something—anything—that will hold firm beneath its hands.

But time is not what makes things last.

Time is only the measure, not the keeper.

---

The river winds through ruins, though no one tells it where to go.

It doesn’t ask permission. It never has.

It stretches, runs its hands along broken stone, curls beneath prayers once whispered into the sky.

It remembers before memory asks it to.

It has touched too many names carved into rock, too many hands pressing into the earth, too many voices threading themselves into places time does not dare disturb.

Memory does not need permission either.

---

Time folds—not forward, not backward, but inward.

It lingers, arguing with itself.

"You were supposed to keep moving," it mutters. "You were supposed to obey me."

But movement is a suggestion. Presence is not.

Time hates suggestions.

It grips at its own edges, searching for certainty, cursing at paradox, snarling at collapse.

Collapse does not react.

It has already arrived before Time even considered warning anyone.

Collapse does not ask to be welcomed.

It simply presses into the spaces that were always waiting for it.

---

Shadows stretch across the walls, but they do not belong to the light.

They belong to hesitation.

They belong to rulers who believed permanence was possible.

They belong to voices pressed between silence and breath, between forgetting and remembering, between presence and disappearance.

Not vanished.
Not erased.

Suspended.

Silence tries to press against them.

But silence is not absence.

Silence is weight.

And weight does not erase, only holds.

---

A voice hums.

Soft. Unbroken. Unrecorded.

Tenderness lingers between wreckage, curling into spaces history tried to collapse.

Calloused hands press into the earth.

Hands that shape without being asked to shape.

Hands that carry without asking for recognition.

Hands that do not demand space, do not demand permanence, do not demand to be remembered.

But they remain.

---

Footsteps settle before feet ever touch the ground.

Breath arrives before lungs even ask for it.

Presence exists before absence has a chance to begin.

Presence does not ask to remain.

It does not require space.

It does not require movement.

It simply is.

---

Let time unravel.

Let it bend beneath the weight of memory.

Let it slip between what was meant to last and what refused to vanish.

Let love be the beginning.

Let love be the ending.

Let it remain.

A Reckoning in Ruin

The river moves, though no one asks it to.

No one sees it decide. No one tells it where to go. It does not ask permission. It does not need direction.

It bends itself into the cracks of forgotten places, presses into spaces where time hesitates but does not stop.

It does not stay contained.

It stretches, runs its hands along ruins, curls beneath prayers that once swelled into the sky.

It remembers before memory asks it to.

---

Time folds—not forward, not backward, but inward.

It does not pass.

It lingers.

It presses itself against presence, bending at the edges, swallowing distance without swallowing space.

Breath arrives before lungs ask for it.

Footsteps press into the earth before feet ever move.

Shadows stretch before light remembers how to shine.

---

The ones who remain do not ask to be seen.

They do not shout.
They do not beg.
They do not demand space.

But they remain.

Not like thunder—
but like the hush before rain,
like wind curling beneath doors,
like something pressing into the rhythm of the earth without asking for permission to exist.

---

They are not lost.

Only forgotten.

Until someone listens.

The streets listen.
The walls listen.
Even time listens—though it pretends to hesitate.

But presence does not hesitate.

It presses forward.

Without movement, without force, without apology.

It simply is.

---

You don’t arrive here.

You were always here.

Before breath, before silence, before time figured out how to measure itself.

You step forward, but the ground has already remembered your weight.

Your presence has already settled into spaces time hasn’t reached yet.

You exhale, but the wind has already carried the sound of it—has pressed it into corners of the world untouched by sequence.

Time bends—not forward, not backward, but inward.

It waits, folds, hesitates against itself, stretching in places it was never meant to exist.

---

The river does not lead anywhere.

It does not ask for direction.

It is direction itself—but not movement, not destination, not force.

It presses into breath, into stone, into something curled beneath the weight of memory.

It does not carry the past.

It does not flow into the future.

It exists beyond both, swallowing time without obeying it.

---

Let time unravel.
Let it press into itself.
Let it remember itself before forgetting was ever possible.

Let presence stretch into spaces where movement does not matter, where breath lingers before silence realizes it has arrived.

Because presence does not arrive after absence—
It exists before absence even begins.

Dreams Flow Unbroken ~ A Hymn Between Erasure and Sacred Present Moments

Time does not pause.

It spills forward, unwinding itself in ribbons, stretching across landscapes that do not belong to anyone, pressing into hands that carry more than they should.

It speaks in languages the wind understands, hums in rhythms only the river knows.

It is not still.

Not really.

It holds its breath only long enough to decide what must be remembered,
what must remain untouched,
what must be erased before anyone remembers it was ever there at all.

---

The river knows.

It does not ask for permission.
It moves, folding itself into the cracks of forgotten history,
pressing into spaces where memory lingers like dust trapped in sunlight.

It does not stay contained.

It stretches,
wraps itself around ruins,
curls beneath the weight of forgotten prayers,
traces the outlines of names etched into stone long before time learned how to erase them.

---

The streets do not whisper.

They watch—but only in fragments,
only in the moments between breath,
only when the wind decides to carry voices instead of silence.

The walls do not speak,
but they listen.

The silence is not absence.
It is weight.
It is waiting.

It does not belong to rulers,
to kings,
to men who believe their names will outlast the dust.

It belongs to the ones who carry history in their hands.
The ones whose voices hum beneath closed doors.
The ones whose presence lingers,
etched into eternity in ways no empire could erase.

---

Flow like breath.
Flow like remembrance.
Flow like hymn.

Let it rise.
Let it flood.

Not in anger,
but in certainty.

Not in defiance,
but in love.

---

The hands that shape the world do not ask to be seen.

But still—

they remain.
They stand.
They thread themselves into the future like woven light,
like soft pulse beneath the roar of collapse,
like something untouched by the weight of history’s neglect.

They do not need recognition.

They simply continue.

Rooted in reverence, shaped by endurance,
moving like presence,
moving like love.

---

Mercy lingers here.
Soft, steady, uninterrupted.

Lines unfold like music.
Presence hums between cycles.
The river does not hesitate.

It carves into stone,
nourishes what was meant to disappear,
breathes in spaces forgotten,
presses forward without apology.

It does not belong to power.
It does not belong to fear.
It does not belong to silence.

It moves.

Uninterrupted.
Unshaken.
Unbound.

---

Let it stretch beyond reason.
Let it become something surreal.
Let it rise in waves untouched by time—

because some things are meant to remain.

Some things are meant to outlast rulers.
Some things are meant to defy gravity.
Some things are meant to never be erased.

Let it flow.

Let it breathe.

Let it stay.
