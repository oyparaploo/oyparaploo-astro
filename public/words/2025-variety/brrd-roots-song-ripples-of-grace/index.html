Poem · 2 minutes

Soil and Sky

Song

Ripples of Grace

A Song of Reflection, Resilience, and Eternal Whispers

Ripples of Grace (Lyrics)

[Verse 1]
The brook hums softly in my chest,
A mirror of the world, unrest.
It carries wars, it carries peace,
A melody that never ceased.

Whispers rise as storms break free,
Calling to the lost like me.
Will we listen, will we kneel,
Before the cracks become too real?

[Chorus]
Ripples of grace, flowing wide,
Secrets carried by the tide.
A quiet hum, a gentle plea,
Will we listen to eternity?

[Verse 2]
In the garden, we pull the weeds,
Small victories against their seeds.
But shadows grow, they never fade,
Built on the choices that we’ve made.

“Why rebuild what’s bound to fall?”
I ask the brook, I ask it all.
And yet the ripples call me near,
A truth I’ve tried too long to fear.

[Chorus]
Ripples of grace, flowing wide,
Secrets carried by the tide.
A quiet hum, a gentle plea,
Will we listen to eternity?

[Bridge]
Kneeling by the rising stream,
Where voices flow like fragile dreams.
We plant the seeds, we light the way,
For shadows can’t outlast the day.

The storm arrives, the waters rise,
I hear the brook, I hear the skies.
A hum beneath the chaos grows,
A truth as deep as rivers know.

[Verse 3]
Victor’s words, the guilt he hides,
Julian’s shadows cast outside.
Miriam’s seaweed gently sways,
In the light that finds her through the haze.

The brook remembers all we’ve lost,
The bridges burned, the lines we crossed.
Yet it flows, unbroken, strong,
A ripple begging us to belong.

[Chorus]
Ripples of grace, flowing wide,
Secrets carried by the tide.
A quiet hum, a gentle plea,
Will we listen to eternity?

[Outro]
I keep planting, seeds of hope,
Through storms and shadows, I learn to cope.
The brook’s rhythm, it holds me tight,
A ripple of grace in the fading light.

Ripples of grace, flowing wide,
Carrying whispers of what survives.
A timeless song beneath it all,
Will we rise before we fall?
