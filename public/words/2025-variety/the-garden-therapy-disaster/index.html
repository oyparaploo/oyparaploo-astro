## Evergreen Recovery Center, Olympia, WA
## March 2024

Six people in "Horticultural Healing" circle around dead plants. The facilitator, GREGORY, holds a whiteboard: "Your Trauma = Their Nutrients!"

**GREGORY**: Welcome to week three. How are your emotional support plants?

Everyone lifts brown, wilted specimens.

**THISTLE** (26, they/them): My basil died Tuesday. I think it absorbed too much of my anxiety.

**GREGORY**: Plants can't actually absorb human emotions—

**SANI** (44): Then why are we here?

**GREGORY**: It's metaphorical.

**RAMIEL** (31): For

50 a session, metaphors better fucking bloom.

## The Assignment

**GREGORY**: Today we're composting our negative self-talk. Write your worst thought on this biodegradable paper. We'll bury it. New growth will emerge.

**AWOL** (55, named Carol but insists on AWOL): I wrote "I ghost everyone who loves me."

**SHUMUL** (38): Mine says "I self-sabotage because success feels like lying."

**THISTLE**: "My pronouns confuse plants."

Everyone stares.

**THISTLE**: That's not my worst thought. Just seemed funny.

**GREGORY**: Let's... bury them.

---

## The Burial

They go to the center's garden. It's mostly concrete with raised beds full of dead vegetables.

**SANI**: This is where hope goes to die.

**RAMIEL**: I buried my ex's sweater here last month. Nothing grew.

**AWOL**: Maybe it needs actual compost. Not just... feelings.

**GREGORY**: The feelings ARE the compost.

**SHUMUL**: That's not how nitrogen works.

Gregory's eye twitches. He's an MBA, not a botanist. Started this after his startup OopsPath™ failed.

**GREGORY**: Just bury the papers.

---

## The Confession

While digging, AWOL unearths something: a laminated card reading "30 Days Sober."

**AWOL**: Whose is this?

Silence.

**GREGORY**: We don't—

**RAMIEL**: Mine. Eight months ago. First session. You said laminating would "preserve the intention."

**GREGORY**: And?

**RAMIEL**: Relapsed that night. Been lying since.

**SANI**: About sobriety?

**RAMIEL**: About everything. The plants. The journaling. I haven't done any of it.

---

## The Unraveling

**THISTLE**: I killed my plant on purpose. Overwatered it. Wanted to see if failure felt like control.

**SHUMUL**: I've been bringing the same dead succulent for six weeks. Named it "Steve."

**AWOL**: I don't have trauma. I'm here because my daughter thinks I need friends.

**SANI**: I've been stealing Gregory's plants and reselling them on Facebook Marketplace.

Everyone turns to Sani.

**SANI**: What? They're overpriced. I'm providing a service.

---

## Gregory Breaks

**GREGORY**: You're all insane.

**AWOL**: We're paying you to talk to plants.

**GREGORY**: It's therapeutic!

**THISTLE**: It's capitalism. You're selling us expensive dirt and calling it healing.

**GREGORY**: My startup failed! My wife left! I needed something!

**RAMIEL**: So you prey on other broken people?

**GREGORY**: I'm trying to help!

**SHUMUL**: You're trying to profit.

Gregory throws his clipboard. It lands in the dead tomatoes.

---

## Real Talk

They sit in the dirt. No ceremony. Just six fucked-up people and one failed entrepreneur.

**GREGORY**: I don't know what I'm doing.

**AWOL**: None of us do.

**THISTLE**: That's the first honest thing said here.

**SANI**: Want to know something? I made $400 reselling your plants.

**GREGORY**: That's theft.

**SANI**: It's redistribution. Want half?

Gregory laughs. Actually laughs.

**RAMIEL**: This is insane.

**SHUMUL**: This is the most real this group's ever been.

---

## The Decision

**THISTLE**: What if we actually grew something?

**AWOL**: Without the metaphors?

**THISTLE**: Just... vegetables. No trauma. No healing. Just food.

**GREGORY**: That's not therapeutic.

**RAMIEL**: Neither is this.

They look at the dead garden.

**SHUMUL**: I know how to grow tomatoes. Real ones.

**SANI**: I can get cheap soil. Don't ask how.

**AWOL**: My daughter's a botanist.

**GREGORY**: This isn't what people are paying for.

**THISTLE**: Then stop charging.

---

## Six Weeks Later

The garden's alive. Actual vegetables growing. The group meets weekly. Free now. Gregory got a job at Home Depot.

**RAMIEL**: Forty-three days sober.

**AWOL**: My daughter comes now. She likes Thistle.

**THISTLE**: We're starting a podcast. "Plants Don't Care About Your Feelings."

**SANI**: I'm their business manager.

**SHUMUL**: Steve the succulent is still dead. But the tomatoes are incredible.

**GREGORY**: My ex-wife asked for the recipe.

They're harvesting cherry tomatoes. No metaphors. No healing narratives. Just people who found each other in expensive dirt and decided to grow something real.

**AWOL**: We should name the garden.

**THISTLE**: "The Emotional Compost Pile"?

**RAMIEL**: "Wild Bloom Recovery"?

**SANI**: "Gregory's Failed Business Memorial Garden"?

They laugh. Gregory throws a tomato at Sani. Sani throws one back.

**SHUMUL**: How about just "The Garden"?

They consider it.

**GREGORY**: Yeah. Just the garden.

**End**
