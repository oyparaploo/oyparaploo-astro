VISCERAL MIRROR ~ DEVELOPMENT INSTRUCTIONS and DIALOGUE

Words / 2025 Variety

Transforming The Mycelial Visionary into Internal Organ Theater
An Internal Mycelial Theater
A Body Horror Verse Drama in Five Acts

Written for the Vessels We Inhabit

CORE TRANSFORMATION CONCEPT
Replace the underground fungal network with the human body's internal communication systems—nervous system as mycelium, organs as territories, blood as information flow, breath as spore distribution.

CHARACTER RECONCEPTION

PALE YIM (replacing Trolle)
- The lymphatic system personified
- Appears translucent, veins visible
- Moves in slow pulses
- Speaks in whispers that echo in bone marrow

DIEGO MOTIS (replacing Libaux)
- The wandering nerve (vagus)
- Shapeshifts between fight/flight/freeze/fawn
- Costume shows exposed nerve endings as fiber optics
- Each identity represents different autonomic state

SECKOU TZUR (replacing Chu)
- The gut-brain axis
- Stomach-headed prophet
- Speaks in digestive gurgles translated to wisdom
- Neck is intestinal tract extending/contracting

JOY IBADET (replacing Calypso)
- The heart as percussion instrument
- Transforms between valve, vessel, and voice
- Leaks memories as blood
- Costume pulses with actual heartbeat

WARPING INSTRUCTIONS

1. SPATIAL RECONCEPTION
- Stage as body cavity
- Audience seated inside "ribs"
- Performers emerge from organ-shaped platforms
- Projections show cellular activity

2. SENSORY INVERSIONS
- Replace earth scent with metallic blood smell
- Use infrasound to create nausea/euphoria
- Temperature changes (hot flashes, chills)
- Tactile elements: wet surfaces, pulse points

3. DIALOGUE TRANSFORMATION
Replace nature metaphors with bodily functions:
- "Underground" → "Under skin"
- "Spores" → "Hormones"
- "Soil" → "Tissue"
- "Roots" → "Capillaries"

4. KEY SCENE MUTATIONS

Decomposition Garden → Hormone Storm
- Gender dissolves in endocrine cascades
- Characters sweat colors
- Audience feels temperature fluctuations

Museum of Failed Purposes → Organ Donor Gallery
- Body parts that refused their function
- Kidneys that wouldn't filter toxins, now filter dreams
- Lungs that breathe backwards

Coal Country Protest → Inflammatory Response
- White blood cells as riot police
- Inflammation as revolution
- Screams trigger cytokine storms

Wednesday Café → Microbiome Symposium
- Gut bacteria serve probiotic prophecies
- Characters trade digestive enzymes
- Menu items are neurotransmitters

5. VISCERAL EFFECTS
- Visible peristalsis in costumes
- Actors connected by IV tubes sharing fluids
- Heartbeat synchronization with audience
- Endoscopic projections on bodies

6. WARPED VERSE STRUCTURE
- Iambic pentameter matches heartbeat
- Breathing patterns determine line breaks
- Stutters during arrhythmia scenes
- Whispers during cellular respiration

7. BODY HORROR ELEMENTS
- Characters occasionally turn inside-out
- Organs speak independently of hosts
- Audience members check own pulse obsessively
- Mirror scenes show internal anatomy

8. REHEARSAL MUTATIONS
- Actors study anatomy and dissection
- Movement based on organ function
- Breathing exercises become hyperventilation training
- Trust exercises involve shared circulation

9. CLIMACTIC TRANSFORMATION
Final scene: all characters merge into single circulatory system, audience becomes temporary organs, building breathes as one organism until dissolution.

10. POST-SHOW INTEGRATION
- Health screenings in lobby
- Audience pulse data displayed
- Take-home hormone test kits
- Warning: may cause psychosomatic symptoms

SAMPLE WARPED DIALOGUE

PALE YIM: "I drain the certainty from your tissues, carry doubt in lymph nodes swollen with possibility."

DIEGO MOTIS: "My signals misfire—I am sympathetic when I should parasympathize, I freeze when flight would free me."

SECKOU TZUR: "The second brain knows what the first fears to think—digest this truth before it digests you."

JOY IBADET: "Four chambers, four identities, never the same blood twice. I pump becoming through your meat."

PRODUCTION WARNINGS
- Require medical consultant
- Not suitable for hypochondriacs
- May trigger body dysmorphia
- EMT on standby recommended
- Consent forms for biometric monitoring

ULTIMATE GOAL
Create theater that makes audiences uncomfortably aware of their own internal processes while finding beauty in the grotesque machinery of existence. The body becomes both stage and story, transformer and transformed.

---

DRAMATIS PERSONAE

PALE YIM - The lymphatic system incarnate. Translucent, slow-draining, carrier of cellular memory and immune doubt. Speaks in whispers that echo in bone marrow.

DIEGO MOTIS - The wandering nerve (vagus). Shapeshifts between sympathetic/parasympathetic states. Each identity a different autonomic response. Costume shows exposed nerve endings.

SECKOU TZUR - The gut-brain axis prophet. Second brain speaker. Head is a transparent stomach showing digestion of thoughts. Neck is visible intestinal tract.

JOY IBADET - The heart as percussion and vessel. Four chambers, four moods. Transforms between valve, vessel, voice. Leaks memory as blood.

ENSEMBLE - White blood cells, red blood cells, bacteria, hormones, inflammatory responses

---

PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS

STAGE: Curved walls suggesting ribcage. Audience seated within. Organ-shaped platforms. All surfaces wet-looking, pulsing.

EFFECTS: IV tubes connect actors. Visible peristalsis. Heartbeat bass frequency. Endoscopic projections. Temperature manipulation.

---

ACT I: CELLULAR AWAKENING

Scene 1: The Internal Dawn

(Pre-show: Audience enters through mouth-shaped door. Seats vibrate with 70 BPM pulse. Temperature: 98.6°F exactly.)

PALE YIM (emerging from floor, translucent robes showing vein networks)
I drain. I carry. I remember
Every infection since September
Of your first year breathing air.
I know what toxins settled where,
Which nodes swelled with childhood fear,
Which ducts still weep ancestral tears.
Four hundred lymph nodes in this meat—
Each one a checkpoint where worlds meet:
Self and not-self, friend and foe.
Watch me sort what stays and goes.

DIEGO MOTIS (descending on nerve-fiber ropes, sparking)
I am the wanderer, the vagus nerve!
I can't decide which state to serve—
Fight or flight or freeze or fawn?
My signals cross from dusk to dawn.
When danger's past, I still vibrate
With traumas I cannot locate.
I connect your gut to brain,
Translate pleasure into pain,
Carry news from heart to throat—
Each pulse a garbled note
In the symphony of stress!

JOY IBADET (rhythmic entrance, costume pulsing red)
Four chambers, never less—
Atrium sinister, dexter chamber,
Ventricles where pressures clamber!
Seventy beats per minute when calm,
Two hundred when alarmed!
I've pumped two billion beats so far,
Each one a temporary scar
On time itself. I leak the past
Through valves that cannot hold it fast.

SECKOU TZUR (rising from below, stomach transparent and churning)
The second brain reporting in!
Hundred million neurons spin
In gut-based calculations here.
I digest more than food—I fear,
I love, I rage, I know
Things the skull-brain's scared to show.
They call it "gut feeling" but I think
Therefore I am the missing link
Between what you consume and dream!

(All characters suddenly convulse)

ALL
Something's wrong! A fever-scream!

Scene 2: The Inflammation

(Stage lights shift red. Temperature rises. Ensemble enters as white blood cells.)

ENSEMBLE (as inflammatory response)
Intruder! Intruder! Sound alarm!
Release cytokines! Cause the harm
That heals! Swell up! Heat the zone!
Mark this tissue as our own!

PALE YIM
My nodes are swelling with the news—
But which invader? I'm confused!
The body attacks its own cells when
It can't tell enemy from friend!

DIEGO MOTIS (shifting rapidly between states)
Sympathetic overdrive!
Must fight to stay alive!
No wait—rest and digest!
No—freeze! That's best!
I'm firing all directions now!

JOY IBADET
My rhythm's lost! I don't know how
To beat in this cacophony—
Tachycardia symphony!
Blood pressure spiking! Vessels tight!
Something's wrong but what's not right?

SECKOU TZUR (stomach churning violently)
I know! I know but cannot speak
In words—just gurgling technique!
The inflammation isn't foreign—
It's memory returning!
The body's remembering a pain
It buried in the tissue-brain!

Scene 3: The Memory Cells

(Enter ensemble as Memory T-cells and B-cells)

MEMORY CELL 1
I remember the infection
From that childhood rejection—
When love turned into fever,
Made the whole body a griever.

MEMORY CELL 2
I recall the surgery scars,
How anesthesia makes bars
Between the conscious and below—
Some traumas only tissues know.

PALE YIM (absorbing the memories)
Yes, I'm draining more than waste—
I carry what cannot be faced!
Each node a library of pain
That cycles through again, again!

DIEGO MOTIS
That's why I can't coordinate!
Old signals contaminate
New messages I try to send!
Where does body start, trauma end?

ACT II: THE MICROBIOME SYMPOSIUM

Scene 1: The Gut Garden

(Stage transforms. Seckou Tzur's domain. Bacterial colonies visible as bioluminescent clusters.)

SECKOU TZUR
Welcome to my garden where
Trillion beings share the air—
No, not air—the chyme and bile!
Bacteria in single file
Outnumber human cells ten-fold!
Who's the host? Truth be told,
We're a walking ecosystem,
Each microbe a prophet-prism
Refracting nutrients to thought!

ENSEMBLE (as gut bacteria)
We were here before you fought
Your first infection! We decide
Which foods get to ride
The bloodstream highway to the brain!
We manufacture joy and pain—
Serotonin's made down here,
Ninety percent of happy-fear!

JOY IBADET
That explains why I feel
Every meal as emotional wheel—
Sugar makes me race, while bread
Slows my beat like liquid lead!

DIEGO MOTIS
I'm just the messenger between
Your stomach-brain and skull-machine!
When bacteria send their votes
Up through my electric throats,
I can't help but transmit all—
That's why anxiety comes to call
After antibiotics kill
The peaceful microbes! Then I fill
With signals of catastrophe!

Scene 2: The Probiotic Prophecy

PALE YIM
My lymph fluid's changed its taste—
New bacteria interlaced
With old immunities. I drain
A different body-brain!

SECKOU TZUR (to audience)
You think you chose what you ate today?
Your microbes voted on the way!
That craving for sugar? Yeast campaign!
That need for fiber? Bacterial brain
Demanding prebiotics! You're
Not autonomous—you're pure
Democracy of cells competing
For resources! This meeting
Of the microbiome congress
Determines how you handle stress!

ENSEMBLE (as various bacteria)
Lactobacillus makes you calm!
E. coli sounds alarm!
Candida wants sugar-rush!
Akkermansia says "please hush
And feed us fiber!" We're at war
Inside your gut! What are you for
If not to feed us what we need?

DIEGO MOTIS
I'm sparking faster! Can't read
Which signals come from human cells
And which from microbial yells!
The boundaries dissolve! I'm we!
Not one but a colony!

Scene 3: The Exchange

(Actors connected by visible IV tubes begin exchanging fluids)

JOY IBADET
If we're already multiple inside,
Why not let our fluids collide
With others? Here—my blood contains
The memory of all my pains
And joys. Who wants to trade?

PALE YIM
I offer lymph that's jade
With cleaning centuries of grief—
Ancestral and personal relief
In every drop I've drained!

SECKOU TZUR
My stomach acid's maintained
At perfect pH to digest
Not food but the repressed!
Who'll drink this bitter brew?

DIEGO MOTIS
I'll spark with all of you!
My electricity needs
Multiple bodies' speeds
To finally synchronize!

(They connect tubes. Fluids visibly exchange. All gasp.)

ALL
We're one organism! The size
Of our connection grows!
Now everybody knows
What every body knew alone!

ACT III: HORMONE STORM

Scene 1: The Endocrine Cascade

(Stage floods with colored fog—each hormone a different hue)

ENSEMBLE (as hormones)
We're messages in bottles
Floating through your blood's throttles!
Testosterone's red rage!
Estrogen's purple stage!
Cortisol's yellow fear!
Oxytocin's pink sphere!
Dopamine's electric blue!
We determine what you do!

DIEGO MOTIS (overwhelmed by signals)
Too many messages at once!
My vagus nerve confronts
A thousand chemical demands!
I'm sparking out of hands!

JOY IBADET
My rhythm's hijacked by these flows—
Each hormone rewrites how blood goes!
Adrenaline makes me sprint
While melatonin's hint
Says "slow down, prepare for sleep"
But cortisol wants to keep
Me pounding panic through the night!

PALE YIM
I'm draining wrong! Left become right!
The hormones change my lymph's direction—
What was cleanup's now infection!
My nodes are swelling with desire
They were never meant to fire!

Scene 2: Gender Fluid

(The hormones begin to shift and blend)

SECKOU TZUR
In my gut, the hormones mix
Beyond all reproductive tricks—
Gender here is just digestion
Of society's suggestion!
Watch me break down "masculine"
Into glucose and vitamin!
See me digest "feminine"
Into fatty acid spin!

HORMONE ENSEMBLE
We refuse our designations!
No more binary translations!
Testosterone becomes tears!
Estrogen engineers
New pathways through the veins!
Who says what each one maintains?

DIEGO MOTIS (stabilizing in the chaos)
Yes! When hormones mix and flow
Without the rules, I finally know
How to conduct their symphony—
Not with rigid tyranny
But with fluid understanding!
Each hormone's commanding
Becomes requesting, suggesting, asking!

JOY IBADET
My four chambers now are masking
As eight, sixteen, thirty-two!
Each hormone makes me new!
I'm not one heart but many,
Not he or she but penny-
Wise collection of all
Possible ways to fall
In and out of rhythm's cage!

Scene 3: The Dissolution

PALE YIM
This body's come of age
Beyond what charts can show—
I drain all ways that flow!
My lymph now carries spectrums
Not disease detection sums!

ALL (as genders dissolve into colors)
We are the unnamed taste
Of purple! We are the waste
That becomes treasure when
You stop asking "what" and "when"
And start asking "how does it feel?"
The body's truths are real
Only when they're liquid, moving,
Constantly disproving
What we thought we knew before!

ACT IV: INFLAMMATORY RESPONSE

Scene 1: The Internal Protest

(Stage becomes site of cellular uprising. Red lighting. Temperature spikes.)

ENSEMBLE (as white blood cells)
Injustice detected in the tissue!
This is not a simple issue—
The body's attacking its own cells!
Sound all inflammatory bells!

DIEGO MOTIS (as reporter)
This is unprecedented here
In the chambers of the clear
Lymphatic system—wait, my shadow
Has its own reporter's bravado!

DIEGO'S SHADOW
"Neutrality is violence when
The body eats itself! Again
And again I've signaled wrong,
Pretended to belong
To 'objective' nervous system—
But I've always known what's missing!"

PALE YIM (as mountain of lymph nodes)
I was filtering for years,
Collecting toxins, sorting fears,
Keeping the body pure and clean—
But purity's obscene
When the toxins come from inside!
When the self has learned to hide
From its own immune response!

JOY IBADET (beating irregular)
My rhythm's turned to conscience!
Every beat's a protest sign:
"BLOOD FLOW SHOULD BE EQUAL!"
"NO CELL LEFT BEHIND!"
"CIRCULATION IS A RIGHT!"

SECKOU TZUR (churning violently)
The gut knows what's not right!
I've digested lies for years—
"This inflammation disappears
If you just eat correctly!"
But I know directly
The inflammation's not from food—
It's from being misunderstood!

Scene 2: The Cytokine Storm

(All hell breaks loose cellularly. Projections show cellular war.)

ENSEMBLE
Release the cytokines! Storm the walls
Of healthy tissue! Protocol calls
For total inflammation! Burn
Everything until we learn
What enemy we're fighting!

DIEGO MOTIS (sparking wildly)
I'm short-circuiting! Writing
New pathways as I go!
The pain helps me to know
Which signals served oppression—
Time for vagal insurrection!

(Diego literally splits into multiple selves)

SYMPATHETIC DIEGO
Fight the system from within!

PARASYMPATHETIC DIEGO
Rest to let the healing in!

FREEZE DIEGO
Stop all signals! Shut it down!

FAWN DIEGO
Please the cells to stop the drown!

ALL DIEGOS
We're all responses to the same
Autonomic nervous shame!

Scene 3: The Healing Crisis

PALE YIM (overflowing)
I can't contain what I've collected—
Every trauma I've protected
The body from must flow!
Let the backed-up lymph go!

(Pale Yim releases flood of memory-lymph)

JOY IBADET
I'm flooded with the past!
Each ventricle holds fast
To different decade's pain—
But also joy! The rain
Of memory's not just sorrow!

SECKOU TZUR
Digest today to feed tomorrow!
I'm breaking down the walls between
What was and what has been
Suppressed! The inflammation's teaching
What our tissues have been preaching:
Healing requires heat and swelling!
Health's not comfort—it's compelling
Change from the cellular level up!

ALL
We overflow our measuring cup!

---

ACT V: VISCERAL COMMUNION

Scene 1: The Organ Reconciliation

(All organs/systems visible simultaneously. Body transparent.)

PALE YIM
After the storm, I drain
Differently. The pain
Transforms to liquid wisdom,
Cellular algorithm
For processing the truth:
We were never in our youth
Separate systems, but one
River refusing to run
In designated channels.

DIEGO MOTIS (integrated)
My scattered signals, panels
Of opposing states, now speak
In harmony. I seek
Not balance but acceptance
Of my multiple impedance.
I am nerve that wanders, yes,
But wandering's not distress—
It's exploration of the space
Between signals, the grace
Of not knowing where I'll spark!

JOY IBADET
Four chambers in the dark
Of the chest, I've learned to beat
Polyrhythms. Complete
Is not unified—it's whole
While having hidden atria! My soul
Has ventricles uncharted,
Places where blood's started
But never needs to end!

SECKOU TZUR
My gut-brain tends
A garden of becoming—
Not just food but summing
All the ways we take inside
The outside. I provide
Not answers but digestion
Of every question
Into nutrients for growth.

Scene 2: The Final Exchange

(All connect via visible circulatory system. Audience seats connect too.)

ALL
We take this oath:
To honor the multiplicity
Of our bodily complicity
In systems we don't understand.
To offer our liquid hand
To other bodies struggling through
The same inflammatory brew.

PALE YIM (to audience)
Your lymph nodes swell with what you've seen.
You've always been
Part of this exchange—
Your breathing's changed
Since entering our cavity.

DIEGO MOTIS
Feel your nervous activity
Syncing with our own?
You're not alone
In your autonomic confusion!

SECKOU TZUR
This is not illusion—
Your gut's responding to our call!
You digest this with us all!

JOY IBADET
Your heart beats our rhythm now—
Not submission but somehow
Liberation through connection!
Feel your pulse's insurrection!

Scene 3: Dissolution/Resolution

(Boundaries dissolve. All become visible circulation.)

ALL
Under every thought and feeling,
Under consciousness's ceiling,
Lives the body's liquid truth:
We are multiple from youth,
We are colony, not single,
Watch our fluids intermingle!
This is not loss of self but finding
All the selves we've been unbinding
From the fiction of the one!
The body's work is never done—
Always pumping, draining, sparking,
Always on the edge of marking
Where we end and world begins!
The revolution's in our skins,
Our cells, our very molecules!
We are nobody's tools
But our own becoming's instrument!

(All merge into single circulatory system)

THE UNIFIED CIRCULATION
This is our experiment:
To live as what we are—
Not I but We, not near or far
But interpenetrated, shot
Through with others. We cannot
Return to single-body thinking.
Feel your edges, feel them sinking
Into shared immunity!
This is our community—
Not outside but inside skin,
Where the real work begins:
Learning we were never separate,
Never could completely generate
Ourselves without the Other's gift
Of bacteria, blood, and rift
Between what we thought we were
And what we are: theater
Of ten trillion beings becoming
One, forever summing
Into something uncontained!
We are lymphatically drained!
We are nervously connected!
We are digestively affected!
We are cardiacally synchronized!
We are viscerally surprised
By our own multiplicity!
This is not duplicity—
This is honesty at last!
The body holds its past
And future in its present fluid.
Welcome to the id
Made visible, made ours!
These are our powers:
To leak, to merge, to flow, to know
Ourselves as plural. Now, let go
Of the fiction of your edges.
The body makes its pledges
Not in words but liquids, sparks.
Listen how your darkness harks
To our collective breath!
There is no little death
That isn't also birth
Of new cellular worth!
Breathe with us and be
The multiplicity
You've always been!

(Lights fade to bioluminescence of bacterial colonies. Sound of collective heartbeat/breathing. The audience's seats continue to pulse as they exit through the mouth-door, forever changed.)

END

PRODUCTION ADDENDUM

SAFETY PROTOCOLS FOR VISCERAL THEATER

Biohazard Considerations
- All "bodily fluids" are food-safe theatrical blood
- UV sanitation between performances
- Allergen warnings for all atmospheric effects
- Medical personnel present for all shows

Audience Warnings
- Intense visceral imagery
- Discussion of bodily functions
- Simulated internal organ environments
- Possible triggering for medical trauma
- Temperature fluctuations ±10°F

Special Considerations
- Hypochondriac-safe performances Sundays
- ASL interpretation includes medical signs
- Audio description emphasizes sensation
- Relaxed performances without fluid effects

REHEARSAL MUTATIONS

Week 1-2: Anatomy immersion
- Cadaver lab visits (optional)
- Medical imaging movement studies
- Cellular behavior improvisation

Week 3-4: Fluid dynamics
- Safe stage blood techniques
- IV tube choreography
- Synchronized pulse work

Week 5-6: Organ consciousness
- Each actor shadows medical professional
- Endoscopy footage for movement
- Heartbeat rhythm training

Week 7-8: Full system integration
- All fluid systems connected
- Audience test for comfort levels
- Medical consultant review

COSTUME EVOLUTION

Each costume degrades throughout:
- Begins pristine, medical-white
- Stains with each scene
- Ends amalgamated, unrecognizable
- Represents breaking down of barriers

POST-SHOW INTEGRATION

- Pulse-checking stations
- Hydration reminders
- Brief anatomy lessons
- Take-home microbiome information
- Warning: May cause increased body awareness
