Whatever Here Happens to Be

Words / 2025 Variety

Lead-in

Four practitioners of presence have discovered the same unnamed truth without sharing it—that staying still requires more courage than any journey, that "here" is not a place but a practice of refusing to be swept into the world's constant performances of arrival and departure. They maintain their positions loosely, like boats anchored but still moving with the water, while innocent unknown creatures and guilty normal people pass through their perception, each passage defining itself against their conscious stillness.

---

The Story

Kerren knows the difference between stubborn resistance and conscious staying. Their hands press against the window in Grand Marais, not pushing, just maintaining contact with whatever here happens to be.

The tides of Lake Superior do their eternal work. Rising. Falling. Coming. Going.

And they are not going anywhere.

Morning ice. Breathing against glass.

*

In San Diego, Coline tastes the salt that refuses to leave even when the tide pulls back. Her kitchen holds the quality of being rooted in whatever ground or water she's on—linoleum that remembers sand, walls that hold ocean sound even in stillness.

Innocent unknown creatures come and go. Gulls she won't name. Fish she won't identify. They maintain their innocence because she doesn't capture them literally or with her memory.

She's staying here. Whatever here tastes like this morning.

Coffee grounds settling. A kind of arrival.

*

Bastien's fingers find the weave of fabric that stays constant while everything else shifts. Quebec's morning light moves across his workspace, but he maintains his position. Not stubborn resistance but conscious staying.

The world moves through his perception without pulling him into its motion. Guilty normal people hurry past his window, chasing fixed moments that won't stay fixed. He continues his thread—literal thread through literal fabric, but also the thread of presence that refuses to snap.

Needle through cloth. The smallest declaration.

*

Wind carrying promises it won't keep.

*

Elias listens to Copenhagen's canals doing what water does—moving while staying, staying while moving. They're learning the opposite of seeking, creating a point that allows movement to define itself against their stillness.

Tides rise and fall along the shore and they are not going anywhere.

Not the Baltic's shore. Not any specific shore. The shore that exists wherever water meets something that stays.

Bicycle wheels on wet stone. Distance maintaining itself.

*

This is their declaration of presence, these four. Not declaration—practice. Not practice but not not-practice.

Kerren's window fogs and clears and fogs again with their breathing, each cycle a small tide that refuses to pull them anywhere. They watch innocent creature thoughts pass through—memories that could become stories, futures that could become plans. But they let them maintain their innocence by not capturing them.

They're experiencing tides rise and fall. And they're staying here.

*

Coline understands through her tongue what her mind won't grasp. The taste of staying. Salt that remains when water leaves, minerals that hold their ground, the way flavor persists even after swallowing.

She thinks of the modern world's constant arrivals and departures. Packages delivered. People leaving. News cycling. Updates updating.

But she's establishing a stillness against all that cyclical motion.

Spoon not stirring. Just resting.

*

Bastien's work continues without progressing. Each stitch exists in its own here, not trying to get anywhere, just maintaining position in the fabric. The thread continues but doesn't seek. It allows the pattern to define itself against its presence.

Guilty normal people keep passing, pulled into the motion of getting somewhere. He watches them without judgment, without being swept into their trajectories.

His thread continues. Their passages continue. Neither requires the other to stop.

*

Afternoon light. Refusing to apologize for shadows.

*

The four feel something—not connection, that would require seeking each other. More like... recognition of the same stillness, the same refusal to be pulled into arrivals and departures.

Kerren knows through their palms against cold glass.
Coline knows through salt that won't rinse away.
Bastien knows through thread that stays where it's sewn.
Elias knows through notes that maintain position even as sound travels.

They're all practicing presence without making presence a destination.

*

Creatures remain unknown across all four cities. Not just birds and fish but thoughts, impulses, the small wild things that move through consciousness. They maintain their innocence precisely because no one captures them with knowledge or memory.

A crow lands near Kerren's window. They don't think "crow." They think: something dark that knows how to arrive without apology.

*

In San Diego, Coline watches the ocean do what oceans do. Rise. Fall. Arrive. Leave. She's not going anywhere, even as everything around her insists on movement.

Her stillness isn't stubborn. It bends like kelp, yields like sand, but maintains its essential here-ness.

Shells tumbling in surf. The sound of not choosing.

*

Bastien discovers that staying requires its own kind of movement. Small adjustments. Breath continuing. Blood circulating. But none of it trying to get anywhere, just maintaining the continuous presence in whatever here he inhabits.

The fabric under his hands could be anything—wool, cotton, silk. What matters is that it stays while his fingers move across it, defining texture through contrast.

*

In Copenhagen, Elias plays the same note. Not the same—similar. Not similar—recurring. Each time it sounds, it creates a here that wasn't there before.

They're creating fixed points that allow movement to define itself. Each note a small rebellion against the world's insistence on departure.

*

Evening beginning. Not arriving, just becoming visible.

*

The tides continue because tides don't know how to stop. In Grand Marais, Superior's waves arrive and leave and arrive and leave. Kerren watches without counting, without remembering yesterday's waves or anticipating tomorrow's.

They are students of whatever here happens to be.

*

Coline sets down her cup. Not goodbye to the coffee, not hello to the emptiness. Just setting down. The cup stays where it is, establishing its own stillness against the kitchen's subtle movements—refrigerator humming, clock ticking, walls settling.

She's learning what the cup knows: how to be here without insisting on it.

*

Darkness considering itself. Not falling but deepening.

*

Bastien's thread runs out. He doesn't reach for more. Just sits with the ending that isn't an ending, the thread that stops but doesn't conclude.

Innocent unknown creatures move through the dimming light outside. Moths, maybe. Dust motes. Thoughts that refuse to become words. They pass without his participation, maintaining their uncolonized trajectories.

*

Elias understands something. Not understands—receives. The canals are teaching them about staying while flowing, flowing while staying. Water that moves but is always here, wherever here happens to be in the moment.

They stop playing. Not silence—just not adding to what's already sounding.

*

The four sit in their different darknesses, each darkness the same darkness wearing different shadows. They're not going anywhere. Have never been going anywhere. Will never be going anywhere.

This is conscious staying. Not against movement but beside it, creating the stillness that lets motion know itself.

*

Night sounds checking in. Not staying.

*

Kerren removes their hands from the window. The glass holds their warmth briefly, then lets it go. This too is a kind of tide—heat rising and falling, presence and absence taking turns.

But they're staying here. In this chair. In this room. In this practice of presence that isn't a location.

*

Coline tastes the evening: cooling air, distant salt, something flowering in darkness. Each flavor arrives without announcement, leaves without goodbye. She doesn't follow any of them, doesn't resist any of them.

She's the still point they move through.

Moth against screen. Soft insistence.

*

Bastien's hands rest. Not empty—available. Ready to continue the thread when thread continues itself. But not seeking, not reaching. Just maintaining position in whatever here his hands inhabit.

*

Elias listens to the nothing that isn't nothing. Canal water lapping. Distant voices. The sound of a world that never stops arriving and departing.

And they are not going anywhere.

*

Silence approaching—

But not approaching. Already here. Has always been here.

*

The tides rise and fall along all shores—Superior, Pacific, St. Lawrence, Baltic. The four feel the pull and don't follow it. Feel the push and don't resist it.

They're students of this not-moving that isn't stillness.
Students of this staying that isn't stuck.
Students of this here that isn't a place.

*

Innocent unknown creatures continue their passages. Through air, through water, through consciousness. Maintaining their innocence through the four's refusal to capture them with memory or knowledge.

Everything passes through.
Nothing pulls them anywhere.
They continue their thread.

Whatever here happens to be.

*

Arriving—

No. Already here.

Not arriving—

No. Never left.

Both—

The practice continues.
The tides do their eternal work.
The creatures maintain their innocence.
The four are not going anywhere.

Here.

Whatever here—

*

Happens to be.
