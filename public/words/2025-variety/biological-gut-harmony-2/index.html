Biological Gut Harmony II

← Words / 2025 Variety

Balance: A healthy gut microbiome is diverse and
balanced, with a variety of beneficial bacteria that help
keep harmful pathogens in check. Digestion: A balanced gut
microbiome aids in breaking down food and absorbing
nutrients efficiently. Immune System: It helps regulate the
immune system, protecting against infections and
inflammation. Mental Health: The gut-brain connection
means that gut health can influence mood and mental
well-being. Gut bacteria produce neurotransmitters like
serotonin, which affect mood.
