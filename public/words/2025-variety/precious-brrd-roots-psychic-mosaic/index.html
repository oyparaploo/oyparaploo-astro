Hymns and Visions

Precious Brrd Roots Psychic Mosaic

The outsider’s gaze, an undercover refrain,
A brrd artist weaving through human terrain.
Episodes unfold in scripts and scrawl,
Fragments of moods—both fleeting and small.
Projected thoughts, abstract and true,
Colors that tremble, shifting hues.
Ancient skies echo wisdom profound,
Honoring elders where art is found.
Raw, incomplete, yet rich with heart,
Stillness whispers through recycled art.
Wearables spun from forgotten lore,
Breathing new purpose into what came before.
Therapy blossoms in virtual streams,
Reality bending in pixel dreams.
Cutting-edge tools, the lines entwined,
Worlds interpreted—one moment divine.
Sincerity hums like a vow subdued,
Honesty etched in a tranquil mood.
Comedy rising from the normal and plain,
Where crowns meet soup in a playful refrain.
Fish glide through skits of joyful delight,
Casting shadows that flicker in light.
Entertainment simple, endearing, sincere,
Drawing the heart ever closer, near.
Connections bloom, essential and rare,
Objects imbued with meaning to share.
Through the artist’s hand, both bold and wise,
A mosaic unfolds where beauty lies.
