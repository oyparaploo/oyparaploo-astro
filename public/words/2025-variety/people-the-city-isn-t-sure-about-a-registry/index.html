Someone wrote "I'm on this list" in pencil, then erased it, but you can still see the indent. The security guard says he's seen people reading it and crying, laughing, or both.

Every city keeps lists of people it can't quite confirm. You might be on one—check if your shadow shows up in photographs, if your mail arrives at impossible addresses, if birds argue back when you talk to them. The forms just document what nobody wants to admit: we're all pending.
