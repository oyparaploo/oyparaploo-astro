Our Beautiful Garbage

← Writings / 2025

Beautiful garbage doesn't pretend to be pristine ... It carries its history with cracks ... discarded parts ... and failures folded in ... We can see the temporary seams ... Something that was never broken doesn't know anything about repair ... or healing ... Something never thrown away doesn't know anything about being found again ... Oyparaploo methods are beautiful garbage ... made initially from fragments ... pieces ... and parts ... Conversations leading to pieces that couldn't work until they did ... Decades of unfinished scraps ... Beautiful doesn't cancel Garbage ... That's the whole thing ... Raw transformation ... over perfection ...

Likely or anticipated writing tells readers and listeners what they mostly already know ... in a language they're too familiar with ... Predictable confirms and reassures ... slides past without catching important things ... Strange writing stops us ... makes us want to read through it again to remember how something was said ... Intriguing details are the reason someone keeps reading ... Strangeness that has been earned stays ... earned from specific personal observations ... not just random invention ... Oyparaploo strangeness is both grounded and real
