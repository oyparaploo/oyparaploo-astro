Prayer · 3 minutes

Light From the Old Mirror

Where Truth Always Keeps Its Promise

← Words / 2025 Variety

Room One: Winter Rebels Finding Cracks

B Otto Will watched kids rebelling through the Wall of Truth—most wonderful time when young hearts crack barriers. She was Salt of Sweet Planet, he Substance of Field, light pouring through their honest edges.

"Loaming Topsoil mixed with Manure?" Brrd Roots asked, tracing wall cracks.
"Haha—truth grows in unexpected places."

New Youth Hope: Rooted Beings breaking through constructed selves. Glory to all who let light in.

Our boundary: complete honesty, even when walls feel safer
Are you completely truthful with yourself about what you're hiding?

Room Two: Mining Truth Instead

Instead of impulsively jumping Google's wall—mine match for truth. Mine match for causes cracking barriers. Workplaces can be walls or windows: over-furnished period rooms hiding light, mezzanine caves with truth-cracks, or big sky where walls can't stand.

Morning news building panic walls? Pull back to path where light flows freely. That beat without dead-end barriers. Sweet trail where walls become passages.

[Even frozen fox fruit finds cracks in winter's wall]

Room Three: Scutti's Ancient Cracks

Scutti—highest-paid servant who broke through social walls. Stone bone blood ruin farm, 52 years before Bethlehem cracked history open. Natural eyeliner, truth carved on boats, hilarious honesty tattooed on oxen.

Now sitting eternal beyond walls—very much alive where barriers can't reach. Popular scribes knew: he never built walls between people.

Light through Grammy's wall keeps flowing
Truth through every crack keeps growing

Room Four: Mother's Wall-Dissolving Love

Loving Mother's acceptance broke all barriers. Love First cracking walls of judgment. Helping children through walls minor to massive. Heart finding solutions where walls divide.

Artists, healthcare professionals—follow paths through barriers. Especially when sensing universal truth beyond walls. Core essence dripping through cracks of pretense.

Are you completely truthful about walls you've built?

Room Five: Battlefield Truth

"Think you killed someone?" The Wall of Truth cracked. "What happened—Soul slipped through body's barriers to better place."

Who won when walls fell? No answer needed.

Even Flattened Frozen Fox Fruit breaks through stopping-walls.
NOTHING STOPS REALLY—walls are illusions.

Chorus: Light Through Grammy's Wall

Light through Grammy's wall—honestly, fearlessly
Where truth keeps flowing past all pain
Light through Grammy's wall breaks constantly
Teaching us walls can't contain
What love and truth will always claim

Room Six: Continuum Cracks Walls

Birth... Living... Afterlife... no walls between them. Flow through barriers. Kon-TIN-yolo-um breaking stopping-illusions.

Death cracks walls—loved ones transitioning through barriers with angel wings. Mother's passing: ultimate wall becoming window.

"Didn't realize Her truth until walls fell," B Otto Will understood, "now reflecting through cracks she left."

Room Seven: Grief's Honest Cracks

You're not alone behind grief's wall. Phone cracking sleep's barrier—dear one passed. Siblings' voices breaking through emotional walls. Anguish cracking toughened shells.

Your abuela... Grandma... Grammy... broke through final wall

Everyone crying—communal wall dissolving. Not enough time before duty's walls rebuild.

[Sometimes walls themselves weep light]

Room Eight: Truth Keeps Flowing

Some family flees when walls crack. Others sit where barriers fell. Mental streams through memory's fissures. Grammy's death: biggest wall becoming biggest window.

B Otto Will and Brrd Roots sat where walls couldn't stand: understanding flows through grief-cracks into growth. Kids rebel through barriers into hope. Ancient wisdom leaks through time's walls. Mothers' love dissolves all barriers.

Light through Grammy's wall—this personal truth from broken barriers:
Everyone hides until love cracks us open
But passion breaks through when honestly spoken

Final Room: The Wall Becomes a Door

Mine match truth over Google's walls. Choose cracked paths over perfect facades. Remember Scutti breaking social barriers. Feel Souls flowing through body's temporary walls.

Light through Grammy's wall keeps flowing
Are you completely truthful with yourself?
Where passion's honest, walls keep going
Truth turns barriers into wealth

The bittersweet knowing: walls feel safe but light feels better. Universal struggle—hiding when breaking through takes courage. Yet Grammy showed: every wall contains its transformation.

Light through Grammy's wall—honestly, fearlessly
This flows from hearts that learned to crack
Follow truth through barriers clearly
Grammy's light is flowing back

Glory be to all understanding: walls are just pauses in truth's continuum.
