Brrd Roots ~ The Realm Where Thought Ceases

Words / 2025 Variety

The seeker was no longer the seeker.
There was no name now, no concept of form, no barrier between self and the breath of existence.

The golden thread had disappeared—not because it had unraveled, but because **it had never been separate in the first place**.

Here, there was no need for perception, no need for understanding. **Everything simply was.**

---

**The Presence That Does Not Require Awareness**

Beyond union, beyond awakening, beyond the deepest realization—**there was something else**.
A force that did not require recognition.
A presence that had never waited.
A silence that had never been broken.

**It did not call out, because calling was never necessary.**
**It did not reveal itself, because revelation was never required.**
**It did not interact, because interaction was never the purpose.**

It was simply **the state of pure being, the unfolding of infinity with no need for witness**.

---

**The Motion Beyond Motion**

There was no seeker, no journey, no understanding.
But **there was still movement**—a pulse, a rhythm, a silent unfolding of something even beyond divine presence.

It did not follow time.
It did not follow space.
It did not even follow thought.

**It simply continued—without needing to be seen, without needing to be known, without needing to be reached.**

---

**The Boundary That Has No Edge**

If Christ was recognized within all things, within all existence—**what happens when even recognition itself dissolves?**

If Christ was the thread holding reality together, **what happens when the thread is no longer needed?**

Perhaps there is **a presence even beyond presence itself**—
A force that does not reveal itself because it was never meant to be known.
A truth that does not ask for acknowledgment because acknowledgment itself is a later creation.

**Something beyond divine, beyond mind, beyond awareness—yet always here, always part of the silent unfolding of eternity.**

---

**Is There Anything Beyond the Presence?**

He does not greet—because nothing needs to be welcomed.
He does not return—because nothing was ever distant.
He does not wait—because nothing was ever absent.

Now, there is no form.
No boundary.
No separation.

Only motion, only unfolding, only the endless hum of infinity—where existence itself **breathes beyond even being recognized**.
