Play · 9 minutes

Any Heaven Worth The Name Must Be Green

← Words / 2025 Variety

A Play in Four Acts

By [Author Name]

CHARACTERS

ANE - 40s, botanist. Carries pressed flowers and the weight of recent loss. Speaks in scientific wonder mixed with grief.

IGNASI - Ageless. Flows between spiritual traditions like water. Sometimes channels ancient texts. Moves like wind through leaves.

SANDRINE - 30s, news reporter. Professional composure breaks into prophetic vision. Discovers the limits of observation.

THIERRY - 50s, gardener. Silent until necessary. Speaks through cultivation. His movements are prayers.

ENSEMBLE - The Bereaved, Garden Spirits, Protesters, Memories. 6-8 performers who transform throughout.

SETTING

A liminal garden between death and growth. Some plants grow backward into seeds while others bloom eternally. Time moves in spirals. The space should feel both real and impossible—a place where physics bends to accommodate metaphor.

TIME

The purple hours before dawn, though time becomes increasingly fluid as the play progresses.

PRODUCTION NOTE

This play requires a blend of naturalistic acting and heightened theatrical poetry. The garden is both literal and metaphysical. All transformations should feel inevitable rather than magical.

ACT ONE: THE QUESTION TAKES ROOT

Scene 1

Lights rise slowly on ANE, center stage. She holds a pressed delphinium, translucent as insect wings. Around her, the garden exists in multiple states—some plants withering, others in eternal bloom.

ANE
So here's what keeps me awake in those purple hours before dawn, when the world feels thin as moth wings—

She pauses, considers the flower

Does photosynthesis continue after we die?

A laugh like glass breaking gently

I know how it sounds. Like something you'd overhear in a college dorm at 2 AM. But last night—no, every night since—I hold this flower my mother pressed the day before the diagnosis. Delphinium. Blue as her hospital gown. Blue as the veins in her hands when the morphine stopped working.

IGNASI materializes from the garden shadows

IGNASI
The question grows roots before we notice. I've heard it whispered in seven languages, seen it written in gardens from Toledo to Kyoto.

ANE
You were there? At the hospital?

IGNASI
I am wherever the living contemplate the dead. Which is everywhere, always. Your mother—she asked about the roses.

ANE
(Sharp intake of breath)
How could you know—

IGNASI
The roses in room 347 bloomed out of season. The nurses couldn't explain it. But you knew, didn't you? The moment she stopped breathing, they opened. Every one.

THIERRY enters upstage, begins planting something that glows faintly

ANE
That's not scientifically possible.

IGNASI
No.
(He smiles)
It isn't.

Scene 2

SANDRINE enters with camera crew—ENSEMBLE members who move mechanically

SANDRINE
(Professional broadcast voice)
This is Sandrine Martel reporting from...
(Confused)
From where? Producer, where's my location? The prompter's showing nothing but green static.

ANE
(To Ignasi)
Who is she?

IGNASI
Someone about to remember what cameras can't see.

SANDRINE
(To camera)
We're investigating reports of impossible botanical phenomena. Flowers that bloom without light. Trees that bear fruit in winter. Gardens that grow in hospital rooms where—

She stops, staring at THIERRY's planting

SANDRINE (CONT'D)
Sir? Sir, what are you planting?

THIERRY continues working in silence

ANE
He doesn't speak. Never has, far as I know. But look—

Where THIERRY planted, a small tree spirals upward, its leaves shifting through all seasons simultaneously

SANDRINE
(Backing away)
That's not... Camera, are you getting this?

CAMERAMAN
(ENSEMBLE member, mechanical)
The lens sees only what believes in endings.

Scene 3

IGNASI
(To audience)
Every tradition knows this secret: paradise smells like your grandmother's garden. Not because heaven imitates earth, but because earth remembers heaven.

ANE
My grandmother grew tomatoes that tasted like joy. Literally. She'd say, "Annie, suffering is just compost for compassion." I thought it was metaphor until—

SANDRINE
(Increasingly agitated)
Why can't I focus? The teleprompter keeps showing me things that haven't happened yet. Protests about chlorophyll rights. Signs reading "My Grandmother Deserves Eternal Photosynthesis."

IGNASI
Time moves differently in gardens. Ask any seed waiting three seasons underground.

THIERRY pulls a fully formed apple from the soil, offers it to ANE

ANE
(Taking it)
It's warm. Like it's been in sunlight, but...
(Realizes)
The sun hasn't risen yet.

THIERRY
(Finally speaking, voice like earth turning)
The sun never sets in the garden inside.

SANDRINE
(To camera, desperately)
Cut to commercial. CUT TO—

Lights shift violently. Time fragments.

ACT TWO: ROOTS DEEPEN

Scene 1

The stage transforms. Different areas represent paradise visions from various traditions. ENSEMBLE becomes Garden Spirits.

IGNASI
(Moving through spaces)
Here—the rivers run backward, carrying prayers upstream to their source. Here—fruit regenerates with each bite, abundance mocking scarcity. Here—

ANE
(Following)
But how? The energy has to come from somewhere. First law of thermodynamics—

IGNASI
Love. The energy comes from love. Every act of kindness generates photons in dimensions your instruments can't measure.

GARDEN SPIRIT 1
(Ethereal)
In my tradition, trees grow wishes instead of leaves.

GARDEN SPIRIT 2
Each pomegranate seed contains a secret kindness—alms given anonymously, forgiveness offered without being asked.

GARDEN SPIRIT 3
The lotus ponds birth consciousness itself. Souls emerge from buds that never knew mud.

SANDRINE
(To herself, camera abandoned)
I've covered wars. Famines. Elections. But this—why can't I look away from that impossible tree?

Scene 2

Sudden shift. A protest rally manifests through physical theater. ENSEMBLE holds surreal signs.

PROTESTERS
(Chanting)
More chlorophyll for the dead!
Let our gardens transcend!
More chlorophyll for the dead!
Where does photosynthesis end?

SANDRINE
(Building to frenzy)
No, no, NO! You're looking at this all wrong! Don't you understand? The gardens aren't coming—they're already HERE!

She grabs the nearest protester

SANDRINE (CONT'D)
THEY'RE GROWING INSIDE YOUR CHEST! Every breath you take is photosynthesis! Every kindness is nitrogen for the soil of heaven! STOP LOOKING UP WHEN YOU SHOULD BE LOOKING IN!

She throws her microphone. It transforms into a bird mid-flight.

ANE
(Awed)
The bird... it's made of light.

IGNASI
Everything is, eventually.

Scene 3

THIERRY works steadily, creating an impossible garden. Others watch, mesmerized.

THIERRY
(As he plants)
This morning glory climbs toward light that hasn't arrived yet. For the uncle who loved fishing but never saw another dawn. These roses grow thorns that heal instead of wound. For hands arthritis stole before they could hold grandchildren.

ANE
You're planting for the dead.

THIERRY
I'm planting for the living who think beauty ends.

He hands ANE seeds that glow

THIERRY (CONT'D)
Your mother's delphinium. Plant them.

ANE
But she's gone.

THIERRY
Then plant them for her going. Plant them for her staying. Plant them for the both that are true.

ACT THREE: THE GARDEN INSIDE

Scene 1

All characters drawn together. The garden has become surreal—gravity optional, time visible as colored streams.

ANE
(Planting)
I feel her. Not metaphorically. Actually. Like photosynthesis in my bloodstream.

IGNASI
The mystics call it the subtle body. Scientists might say quantum entanglement. Gardeners just call it roots.

SANDRINE
(Transformed, peaceful)
I can see my reports now. All of them. Every tragedy I covered—each one composting into something else. The murdered children becoming rain forests. The war dead growing into peace treaties written in chlorophyll.

ENSEMBLE
(Softly, building)
In the garden inside, in the garden inside,
Where tears become rivers and rivers divide,
In the garden inside, in the garden inside,
Where nothing is lost, only changed by the tide.

Scene 2

IGNASI
(To audience)
Listen—every tradition agrees, though they use different seeds. The one who loved gardens doesn't lose them. The one who fed birds finds birds made of light. The one who planted kindness harvests eternities.

ANE
But what about photosynthesis? My question—does it continue?

IGNASI
What do you think photosynthesis is?

ANE
Light becoming life. Carbon dioxide and water transformed into glucose and oxygen through chlorophyll and—

IGNASI
No. What is it really?

ANE
(Long pause)
Transformation. The invisible becoming visible. Energy changing forms but never disappearing.

IGNASI
Like death.

ANE
(Breakthrough)
Like death.

Scene 3

THIERRY creates a central garden. All watch as he works.

THIERRY
The pomegranate that holds rubies instead of seeds—I planted it for the nurse who worked doubles to pay for her daughter's treatment. The wish-fulfilling tree—for everyone who couldn't afford medicine. The eternal roses—for love that outlasts the lovers.

SANDRINE
I want to report this, but there's no language—

IGNASI
Then grow new language. Let words fruit from your tongue like dates from a palm.

ANE suddenly gasps, drops to her knees

ANE
The delphinium. They're growing. But not up—growing in. Into me. I can feel them. Blue as her hospital gown, blue as the veins in her hands, blue as the last word she said—

ALL
(Overlapping)
Garden.

ACT FOUR: ANY HEAVEN WORTH THE NAME

Scene 1

The protest rally returns but transformed. Signs now read mystical truths.

PROTESTERS/ENSEMBLE
(Holding signs that read:)
"Yes" (in green letters growing leaves)
"The Earth Remembers Forward"
"Photosynthesis Is How Light Prays"
"My Grandmother's Garden Grows In My Dreams"

SANDRINE
(To the crowd, no longer angry but urgent)
I've seen it! The garden inside! Every kindness you've done is growing there. Every cruel word composting into wisdom. Every death becoming a door!

Scene 2

Stage becomes layered with time—past, present, and future visible simultaneously.

ANE
(To her mother's presence)
I understand now. The roses in your hospital room—they weren't blooming because you were dying. They were blooming because you were transforming.

MOTHER'S VOICE
(From the garden itself)
Annie, love, photosynthesis never stops. It just changes wavelengths. I'm in the ultraviolet now, the infrared. Colors you haven't grown eyes for yet.

IGNASI
(To all)
This is what the traditions know—heaven isn't empty clouds but fulfilled earth. Not harps but harvest. Not perfection but perfection's seed.

Scene 3

All characters work together, creating a garden that exists in multiple dimensions.

THIERRY
(Teaching through action)
See? Plant sorrow here—joy grows. Plant rage here—understanding fruits. Plant fear—

ANE
—and courage blooms. I know. I know now.

SANDRINE
(In wonder)
My microphone became a bird. My anger became cultivation. My reports became seeds for forests I'll never see but somehow tend.

IGNASI
Every tradition ends here—in green. Whether you call it paradise, pure land, or garden of delight. Because any heaven worth the name—

ALL
Must be green.

They stand in the garden that is simultaneously dying and eternal. Each speaks the final words in their own understanding:

ANE
Green as chlorophyll continuing through dimensions.

IGNASI
Green as the space between prayer and answer.

SANDRINE
Green as truth growing wild past cameras.

THIERRY
Green as the world before we learned it could end.

ALL
(Together)
Green as spring. Green as hope. Green as what remains.

Lights fade slowly to green. In the darkness, we hear photosynthesis—a whisper that sounds like breathing, like growing, like the earth remembering how to heal.

BLACKOUT

END OF PLAY

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

SET
- Multi-level platform for dimensional gardens
- Projection surfaces for time-stream effects
- Trap doors for magical gardening
- Flying rig for bird transformation

PROPS
- Pressed flowers (delphinium featured)
- Glowing seeds (LED/fiber optic)
- Transforming microphone/bird puppet
- Protest signs that grow leaves
- Impossible plants (mechanical/projection)

SOUND
- Layered botanical soundscape
- Hospital equipment morphing to rain
- Photosynthesis as audible whisper
- Multiple language prayer rhythms

LIGHTING
- Purple pre-dawn wash
- Color-coded spiritual traditions
- Growing green underglow
- Time-stream effects
- Final immersive green

COSTUMES
- ANE: Lab coat becoming vine-covered
- IGNASI: Color-shifting robes
- SANDRINE: News suit to garden dress
- THIERRY: Simple with infinite pockets
- ENSEMBLE: Transparent mourning/petal layers
