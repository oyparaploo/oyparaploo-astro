Poem · 1 minute

The shadow is the hope ... if we hope to understand our shadow ... Will actually gain more wisdom about what has been accumulated in the collective and personal unconscious of our lives ... The human being has been receiving something that nature cannot give us ... which is our shot ... but nature doesn't give us our shot ... Human interaction gives us our shouting ... whether we make our motives transparent or not ... that's what gives us our shot ... We have all accumulated a lot of self doubt ... a lot of wounds ... Personal ... family ... societal ... wounds ... This is the inner darkness that will become the source for a new civilization ... a new kind of enlivened and enlightened humanity ... Because we have to agree on sharing something that is becoming far more abundant ... because it will never end in the way that we think it will end ... that some other being will save us from ourselves ... We're creating ourselves for the first time ... We're receiving something from each other that is not given by the divine ... the act of will is really the critical part of our humanity ... The challenge of our world is the fact that we are now in the tension between scarcity and abundance ... and it's getting harder for us to choose abundance because we don't want to include other people in it ... This is the darkness ... This is a limitation of our real effort to understand that the personal conscience is now becoming the collective conscious ... we will have in ourselves all of the trauma ... which is a good thing ... because it is actually a substance that requires to enter into our life to be transformed ... it will not stop ... There will be no psychology for trauma to the degree in which we can predict behavior and preferences of our own survival ... We've already crossed the space in which we are in a shared reality ... Both were sharing the shadow first ... and if the shadow was to become enlivened with purpose ... we have to understand the nature of abundance ... which is what is beginning ... We think the body is only a kind of inherited space ... We're not understanding our bodies ... The body does not only have genetics in it ... Interested the diversity of feelings and relationships ... This is our ecology and consciousness ... we have to do with the unconsciousness of our shared lives and develop a discipline of integrating it into consciousness ... So this is the effort of adequating our will in relation to the crisis Because we have to create a governance model in which abundance is true ... The way nature speaks ... The things that we're talking about is not nature ... we're talking about our human will ... in which we are really not willing to be human at the level at which we can express creations for this ... Because it is still a mystery
