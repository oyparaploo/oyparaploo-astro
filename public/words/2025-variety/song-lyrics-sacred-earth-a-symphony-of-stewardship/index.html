Sacred Earth ~ A Symphony of Stewardship

← Words / 2025 Variety

(Verse 1)
Under the stars in Assisi’s embrace,
Saint Francis walks through creation’s grace.
The leaves whisper soft, the stream hums low,
A symphony calling where divinity flows.

The stars above like diamonds scatter,
A hymn of the heavens where silence matters.
With every breath, a prayer takes flight,
Creation’s pulse, a song through the night.

(Chorus)
Oh, Earth, so sacred, so worn, so true,
Each cry of the soil, every shade, every hue.
Protect, embrace, let mercy rise,
This fragile home under infinite skies.

(Verse 2)
Pope Francis writes with the weight of the world,
Ink spills like tears where chaos is hurled.
Forests to ash, the oceans suffocate,
The cries of creation rise before it’s too late.

Still hope is woven in every line,
A plea for harmony, a chance divine.
“Our common home,” his words implore,
A love for the Earth, and all we restore.

(Chorus)
Oh, Earth, so sacred, so worn, so true,
Each cry of the soil, every shade, every hue.
Protect, embrace, let mercy rise,
This fragile home under infinite skies.

(Bridge)
Through stars that glimmer and skies that weep,
Through winds that whisper and oceans deep.
Saint Francis speaks through the rustling leaves,
“Protect what gives and what receives.”

Pope Francis calls from a world at war,
“The cries of the poor, the Earth’s scarred core.
Together they plead for justice and care,
Will we rise and answer their prayer?”

(Verse 3)
To Saint Francis, the Earth’s a hymn of praise,
A language of wonder that time can't erase.
“Brother wind,” “Sister water,” he sighs,
Love flows in creation, in soft goodbyes.

Pope Francis, a voice that echoes through time,
“We are stewards, and our task is divine.
Every glacier, every tree, every sea,
Calls to protect what’s sacred, to let it be free.”

(Chorus)
Oh, Earth, so sacred, so worn, so true,
Each cry of the soil, every shade, every hue.
Protect, embrace, let mercy rise,
This fragile home under infinite skies.

(Outro)
Oh, the beauty in nature’s embrace,
Oh, the balance of life’s quiet space.
Saint Francis kneels, his prayers ascend,
Pope Francis calls, a voice to mend.

Creation speaks, through stars and streams,
Through oceans wild and fragile dreams.
Protect, cherish, let love be sown,
For Earth is not ours—it’s not alone.

Simplicity and Humility. Saint Francis of Assisi was renowned for his embrace of poverty and his commitment to living a simple life. Similarly, Pope Francis chose to live modestly, forgoing many of the traditional luxuries associated with the papacy. For example, he resides in a small apartment rather than the Apostolic Palace and often uses simple transportation, such as a Fiat, instead of more opulent vehicles. Advocacy for the Poor. Saint Francis devoted his life to helping the marginalized and destitute, embodying the Gospel's call to care for the least among us. Pope Francis has echoed this mission, emphasizing a "Church for the poor" and consistently advocating for social justice and economic equality. Environmental Stewardship. Saint Francis is famously associated with a deep love for nature and is considered the patron saint of ecology. He saw all of creation as a reflection of God's glory. Pope Francis has carried this legacy forward, most notably through his encyclical Laudato Si', which calls for urgent action to combat environmental degradation and climate change. Radical Approach to Faith. Both Saint Francis and Pope Francis are seen as reformers who challenge the status quo. Saint Francis was considered radical in his time for his literal interpretation of the Gospel and his rejection of material wealth. Pope Francis, too, has been described as a reformer, addressing issues such as clericalism, corruption, and inclusivity within the Church. Joy. Saint Francis emphasized joy as a cornerstone of faith, even in the face of suffering. Pope Francis has similarly highlighted the importance of joy in Christian life, urging believers to share the Gospel with enthusiasm and hope.
