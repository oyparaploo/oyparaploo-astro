A Light Through the Ruins

Script For A Performance

← Words / 2025 Variety

Title: A Light Through the Ruins

Act I: Foundations of Ambition

(The stage is consumed by towering skyscrapers and dim lighting, casting long shadows. The Voices hum faintly, blending whispers of forgotten truths with the mechanical noise of an industrial world. Silvano, clutching the plaque of the Silvano Foundation, stands at center stage.)

Voices (whispering faintly):
The forgotten fall… The Earth groans… Greatness crumbles…

Silvano (fragmented free verse):
I built these walls to hold the world.
To give shelter, to give hope—
Stone is strong. Dreams are eternal.
Yet why does this weight press more than stone?
Is this foundation firm—or is it hollow?

(Amanda enters, her steps echo softly. She carries a sapling wrapped in cloth and pauses beside Silvano. Her voice is clear and unyielding.)

Amanda (measured blank verse):
The roots run deep, but life does not take hold.
You built these shelters, but left hearts exposed.
Do you serve the people—or your pride?

Silvano:
You see only what’s lacking, never what I’ve given.
A roof, warmth, dignity—is that not enough?

Amanda:
The work born from ambition often cracks,
For stones laid without love cannot hold light.
Do you truly hear their voices through these walls?

(She kneels, placing the sapling gently onto the ground, and exits. Silvano watches, silent. Victor enters, his tattered notebook clutched to his chest. His words spill with urgency, almost desperation.)

Victor (rhymed quatrains):
I sought truth in reason’s grasp,
But all the answers fade too fast.
My books grow empty, my thoughts decay,
Wisdom falters and slips away.

Silvano:
And what would you have me do? Wisdom means action—
Progress, creation. That is our salvation.

Victor:
Progress? Look closer! These towers rise—
Cold against the weary skies.
We both grasp at shadows, hoping for light.

(The Beggar emerges from the shadows, holding a glowing loaf of bread. The Voices fall silent. His presence exudes simplicity and profound warmth.)

Beggar:
Silvano, Victor—you build for permanence,
Yet no foundation lasts without love.
What is ambition if it doesn’t cradle the soul?

(The Beggar kneels, placing the glowing bread at Silvano’s feet. Its faint light reveals cracks forming in the skyscrapers’ facades. The Beggar exits, leaving Silvano and Victor in quiet thought.)

Act II: The Mirror of Grace

(The stage shifts to the dim streets. Lucia sits against a crumbling wall, shivering under a tattered coat. Ivy stands nearby, gripping a crimson ribbon that seems to drip like ink. Victor observes from the shadows.)

Silvano (halting, holding the glowing bread):
Here. Take this bread.

Lucia (bitterly):
Bread? Another scrap from a gilded table?
Do you think bread heals the wounds of being unseen?

Silvano:
Then what can I give? What would be enough?

Lucia (piercing prose):
To be seen.
Not as another hand to feed,
But as someone worth your love.

(Silvano kneels, holding the bread out to her. Their eyes meet as the Voices hum faintly, their harmonies turning soft.)

Voices:
To see… To know… To heal…

(Ivy steps forward, gripping her ribbon. Her voice shakes under the weight of her guilt.)

Ivy (fragmented prose):
I traded my soul for power.
I bartered kindness for greed—
And it left me hollow, a shadow.
Am I too broken for grace?

(Victor approaches, his tone softened.)

Victor (lyric prose):
Grace is not found through worthiness,
Nor is it earned—it flows freely.
Open your hands, Ivy. Let go.

(The Beggar reappears, his voice steady but radiant.)

Beggar:
Ivy—release the ribbon.
Let its shadow dissolve into light.

(Ivy collapses, gripping the ribbon as it transforms into golden threads. Aurora enters, her figure glowing faintly. She kneels beside Ivy, placing her hands over hers. Ivy’s shadow lifts as she weeps.)

Act III: Rebirth in Surrender

(The stage begins to transform—vines and wildflowers grow through the cracks of the structures. Streams of golden light break through the skyscrapers as they dissolve into open skies. Amanda steps forward, holding the sapling.)

Amanda (to Silvano):
True ambition burns brightly,
But it cannot warm without love.
Now plant this—not for power,
But to renew what was forgotten.

(Silvano kneels beside Lucia, planting the sapling into the cracked earth. Aurora places her hands over the sapling, her light guiding its growth. Together, they water it.)

Victor (softly, with clarity):
Perhaps wisdom is not in knowing,
But in surrendering.
To see, to heal, to give.

(The Beggar raises his hands, his voice like a hymn of quiet authority.)

Beggar (final blessing, rhythmic hymn):
Greatness does not rise in towers,
But blooms in hearts unbound.
Build not monuments,
But bridges—
And carry this light beyond the ruins.

(The loaf of bread glows brighter, ascending toward the open sky. The stage becomes a garden bathed in radiant light as Silvano, Amanda, Lucia, Ivy, Victor, and Aurora stand together, tending to the sapling. The Voices harmonize in a triumphant chorus as the curtain falls.)
