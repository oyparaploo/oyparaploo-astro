Pep Talks and Blessings

Message & Character Script 6

(6)

/// Humans while you’re alive
live for all the brightness of life ///
create it during the times when
it’s not there /// even thin cracks
of bright light trying to stream in
are immeasurably valuable ...
against absolute darkness and
any death interests residing ///
