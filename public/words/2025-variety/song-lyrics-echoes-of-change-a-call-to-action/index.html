Song Lyrics ~ Echoes of Change ~ A Call to Action

Words / 2025 Variety

*(Verse 1)*
In the rustle of leaves, in the morning air,
Saint Francis kneels, a quiet prayer.
He asks of love, of walls to fall,
What will we give, will we answer the call?

Through streets of chaos, the noise of the day,
Pope Francis walks with courage to say:
“The Earth is crying, humanity too,
What must we risk, what must we do?”

*(Chorus)*
Will we let go, will we break through?
Build bridges of hope in all that we do.
The voices linger, urgent and clear,
A call for change, for love sincere.

*(Verse 2)*
Saint Francis whispers, “Simplify life,
Let go of ambition, let go of strife.
Beauty persists, even in the storm,
Transformation waits, daunting but warm.”

Through crowded streets and shadows that weigh,
Pope Francis turns where pain holds sway.
“Division rises, but so can hearts,
Will we begin, or let fear impart?”

*(Chorus)*
Will we let go, will we break through?
Build bridges of hope in all that we do.
The voices linger, urgent and clear,
A call for change, for love sincere.

*(Bridge)*
What shall we sacrifice, what shall we free?
What barriers crumble so love can breathe?
Through whispers of dawn, through city’s din,
Francis calls, “Let this new life begin.”

They ask of us what truths we hide,
What hope we hold, what fears divide.
A legacy burns, both beautiful, raw,
An invitation to answer and stand in awe.

*(Verse 3)*
Through the noise, they whisper still,
A mirror held to our aching will.
Saint Francis asks, “Will you rise to care,
For the cries of the Earth, for the poor everywhere?”

Pope Francis speaks, his voice so bright,
“Act not with fear, but love’s pure light.
Through courage, through kindness, the choice is near,
Will beauty endure through what we hear?”

*(Chorus)*
Will we let go, will we break through?
Build bridges of hope in all that we do.
The voices linger, urgent and clear,
A call for change, for love sincere.

*(Outro)*
Their voices rise in leaves that sway,
In the quiet moments of a dawning day.
Saint Francis kneels, Pope Francis calls,
Through the light of action, beauty enthralls.

In chaos, in stillness, their truths remain,
A choice for love, to ease the pain.
What will we give, what paths will we take?
Transformation waits—will we awake?
