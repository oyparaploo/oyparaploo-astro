Being Seen, Being Loved

Fame and Deflation (Fragments)

For entertainers ... your first late night appearance is a huge deal ... it's when you've made it ... It's your moment of arrival ... the thing that made everything else worthwhile ... Late night appearances are where people are supposed to be funny ... charming ... You're the first person I'm willing to be truly myself with when it's late and I'm unguarded ... You're the first person who made staying up late feel like a celebration rather than insomnia ... It could mean You're the first person I'm testing my material on ... "You're my debut into a bigger ... brighter world I didn't know existed ... It's actually quite endearing ... It takes the language of professional achievement and applies it to personal connection ... suggesting this person is both an accomplishment and a performance ... a milestone and an audience ... This is everything I dreamed it would be! ... Every notification is a dopamine hit ... every recognition feels earned ... I can't sleep because I'm reading every comment ... response ... mention ... I was right to believe in myself ... If this happened ... what else could happen? ... I'm also genuinely thankful ... wanting to respond to everyone ... I will use this energy to create more ... do more ... be more ... Can you believe this is happening?! Look at this message! Someone in Mongolia and Tasmania saw my work! ... (Sudden Deflation) Oh. So this is it? ... I thought I'd feel different ... The achievements don't fill holes ... Now I have to maintain this ... They'll figure out I'm just another ordinary person ... Success feels separate from me ... like it happened to someone else ... Now what? ... The goal I worked toward for many years is achieved ... leaving a void ... Yeah, that went viral, back in the day ... I don't know ... It's weird ... I'm still just me ... I'm a genius! ... I'm a fraud ... Wait ... let me check the numbers again ... None of this matters but also it's everything ... (Excited Again) ... My works changed lives! ... People calling me the Thoreau of Beautiful Garbage! (Another Deflation) 37 million people now know I cried with my face pressed against my walking shoes ...Deflation often comes from discovering that external validation doesn't fix internal doubts ... You wake up famous but still ... Your anxieties just have a bigger audience now ... The real authentic excitement comes from possibility ... I spent years trying to get everyone's attention ... only to discover later in life that I'm an introvert ... That's the paradox of fame ... we seek it for transformation but receive it as amplification ... You're not a different person ... you're the same person ... just a little louder and a little more quiet
