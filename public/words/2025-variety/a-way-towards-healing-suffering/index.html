Words / 2025 Variety

This gentle archaeological attention ... turned toward inner experience ... with more humility ... more questions than answers ... That visitor's perspective admitting its own uncertainty ... making space for us to welcome bewilderment into this conversation ... Less teaching ... More wondering assistant and or shadowing ... Carefully brushing dust off buried feelings ... examining emotional artifacts with curiosity rather than judgment ... Not leading ... not following exactly ... but moving in parallel through the bewilderment ... Misunderstanding gives everyone permission to not know ... to be in this process ... This humility changes everything ... instead of wisdom literature or intellectual book life that confidently knows ... we have something more like shared field notes from another territory of loss ... Questions without answers ... Observations without conclusions ... Space where real feeling lives ... We work with fragments ... never seeing the whole civilization ... always inferring ... guessing ... wondering what this shard meant to someone who held it ... That's an additional way to meet grief ... finding pieces ... turning them over in our hands ... wondering what they're trying to tell us about who we were ... who we're becoming ... This gentle ... wondering ... shadowing presence that makes room for not knowing can heal present suffering
