"Do you remember how we loved touching all kinds of roots ever since we were preschoolers, sitting out in nature's sunshine and discovering the unearthed organs of plants? The idea that roots can be both very complex and wonderfully delicate, while also capable of providing incredible anchorage to prevent magnificent, large trees from toppling or tipping over during storms with tornadic winds, is truly fascinating."
