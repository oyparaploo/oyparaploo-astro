Different Therapy (2)

ottootto Life ( Quandary Work )

← Words / 2025 Variety

Apprehension or anxiety that something terrible will
happen is normal and of course goes all the way
back to ancient times. Today understand unease and
anxiety better. Face it straight on and get a good
grasp of it. By pouring it into ourselves, we can
quietly laugh about it. Also, further explore dreads
and consternations in different ways. Use your human
expressions to help others. Be of service to chosen
receivers of your stuff. Your personal meaningful
spoken content, material things and virtual realities.
