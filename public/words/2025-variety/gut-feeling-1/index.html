Gut Feeling I

← Words / 2025 Variety

When someone asks “is your gut okay with it,” they are
essentially asking whether you feel comfortable or confident about a decision or situation on an instinctual, emotional
level. It’s about checking in with your inner sense of what is right or wrong for you, rather than relying solely on logic or
external advice. Our “gut feelings” or instincts can often
provide valuable insights because they are based on our
subconscious processing of experiences, knowledge, and emotions. So, when you listen to your gut, you’re tapping into this deep well of wisdom that can guide you in making decisions
that align with your true self. Have you ever had a situation where your gut feeling played a crucial role in a decision?
