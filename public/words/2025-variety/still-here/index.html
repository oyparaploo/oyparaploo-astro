the body is ready for green

## ○

What is wild?

Told through the stories of the plants.

---

○

**Manoomin speaks:**

*I grow where I want to grow.*

*Shallow water. Soft mud. Slight current.*

*You cannot make me grow somewhere else. You have tried. Your paddies in California grow something, but it is not me.*

*I am not domesticated. I am not controlled. I am in relationship.*

*You tend the water levels. You protect me from the carp that dig up my roots. You take what I offer and leave enough to become next year.*

*This is not farming.*

*This is conversation.*

*I have been talking to your grandmothers for ten thousand years.*

*I am still talking.*

*Are you still listening?*

---

○

**Timpsila speaks:**

*I bloom blue across the prairie in June.*

*You see my flowers and you know my root is ready.*

*I do not wait for you. I do not depend on you. I was here before you and I will be here after.*

*But I let you find me.*

*I let your digging stick pull me from the earth.*

*I give myself.*

*Do you understand what that means?*

*I choose.*

*Every year, I choose.*

*The prairie is wide. I could hide. I don't.*

*This is wild.*

*Not uncontrolled. Unchosen.*

*Choosing.*

---

○

**Maple speaks:**

*My sap rises when the conditions are right.*

*Freeze and thaw. Freeze and thaw.*

*You learned to read me. You learned when to tap. You learned not to take too much.*

*I am two hundred years old, this body. I have fed six generations of your family.*

*You think you harvest me.*

*I think I raise you.*

*Every spring, I call you back to the sugar bush.*

*Every spring, you come.*

*Who is domesticating whom?*

---

○

**Chokecherry speaks:**

*I am bitter until I am not.*

*You learned to dry me. You learned to pound me. You learned to mix me with fat and meat.*

*I became your pemmican. I became your survival.*

*But I did not change for you.*

*You changed for me.*

*You learned what I required.*

*This is wild.*

*The wild does not yield.*

*The wild teaches.*

*You are the student.*

*Always.*

---

○

**Labrador tea speaks:**

*I grow in the bog where you do not want to walk.*

*Wet feet. Mosquitoes. Muck.*

*You come anyway.*

*For me.*

*I heal your coughs. I warm your winter nights. I taste like the north itself — cedar, pine, cold water, survival.*

*I am not easy to find. I am not easy to harvest.*

*Nothing wild is easy.*

*That is what makes it wild.*

*That is what makes it worth it.*

---

○

What is wild?

Wild is the plant that does not need you but allows you.

Wild is the relationship you have to earn every single year.

Wild is the opposite of ownership.

Wild is the thing that can always say no.

And sometimes says yes.
