6 Tips for Deepening Your Connection to Art

← Words / 2025 Variety

1. Let Yourself Linger: It’s tempting to move quickly from piece to piece, especially when there’s so much to see. But lingering is where the magic happens. Notice the imperfections, the hidden textures, the moments where the creator’s hand faltered. (Or you know, appreciate how the museum lighting perfectly illuminates your selfie.)

2. Embrace the Awkward: Some art might make you uncomfortable—confused, even. That’s okay. Let those moments sit with you; they’re often the ones that change how you see the world. (Still lost? Just shrug and say it’s “avant-garde.” Works every time.)

3. Find the Vulnerability in Art: Art tells stories, but it also holds quiet confessions. Look closely enough, and you might find echoes of your own struggles, joys, and uncertainties staring back at you.

4. Transform Spaces into Experiences: Immersive exhibits are more than a trend—they’re an invitation to feel. Dim the lights, add music, and give art the drama it deserves. (Think less “museum quiet” and more “this could be a movie trailer.”)

5. Champion the Broken Pieces: Not everything has to be polished to perfection. Sometimes the incomplete, the flawed, and the unconventional hold more truth than anything else. (After all, even a chipped mask can have its day in the spotlight.)

6. Invite Honesty in Creation: Vulnerability in art isn’t just brave—it’s magnetic. Let the imperfections shine, because the audience isn’t looking for perfection; they’re looking for authenticity.
