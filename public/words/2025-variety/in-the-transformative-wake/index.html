In the transformative wake of the 20th century…

Words / 2025 Variety

In the transformative wake of the 20th century, the United States forged its modern identity through bold policies that lifted a nation from the depths of the Great Depression and spurred unprecedented industrial and economic growth. Yet, beneath this luminous rebirth lies a legacy of exclusion—a series of well-intentioned reforms, like the New Deal and GI Bill, that were unevenly applied and steeped in discriminatory practices, systematically favoring white families while leaving marginalized communities to weather the bitter consequences of redlining, biased lending, and segregated education. This enduring architecture of inequality has not only scarred the economic landscape but also wounded the very fabric of our collective soul, calling on us to confront our past with deep empathy, extend human mercy, and work toward a future illuminated by justice, healing, and true shared prosperity.
