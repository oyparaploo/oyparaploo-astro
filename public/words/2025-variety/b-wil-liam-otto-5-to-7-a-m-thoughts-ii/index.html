b. Wil Liam (2)

otto ( 5 to 7 a.m. Thoughts II )

← Words / 2025 Variety

Many of us have lucid recollections that are similar
to the memories of elephants. Also, we accumulate
... amass ... dumbass stockpile ... to keep track of and
work hard to sort out our stored remembrances ... as
live human - animal slow flowing physical movement
to inanimate collected varieties of stuff. We can get
intensely and deeply solemn about it and lightly joke about it during the same hour. It’s a mindful trade
and business that hopefully doesn’t become too much
of a craze. Resulting in causing mobs to turn on us
oblique virgule agitators - aggravators. Weisenfelt spitz
teda ahmen ... eh bio ama oho ... wild eso ah mala ...
pato peto joy mava wewa. Tu neu hong kong soho.
