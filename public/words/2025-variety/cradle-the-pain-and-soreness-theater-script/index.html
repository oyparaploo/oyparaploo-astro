Cradle The Pain and Soreness (Theater Script)

Words / 2025 Variety

MOVEMENT I:

Spore Chorus (in rhythm)
A pulse.
A touch.
A listening.
We collect everything.
We loop.

Liora
I show up late.
Not dramatically—just enough to dissolve.
Behind my eyelids:
an unsent memo.
A buried letter beneath violet roots.
Style. I call it.

MOVEMENT II: Myth of Soft Burning

Spore Chorus (chanting softly)
Before root met rain,
before light flirted with leaf,
there was Myco.
Born of moon-mist.
Threaded, not walked.

Liora (reading from a decayed book)
"I coiled first.
I folded.
I looped—
Not wise.
Afraid to be misunderstood."

Spores begin to flinch, physical stutters spreading like echo.

Root Memory (rising)
Even radiant spores flinch.
Even prophecy curls inward before unfolding.

Spore Chorus (overlapping voices)
Even myth repeats
its own wound
with rehearsal.

MOVEMENT III:

Spore Chorus (gentle, lullaby-tone)
You call it night.
We call it archive.
Not a library.
A labyrinth.

Liora
Sleep folds my mind into vapor.
I wake beneath a pillow
that mapped my fears
in mycelial curls.

Spore Chorus
We hold the rot like a child.
We remember:

Liora (kneeling)
So many signs.
Too awkward.
Too sacred.

MOVEMENT IV:

Spore Chorus (layered whispers)
We descended—
not into bed,
but into chilled roots.
A cathedral of frost.

Shadow Characters begin a fragmented call and response:

“Do you remember the orange crayon?”
“No. But my fingers do.”
“What about the leaf?”
“It flinched.”
“And your mother’s silence?”
“Boils with water.”

Root Memory wraps around wrists. Movement tightens.

Spore Chorus
We are the pause in your laughter.
We are the frost in your joy.

Liora (voice soft, breaking)
I touched the center root.
It whispered,
“You grow downward.
And that, too, is grace.”

MOVEMENT V: Grotesque Splendor

Spore Chorus (verbs leading, rhythmic)
We press.
We reach.
We stir.
We graze.
We sculpt.
We metabolize.

Sound grows. The archive hums through sediment.

Liora
Emotion molds like forgotten food—
pungent.
Holy.
Sticky truth.

Spore Chorus (pounding beat)
We etch.
We bleed.
We grow.

We dress grief in loam.

Root Memory (now flowering from the frost stage)
You are composting hesitation.
You bloom sideways.
And still—
you’re enough.

All characters gather in loose circle. Liora stands center. Threads of light connect everyone.

Spore Chorus
You are not broken.
You just loop beautifully.

Liora (soft smile, barely audible)
I’m not healed.
But I’m remembered.

ACT I: CARTOGRAPHY

SPORE CHORUS
Downward.
Always downward.
In silence,
with verbs.

Liora moves like a thought trying to become language.

LIORA (monologue)
I walk late enough to be forgotten.
I sit near trees who keep secrets better than people.
My phone contains drafts, not texts.
My healing?
Cracker crumbs in a parked car.
“This is symbiosis,” I whisper, and nobody hears.

CHORUS (overlapping)
We do not name.
We observe.
Each tendril is a question.
Each fungal moment—messy.

LIORA
I've joined a composting cult.
Kevin is my accountability mushroom.

Ensemble creates circle. Kevin is revealed—simple puppet or voice.

KEVIN (deadpan)
You are twenty minutes late to compost check-in.
I forgive you.
Again.

The Fractured Sun’s Fable

*Myco emerges through layers of radiant fabric.

MYCO
Long ago, before root and rain exchanged numbers—
I bloomed sideways out of a compost heap.
Born of moon-mist and forgotten receipts.
I threaded through dimensions
like someone late to their own enlightenment.

CHORUS
With one filament,
Myco touched stone.
With the second,
kissed a seed.
And the third—
posted: "Feeling spore-y"

MYCO
I sang in expired coupons.
My gospel?
Dirt.
My cathedral?
Memory.
My TikTok?
Banned for spiral codes.

EARTH (serious)
We remember your myth
not because it inspires—
but because it loops.

ACT II: MIDNIGHT LULLABIES

CHORUS (singing softly)
You call it night.
We call it archive.

LIORA
Every regret between my teeth
is composted under my pillow.

CHORUS
We braid your breath into filament.
Your name dissolves into soil songs.

The Underground Choir

Kemi, Jasper, Minh appear, ghostlike. Movement slow and deliberate.

KEMI
My mother called me quiet thunder.
I was interrupted in every language.
Now I speak in moss and closed captions.

JASPER
I don’t memorize joy.
Every root asks:
“Did you mean it?”

MINH
My lullabies scattered with migration.
Three countries.
One guilt—forgetting her face.

Chorus hums underneath them. Frost light flickers.

ROOT (deep voice, echoing)
We are the frost in your joy.
We are the pause in your laughter.

LIORA (softly)
I touched the center.
It whispered:
“You grow downward.
And that, too, is grace.”

ACT III: ENTANGLEMENT

Whimsical Wordplay and Melancholy

The set transforms into a foggy dreamscape.

CHORUS
We whisper alphabet soup.
We wear metaphorical knitwear.
We take notes in root cursive.

LIORA
Dreams detour.
My archive?
Ghosted dialogue and misfiled longing.

Confetti made from diary clippings falls.

CHORUS
Let us roam your braids of thought.
We cross-reference ache
with soft murmurs.

Repetition as Rebellion

MYCO
Repetition is our rebellion.
Memory is duct-taped.
Myth blooms from glitch.
We loop proudly.

LIORA (to audience)
I have bloomed sideways.
I archive gently.
I am compost.
I am becoming.

CHORUS (last time, in harmony)
You are remembered.
You loop beautifully.
You grow downward.
And that, too—
that, especially—
is grace.

“You Are Not a Mispronunciation”
A Theatrical Composting Performance in Three Acts

ACT I: Rootlike Coolness

Opening tableau:
Liora (as Flarenth) stands center, surrounded by the Chorus of Roots. Their limbs stretch and contract like slow choreography; a fungal pulse beneath their dialogue.

CHORUS OF ROOTS (chanting rhythmically):
We see your ache
before you name it.
We won’t force healing—
only breath.

LIORA (addressing the audience and herself):
One root hummed a fugue of our childhoods.
Soft. Imperfect.
Like moss reciting lullabies in broken vowels.
Some roots wrapped gently.
Some clutched like grief does.
Tight. Then tighter. Then quiet.

CHORUS (in layered voices):
Roots don’t point one way.
They burrow.
They choreograph.
They touch what we buried
and renamed healing.

MYZRAEL (entering with moss cloak):
Speak—speak the silence, silence the stone-stone.
We are the echo braided through bark’s forgetting.
Come forth, Flarenth—ignite the ancient loops.

FLARENTH:
We bring flare to the fungus.
We brand myth into mold.
What memory doesn’t burn, stagnates.

SOLORIN:
We cultivate the chords you forget.
Our language swells where yours crack.
You scorch, you echo—we sustain.

ACT II: The Archive Beneath

KEMI, JASPER, MINH step into the lamplight one by one.

KEMI:
My mother called me quiet thunder.
Every language spoke over me.
Now the frostroot sings in vowels I never mastered.

JASPER:
I flirt with laughter, but can’t memorize joy.
Each fungal thread asks:
Did you mean it? Did you mean it?

MINH:
Three migrations.
One lingering guilt.
I forgot the shape of my grandmother's voice.
Now spores echo lullabies I once sang.

LIORA:
I walk differently now.
I hum in silence.
I collect spores of soft becoming like library fines.
When I forget who I was supposed to be,
the roots remember—
even if they charge late fees.

CHORUS:
You are more than one story.
A network of contradictions.
Scripted by survival,
Improvised by sorrow,
Made holy by connection.

ACT III: Fluency and Farce

ZENTHRA (glittering):
Come forth, Vurrmok—ignite the ancient loops.
Like a podcast fungi listen to on long hyphal commutes.

VURRMOK:
We brand myth into mold
and sell it in compost bins—six font variants, ethically vague.
What memory doesn’t burn stagnates
and gets archived in an app used only for journaling regret in cursive.

THALINE:
Even fire ferments into boutique kombucha.
We’re emotional support spores
inside the wellness-industrial complex.

CHORUS:
Repetition refines.
Reposting is healing.
Hashtag scorch.
Caption: “Rooted but still vibing.”

SOLORIN (sorrowful):
She speaks in future echo.
Not trending.
Truthing.

ZENTHRA:
She is pattern’s new prophet.
Streaming live
from a moss-lined bunker of recycled buzzwords.

Closing Tableau

Spores fall from above. Liora sings.

LIORA (softly):
Speak your silence aloud.
Let your ache compost.
Grow downward.
Beautifully.

CHORUS (final echo):
You are not a mispronunciation.
You are a ceremony of selves.

Lights dim. Spores shimmer.
