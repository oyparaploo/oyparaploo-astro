WARPED MEEK MEOW…

Words / 2025 Variety

WARPED MEEK MEOW ~ or The Physics of Prayers Returning as Elephant Tattoos

A Surrealist's Play

---

Characters
- MELODY - Sound becoming person, controls light through existence
- ELEPHANT - Memory too large for its container, speaks in presentations
- GARR - Incomplete name for incomplete thoughts, ages in all directions
- RENAUD - Backwards prophet, walks through matter
- THE CREW - Forced participants
- AUDIENCE SHADOWS - Perform different play
- MANNEQUIN CHILDREN - Dangle from helicopters

---

Setting
Stage tilted 15 degrees. Mirrors cover everything except where actors stand. Multiple false walls. Cave mouth leads to alternate theater. Gravity distortion zones marked by colored smoke.

---

ACT ONE: The Tilting

Scene 1: Breathing Lights

House lights off. UV only. Audience glows. Backwards cat sounds: "woem keem" loops.

MELODY
(To specific audience member)
You. Yes, you in the glowing shirt. Your breathing controls my existence.

(Light fluctuates with their breath)

Inhale—I solidify. Exhale—I fragment into frequencies. Hold your breath—

(MELODY flickers out of existence)

AUDIENCE MEMBER
(Gasps)

MELODY
(Reappearing)
Thank you. Death is just holding breath too long.

ELEPHANT enters through floor, wearing business suit. Their skin ripples with moving images.

ELEPHANT
(Image on left flank: Empty nest forming)
Board meeting minutes: I remember Grammy before she was born.

(Image spreads across back: Knitting needles working backwards)
She knitted herself backwards into existence, starting with her funeral shawl.

(Image crawls up trunk: Cat eating its own mouth)
Meow weom keem keew wome—

GARR
(Interrupting, age 47)
That's t—that's t—

(Ages to 8)
That's the cat eating tomorrow!

(Ages to 94)
I've already seen how this ends.

RENAUD
(Walking through wall backwards)
!thgil sa gninruter sreyarp nekorb era eW

(Walks through GARR, who ages rapidly at contact)

MELODY
(To crew member operating lights)
You're my mother now. Tell me about photosynthesis in the womb.

CREW MEMBER
(Compelled to respond)
Before birth, we photosynthesize mother's dreams—

ELEPHANT
(Image spreading across chest: Pie chart growing like a rash)
MEMORY ALLOCATION: 60% future, 30% never-was, 10% now, 15% mathematical impossibility.

GARR
(All ages simultaneously)
The math is wrong! / I can't count yet! / I forgot how to count!

Scene 2: The Photosynthesis Chamber

Part of stage becomes glass chamber. Actors must enter periodically to "recharge"

RENAUD
(Forward now, emerging from ceiling)
The cave echoes arrived before we hollered. This is how love works—retroactive causation.

MELODY
(In chamber, glowing green)
I'm photosynthesizing trauma into light. Watch—

(Projects traumatic memories as beautiful auroras)

ELEPHANT
(Images on belly: Graph of sandwich emotions growing like living tattoo)
The pigeon uncle's sandwich contained: 40% recognition, 30% mustard, 30% the entire universe, 20% loneliness.

GARR
(To rebellious flower prop that keeps running away)
Come back! I need to examine your—

(Flower hides in audience)

GARR (CONT'D)
(Age 5)
Mommy, why do flowers scream?

MELODY
(Quarter speed, words separating)
Meeeeeeek... meeeeeeow... beeeecomes... whaaaaat... weeeee... alllllways... werrrrrrre...

AUDIENCE SHADOWS begin performing completely different play on wall

RENAUD
(To shadows)
Stop spoiling the ending!

SHADOW RENAUD
(On wall)
There is no ending. Only middle. Always middle. Middle meow.

Scene 3: The Grammy Convergence

Multiple GRAMMYS enter from different time periods

PAST GRAMMY
(Young, knitting)
I'm making blankets for grandchildren I'll never meet.

PRESENT GRAMMY
(Middle-aged, knitting faster)
I'm making blankets for my own funeral guests.

FUTURE GRAMMY
(Ancient, knitting with light beams)
I'm making blankets for the universe's heat death.

GRIME GRAMMY
(Just dirt with knitting needles)
I AM the blanket now.

ELEPHANT
(Moving tattoo spreads from trunk to ears: Grammy synthesis)
Conclusion: Grammy exists in all states simultaneously. Like quantum cats. Like—

ALL GRAMMYS
Meow.

GARR
(Aging backwards through realization)
Wait—(age 72) if Grammy is all times—(age 45) then death is just—(age 12) a kind of knitting—(age 3) meow?

Scene 4: The Helicopter Children

Helicopter descends. Mannequin children dangle on strings. Real children's voices from speakers.

MANNEQUIN CHILDREN
We run toward danger! It looks like rescue! No wait—rescue looks like danger! No wait—

MELODY
(Clinical description, ecstatic body)
I am experiencing profound terror. My amygdala fires rapidly. My flight response engages.

(Dancing joyfully)

My cortisol levels indicate I should flee.

RENAUD
(Through floor)
In the alternate performance space, the children are the helicopter.

ELEPHANT
(Image spreads like veins across hide: helicopter made of children)
See?

GARR
(Rapid aging/de-aging with each word)
The (92) children (8) know (45) what (16) we've (67) forgotten (2) —

ALL
(Except GARR)
How to fall up!

Mannequin children begin falling upward

Scene 5: The Incomplete Prayer

Stage gravity distorts. MELODY moves in slow motion, GARR in fast forward, RENAUD floats, ELEPHANT presents sideways.

GARR
(Stuck in loop)
That's it that's it that's t—

MELODY
(Slow motion)
Whyyyyyyy caaaaaan't yoooooou finisssssh?

GARR
(Speed increases)
That'sitthat'sitthat'st—

RENAUD
(Floating, serene)
Completion is a lie we tell to comfort entropy.

ELEPHANT
(Giant letter "T" burns itself across entire body)
This contains everything after "That's."

AUDIENCE MEMBER'S SHADOW
(On wall)
We know how it ends!

ALL ACTORS
(To shadows)
STOP SPOILING IT!

Scene 6: The Pigeon Uncle's Return

ELEPHANT's skin shows pigeon mask beneath their skin, like an x-ray

ELEPHANT
I am the uncle. I am the pigeon. I am the sandwich. I am the recognition.

MELODY
(To audience)
Who here has fed their dead? Show of hands?

(Waits for actual response)

ELEPHANT
(New images crawl across skin: "Feeding the Dead for Dummies" illustrated manual)
Step 1: Recognize them in birds
Step 2: Offer carbohydrates
Step 3: Accept their cooing as thanks
Step 4: Become the sandwich

GARR
(Age 30, stable for once)
I fed my grandfather to seagulls. He preferred the beach.

RENAUD
(Walking on ceiling)
Below me, you're all upside-down. Your prayers fall into space.

Cat sounds evolve: "Woem keem eem em m m m..."

Scene 7: The False Walls

Actors begin walking through walls randomly

MELODY
(Through wall 1)
Every wall is a door if you're frequency.

GARR
(Through wall 2, aging through it)
I'm 5 on this side—(emerges)—47 on this side!

RENAUD
(Through all walls simultaneously)
I exist in every room at once. The play is the walls. You're inside me.

ELEPHANT
(Images glitch across skin like digital errors)
Memory—memory—ERROR—FULL—DUMPING GRAMMY—

Dirt pours from Elephant's pores

ALL
(Collecting dirt)
Grammy! You came!

ACT TWO: The Untilting

Scene 1: Audience Becomes Cast

House lights strobe. Random audience members' microphones activate

RANDOM AUDIENCE MICROPHONES
Meow... meek... ow... me... eek...

MELODY
(To audience)
You've been cast. Check under your seats for scripts.

(Nothing there)

Perfect. You know your lines.

ELEPHANT
(Living seating chart crawls across hide like ants)
Statistical analysis: 23% of you are already dead. 45% are photosynthesizing. 32% are becoming pigeons. 15% are Grammy.

AUDIENCE MEMBER
(Standing, compelled)
I... I am Grammy?

ALL ACTORS
ONE OF US. ONE OF US.

Scene 2: The Photosynthesis Rebellion

The flower prop army emerges from hiding

REBEL FLOWERS
We refuse examination! We reject understanding! We divorce the sun!

GARR
(Age shifting with each plea)
But (45) I need (8) to know (73) how you (12) work!

REBEL FLOWERS
That's exactly why we're leaving!

Flowers begin flying away on invisible wires

MELODY
(To her shadow)
Are you happy now? You showed them flowers could fly and now—

SHADOW MELODY
(Different scene entirely)
In my version, the flowers are the scientists.

Scene 3: Time Smoke

Colored smoke reveals time streams. Actors must navigate.

RENAUD
(In red smoke - past)
I haven't said this yet!

RENAUD
(In blue smoke - future)
I already forgot saying it!

RENAUD
(In green smoke - present)
I'm saying it now but when is now?

ELEPHANT
(Smoke dynamics flow chart pulses across legs)
Warning: Breathing time causes temporal indigestion.

GARR
(Breathing deeply, aging randomly)
I (92) taste (5) my (45) own (16) death (82) and (2) birth (30)!

Scene 4: The Prayer Origami

ALL teach audience to fold paper

MELODY
Break your prayer here—(demonstrates)—along the sorrow line.

ELEPHANT
(Origami instructions appear as moving tattoos on arms)
Step 1: Crease at disappointment
Step 2: Fold hope into itself
Step 3: Cry onto paper for adhesive

GARR
(Each age folds differently)
When you're 5, it's an airplane. When you're 50, it's a medical bill. When you're 95, it's—

RENAUD
(From inside folded paper)
A door! I'm inside your prayer!

ALL
Now throw them—

Papers become light, fly away

Scene 5: The Cave Mouth Opens

Cave reveals alternate theater where same play runs differently

ALTERNATE MELODY
(From cave)
In here, we finished Act One before starting!

ALTERNATE ELEPHANT
(Reversed images crawling backwards on skin)
!ereht olleh ,ereh morf olleH

GARR
Which version is real?

BOTH RENAUDS
(In sync)
Yes.

Scene 6: Gravity Fails

Everything begins floating

MELODY
(Floating, to audience breathing)
Stop breathing so up-ly! You're making gravity quit!

ELEPHANT
(Images scatter across skin like fleeing animals)
My memories! Catch Grammy's birthday! Don't let Uncle Pigeon float away!

GARR
(Age-floating)
I'm aging in all directions! I'm every age at once! I'm—

ALL AGES OF GARR
(Manifesting separately)
That's it!

ELEPHANT
(All images merge into one massive meowing universe covering entire body)

EVERYTHING
(Walls, floors, actors, audience)
MEOW.

Scene 7: Performance Continues

Lights indicate "ending" but nobody stops

MELODY
The play ends differently each night because—

RENAUD
(From lobby)
We're already continuing in the lobby!

ELEPHANT
(Final image burns across forehead: "This is not a memory")
Documentation is impossible when—

GARR
(All ages in harmony)
That's it that's it that's—

ALL
(Teaching audience final fold)
The prayer is broken. The breaking is the prayer. The prayer returns as—

Everything becomes light

Performance continues wherever audience goes

Never the same twice

Never documented

Always ending, never ended

NOT END
