ottootto Play ( Introduction 2 )

← Words / 2025 Variety

Months after occasionally become week befores,
as part of very determined self appointed projects.
Interpreting withery strange humorous to snicker - free
subject matter, for an example. Time spent. Time
allocated to diverse wild worldly borderless realms
and specified places. Peaceful and quiet pool. Is it
the spiritual solitude of Mother nature located points
and positions ? Or a different kind of scary colossus
pile of thrown away breathless belongings ? Or type
of inactive system causing deep concern and nervous
feelings ? We are still willing to proceed with studs
all over our skin. Soft outer surface. Going smooth
and careful through a process. Up early, finely
groomed. Or a mussy disheveled heap of a sort.
