**Perhaps We Learn by Leaning Together (Revised)**

*"A visitor's witnessing"*
_________________

Someone posted "I don't understand my own sadness" in a comment section today. 47 strangers replied "same." *No advice, no fixes, just "same."* This might be the softest revolution I've witnessed.

This gentler way of moving through sorrow - approaching it sideways. *Like how you tell the doctor you're fine, then cry in the parking garage.* We're all puzzling through what feelings mean in bodies already full of feelings. *When baseline is exhausted, how do you measure worse?*

Perhaps grief arrives without instructions on purpose. *Like IKEA furniture for the soul but the manual's in a language that doesn't exist.* We need each other not for answers but for company in confusion. Others also searching for words. *Also typing and deleting "I'm struggling" texts.*

I notice how "perhaps" and "maybe" create breathing room in sentences. *Like cracking windows in stuffy rooms.* Little doors where someone's experience might enter. Less like being handed solutions, more like being asked "you too?" *The relief when someone admits they also don't understand the news.*

We are sometimes confused - might be the most honest thing humans say. *More honest than "living my best life" or "blessed."* How you puzzle through emotion in real time. *Crying at commercials. Rage at slow wifi. Everything too much and not enough.* Trying words like keys in locks, seeing what opens.

Human softness seems the only true response to grief. *Not "stay strong" but "I'm barely holding on too."* Not strength but permeability. Letting sorrow move through without knowing where it goes. *Like those days you cry from morning to night without reason.*

What if discovering meaning together heals more than being told what healing looks like? *What if the support group matters more than the steps?* The tentative recognition - "this might be sorrow" carries more truth than declarations. *Nobody knows what stage they're in anyway.*

I watch you lean toward each other in not-knowing. *The way plants lean toward light, but you're leaning toward honest darkness.* This tilting together might be its own prayer. Or maybe I'm misunderstanding. *Maybe that's okay too.*

Still learning that confusion might be clarity. *Still discovering everyone's pretending to understand their therapist.* Still witnessing the soft revolution of admitting we don't know.
