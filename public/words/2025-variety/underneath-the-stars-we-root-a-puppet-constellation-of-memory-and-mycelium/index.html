DRAMATIS PERSONAE
CÉLESTE - The Dreamer
A puppet who floats between worlds, afraid that landing means choosing. Built of translucent organza
and moonlight.

EMMA - The Archive Keeper
The collector of forgotten moments, keeper of emotional threads. Ancient and motherly, made of earth
and memory.

OLIVIER - The Root-Walker
He who speaks in pulses and pauses, moving with the broken rhythm of roots through stone.

MAUD - The Hesitant Questioner
She moves through the world like a question she's afraid to ask. Secretly in love with the rhythm of
persistence.

PRIVAT - The Memory-Weaver
Collector of spores of soft becoming, dancer between connections. Trails phosphorescence.
KEREN - The Choir Voice
Underground singer of forgotten choices. Olivier's root-twin. Her body resonates with ancient songs.

PEDER - The Translator
Carrier of seven languages and untranslatable sorrows. Bonds with Jun over displacement.

ALELA - The Shapeshifter
Moth-myth transformer who knows the weight of being multiple. Céleste's shadow-self.

JUN - The Border-Crosser
Keeper of folded maps and displaced stories. Carries home in a small suitcase.

LIZZ - The Night Guardian
Midnight archivist of ache and tenderness. Emma's first student and shadow-keeper.

MARGUERITE - The Playwright
84-year-old narrator who appears in prologue. Lives between three homes and many languages.

SETTING
The stage exists in three levels:

SKY LEVEL - Where Céleste floats and stars project
A space of suspension and choice-avoidance

EARTH LEVEL - The main playing area
Where most action occurs, meetings happen, connections form

UNDERGROUND/BRRD LEVEL - Below stage or suggested through lighting
The root realm where memories archive and networks pulse

The entire space is connected by phosphorescent threads that become visible as the play progresses,
eventually revealing both mycelial networks below and constellation patterns above.

TIME
The eternal present of dream-time
Also: a Tuesday
Also: the space between heartbeats
Also: now

STAGING CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

The Floating Challenge
Problem: Céleste must appear to float throughout Act One

Solutions: Counterweight system, platform shoes with wheels, mirror placement
Budget option: Black-clad puppeteers as "invisible" support

The Root Network Challenge
Problem: Making mycelial connections visible and dynamic
Solutions: UV-reactive string, projection mapping, fiber optic cables

Budget option: Glow-in-the-dark yarn and blacklight

The Transformation Challenge
Problem: Alela must shift seamlessly between moth and human
Solutions: Reversible puppet, two puppeteers trading, magnetic costume pieces

Budget option: Shadow play transformation

PROLOGUE: AN INTRODUCTION UNLIKE ANY OTHER
A single spotlight illuminates an empty rocking chair. The sound of a radiator clanking. Stars project slowly
across the ceiling. A voice speaks from offstage - warm, aged, amused.

VOICE OF MARGUERITE
Alors (So then), you want to meet me before you meet my puppets? Très bien (Very well). But I warn you -
I am more root than flower these days. More brrd than bird, if you understand. I move slowly, like winter
sap. I am... how do you say... chilled by time but warmed by memory.

An elderly woman enters, dressed in layers of scarves and time. She carries a battered suitcase covered in
stamps and small painted stars.

MARGUERITE
(to audience) I am Marguerite Dubois-Chen. 84 years old. Born in Marseille when the world still believed
in telegraph romance and stars told stories. I have roots in three places - Paris where I freeze and write,
Oregon where I gather mushrooms and remember, Provence where I dry lavender and forget on
purpose.

She opens the suitcase. Puppets tumble out like constellations falling to earth.

MARGUERITE (cont'd)
These puppets? They are my système racinaire (root system). They grow from the ancient anthropological
truth that we are all connected - from the first humans who painted caves to map the stars, to the
teenagers who paint their feelings on TikTok under LED stars. We are all leaving marks, seeking
connection, growing sideways when we cannot grow up.

She picks up a puppet, cradles it.

MARGUERITE (cont'd)
Tonight, you will meet the underground. The brrd - the chilled spaces where we slow down enough to
feel. Where roots do their patient work of breaking stone into soil, making the physical into the mythical.
Where the ancient meets the immediate, like finding your grandmother's constellation map on a
stargazing app.

She settles into the rocking chair. Above her, a constellation forms that looks suspiciously like mycelial
networks.

MARGUERITE (cont'd)
The anthropologists tell us humans have always been networked. The mycologists tell us forests have
always been internets. The astronomers tell us we are all stardust. I tell you - we are both rooted and
celestial. Both brrd and flight. So... shall we begin this puppet show? Or as my grandson says, "Mémé, less
talking, more magic!"

Lights fade on her knowing smile. A shooting star crosses the projection.

ACT ONE
L'Archive S'éveille (The Archive Awakens)

Scene 1: The Floating and the Grounded
Stage dark as the inside of a seed. Phosphorescent threads appear slowly, like neural pathways drawn by
patient hands. Stars project faintly on the scrim. The stage is divided into three levels - sky, earth, and the
brrd underground where things move slowly and significance accumulates like sediment.

TRANSITION: Light rises like dawn through soil. Sound of roots growing - a creaking, patient music. A
constellation that looks like roots appears above.

CÉLESTE floats in, suspended by invisible threads. Movement: [F] Arms swim through air, feet pedal slowly,
head tilts back to stars.

CÉLESTE
In the breath between soil and silence... je dérive (I drift). Not falling, not rising. Just... threading through
crépuscule (twilight) like someone tracing their own delay. The anthropologists would call this "liminal
space." I call it... Tuesday.

EMMA pushes through the stage floor like a mushroom after rain, carrying the physical weight of memory.
Movement: [R] Hands pat pockets, checking her collection. Each gesture deliberate, rooted.

EMMA
Un pouls. Une touche. Une écoute. (A pulse. A touch. A listening.) I collect everything - even the dreams
you think you've forgotten. This one? (holds glowing thread) This is from the time you almost said "Je
t'aime" (I love you) but coughed instead. Very brrd of you - very chilled, very careful. We've all done it, ma
petite.

EMMA
Spores that remember. Your douces erreurs (soft mistakes), your glorieux évitements (glorious avoidances).

She guides MAUD forward with the patience of geological time.

EMMA (cont'd)
Ma chère (My dear), your hesitation is not weakness. It's careful beauty - like roots finding their way

Scene 3: Collecting Soft Becoming
TRANSITION: Sound of wind chimes made from keys. Light shifts to aurora colors. Stars swirl overhead.
Enter PRIVAT.

PRIVAT dances between the others, trailing phosphorescence. Movement: [C] Spiraling between puppets,

PRIVAT

MAUD
(reaching with the courage of the deeply cautious) Puis-je (May I)... may I touch it?

PRIVAT
Bien sûr (Of course). You've earned every tender thing you're afraid to want.

MAUD touches the thread. Her whole body illuminates - stars appear in her shadow.

PRIVAT (cont'd)
The universe owes you softness. It's an ancient debt - from when the first root split the first stone, from
when the first star decided to fall.

Le Mémoire Mycélien (The Mycelial Memoir)

PLAYWRIGHT'S NOTES

A Letter to Future Directors
Mon cher metteur en scène (My dear director),

This play was born from insomnia and love - the two great teachers. It is anthropological evidence that
we are all connected, all brrd (chilled into wisdom), all rooted in the ancient truth that to be human is to
be beautifully entangled between earth and stars.

Staging Challenges Summary:
1. Vertical Space: Three levels must feel distinct yet connected

2. Transformations: Quick changes need rehearsal and backup plans
3. Language Barriers: Physical translation is key

4. Puppet Coordination: Minimum 20 puppeteers for smooth show
5. Technical Elements: UV, projection, and traditional lighting must blend

Movement Notation Reminders:
Use the notation system throughout rehearsal
Each puppet should have a primary movement quality
Transitions between movement styles show growth

The ensemble moments need the most rehearsal
Let puppets breathe - even in stillness [*]

The Songs:
These are meant to be simple, memorable, and participatory. Teach them to your audience if you dare.
The melodies should feel both ancient and contemporary - like lullabies remembered from dreams.

Final Brrd Wisdom:
This play lives in the space between - between languages, between earth and sky, between ancient and
immediate. Stage it like you're planting a garden that will bloom after you're gone. Because you are.

Remember: we are all perfectly brrd - perfectly chilled into our perfect shapes. Some of us just take longer
to know it. The stars have always known. The roots have always known. Now you know too.

Avec tout mon amour mycélien et stellaire (With all my mycelial and stellar love),

Marguerite Dubois-Chen
Paris, janvier 2025
(Written while my radiator clanks its ancient song and Orion watches through my window)
