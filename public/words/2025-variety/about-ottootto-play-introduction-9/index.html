Human history has been very hostile but we won’t
go into those realities here. We interpret visually and
other ways disintegrations of human barricades
conveyed through concepts and completed evidence.
Earthling and other - world - like being performances.
Complex alive - inanimate site installations. We have
lots and lots of things to play on for unfair realities
of history going way back. Censorships, banishments,
deportations, ostracism, etc. An us self-assignment is
an exercise and often a prayers rendition recital.
