The Veil of Change

Words / 2025 Variety

Beneath a wide-brimmed hat with a silken veil,
A storm is brewing—a long-forgotten tale.
The air grows heavy with hunger's weight,
While injustice rumbles, demanding its fate.
Discrimination, its roots gnarled and old,
Racism, a shadow both brazen and cold.
Yet amidst these storms, hope softly ascends,
Carried by communities, where resolve never bends.

Here they stand, unyielding, resolute,
Without fresh waters their springs dilute.
Without the glow of electricity’s light,
Still they illuminate the world with their fight.

Guided by goals both humble and grand,
Empathy blooms where the weary stand.
They cradle despair with a gentle embrace,
And find strength in the struggle etched on each face.

Circumstances cruel, wild and unbound,
Yet resilience echoes—a defiant sound.
They see the threads that bind humankind,
An enduring fabric, by compassion entwined.

Through self-determination, noble and wise,
They lift their voices to the endless skies.
Directing resources with purposeful care,
To balance the scales, to build and repair.

Their actions ripple like rivers that yearn,
Toward equity’s promise, where hearts can return.
Flowing toward revitalization’s bright hue,
Breathing life into societies anew.

Under the wide-brimmed hat, with a veil so slight,
A vision emerges in shimmering light.
Where hope replaces hunger’s hollow tune,
And justice rises—a radiant monsoon.

In every storm, there lies a spark,
A chance to illuminate the darkest mark.
Strong, diverse communities light the way,
Forging tomorrow from the trials of today.
