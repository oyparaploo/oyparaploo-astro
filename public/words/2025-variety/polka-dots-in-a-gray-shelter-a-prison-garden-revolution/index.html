**POLKA DOTS IN A GRAY SHELTER ~ A Prison Garden Revolution*

### **Les Subsistances, Lyon**

---

## **PRE-SHOW**
Audience enters through mock prison intake. Given gray jumpsuits over clothes. Fingerprinted with washable ink that turns colors throughout show.

---

## **ACT ONE: The Gray Diagnosis**

*Stage: Prison library, entirely gray. Five women in gray jumpsuits.*

**TOOTS** (*60s, lifer, tending invisible garden in corner*):
Twenty-three years of gray walls.
Know what gray does to your brain?
Flatlines it. Like that monitor when someone dies.
*Mimes planting*
But I grow invisible tomatoes.
Guard thinks I'm crazy.
Maybe. But my invisible garden keeps me human.

**ALICE** (*30s, prison librarian, civilian*):
Budget for books: zero.
Budget for more gray paint: infinite.
These women read the same twelve books repeatedly.
*Holds up tattered book*
This one's held together with prayer and contraband tape.

**CELESTE** (*40s, guard, exhausted*):
Don't look at me like I'm the enemy.
I see the same gray sixteen hours daily.
My therapist says I have "environmental depression."
From a building. I'm depressed by architecture.
Three guards suicide this year.
The walls are killing us too.

**FAYE** (*25s, new arrival, panicking*):
This can't be real. Everything's gray?
Where's color? Where's... anything?
My cell walls, gray. Floor, gray. Ceiling—

**ORNELLA** (*35s, artist before incarceration*):
Gray. I know. I counted forty-seven shades.
Had nothing else to do for five years.
Used to paint murals. Now I dream in grayscale.
Sometimes forget what purple looks like.
Then remember and cry.

## **ACT TWO: The Polka Dot Conspiracy**

*ALICE smuggles in colored paper circles*

**ALICE**:
I brought something. Could lose my job.
*Reveals polka dots*
But Norwegian prisons have art. Gardens.
Twenty percent recidivism.
Ours? Sixty-eight percent.
We're manufacturing repeat offenders.

**TOOTS** (*touching colors reverently*):
Red. God, RED.
My eyes are drinking it.
Feel that? Dopamine. From circles.

**ORNELLA**:
We could... stick them places.
Start small. Guerrilla joy.

**CELESTE**:
I shouldn't allow this.
*Pause*
I'm taking my break. Twenty minutes.
What happens during my break isn't my responsibility.

*Women begin placing dots everywhere*

**FAYE**:
Is this illegal? Decorating?

**TOOTS**:
Everything's illegal here.
Might as well be illegal with color.

*The gray space transforms with dots*

**ORNELLA**:
Look. My brain is... waking up.
Like someone turned lights on after decades.

## **IMAGISTIC AMPLIFICATIONS**

### **The Gray Matter Atrophy**
Extended exposure to gray environments causes measurable hippocampal shrinkage—the brain literally gets smaller. Neural pathways requiring visual stimulation begin pruning themselves. Prison gray creates actual brain damage reversible only through color reintroduction.

### **The Polka Dot Neural Burst**
Circular patterns activate specific neurons in the fusiform face area—the same region recognizing human faces. Dots trigger social recognition patterns, making environments feel inhabited by friendly presence rather than hostile emptiness.

### **The Garden Synapse Garden**
Gardening creates new synaptic connections at any age—neurogenesis through horticulture. Hands in soil activate prehistoric neural patterns from 10,000 years of agricultural evolution. Prisoners growing food literally grow new brain architecture.

### **The Collective Color Contagion**
Mirror neurons fire when observing others experience color joy, creating synchronized dopamine release across groups. One person's joy at seeing red triggers adjacent brains to produce similar chemicals—joy becomes literally contagious through visual proximity.
