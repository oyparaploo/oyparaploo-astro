Words / 2025 Variety

### **Connecting Fragmented Identities and Distant Places**

This story, with its rhythmic words and layered themes, serves as a bridge—uniting identities fractured by borders, histories, and silences. It connects distant places, weaving together cities divided by walls, lands scarred by colonial power, and people separated by memory itself. Like a melody shared across cultures, the writing resonates with those who carry fragmented pieces of themselves, seeking wholeness. Each phrase acts as a thread, pulling disparate lives into a shared tapestry—a map not of geography, but of belonging.

---

### **Casual Reflections: A Conversation Between Actors**

Two actors sit backstage, their costumes still heavy with the weight of their roles.

“Mortality is a strange thing,” one begins, their voice thoughtful. “We know it’s inevitable, but we don’t live like it is. And then when it brushes against us—through loss, through absence—it’s like it expands the way we feel time. Each second feels stretched, infinite, but also unbearably fragile.”

The other nods, fiddling with the sleeve of their costume. “And it’s tied to belonging, isn’t it? To love someone, to love a place or a memory, is to make it a part of you. But that means you carry the fear of losing it—the hollow space that absence leaves. Yet, isn’t that what endurance is? Choosing to love deeply, even when it risks breaking you?”

They sit in silence for a moment before the first actor whispers, “Maybe the act of loving and remembering is what holds the world together. Maybe that’s what makes us human.”

### **Reflections in Second Person: Identity and Absence**

You carry absence with you. It rests in the spaces between your ribs, folds into the curves of your fingerprints, and hums in the echoes of every place you’ve left behind. Sometimes, you feel its sharpness, like broken glass catching the light. Other times, it wraps around you softly, a memory almost forgotten but never truly gone.

You are more than one identity, more than one place. You are threads woven across time, voices layered within your thoughts. Absence is part of you, but it doesn’t define you—it shapes the spaces where you rebuild, where you remember, where you reclaim. In the hollow places, you plant new roots. In the quiet moments, you find your voice.

---

### **A Dreamy Vision of Gaza**

Imagine Gaza bathed in golden light, olive trees swaying gently in a breeze that carries the scent of earth and salt. The Mediterranean stretches endlessly, its waves lapping against the shore in a steady rhythm, as though the sea itself is breathing. Above, seagulls glide in arcs against a sky painted with soft, watercolor blues and whites, their cries echoing like a distant melody.

In this vision, the land feels alive—each stone, each tree, imbued with memory. The olive trees seem to whisper stories of resilience, their roots reaching deep into the soil, anchoring the present to the past. The sea, vast and timeless, becomes a metaphor for longing, its waves connecting the seen and unseen, the physical and the metaphysical. Here, the sacred and the ordinary blend, creating a dreamscape where time feels suspended, and yearning transforms into connection.

---

### **Yearning: Bridging the Physical and Metaphysical**

Yearning exists in the space between the tangible and the infinite. It is the way a tree’s roots stretch unseen toward water, the way a melody lingers in the air after the last note is played. To yearn is to reach for something beyond what is, to feel the pull of the eternal while grounded in the physical.

In this story, the physical world—its textures, sounds, and silences—becomes a doorway to the metaphysical. The rough bark of an olive tree, the ripple of a bird’s wings, the hum of the earth—they are all reminders of the unseen threads that connect us to something greater. Yearning is not just longing for what is absent; it is a bridge that carries us toward understanding, toward belonging, toward the eternal.

---

### **Closing Reflection**

Through poetic language and resonant imagery, this story draws together fragmented identities and distant places, offering a space where yearning transforms into connection. It reminds us that absence is not an ending—it is an opening, a call to remember, to love, and to endure.

*(Holy Spirit)* “Let your yearning rise like a melody, carrying the physical into the eternal, weaving longing into light.”
