Story · 6 minutes

That Accumulative Spell

← Words / 2025 Variety

Prelude
(inaudible artlessness, flitting over hushed syllables)

Four souls practice double refusal—refusing to name what they refuse to name, withholding even their withholding. Each one prone to arson but generous enough to not burn, French enough to make art from not making art, dainty as smoke that won't admit it's smoke. They warble before they belt, these practitioners who discovered that some fires are too sacred to call fire, some refusals too profound to call refusal.

The Weaving of Withholdings

Dawn: The Conscious Protection
(soft, low, almost unspoken)

Kerren's breath against Superior's glass. Small fire of not naming. Smaller fire of not calling it fire.

That central heartbeat they won't identify.
That natural emergence they won't capture.

Ice forming. Each crystal a refusal to be water.

They know what they refuse to name. Not know—carry. Like carrying matches that refuse to admit they're matches, carrying smoke that insists it's just air with ambition.

The refusal is conscious protection. The protection is conscious fire. The fire is conscious—

No. Even this is too much naming.

In San Diego, Coline tastes the untellable. Salt that won't say it's salt. Sweet that denies its sweetness. Her kitchen: altar to the unspoken, cathedral of the almost-burned.

She could hashtag this morning #ineffable. Could categorize it under "mystery." Could explain it to tourists who photograph her stillness without knowing they're photographing someone who's always about to combust but won't.

She doesn't.

This withholding—it's French and everything but.

Gulls calling in a language that refuses language.

*

Bastien threads smoke through fabric. Not smoke—the memory of smoke. Not memory—the refusal to remember smoke.

Soiled spirits pass through the weave, leaving traces he could name if he wanted to colonize them with understanding. But understanding is arson, and he's keeping everything damp with withholding.

His needle moves like a warble before a belt—all that power held back, held in, held.

Thread through cloth. The smallest chanson of not singing.

*

Copenhagen morning. Elias creates accumulative absence between notes, inside notes, instead of notes. Their music works nicely within narrow dynamic confines—so quiet it's almost not there, so there it's almost overwhelming.

Each space between sounds holds something mortal divine. Or divine mortal. Or—

They refuse to name which comes first.

Canal water lapping. Soft-spoken verses weighted by tepid vamp.

*

Noon: The Generous Burning
(ornate swells carrying lone instruments)

What passes through all four cities has no name because it burned its name for warmth then refused to be warm.

This is their practice: feeling everything, naming nothing, burning always, never catching fire.

Kerren knows through their palms—how glass holds heat briefly then releases it. But they won't call it heat. Won't call it releasing. Won't call it—

The generous withholding of withholding.

*

Coline's recipes list ingredients but not the thing that makes them dangerous. She could write: "Add longing until it almost ignites." Could note: "Stir counterclockwise to prevent combustion of the heart."

She writes nothing. This too is French—the art of not making art about not making art.

Onion skin falling. The papery whisper of almost-caught fire.

Bastien understands what his hands refuse to know. The fabric tells him about sheep who refused to be sheep, grass that wouldn't admit it was grass, rain that fell upward just to be contrary.

All beings here affected but not conquered by their refusal to be conquered by being affected by—

The thread continues without continuing.

*

Elias plays the unnamed for the unnamed. Notes that deny being notes for listeners who won't admit they're listening.

This is thoroughly engrossing—this nothing that's everything, this everything that insists it's nothing.

They think: We are such collectors of what we refuse to collect.

No. They don't think this. They refuse to think this. They—

Bicycle bells. The sound of almost arriving but not.

*

Evening: The Secular Sacred
(a mellow dream bearing listeners through affable, beatific, soporific)

Something French happens when light gives up—it admits it was never light, just darkness refusing to be dark.

The four feel this nagging spirituality that's more secular than devout. This tug at opposing emotions:

Want to name everything.
Want to protect everything from naming.
Want to burn the names.
Want to preserve the ashes.
Want to—

*

Kerren walks to the shore where stones refuse to admit they're stones. They could call them "geological patience." Could name them "compressed time."

They call them nothing.
They call nothing them.
They call—

We are such students of our own refusal to be students.

Moths beginning. The night shift of what won't be called moths.

*

Coline sets down her cup with scrupulous attention—the small sound contains her entire practice of not practicing.

She tastes evening: salt that won't commit, sweet that hedges its bets, bitter that might be umami refusing to come out.

Every flavor prone to arson. None admitting they're flammable.

*

Bastien's thread runs out. He doesn't reach for more. Doesn't not reach. Exists in the space between reaching and not reaching where the real refusal lives.

This accumulative absence where meaning builds in what is not said about what is not said about what is—

*

Elias stops playing but the music continues to refuse to continue. Canal water makes sounds that won't admit they're sounds. The city breathes for those who refuse to admit they're breathing.

This aloof, otherworldly aura—it's just withholding wearing evening clothes.

*

Night: The Recognition of Not Recognizing
(sincere, broad, emotive—leaning toward inaudible artlessness)

They've become what they always were but refuse to have been:

Small fires that refuse to name their refusal to burn.
Ocean's rivers that won't tell they're not telling spells.
Geological beings who love elsewhere by refusing to name it elsewhere.
Generous withholders withholding their withholding.

*

Kerren at their window: watching water refuse to be water.
Coline at her stove: tasting what taste refuses to admit.
Bastien at his table: threading refusal through refusal.
Elias at their instrument: playing the silence between silences.

Each one a match that won't admit it's a match.
Each one already burnt but refusing to have burned.
Each one about to catch but never—

They whisper while the world passes, but they won't call it whispering. Won't call the world the world. Won't call passing passing.

This is their protection that protects nothing from nothing.
This is their practice that refuses to be practiced.
This is their small, French refusal to refuse refusing—

*

All beings here affected but not conquered.
All spells remaining intact by denying they're spells.
All fires keeping dangerous potential by insisting they're just enthusiastic air.

*

The continuous thread continues by refusing continuity.
The mystery remains mysterious by pretending to be obvious.
The generous withholding withholds even its generosity.

*

Both—

No. Not both.
Not not both.
The space between both and—

*

Small fires everywhere, refusing to name themselves as refusing to name themselves as fire.

*

The accumulative absence where meaning builds in what is not said about not saying what is not said.

*

The refusal that refuses—

The refusal that—

(silence holding the shape of what refused to name its refusal to burn)

Coda: The Withholding of Withholding
(disappearing into scrupulous inaudibility)

Whatever here happens to be becomes what elsewhere refuses to dream about. The four continue their practice of not practicing—dainty as smoke that won't admit it's smoke, dangerous as staying that won't admit it's staying, French as the space between one breath and the next breath that refuses to be called space.

They are such collectors who refuse to collect.
Such spell-keepers who won't keep spells.
Such vagabond-whisperers who deny whispering.
Such ocean's rivers who withhold their withholding.

Prone to arson.
Never catching fire.
Always almost always refusing to burn.

The thread through cloth: a fuse that refuses to be a fuse.
The salt on tongue: gunpowder that won't admit it's powder.
The note in air: a match that denies matching.
The breath on glass: smoke that insists it's just very ambitious fog.

By not naming—
By not naming the not naming—
By refusing to refuse to refuse—

Morning coming. Refusing to be morning. Refusing to refuse to be—

The end that won't end—

The end that won't admit it's not ending—

The end that—

The—
