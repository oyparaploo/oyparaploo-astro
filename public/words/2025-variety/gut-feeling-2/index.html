Gut Feeling II

← Words / 2025 Variety

Gut feelings, also known as “hunches” or “instincts,”
refer to the immediate, often subconscious emotional
responses to situations or decisions. These feelings are
not based on logical analysis but rather on an internal
sense of knowing. What is Intuition? Intuition is the
ability to understand or know something immediately,
without the need for conscious reasoning. It’s often
described as a “sixth sense” or an inner voice that provides
guidance based on past experiences and knowledge
stored in the subconscious mind.
