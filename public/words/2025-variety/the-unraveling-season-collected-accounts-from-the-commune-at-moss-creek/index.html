Fragment One: The Jellyfish Kitchen (March 15, 2023 - Baik's Journal)

I'm glowing now. Actually bioluminescent, like those deep-sea medusae that pulse with infection-light. I float three inches above my kitchen chair, tentacles (I refuse to call them fingers anymore) trailing into cold coffee.

The polyps started last week—tiny mouths across my shoulders, each one whispering a failure: the abortion, the theft, letting Mom die alone, choosing David over stability, the fire I started at twelve, the words I never said. They speak constantly in languages I've never learned but understand perfectly.

Kai visits daily with his storm-tracking equipment. His eyes bleed rust-red from staring at electromagnetic patterns. "Tomorrow's hurricane is already in your bones," he laughs, showing me readings that shouldn't exist—weather moving backward through my nervous system.

God made me a jellyfish but kept my consciousness scattered through ten thousand stinging cells. No brain, just pure response. It's freedom, really. Thought without thinking.

Fragment Seven: The Self-Eating Season (April 3, 2023 - Case Worker Report)

Subject "Baik" presented in mantis form—raptorial forelegs, compound eyes seeing time fractally. During interview, she casually consumed her own left arm, explaining: "Mantises eat their mates, but I have no mate, so I eat myself. It's economical."

The arm regenerated within two hours but came back smaller, more translucent. She notes each regeneration is "less complete." Projects total dissolution by September.

When asked about pain, subject laughed: "Pain assumes there's something separate from the experience. I AM the consumption."

Fragment Twelve: The Wanderer's Account (April 20, 2023 - Anonymous Blog)

Lost everything when Marcus joined the wall project. Not dead—worse. He pressed himself into wet cement until his consciousness merged with the building. The walls hum his thoughts now. Sometimes they scream.

Found Baik on Highway 49, all her forms failing simultaneously—jellyfish bells deflating, mantis segments collapsing inward, something she called her "tardigrade state" experiencing centuries between heartbeats.

The storm-lichen descended—thirty-six people who'd merged consciousness to escape individual suffering, now experiencing collective agony instead. They move like one organism, speak in harmonized screaming, offer water that burns like acid-love and roses whose thorns grow inward after you touch them.

I helped her back to consciousness, which meant returning her to suffering. This is mercy here.

Fragment Three: The Physics of Mercy (May 1, 2023 - Kai's Storm Log)

Tracking Baik's changes like weather patterns. She makes suffering look like transformation through divine mathematics:
- Medusa form: -7°C internal temperature, blood like antifreeze
- Mantis state: Consuming 3kg of self-tissue daily
- Tardigrade suspension: Heart rate of 1 beat per geological epoch
- Void-kraken reaching: Tentacles measured in dimensions that don't exist

The storms move backward through her, erasing history. Rain falls upward from her skin. These are gifts—protection from remembering when physics worked properly.

Fragment Twenty-Two: The Mother Doctrine (May 15, 2023 - Teaching Circle Recording)

BAIK: [Speaking through dissolving vocal cords] Women must be completely faithful until nothing remains. Watch—

[Sound of tearing]

BAIK: I'm pregnant with impossibility. Sometimes medusa-spawn that dissolve before forming. Sometimes mantis-eggs devouring each other in utero. Sometimes a tardigrade child experiencing eternity before birth.

STUDENT: Is it real?

BAIK: Reality is selfish. I'm giving birth to selflessness itself. Once it's outside me, I'll finally be empty enough to be loved properly.

The thing is never born. Or it's born constantly, each birth negating the last.

Started as meditation. Then fasting. Then letting myself scatter like [static] across frequencies. I'm in your radio, your microwave, your neurons firing.

Fragment Fifteen: The Portrait Series (June 20, 2023 - Gallery Statement)

Zara Chen's new work uses pigments made from Baik's molted shells, her shed tentacle-skin, crystallized hemolymph. The paintings are prophetic—showing tomorrow's transformation today.

The centerpiece: Baik as all forms simultaneously failing. Medusa bells collapsing. Mantis consuming itself in infinite loop. Tardigrade frozen in eternal moment. Void-kraken tentacles reaching through canvas into parallel dimensions where she chose differently.

Chen explains: "I paint with her discarded humanity. She sheds, I document. It's the most honest portrait possible—using the subject as the medium."

Visitors report temporal displacement after viewing. Several claim to have seen their own futures dissolving.

Fragment Thirty-One: The Crystalline Wanderer (July 4, 2023 - Medical Report)

Patient "Chen" presents with crystalline growths erupting through skin. Claims to carry deceased partner's consciousness in the crystals. The formations are 73% quartz, 27% organic material that shouldn't exist.

During examination, heard distinct voice from largest crystal (temporal, 7cm protruding): "She's still making my mistakes. I comment on everything. Critically."

Patient refuses removal, states: "She's teaching me through piercing. Each crystal is a lesson growing inward."

Note: Crystals emit electromagnetic frequency matching human thought patterns. Impossible but verified.

Fragment Forty-Five: The Gender Punishment (August 1, 2023 - Transformation Log)

Baik's changes transcend species—gender becomes fluid punishment:
- Medusa form: Reproduces asexually while dying
- Mantis state: Becomes male to eat herself more completely
- Tardigrade form: No gender, suspended between categories
- Void-kraken: All genders from parallel selves simultaneously

"God made biology a joke," she explains, currently possessing reproductive organs that exist in eleven dimensions. "I'm the punchline—too fluid to define, too defined to be free."

Fragment Sixty: The Living Buildings (September 15, 2023 - Building Inspector)

Moss Creek structures have developed organic components:
- Walls: Breathing at 12 respirations/minute
- Floors: Pulse matching collective heartbeat
- Ceilings: Grow neural tissue, appear to dream

Resident "Woozy Pupil" (age 9) discovered how to make architecture feel pain. They celebrated this as breakthrough—now buildings can suffer with residents. "Solidarity," they call it.

The fungal networks have merged with human nervous systems. Privacy means transparent skin showing rotting organs so others can "pray more accurately."

Note: Condemned for habitability. Residents refuse to leave. Buildings won't let them.

Fragment Seventy-Three: The Storm Colony (October 1, 2023 - Ornithologist's Report)

Thirty-six large birds circle continuously above Moss Creek. Never land. Closer inspection reveals they're not birds but the "storm-lichen"—humans who merged consciousness and grew wings to escape earth.

They defecate acid rain—tears of empathy that burn what they bless. Their synchronized shrieking registers as prayers on certain frequencies. They've forgotten individual names, refer to themselves only as "the spreading."

One descended briefly, spoke in unified voice: "We thought collective consciousness would ease suffering. Instead, we experience everyone's pain simultaneously. This is God's humor."

Fragment Ninety: The Void Reaches (October 31, 2023 - Physics Department)

Subject "Baik" demonstrated what she calls "void-kraken form"—tentacles reaching through dimensional tears to access parallel universes:

- Universe A: Retrieved ashes that smell like the law degree she never earned
- Universe K: Brought back pure vacuum that makes people forget their names
- Universe Z: Pulled through a mirror showing everyone as Baik, slowly transforming

She offers glimpses of alternate selves for donations. Most visitors experience psychological break. The ones who don't join the commune immediately.

Note: Dimensional tears remain after tentacles withdraw. Growing wider.

Fragment Last: Current State (November 15, 2023 - Final Documentation)

I remain unchanged—Baik calls this "God's cruelest joke," forcing me to witness with clarity while everyone evolves into horror.

Current conditions:
- Baik: Cycles through all forms simultaneously, each transformation worse
- Chen: Walking geode, partner's voice echoing from every crystal facet
- Asha: Exists in every electronic device within 50 miles
- Kai: Projects weather patterns in tumor-clouds spelling "MERCY"
- Zara: Paints with blood that never clots, tomorrow's suffering today
- Storm-lichen: Circle eternally, weeping acid benedictions
- Woozy Pupil: Teaching walls to scream musically

The recursive pregnancy continues—Baik contains infinite versions of herself, each pregnant with more versions, fractally expanding until she's swollen with every possible future.

"This is love," she gurgles through what might be a mouth. "God loves us too much to let us be stable. Every transformation proves divine attention."

They teach the holy mysteries:
- Suffering and grace are molecularly identical
- Identity is violence against possibility
- Deterioration is the only honest improvement
- We're supposed to be so much more, which is why He helps us become so much less

The story flows like infected lymph through Moss Creek's veins. Not ending but continuing, each iteration worse and holier.

Addendum: The Gospel According to Moss Creek

Found carved into my bedroom wall by fingernails:

"We are God's favorite joke. Not crown of creation but punchline. Every transformation is mercy disguised as horror. Every horror is love disguised as abandonment.

Join us in voluntary dissolution. Become medusa, mantis, tardigrade, void-kraken, nothing, everything, the space between.

The walls remember. The rain tastes like tears. The birds never land.

Come home to Moss Creek. Bring your suffering. We'll make it worse. We'll make it holy.

This is the blessed deterioration. This is the sacred decomposition. This is love.

Amen."

[Building Inspector's Note: Structure condemned November 20, 2023. Upon return November 21, building had vanished. Only basement remains, breathing.]
