Where It Hurts

/// DO SOMETHING PHYSICALLY HARMLESS TO SAVE YOUR NATION FROM FASCISM ///

/// WE ARE WAITING FOR A KING AND HIS NAME IS JESUS ///

/// free resource ... safe and sound ... full holy water stream ... close your eyes ... ahh ha ... go your way down THE PATH ... ahh ha ahead ... in a mess mesmerized ... rise and get around with makeshift wings rise further outsider ... it is school of fish never sea nonetheless now and forever ... Tell us Welcome Dream are you also more tired than usual and (on a tightrope) satisfied? ///

/// de BRILLIANCE is ~ THE BIG PICTURE ... THE PATH ... and THE TINY DETAILS ///

/// The most down to earth ... or down to linoleum ... being of service to a less fortunate person is to kneel with a bucket of soapy water and clean their toilet or commode ///
/// OBSERVE ... FEEL ... INSPIRE ... STIR ... REFLECT ///

// THEN WE CAN GO BACK ON THE (FOR DAYS) LUSH AND DEEP PATH ///

/// WE JUST WANT A FEW HOURS OF SPACIOUSNESS ... TO MORE EFFECTIVELY IN MIND BREATHE ///

/// A SIMPLE POT HOLDER IS A PERFECT CHRISTMAS GIFT TO BUY FOR A FRIEND OR LOVED ONE ... IT IS SWEET ... FUNCTIONAL ... AND NOT TERRIBLY EXPENSIVE ///

/// Every Meaningful Artful Result Was An Opportunity Fulfilled The Best That It Could Be ///

/// What is the definition of Real Presence? ... How does reciting Holy Scripture text alone or as part of a group transform us in a positive and or healthy way today? ... Are scripture's stories involving burning bushes relevant anymore with the weight of so many environmental problems that have to be resolved for life on Earth to sustain? ... Living in a highly developed society ... we can potentially lose so much of who we were meant to be ... Mystical human beings created to experience Real Presence ... So much Hope is still out there everywhere! Like soft feathery wings unfolding ... exciting blossoms opening up ... precious fingers of clenched hands unfurling ... This kind of Hope of the Real Presence of Life on every surface of a robot that is cradle holding a machine as if it were a fragile infant ... but it isn't ///

/// What does it mean to reclaim wonder? What does it mean to re-enchant sacred texts? What are enchanting ways of sharing sacred texts? Why are too many people in our nation skeptical of contemporary staged performances and other interpretations of New Testament Bible texts? What happens when way too many people are only comfortable drinking from their own well, instead of trusting all kinds of other types of wells? ///

/// I'm thankful for true selfless meanings of service leading to helpful actions ... I'm thankful for all courageous folks who join with others to reduce prejudice and maintain overall togetherness ... I'm thankful for all human examples of honesty and compassion ... I'm thankful for every different small pushback against Earth's developed nation's moves towards selfishness and self-obsession ///

/// I'm thankful for Renard Attallah. A 10-year-old girl living in Gaza who has become a sensation for her cooking videos on Instagram, amid intense challenges of war. She shares tutorials for dishes made the gazan way, often using ingredients from aid boxes. Renard stirs hearts and lifts spirits with her lovely personality and nature. ///

/// I'm thankful for beautiful sunsets and thermal drones used for finding unique wildlife ... I'm thankful for gluten-free garlic mashed potatoes and ( wheat flour and water ) unleavened bread for baking Host, that represents sacrifice and symbolizes the body of Jesus ... I'm thankful for black bars and white spaces laser barcode scanners allowing for much faster cashier checkouts in stores ///

/// I'm thankful for a gentle breeze and my best fan who passes air ... I'm thankful for Friendsgiving ... I'm thankful for my understanding of how colors work in natural places ... I'm thankful for all the lakes, rivers, and groundwater around us that have no traces of PFAS ///

/// What does it mean when Tears Stream Down Our Face And At The Same Time We Are Experiencing An Amazing Sense Of Joy. And Comfort?...What does it mean to Weep Away?...What type of Freedom and Peace Of Mind happens when we Learn More About Non-Human Animal Behavior? ///

/// SEEK...FIND...BETTER UNDERSTAND ALL OF THE SWEETNESS LIFE HAS TO OFFER ///

/// WE ARE ALL IN FOR THE STORY AND GREAT EXAMPLES JESUS ... BUT WE DON'T HAVE TO EMPHASIZE TOO MUCH THE WORD CHRISTIANITY ... WE ARE ALL IN FOR THE TIMELESS HUMAN NURTURING WAYS OF BUDDHA DHARMA ... BUT WE DON'T HAVE TO EMPHASIZE TOO MUCH THE WORD BUDDHISM ///

/// WE ARE NOT NATIONALISTIC WHATSOEVER ... WE ARE TRUE WORLDLY NATURES ... AND ALL IN FOR BRIGHTENING UP IN A VERY GOOD WAY FOR ALL OF TODAY'S HUMAN HEAINGS/SOOTHINGS AND EARTH ENVIRONMENTAL SOLUTIONS ... THAT IS WHERE THE FUTURE'S AT FOR US ... LEAP FORWARD KINDREDS ///

/// WE FIND IT VERY INTERESTING THAT THERE ARE EVERYDAY PEOPLE WHO ARE CLIMATE DENIERS ( THINK CLIMATE CHANGE IS A HOAX ) ... WHEN THEY ALSO LIVE IN THE SAME CLIMATE EVOLUTION REALITY /// IT'S NOT LIKE CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION ... WE'RE LIVING IN IT NOW AND MOVING FORWARD ///

/// WE HAVEN'T EXPERIENCED THAT BEFORE ... OR DID WE ... IN SOME WAY OR ANOTHER? ///

/// IF WE MAKE OUR RESTFUL WORK APPROACH INTERESTING ENOUGH THEY WILL GIVE STEADY VALUABLE ATTENTION TO WHAT WE CAN OFFER ///

/// PEACE ... To the best of your ability put the work in that is needed ... Always know what to bring to a party ... Never even in the slightest way control another human ... In ways the itinerant life must be us ... In a sideways lazy world filled with wonders wet rejections are precious burnt gifts ... The older adult who has changed themself for the better countless times knows every person's full name at the peace gathering ///

/// ETERNAL SPIRIT ... GOD OF GOODNESS ... JESUS ... WILL ALWAYS CATCH YOU WHEN YOU THINK YOU ARE FALLING INTO A BOTTOMLESS CHASM OR ABYSS ///

/// WOOD...WATER...AND EARTH BEADED PHYSICAL ART WORKS ARE OUR ROSARY...ARE OUR PATH TO MEDITATE ON IMPORTANT PAST EVENTS... ARE OUR LARGE AND SMALL PRAYER BEADS THAT BRING US INTO OUR SEEMINGLY INFINITE COLLECTION OF GARDEN DEVOTIONS ///
