Calming the Inner Noise

ottootto Life (Anxiety Aid)

I said to myself, we need to be curious explorers,
abnormal surveyors, weird prospectors, and or unusual
sports. And enjoy all of the good things of life. Trying
our best to be a silver and gold chalice instead of a
thin and shiny glass bowl. With a purposeful human
social strategy and master mission. Great Mother Earth Spirit and God in heaven have both given us the
seals of approval, so to speak, to use our mortal
feelings in highly constructive ways. During unmeasured time it is not foolish to sing in a variety of ways,
no more than it is foolish to laugh. Strangers in your
life and your kindred group might feel anxious.
Time permitting attract and communicate to them, in
a gentle way, that it’s okay to express warm chords .
