Warped Plays

The Secret Manual Of Golden Transmutation

Whispered Between Johannesburg's Mine Dumps and Berlin's Kreuzberg Prayers

[To be spoken in the quietest voice that still carries across an empty midnight blue theater. Each word should
arrive like dust motes in stage light, like the call to prayer mixing with subway trains, like gold flaking off old
things.]

FIRST TEACHING: The Window of Perception
(spoken like a lullaby for insomniacs)

Listen... nearer... there is a window. Not any window—THE Window. In Hillbrow, in Neukölln, in the spaces
where children kick walls in seventeen languages.

It must be dirty with:

fingerprints of refugee children who dream in colors they can't name
[whisper softer] tears that taste like the Indian Ocean and the Spree

breath marks from noses pressed against glass, watching, always watching

The Four States of Viewing:
[Let each state echo like footsteps in an empty Gold Reef City mine shaft]

1. The Mystical Gaze: Sssshhh... press palms like pressing palms at the Kotel, at the Hindu temple in
Mayfair, at the Turkish mosque in Kreuzberg. Gold appears as light between your eyelashes. (pause)
Like catching Johannesburg sunset in your throat.

2. The Observant Stare: Let your nose run like Berlin winter, like Joburg dust. See how gold becomes
the color of expired residence permits, of fool's gold in abandoned mines.

3. The Broken Glare: (almost inaudible) Spit... quietly... The gold is now taxi rank puddles, U-Bahn
station stains. This is also prayer.
4. The Anarchist Vision: Draw your symbols in condensation. Write "Amandla" and "Nie wieder" with
the same finger. The gold spreads beneath like mine tunnels, like U-Bahn veins. We are all
underground here.

SECOND TEACHING: The Cascading Streams Technique
(in the rhythm of a praise poem meeting a Yiddish lament)
To create the vertical rivers... ai, ai, ai... the amber light that falls like:

Sacred Method:
[Whisper as if teaching a child to read in a banned language]

1. Breathe: "Modimo painted the world before words existed"
2. Apply gold downward like tears on a photograph, like light through burglar bars

3. Each stream pulses with the heartbeat of night shift workers
4. Blue fragments—scattered like broken Delft, like evening over the mine dumps

Profane Method:
[Hiss like steam from a street vendor's pot]

1. Whisper-scream: "Voetsek!" and "Scheiße!" in the same breath
2. Let piss-yellow drip like load-shedding candlewax

3. Each stream is another queue at Home Affairs, another rejected visa
4. Blue spots are hypothermia and heatstroke kissing

The Hidden Third Method:
[Barely audible] Mix both. Sacred and profane dance the pantsula in the same gold dust.

THIRD TEACHING: The Root Structure
(Like secrets passed between night security guards)

Beneath Johannesburg, beneath Berlin, beneath beneath beneath... listen... The Network spreads.

To Reveal the Roots:
In Innocence: Trace like tracing the mine tunnels your grandfather died in, the subway map your
grandmother memorized fleeing. Cool shadows spread under warm gold like the temperature drop at 2
AM.

In Knowledge: See how roots crack concrete in Soweto, push through Teufelsberg rubble. They're tired,
these roots. Tired like cleaning women on the 4 AM train.

In Anger: (whisper building) Recognize them as underground railroads, as places where sangomas meet
Turkish healers meet Jewish mystics in the between-spaces.

The Secret:
[Let this echo and fade] The roots spread in every language, beautiful and terrible and absolutely...
without... mercy...

FOURTH TEACHING: The Central Mandala
(Spoken like a spell that works in multiple time zones)

At the heart... shhhhhhh... at the very heart... The Dark Bloom waits.

Construction:
[Each line softer than the last]

Begin with shadow (the darkness in gold mines, in bomb shelters)
Ring with ochre (Johannesburg soil) and umber (Berlin clay)

It must simultaneously be:
A church window in Sophiatown (the one they destroyed)

The minaret call at 4 AM
A rabbi's whisper in a hidden shul

The silence between your parents' two languages

FIFTH TEACHING: The Transformation Ritual
(Like a fever dream soundtrack)

[Start at normal whisper, get progressively quieter]

STAGE ONE: Light candles. Shabbos candles, Hindu diyas, whatever burns. Believe your gogo's stories
about the ancestors in the gold.

STAGE TWO: (softer) Notice she calls you by your cousin's name. The one who didn't survive the crossing.
Hear parents fighting in languages that don't translate.

STAGE THREE: (barely there) Realize teacher smells like industrial disinfectant and disappointed dreams.
Understand "uitlander" and "Ausländer" mean you.

STAGE FOUR: (just breath now) Pierce something. Mix languages in your mouth like blood and gold dust.
Every surface is a border. Cross it.

SIXTH TEACHING: The Language of Making
(A polyglot's whispered prayer)

The work requires... come closer... requires:

Your mother tongue: For the base (bone truth)

The colonizer's language: For documents (paper truth)

The street's mixture: For survival (blood truth)

Mix freely: "Angifuni to go kuya divine place, suka!"

SEVENTH TEACHING: Materials of Truthful Craft
(Like reading a shopping list in a dream)

Find in the spaces between:
Gold from teeth and wedding rings at Cash Converters

Blue from police lights and Virgin Mary shrines

Black from load-shedding darkness and Berlin winter 4 PM

Red from... [pause] you know where red comes from

THE ULTIMATE TEACHING
(So quiet the audience must stop breathing to hear)

The golden streams are simultaneously:

Light through the highveld grass

Piss in the corners of Alexanderplatz

The space between "legal" and "human"

Yesterday's prayers in tomorrow's gutter

To master this craft, you must: [Each line fainter]

1. Believe in every god

2. Trust none of them
3. Continue anyway

4. See yourself in every window
5. Let the roots spread...
FINAL WARNING
(Like an echo of an echo)

This manual can only be heard by:

Children who translate for their parents at government offices

Night workers who see the city's soft underbelly

Anyone who's been told to "go back where you came from" in more than one country

Those who pray at the intersection of nowhere and everywhere

[Let this last line circle the theater like smoke]

The golden roots keep spreading beneath everything—under Johannesburg's mine dumps, through
Berlin's ghost stations, beautiful and terrible and absolutely without mercy.

Comme toujours. Njalo. Immer.

[Silence. Then, from the very back of the theater, barely audible:]

Go well, young craftworker. May your windows be dirty with the fingerprints of everyone who came before.

ADDENDUM: Midnight Protocol
(To be breathed more than spoken)

If during practice you:

Hear your grandmother's language: She's becoming memory. This is also craft.

Feel the city's weight: Normal. Both cities. All cities.

Taste gold and coal and copper: You're remembering correctly.

Want to scream: Whisper harder. The walls are thin.

Feel the universe's complete indifference: Extend your passport. Art is documentation.

[Final whisper, from all corners at once:]

The theater is empty. The theater is full. You are alone. You are everyone. Listen...
