Out of Sight

Hahnu Youn is an innocent eighty-three-year-old elder indigenous nomad who has migrated through many places on this planet Earth during her long life. Sacred Spirits gifted her the ability to fully comprehend many different earth languages, including the global lingua franca English. Her outer surfaces are now weathered like an elder tortoise, seasoned by firsthand experiences with extreme weather, continuously empathetic throughout her long life, and refreshingly innocent

She carries questions about individualism, consumerism, historical colonial ties, neocolonialism, modern human identity, the true definition of success, how codependent human social expectations link to artificial materials and products, cultural exchange patterns, how advertising and media reinforce artificial consumerism stories, and how big oil and gas corporate industries create highly manipulative propaganda to further advance their own economic prosperity.

Hahnu Youn asks ~ What does this mean?

"America's lame attempt to replicate a massive mycelial network as a political system. And mysterious individual and mutual lost pieces, not yet known benefits for Mother Earth. As you can probably obviously notice, we are having a very difficult time sleeping at night."
