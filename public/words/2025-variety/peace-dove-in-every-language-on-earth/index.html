Peace Dove in Every Language on Earth

Words / 2025 Variety

You understand what you understand. In other tongues, the same bird flies through sounds you'll never parse. The rest dissolves into sound. Seven thousand languages still spoken. Each one naming the same white wings differently, as if peace itself shifts shape at each border. One bird. Its meaning scattered like seeds across every human tongue. Listen. Each translation holds a different shape of hope. Some languages have no separate word for peace—it lives folded inside their words for morning, for breathing, for the space between heartbeats. When the last speaker dies, that particular dream of peace vanishes forever.
