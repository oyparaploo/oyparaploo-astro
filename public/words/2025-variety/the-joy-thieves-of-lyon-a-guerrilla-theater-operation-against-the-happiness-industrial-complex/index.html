## **ARCHITECTURE OF RESISTANCE**

## **ACT ONE: The Factory of Stolen Delights**
*Location: Les Halles de Lyon Paul Bocuse (after closing)*

The market transformed into a "Joy Factory" where happiness is packaged and sold.

**DAWN** (*45, former marketing executive, now joy insurgent*):
I used to sell happiness.
Not make it. Sell it.
Package wonder into purchase-sized portions.
Transform free sunsets into vacation packages.
Turn friendship into social media metrics.

*Shows Powerpoint of joy commodification*

Slide 1: Child laughing (free)
Slide 2: "Happiness Workshop" ($400)
See the markup?

**TIKEN** (*28, they/them, escaped the gender binary*):
They told me my joy had to pick a shelf.
Men's joy: competitive, loud, purchased through victory.
Women's joy: decorative, quiet, purchased through beauty.
But my joy is feral. It shops in no aisles.

*Starts painting their face with both "masculine" and "feminine" joy symbols*

**CHIWON** (*67, Korean grandmother, memory keeper*):
I remember when kimchi was joy, not product.
Every grandmother's batch different.
Now: standardized, packaged, priced.
They stole our fermentation time and sold it back as convenience.

*Opens jar after jar of identical commercial kimchi*

All same. All dead. All efficient.
No grandmother's hands. No waiting. No surprise.

**KUTI** (*22, architecture student from Senegal*):
In Dakar, our buildings curve like dancing.
Here, everything is straight. Efficient. Profitable.
They straightened our joy into productivity lines.

*Projects images of curved African architecture vs. straight European buildings*

Your eyes are starving for curves.
Your joy is dying in rectangles.

**AUGUSTUS** (*39, reformed banker*):
I counted money for twenty years.
Never counted heartbeats.
Measured portfolios.
Never measured breath.
Rich in numbers, bankrupt in sensation.

*Empties briefcase full of shredded money*

This confession costs nothing.
First free thing I've given in decades.

## **IMAGISTIC AMPLIFICATIONS OF THE JOYFUL BODY**

### **The Anti-Capitalist Cell Collective**
Your cells operate on gift economy. The heart gifts oxygen-rich blood asking nothing in return. The lungs gift oxygen without invoicing. Your body is 37 trillion communists successfully cooperating. You are walking proof that capitalism isn't "natural."

### **The Gender-Fluid Nervous System**
Your neurons don't have gender. They fire for flowers and football equally. Your synapses don't care if joy comes from "masculine" or "feminine" sources. At the cellular level, you are already non-binary. Your joy receptors are pansexual.

### **The Memory River System**
Your hippocampus doesn't store memories like files but like water - constantly flowing, mixing, reforming. Every recall changes the memory slightly. You're not a hard drive but a river. Letting go isn't loss; it's flow.

### **The Curve Recognition Complex**
Your eyes have specialized cells that respond to curves - edge detectors that fire more actively for rounded shapes. Capitalism's straight lines literally under-stimulate your visual cortex. Your eyes are anatomically biased toward nature's curves.

### **The Threshold Reset Mechanism**
Your brain performs "event boundary processing" at doorways - literally resetting your neural state. Every passage is a micro-reincarnation. Capitalism turned doorways into efficiency ports, but your brain still treats them as transformation chambers.

### **The Unexpected Joy Cascade**
Surprise triggers norepinephrine release, enhancing memory formation. Unexpected joy literally carves deeper neural pathways than predicted pleasure. Your brain is designed to prefer surprise to safety. Predictability is neurological poverty.

### **The Commons of Breath**
You breathe 11,000 liters of air daily, sharing molecules with every breathing being. The oxygen in your lungs was in someone else's yesterday, will be in trees tomorrow. Breath is the ultimate commons - uncommodifiable, shareable, essential. Every breath is borrowed and gifted simultaneously.

### **The Free Movement Economy**
Your proprioceptors generate joy signals from simple position changes. Standing after sitting, stretching after stillness - each transition triggers pleasure chemistry. Movement joy requires no equipment, no membership, no purchase. Your body generates wealth from geometry changes.

The revolution is cellular.
The resistance is anatomical.
The uprising is biological.
Your body already knows how.
