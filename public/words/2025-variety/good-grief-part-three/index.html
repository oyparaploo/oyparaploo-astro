Poem · 1 minute

This softer ... more wondering way ... feels truer ... there's something about this kind of tentative approach ... this admission that we're all still learning what our feelings mean ... Perhaps ... maybe ... we are always in the process of understanding and creating more room for our receivers to bring their own grief into a space ... This type of love is less like being told what something means and more like being invited to discover meaning together ... We are sometimes confused ... searching for something ... puzzling through language and emotion in real time ... Human softness is true to grief work ... Grief doesn't arrive with clarity but instead with bewilderment ... This voice makes more room for natural uncertainty
