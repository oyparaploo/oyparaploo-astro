**The Beautiful Garbage We Become**

Words / 2025 Variety

*Letter to a Stranger Finding Pieces*

---

Thirty years of picking up broken things. Still learning: everything is already broken. The crack is where light enters.

Life accumulates. Dreams we left. People who left us. Words that can't return to our mouths. The person at 35 who embarrasses the one at 65. Behind us, a trail of beautiful garbage.

I thought transformation meant cleaning up.

But maybe we're made from the mess itself, gently rearranged. The spiral carries everything forward—different altitudes now. That mistake from long ago? Still there. Composted. Feeding something unimaginable then.

Watch what happens:

Dishes become prayers. A dropped mask reveals a face. Death teaches instead of ending. The paths not taken walk beside the one that was.

*

The monk sits still for decades.
The driver never stops moving.
Same search.

Water held in careful palms.
Water escaping through fists.
Same water.

*

Each return to familiar ground—old fear, old loss, old question—adds a layer. Glass picked up at 35 carries different light at 65. This isn't failure. This is how understanding grows: one revolution at a time.

What if we're already where we need to be? In the workshop where broken things get interesting. Where garbage transforms. Where spirals remember what circles forget.

Maybe there's no future transformation.

Maybe it's this. Now. These pieces arranging and rearranging. Small deaths. Smaller resurrections. Something unexpected emerging from the pile.

Beautiful garbage. Holding light in the cracks.
