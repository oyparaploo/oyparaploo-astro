Making a Living

Mystery of Disposable and Obsolete Human Employment (Part One)

From mineral to derivative ... from human need to financial instrument ... from physical reality to simulation ... from simulation to pre-emptive elimination ... capital achieves its thousand-year goal of escaping material constraints ... The torch trembles in a grip and suddenly wild love breathes ... but love is not a market efficiency ... Humans don't get exploited in this system ... They get categorized as inefficiency and removed ... Not through violence but through irrelevance ... This isn't future speculation ... The infrastructure is being built now ... Cinema is where shadows hear a choir of footsteps ... Stone coins remember sweeping the burning flesh of man ... when money needed bodies to move it ... Every efficiency gain is a step toward human economic obsolescence ... Every successful automation is proof of concept ... The hands of an accountant's clock are too large for mortal time ... No silver linings ... just the mechanics of replacement operating at scale ... Darkness can point to answers that we don't know how to ask for ... but the questions are already deprecated ... We are the beautiful garbage now ... The accountant's clock has already struck
