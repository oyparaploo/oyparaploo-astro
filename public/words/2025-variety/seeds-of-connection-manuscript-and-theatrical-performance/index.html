**Instructions for Creating "Seeds of Connection" - A Theatrical Performance**

Words / 2025 Variety

## Core Concept
Transform the audience's perception of growth and interconnection by creating a living, breathing performance that moves like mycelial networks between indoor and outdoor spaces, where five performers embody different stages of transformation.

## Character Assignments & Movement Patterns

**Bertrand** - The Dormant Seed
- Begin curled in fetal position center stage
- Movement quality: contained tension, micro-trembles suggesting internal potential
- Costume: Layered fabrics that can be shed throughout performance
- Sound signature: Deep hums, heartbeat rhythms (*ba-dum ba-dum*)

**Jeanne** - The Water Element/Erosion Force
- Fluid, persistent movement patterns
- Carries vessels of actual water, creating *drip drip drip* soundscapes
- Traces spiral patterns around other performers
- Costume: Flowing blues and silvers that catch light

**Francioz** - The Underground Network
- Moves primarily on ground level, creating physical connections between performers
- Uses long ribbons or ropes to literally connect characters
- Whispers "fragments" of text to audience members
- Costume: Earth tones with visible root-like attachments

**Zebee** - The Light Seeker/Photosynthesis Dream
- Vertical movement emphasis, always reaching upward
- Carries portable lights, mirrors, or reflective surfaces
- Creates "green tongue" moments through emerald scarves or projections
- Costume: Gradients from dark to bright green

**Emily** - The Consciousness Observer/Time Keeper
- Moves between all spaces, sometimes backwards
- Rings small bells (*ping ping ping*) at transformation moments
- Breaks the fourth wall to speak directly to audience about "NOW NOW NOW"
- Costume: Prismatic, color-shifting materials

## Spatial Design & Movement Flow

### Act I: Indoor Germination (20 minutes)
1. Begin in complete darkness with only breathing sounds
2. Bertrand's first *CRACK!* illuminates the space
3. Other performers emerge from audience, not from backstage
4. Create "neural network" formations using bodies and ribbons
5. Build to cacophony of overlapping text fragments

### Transition: The Breaking (5 minutes)
- House lights suddenly full
- All performers freeze except Emily
- Emily guides audience outside while speaking about "necessary breaking"
- Sound of actual seeds cracking played through hidden speakers

### Act II: Outdoor Expansion (25 minutes)
1. Performers already positioned at various heights (if possible: trees, platforms)
2. Audience discovers them in tableau
3. Francioz has created a ground pattern with natural materials
4. Movement becomes increasingly synchronized
5. Culminates in a "forest dance" where audience is invited to join

### Return: Integration (10 minutes)
- Slow procession back inside
- Space transformed with projections of microscopic/cosmic imagery
- Performers exchange costume pieces, becoming hybrid beings
- End with all five creating a human mandala, breathing as one

## Technical Elements

**Sound Design:**
- Layer natural sounds with electronic processing
- Use contact microphones on performers' bodies
- Install speakers in unexpected places (under seats, in trees)
- Create "purple hope" moments with specific harmonic frequencies

**Lighting:**
- Begin with single pinpoint, expand to full wash
- Use UV lights for "invisible connection" moments
- Outdoor section uses sunset/natural light as dramaturgy
- Shadows as important as light

**Props & Materials:**
- Actual seeds for audience to hold
- Water in various containers
- Stretchy fabric to show expansion/contraction
- Mirrors to fragment and multiply images
- Soil (safely contained) for sensory moments

## Audience Interaction Protocols

1. **Seeding Moments:** Give each audience member a seed packet upon entry
2. **Touch Permissions:** Performers may gently touch audience members' shoulders (with pre-show consent)
3. **Whisper Networks:** Francioz passes messages through audience
4. **Movement Invitations:** Zebee teaches simple gestures audience can mirror
5. **Collective Breathing:** Emily conducts group breathing exercises

## Performance Notes

- Embrace neurodivergent time: scenes can expand/contract based on energy
- If something "breaks" (prop, sequence), incorporate it as transformation
- Text should feel discovered, not memorized
- Overlap languages if possible (French fragments?)
- "Mistakes" are mutations - keep them
- End time should align with actual sunset if possible

## Safety & Accessibility

- Clear pathways for all movement abilities
- Offer stationary viewing option for outdoor section
- Provide sensory bags with seeds, textured fabrics
- Sign language interpreter as sixth performer
- Quiet spaces available for regulation

## Final Integration

The performance succeeds when audience members feel they ARE seeds, experiencing their own potential for transformation. The indoor/outdoor journey mirrors the darkness-to-light growth pattern, while the five performers demonstrate that breaking open together creates forests of possibility.

Remember: *Everything connected by invisible mycelial thoughts...*

# "Seeds of Connection" - Theatrical Dialogue

## Act I: Indoor Germination

### Opening Darkness

**BERTRAND** *(from center, barely audible)*
flesh holds... holds... tiny dormant universes in muscle fiber...

**EMILY** *(from among audience)*
*ping ping ping* against bone walls... time isn't linear in seed-space...

**JEANNE** *(water sounds beginning)*
*drip... drip... drip...*

**BERTRAND** *(louder)*
everything happening NOW and THEN and WILL BE...

**FRANCIOZ** *(whispering to audience members)*
armillaria ostoyae... biggest living thing is underground... did you know? connected... all connected...

### First CRACK!

**BERTRAND**
CRACK!

*(Light explodes as Bertrand unfurls)*

**ZEBEE** *(emerging, reaching)*
photosynthesis dreams in green tongues... chlorophyll prayers climbing climbing...

**EMILY**
necessary breaking... necessary opening... spilling light everywhere...

**ALL** *(overlapping)*
can't contain can't contain CAN'T CONTAIN...

### Neural Network Formation

**FRANCIOZ** *(creating physical connections)*
thread-like roots = neural networks = fungal highways = cosmic web =

**EMILY**
all same pattern repeating fractally into infinity...

**JEANNE** *(to specific audience member)*
water knows the way... erosion is love is patience is TIME...

**ZEBEE**
hope tastes purple sometimes... did you know?

**BERTRAND** *(to another audience member)*
who am I when I'm no longer what I was but not yet what I'll be?

### Building Cacophony

**ALL** *(fragments overlapping, increasing speed)*

**FRANCIOZ**: underground whispers... mycelial thoughts spreading spreading...
**JEANNE**: Mother Earth's heartbeat... ba-dum ba-dum ba-dum...
**ZEBEE**: green tongues licking light addiction...
**BERTRAND**: suspended animation of growth...
**EMILY**: zoom in zoom out doesn't matter... seeds within seeds within seeds...

## Transition: The Breaking

*(House lights sudden full. All freeze except Emily)*

**EMILY** *(to audience)*
Listen... bones know what brain forgets... ancient seed memories encoded in mitochondria... Come. The breaking open is the prayer... staying closed is the fear...

*(Leading audience outside)*

Sometimes weeds are medicine... sometimes medicine is poison... depends who's swallowing...

## Act II: Outdoor Expansion

### Discovery Tableau

**ZEBEE** *(from height, arms spread)*
Light! Light addiction spreading through veins like chlorophyll fire!

**FRANCIOZ** *(from ground pattern)*
Every footstep plants invisible seeds... you're walking through my neural garden...

**JEANNE** *(pouring water in spirals)*
circular like ouroboros eating its own consequences...

**BERTRAND** *(now partially transformed)*
I was seed... am becoming... will be forest...

### Synchronization Builds

**EMILY**
kindness seeds scatter like dandelion wishes... *whoooosh*...

**ALL** *(breathing together)*
*whoooosh*...

**JEANNE** *(teaching audience)*
Press ear to earth... listening with whole body...

**FRANCIOZ** *(spreading ribbons to audience)*
take hold... we're all armillaria ostoyae... one organism pretending to be many...

**ZEBEE**
or many pretending to be one?

**BERTRAND**
perspective shifts like tectonic plates...

### Forest Dance Emergence

**EMILY** *(conducting)*
reaping sowing reaping sowing... but what about diagonal reactions? perpendicular consequences?

**ALL** *(with audience, movement and voice)*
seeds in flesh
in muscle
in memory
in trauma
in joy
in tomorrow
in yesterday
in NOW NOW NOW...

**ZEBEE**
everything has rhythm even silence...

**JEANNE**
even stillness dances...

**FRANCIOZ**
even separation connects...

## Return: Integration

### Procession Inward

**EMILY** *(leading)*
returning but different... elevated... or descended? direction is relative when you're spinning...

**BERTRAND** *(to audience members)*
you carry forests now... in your pocket seeds... in your breathing...

### Final Transformation

*(Inside, with projections. Performers exchanging costume pieces)*

**FRANCIOZ**
I am root becoming leaf...

**ZEBEE**
I am light becoming shadow...

**JEANNE**
I am water becoming breath...

**BERTRAND**
I am seed becoming dream...

**EMILY**
We are one becoming many becoming one...

### Human Mandala

**ALL** *(softly, as one breath)*
precious precious PRECIOUS...
not like uranium... dangerous potential...
but like grandmother's locket...
held close
cherished
protected...

**EMILY** *(final words)*
and the seeds dream of forests...

**ALL**
while forests dream of seeds...

*(Breathing continues as lights fade to single point, then darkness)*

## Performance Notes for Dialogue:

- **Overlap Instructions**: Words in italics can be whispered to individual audience members
- **Physical Scores**: Each line should have accompanying movement
- **Repetition Freedom**: Performers can repeat any phrase that resonates in the moment
- **Language Play**: Replace some words with sounds, gestures, or other languages
- **Audience Echo**: Invite audience to repeat certain phrases, especially "NOW NOW NOW"
- **Volume Dynamics**: Range from barely audible whispers to full-voice celebrations
- **Silence Spaces**: Build in breathing moments where only environmental sounds exist

*Remember: The dialogue is a seed itself - let it crack open differently each performance*
