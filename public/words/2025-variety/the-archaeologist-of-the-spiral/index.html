The Archaeologist of the Spiral

Following the Debris Trail

← Words / 2025 Variety

Someone was here before us, gathering.

Found: fragments arranged in a pattern. Starting with death—always death—as if whoever left this understood that endings teach us how to hold things. Light grip. Water through fingers. That kind of wisdom.

The trail spirals upward. Can't walk it straight. Have to follow its curve.

*

"The many paths," they wrote.
Beetle tracks. Salmon routes.
A thousand ways forward at once.

Someone tried to count them all. The numbers stop mid-page.

*

Then the fragments turn sharp.

Truth-telling, they called it. Masks dropping. Two people in a room where pretense finally cracks. The pieces here are still warm. Same footprints, over and over, deeper each time.

*

Bicycle tracks appear.

Thin lines through the wreckage. Whoever rode this saw it all—the tire scream, particles in the air you shouldn't be able to see. Someone pedaling slow while the world goes fast. Taking notes. Watching.

*

Here's where the rage piles up.

Someone stood watching conversation break under the weight of too-much-too-fast. Wrote: anger isn't separate from speed. Anger IS speed, suddenly conscious, looking at its own reflection.

Thick layers here. Years of watching. Years of knowing.

*

The debris gets beautiful here.

Not pretty—beautiful. Glass catching light wrong. Things left thirty years, ready to be something else.

A note, written repeatedly:
"What is ours to do?"
Different handwriting each time. Like the question kept forgetting how to spell itself.

*

Door fragments. Recent.

An opening that wasn't, then was. Mind-door. Heart-door. Where assignments wait.

Someone wrote "magnificent pieces."
Crossed it out.
Wrote "beautiful garbage."
Left the crossing visible.

*

Last fragment, almost gone:

"The spiral remembers what the circle forgets."

Then nothing. Bicycle tracks heading somewhere I can't see yet but know is there—where fragments stop being pieces and start being whole.

*

I'm just here to notice:

Someone was here. Gathering. Getting ready for something.
The debris trail is the art.
The gathering is the work.
The spiral keeps going up.

What is ours to do?

The question floats there.
The door won't close.
The fragments wait like they know something
we don't yet.
