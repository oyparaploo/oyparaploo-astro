Joy Uprisings

THE JOY TRANSMISSION EXPERIMENT

A Physical Revolution Against Proxy Happiness

Les Subsistances, Lyon

PRE-SHOW ARCHITECTURE

48 Hours Before: Audiences receive biometric wristbands tracking their screen time vs. physical movement. Data becomes part of performance.

Arrival: Two entrances - "SCREEN DOOR" (moving walkway past infinite monitors) or "BODY DOOR" (trampoline pathway through hanging fabrics).

ACT ONE: The Diagnosis

Space: Half the theater is filled with screens showing joy videos (kittens, laughing babies, fails). Other half contains a real trampoline, sunflowers in pots, sensory materials.

OHO (50s, former YouTube addiction specialist, bouncing slowly on trampoline):
I studied 10,000 hours of people watching joy.
Never saw one person's pupils fully dilate.
Screen joy is masturbation without orgasm.
All buildup, no release.

Shows audience's collective screen time from wristbands: "2,847 hours of watched joy this week"

XIMENA (30s, screens strapped to her body, playing videos):
I am 47 tabs of happiness!
Infinite scroll of delight!
Why move when others move for me?
I've seen every combination of baby-plus-animal.
My dopamine receptors are Teflon.
Nothing sticks.

SECKOU (20s, Senegalese dancer, moving constantly):
In Dakar, we have a saying:
"The screen dances so you don't have to."
But screens don't sweat.
Sweat is joy leaving the body to make room for more joy.

Performs West African dance, biometric data shows his endorphin spike on screens

FRANK (40s, hasn't left chair in years):
I watch Olympic gymnastics while sitting.
Extreme sports while lying down.
Dance videos while completely still.
I'm a joy vampire.
Feeding on others' endorphins.

MULATU (70s, Ethiopian, plays actual instruments while videos of music play):
The difference between watching music and making it?
Strikes drum
Your cells vibrate with this.
Points to screen
Your cells observe that.
Vibration is participation.
Observation is starvation.

ACT TWO: The Transmission Problem

Screens begin showing the audience themselves from hidden cameras

XIMENA:
Look! You're on screen now!
Are you feeling joy watching yourself not feeling joy?
Meta-suffering!
The future of entertainment!

OHO (brings out kittens - actual kittens):
Experiment time.
Half of you watch the screen kittens.
Half play with real ones.
Let's measure joy.

Biometric data shows massive difference

SECKOU:
Your mirror neurons are firing at 20% capacity for screen joy.
Touch that kitten?
100% plus overflow into neighboring neurons.
Joy spillage. Joy excess.
That's wealth.

FRANK (finally standing):
My legs are screaming.
Not pain screaming.
"We exist!" screaming.
I forgot I had legs.
Screens don't require legs.

ACT THREE: The Sunflower Trampoline Solution

Crew wheels in more trampolines, places sunflowers everywhere

MULATU:
In Ethiopia, children make toys from nothing.
Wire becomes cars.
Boxes become drums.
Bodies become instruments.
Lyon children need trampolines in sunflower fields?
Your joy has become very expensive.

OHO:
But it works. Watch.

All performers begin bouncing

SECKOU (while bouncing):
Gravity negotiation!
Every jump is a conversation with Earth!
"May I leave?"
"Yes, but come back."
"I will!"

XIMENA (struggling to bounce while watching screens):
Can't... watch... and... bounce...
Brain... choosing... body!
Screens... losing!

Throws screens away, bounces freely

FRANK (bouncing for first time):
My vestibular system is having a religious experience!
My proprioceptors are singing hymns!
Is this sustainable?!

ALL:
Gravity is renewable!
Balance is rechargeable!
Sunflowers are solar panels for joy!

ACT FOUR: The Dozen Alternatives

Each performer demonstrates a sustainable joy architecture

OHO: Night swimming in the Rhône - bioluminescent algae planted for the occasion!

XIMENA: Building snow furniture in Parc de la Tête d'Or!

SECKOU: Dancing in Carrefour at midnight - security footage becomes art!

FRANK: Blindfolded tree hugging in Parc de la Croix-Rousse!

MULATU: Drumming on bridges - each bridge in Lyon has different acoustics!

Audience votes on which to try

ACT FIVE: The Physical Revolution

All screens turned off. Audience given choice: stay seated or join physical activities

OHO:
Your body kept score while you watched.
Zero points.
Time to play.

80% of audience joins

XIMENA (to those still seated):
You're watching others have joy.
That's tonight's metaphor.
How's it feel?

SECKOU (leading group dance):
Joy is only viral physically!
No username required!
No subscription needed!
Just cells agreeing to celebrate!

FRANK (bouncing with audience):
I'm going to be sore tomorrow!
Sore is evidence!
Sore is receipt!
Sore is "I was there!"

MULATU (drumming as audience jumps):
In Ethiopia we say:
"The drum remembers every hand that hit it."
Your body remembers every joy you lived.
Not watched. Lived.

Finale: Biometric data shows audience's collective endorphins higher than entire week of screen watching

INTERNATIONAL DESTINATION ELEMENTS

The Lyon Joy Prescription
- Audiences receive medical-style prescription for physical joy
- Must complete 5 activities before leaving Lyon
- Stamped "Joy Passport" becomes souvenir

The 48-Hour Transformation
- Biometric tracking shows chemical changes
- Published as "The Lyon Protocol"
- Insurance companies recognize as preventive medicine

The Seasonal Variations
- Summer: Rhône swimming integrated
- Winter: Snow sculpture competitions
- Spring: Sunflower planting ceremony
- Fall: Leaf pile jumping orchestrated

Celebrity Joy Ambassadors
- Athletes demonstrate their training joy
- Musicians play while audience bounces
- Your joy data compared to theirs

IMAGISTIC AMPLIFICATIONS OF THE JOYFUL BODY

The Vestibular Symphony
Your inner ear contains three semicircular canals filled with endolymph fluid. When bouncing, this fluid sloshes in patterns creating a "vestibular symphony" - your balance organs literally playing music only your brain can hear. Trampolines turn your head into a concert hall.

The Proprioceptive Map
You have 70+ proprioceptors per muscle reporting position. During physical joy, they fire in patterns creating a "body GPS trace" - a 3D map of your joy journey. Screen watching creates flatline. Physical play creates topography.

The Endorphin Cascade
Physical joy triggers beta-endorphin release - 18-33 times more potent than morphine. Your body is a pharmacy that only opens for actual movement. Watching others move is like watching someone else take medicine.

The Mirror Neuron Starvation
Mirror neurons need completion cycles - see action, do action. Screen watching breaks this loop, creating "mirror neuron blueballs" - activation without release. Physical mimicry completes the circuit, creating what neuroscientists call "embodied resonance."

The Gravity Dialogue
Every jump creates gravitational waves (infinitesimally small but real). You're literally making ripples in spacetime. Sitting still = gravitational silence. Bouncing = cosmic conversation.

The body knows the difference between witnessed and lived joy. It keeps different books, pays different dividends, remembers different stories.

Lyon becomes the city where you trade your screen time for trampoline time, your watched joy for lived joy, your proxy pleasure for direct delight.

The revolution is physical.
The uprising is vestibular.
The future is embodied.
