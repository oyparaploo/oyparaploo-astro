ottootto Play ( Introduction 4 )

← Writings / 2025

Tides flood. Tides go out and never return. The state
of matters, by all practical purposes, at some places is
insane. You don’t have to be a remarkable super
human being to ride the crest of a wave. It’s easier
and better when you’re not going so fast. We have
countless recordings that are also passings. Glimpses
for visitors to our encounters. We love it! Yield, now squeeze past the narrow space brown revered angel.
Bub don’t get too close to the work or you will, after drinking it all in, become a constant - volume - tongue.
That shortly thereafter begins to float around in the
mode of a khaki wildflower garden butterfly. Streaming
imprint in mind, that has remarkable staying power
for even the most frail elder couples, bachelors and
spinsters. The neat, unkept and rough that keep going.
