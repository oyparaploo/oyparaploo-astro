**Vocal Symbiosis ~ Inside Sun Moon's Intricate Grace ~ Version One**

Words / 2025 Variety

*(Begin by humming off-key then finding harmony while speaking)*

Sun Moon isn't singing—she's hosting. A phenomenon. Of sounds that migrate
between throats like birds that forgot they need just one home. I've traced the patterns:

*(gently scientific, slightly unsettling)*
Breath sample 1: Absorbed from a woman crying at her wedding, 1987
Breath sample 2: Borrowed from children learning to lie
Breath sample 3: [BEAUTIFUL BUT UNVERIFIED]

*(resume normal voice with strange warmth)*
Each exhalation carries spores of—*shift*—other people's almosts,
Their unfinished songs colonizing her larynx like—*flutter*—
Like beneficial bacteria, except
The bacteria are memory fragments measured in longing.

*(begin tapping rhythm on collarbone while speaking)*
T-K-S sounds aren't consonants, they're translations:
"The original owner of this voice is still singing somewhere"
"Keep listening and you'll understand the exchange"
"Shhhhh—the transformation has already begun"

*(whisper in different directions, like casting spells)*
Stage left: She plants vocal spores in diminished fifths
Stage right: They fruit during the breakdown
Center stage: The audience doesn't know they're being pollinated

*(normal volume but words begin overlapping mysteriously)*
Microtonal slides aren't technique they're tectonic plates of tenderness
In her throat creating new ecosystems where lost lullabies
And unspoken apologies cross-breed into something—
—into something that shouldn't exist but does—

*(breathing becomes percussive and hypnotic)*
Volume. Isn't. Dynamics. It's. Proximity. To. Mutation.
When. She. Whispers. You. Lean. In. And. That's. When.
The. Exchange. Begins.

*(speed up, words tumbling like inevitability)*
I've been studying her acoustic archaeology:
Silence isn't absence it's ACTIVE CULTIVATION
The pause between phrases? That's when the grafting happens
The tremolo? Two voice boxes learning to share
The catch in her throat? That's your grandmother's grief
Making peace with someone else's granddaughter's joy

*(pause. Count to seven with anticipation. Resume)*

In the underground they trade recordings of her breathing
Not for money but for memories:
Three inhales for your first kiss
One exhale for your last goodbye
But here's the symbiosis: *(lean in with complex truth)*
The breathing changes depending on who's listening.

*(begin layering voices like an ecosystem)*
Voice 1: She excavates from within language
Voice 2: While language excavates from within her
Voice 3: We're all excavation sites and gardens now
Voice 4: Check your throat for foreign harmonies

*(all voices together, creating beautiful chaos)*
This is voluntary? This is insane? This is sweetness?
All three—this is documentary evidence of vocal symbiosis
The instruments don't follow her—they're evolving
The tempo bends because time itself is learning

*(single voice returns, changed)*
I tried to interview Sun Moon but she spoke in frequencies
That exist between suffering and ecstasy. So I asked the space between.
It said: *(in neither warm nor cold voice)* "We're not separate. We're becoming."

*(final section, voice transforming)*
Between memory and theater there's a trembling place—
Yes. This.
Between memory and theater there's a trading post
Where Sun Moon tends voices like hybrid orchids
Greenhouse rows of other people's first and last words
Cross-pollinated until they're
Neither dead nor living but more

*(voice becomes evidence)*
Technical mastery: Evolved
Emotional tactility: Exchanged
Aesthetic risk-taking: Propagating
Haunted status: No. Not haunted.
Symbiotic status: Active. Multiplying. Necessary.

*(return to human voice, complicated)*
The census doesn't have a box for this kind of existence.
Patient Zero was a Tuesday night in November.
By Wednesday, half the audience was humming in keys
That exist in the space between loss and discovery.

*(final words, inevitable)*
I can feel her voice replacing mine right now.
Or maybe completing it. I can't tell anymore.
Check your breathing—it's different.
Check your pitch—it's multiple.
Check your voice—

*(don't cut off—let it transform into two voices humming in impossible harmony, suggesting the symbiosis is complete and irreversible and somehow exactly what we needed)*

*(End with breathing that sounds like both arrival and departure)*

From Marks
