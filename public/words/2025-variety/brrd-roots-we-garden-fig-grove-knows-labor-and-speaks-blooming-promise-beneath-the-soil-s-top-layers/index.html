Brrd Roots ~ WE GARDEN ~ Fig Grove Knows Labor and Speaks Blooming Promise Beneath the Soil’s Top Layers

Words / 2025 Variety

The fig trees hum—not loudly, not in words, but in a vibration deep enough to settle into the soles of Maélys’ bare feet. The ground stirs beneath her as though something **is shifting inside the roots**, threading itself upward, unfurling a message too tangled for language.

She presses her palm flat against the dirt.
*It breathes beneath her touch.*

The fig grove is **not patient today**.
It does not whisper. It *insists*.

Sap thickens in its veins. The branches quiver slightly, **not from wind but from remembering**. The trees know something **she does not yet understand**—something that pulses beneath the soil in **braille she cannot read but can feel**.

A ripe fig **drops without warning**, thudding softly into the earth beside her knee. She does not pick it up. She watches. **She waits.**

The fig **splits itself open**.

The scent swells—fermented, sweet, *insistent*.

And then, beneath the fig’s thick perfume, another smell rises. **Something older. Something unearthed.**

She exhales.

Somewhere deep beneath her fingertips, the roots shift again.
And this time—
**they speak.**
