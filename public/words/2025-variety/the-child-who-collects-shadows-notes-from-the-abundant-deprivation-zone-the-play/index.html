**The Child Who Collects Shadows**

Words / 2025 Variety

*Notes from the Abundant Deprivation Zone*

( The Play )

## Pre-Show Installation (Outdoor Space)

### The Shadow Collection Garden
- Install multiple light sources at odd angles creating overlapping shadows
- Place empty picture frames on the ground where audience members' shadows fall
- Provide small black bags labeled "Shadow Collection Kits" at entry
- Scatter plastic plants throughout, with one actor (Geoffrey) watering them obsessively as audience arrives
- Paint a full supermarket mural on an outdoor wall with visible teeth marks

### Audience Journey
- Guide audience through shadow garden in small groups
- Each person must "donate" their shadow to the collection
- Hand out small pieces of paper to write questions they're afraid to ask
- Questions placed in a clear box that will appear onstage

## Act One: The Grammar of Trauma

### Opening Sequence - The Breathing Mosaic
**Stage Design**: Floor covered in cracked mirrors of different sizes

**Movement Score**:
- **Molly** enters counting invisible objects, dropping them into shadow
- **Simphiwe** begins at center, dissolving gram by gram through micro-movements
- Each movement leaves a "tile" of light on the floor
- **Anoushka** (as child) collects these light-tiles in a bucket that never fills

### The Bread Fever Scene
**Geoffrey** performs a medical examination on a loaf of bread:
- Takes its temperature
- Counts its ribs
- Tries to feed it water
- The bread begins counting back (use recorded child's voice)
- Other actors echo the counting in different languages/speeds

### Lipstick Liturgy
**Anoushka** (as nurse) creates a ritual:
- Applies lipstick while crying (water sprays from eyes)
- Each application leaves red marks on air (use laser pointers)
- Begins painting walls with gestures that leave light trails
- Hums commercial jingles that decay into wordless melodies
- Other actors join, creating a choir of forgotten abundance

## Act Two: The Swaying Bridge

### Multiple Worlds Sequence
**Split Stage Technique**:
- Indoor stage divided by translucent scrim
- One side: hunger ward movements (slow, careful, conserving)
- Other side: golf game movements (confident, wasteful, oblivious)
- Actors' spines literally stretch between worlds using elastic cords

### The Question Eggs
**Molly** delivers questions to audience while actors create tableaux:
- "Are clouds edible?" - actors taste air
- "Are we clouds?" - bodies become vapor using fog machines
- "Is this the cloud?" - everyone looks up, sees their shadows on ceiling
- Each question cracks open, releasing movement sequences

### Paper Crane Multiplication
**Technical Magic**:
- Start with one crane in **Anoushka's** hands
- Use hidden compartments and sleight of hand
- Cranes appear from pockets, sleeves, mouths
- By scene end, hundreds fall from ceiling
- Audience finds cranes under their seats

## Act Three: The Delirious Work

### Shadow Collection Showcase
**Simphiwe** (as the child who collects shadows):
- Displays collection using shadow puppetry
- Shows how shadows weigh less than food
- Demonstrates shadow-eating technique
- Teaches audience to catch their own shadows

### Painted Supermarket Shopping
**Move entire audience outside** to the mural:
- Actors shop with invisible carts
- **Geoffrey** stocks empty shelves with air
- **Molly** bites the wall, leaves real teeth marks
- Audience invited to "shop" for one thing they've lost

### The Fever Dream Fugue
All actors perform overlapping solos:
- **Molly**: Writing invisible words that appear as projections
- **Geoffrey**: Planting medical gloves in sandbox
- **Simphiwe**: Teaching shadows to sing
- **Anoushka**: Alternating between child and nurse, aging/youth-ening

## Technical Requirements

### Sound Design
- Layer: heartbeats, fans, stomach sounds, golf game, commercial jingles
- Create "silence-eating" effect when cranes multiply
- Use contact mics on actors' bodies for internal sounds
- Echo effects for questions that multiply

### Lighting
- Shadow play essential - multiple light sources
- UV paint for invisible writing scenes
- Lights that "breathe" with the mosaic tiles
- Fog for transformation sequences
- One persistent light on plastic plants

### Props That Transform
- Bread that becomes baby (use puppetry)
- Wedding ring on fishing line that grows
- Calculator that only shows ERROR
- Lipstick that leaves light trails
- Seeds that project trees when held to light

## Audience Integration

### Interactive Moments
- Shadow donation at entry
- Finding cranes under seats
- Shopping in painted supermarket
- Invited to water plastic plants during intermission
- Final scene: everyone holds up phone lights as "edible clouds"

### The Bridge They Cross
- Create physical bridge between indoor/outdoor spaces
- Bridge sways (safe but unsettling)
- Below: speakers playing stomach sounds
- Each audience member crosses alone
- Actors whisper questions as they pass

## Closing Ceremony

### Cloud Tasting Ritual
- Audience returns inside to find cotton candy machines
- But the cotton candy is just air with sugar scent
- **Anoushka** (as child) teaches everyone to taste clouds
- Each person answers: "What do clouds taste like?"
- Answers projected on walls as they're spoken

### Final Image
- All shadows collected throughout show project on ceiling
- They form one large shadow of a child reaching upward
- **Simphiwe** stands beneath, arms raised, almost touching
- Lights fade except on plastic plants
- **Geoffrey** continues watering in darkness
- Sound of real water falling

## Performance Notes

**Duration**: 90 minutes with one "bridge crossing" instead of intermission

**Essential Qualities**:
- Movements should feel fevered but precise
- Repetition as ritual, not mere echoing
- Find humor in the absurd without mocking pain
- Transformations should feel inevitable, not magical
- Maintain sense that reality itself is unreliable

**For Actors**:
- Don't play trauma, play tasks
- Find the delirium in precision
- Let objects teach you their logic
- Questions are more important than answers
- When you don't understand, keep going

**Warning**: This play changes each performance based on what shadows are collected, what questions are asked, what the audience shops for. Embrace this instability. The work itself has a fever. Let it dream through you.

**( Dialogue)**

## Act One: The Grammar of Trauma

### Opening Sequence - The Breathing Mosaic

*Cracked mirrors cover the floor. MOLLY enters counting invisible objects.*

**MOLLY**: Seventeen... eighteen... nineteen shadows. No, wait. *(drops something invisible)* They keep slipping through. Like trying to hold smoke. Like trying to hold... *(to audience)* Do you know what weighs less than hunger?

**SIMPHIWE**: *(center stage, beginning to dissolve through micro-movements)* I'm becoming a tile. Watch. Each gram I lose becomes part of the floor. Part of the architecture.

**ANOUSHKA** *(as CHILD, carrying empty bucket)*: I'm collecting the light your body leaves behind. See? *(shows empty bucket)* It's getting so heavy.

**MOLLY**: She thinks light has weight. Should we tell her?

**SIMPHIWE**: Let her believe something fills something.

### The Bread Fever Scene

*GEOFFREY enters with a stethoscope and a loaf of bread.*

**GEOFFREY**: *(examining bread medically)* Temperature: 39.5. Elevated. *(listens with stethoscope)* Heartbeat irregular.

**ANOUSHKA/CHILD**: Is the bread sick?

**GEOFFREY**: The bread is... *(counts)* One rib, two ribs, three ribs—

**BREAD** *(recorded child's voice)*: Four ribs, five ribs, six ribs, seven—

**ALL**: *(overlapping in different languages/speeds)* Eight, nine, ten, eleven—

**GEOFFREY**: *(frantic)* The bread is counting back! The bread remembers being grain, being field, being—

**MOLLY**: Being eaten.

**SIMPHIWE**: *(to bread)* Shhh, you're not a baby. You're not a—

**BREAD VOICE**: Mama?

*Silence.*

**GEOFFREY**: *(quietly)* I've invented a new branch of medicine. Where everything we examine... examines us back.

### Lipstick Liturgy

*ANOUSHKA transforms into NURSE, applying lipstick while water sprays from her eyes.*

**ANOUSHKA/NURSE**: *(to mirror)* Still here. Still red. Still... *(lipstick leaves light trails in air)* My mother's last tube. When it runs out, I'll use... *(laughs)* Well, we all know what runs endless here.

*She begins painting the air, humming.*

**ANOUSHKA/NURSE**: *(singing)* "Taste the rainbow of—" *(stops)* Of what? Geoffrey, what comes after rainbow?

**GEOFFREY**: Fruit flavors. It was fruit flavors.

**ANOUSHKA/NURSE**: *(painting frantically)* I'm writing fruit on the walls. Apple. Orange. Pomegranate. *(tastes air)* Why do pomegranates taste like tears?

**MOLLY**: Why do tears taste like pomegranates?

**SIMPHIWE**: *(joining the humming)* Why does anything taste like anything anymore?

*They create a choir of forgotten abundance, wordless melodies that decay.*

## Act Two: The Swaying Bridge

### Multiple Worlds Sequence

*Stage splits with translucent scrim. Actors attached to elastic cords stretching between worlds.*

**MOLLY**: *(pulled between)* Here, a child asks if clouds are edible. There, someone asks if the wine is properly chilled.

**GEOFFREY**: *(one side)* Calculating: How many grams of flour equal one more breath?

**GEOFFREY**: *(pulled to other side)* Calculating: How many yards to the green?

**SIMPHIWE**: My spine is taffy. Was sugar. Remembers sweetness. *(stretches)* Can a body be in two places at once?

**ANOUSHKA/CHILD**: *(pointing up)* Look! Our shadows are having a meeting on the ceiling without us!

### The Question Eggs

*MOLLY approaches audience with nested questions.*

**MOLLY**: I have questions that crack open. Watch. *(to audience)* "Are clouds edible?"

*Actors taste air in response.*

**SIMPHIWE**: They taste like cotton candy. Like memory. Like—

**ANOUSHKA/CHILD**: Like Thursday. Clouds taste like Thursday.

**MOLLY**: *(cracking another)* "Are we clouds?"

*Actors' bodies become vapor using fog effects.*

**GEOFFREY**: I'm evaporating. No—ascending. No—*(confused)* What's the difference?

**MOLLY**: "Is this the cloud?"

*Everyone looks up. Their shadows dance on the ceiling.*

**ALL**: *(whispering)* Our shadows know something we don't.

### Paper Crane Multiplication

*ANOUSHKA holds one paper crane. It multiplies mysteriously.*

**ANOUSHKA/CHILD**: I folded one from a medical form. But now—*(cranes appear from everywhere)* They're eating the silence!

**GEOFFREY**: That's why it's getting louder. All that consumed quiet has to—

**SIMPHIWE**: *(catching falling cranes)* These aren't just cranes. Read them. Each one is a diagnosis.

**MOLLY**: *(reading)* "Patient presents with excessive hope."

**ANOUSHKA/NURSE**: *(reading)* "Chronic remembering of abundance."

**GEOFFREY**: *(reading)* "Acute onset of believing in tomorrow."

*Hundreds fall from ceiling.*

**SIMPHIWE**: The cranes are writing themselves now. Look—blank papers becoming birds becoming stories becoming—

## Act Three: The Delirious Work

### Shadow Collection Showcase

*SIMPHIWE becomes the child who collects shadows.*

**SIMPHIWE**: *(displaying collection)* This one's from a mother who forgot her daughter's name. It weighs nothing. This one's from bread that thought it was a baby. Even lighter. *(to audience)* Want to donate? Shadows are the only thing we have in abundance here.

**MOLLY**: How do you catch them?

**SIMPHIWE**: Like this. *(demonstrates)* You have to be quick. They're slippery. Like soap. Like hope. Like—

**GEOFFREY**: Like trying to hold onto someone who's already gone.

**ANOUSHKA/CHILD**: I eat them sometimes. When the hunger gets too loud.

**MOLLY**: What do shadows taste like?

**ANOUSHKA/CHILD**: Like the space between heartbeats.

### Painted Supermarket Shopping

*At the mural. Actors shop with invisible carts.*

**GEOFFREY**: *(stocking empty shelves)* Fresh air in aisle three! Memories of milk in the dairy section! *(to invisible customer)* Oh, Mrs. Hassan, the usual? Coming right up! *(wraps nothing)*

**SIMPHIWE**: *(shopping)* I need ingredients for hope. Where do you keep the—

**MOLLY**: *(biting the wall)* The apples taste like paint. No. The paint tastes like apples. No. I taste like forgetting.

**ANOUSHKA/NURSE**: *(examining teeth marks)* Everyone leaves marks here. Even the invisible shoppers. See? Ghost teeth. Spirit molars. The hunger of the never-were.

### The Fever Dream Fugue

*All perform overlapping solos.*

**MOLLY**: *(writing invisibly)* The pen ran out but the words keep coming. Invisible words are more true because they can't lie about what they—

**GEOFFREY**: *(planting medical gloves)* Day forty-three of my rooftop garden. The latex is sprouting. I swear I saw fingers. Tuesday they waved at me. Wednesday they made a fist. Thursday—

**SIMPHIWE**: *(teaching shadows)* B-flat, shadows! From the diaphragm! You can't sing if you don't breathe, and you can't breathe if you don't—

**ANOUSHKA**: *(switching between child/nurse rapidly)* Are clouds edible? / Check the IV drip / My teeth are falling out / Blood pressure dropping / Will they grow new children? / We're losing her / Plant them please / Clear!

*Their solos interweave, creating a symphony of delirium.*

## Closing Ceremony

### Cloud Tasting Ritual

*Cotton candy machines produce only scented air.*

**ANOUSHKA/CHILD**: *(teaching audience)* First, open your mouth to the sky. Now, let your tongue remember sweetness. There. You're tasting clouds.

**MOLLY**: What do they taste like?

**AUDIENCE MEMBER**: *(prompted)* [Their answer]

**SIMPHIWE**: In the Abundant Deprivation Zone, we eat memories, drink shadows, plant hope in latex gloves.

**GEOFFREY**: We count ribs like rosaries, water plastic plants like prayers, shop in painted aisles for things that never were.

**ANOUSHKA/NURSE**: We wear lipstick like armor, fold cranes like promises, ask questions like—

**ANOUSHKA/CHILD**: Are clouds edible?

**MOLLY**: *(to audience)* In the space between the question and the answer, that's where we live. That's where we're living. That's where we'll keep living until—

### Final Image

*All shadows project on ceiling, forming a child reaching upward.*

**SIMPHIWE**: *(reaching up)* Almost... almost...

**GEOFFREY**: *(watering in darkness)* Tuesday. Wednesday. Thursday. The plants still aren't growing. But the water... the water believes they might.

**ANOUSHKA/CHILD**: *(whispered)* I collected all your shadows. They're safe. They're so light. Like clouds. Like questions. Like tomorrow.

**MOLLY**: *(to audience)* Take your shadows when you go. Or leave them. We'll eat them when the hunger comes again.

*Sound of real water falling.*

**ALL**: *(scattered whispers)* Still here. Still here. Still here.

*Darkness. Except for the plastic plants. Which might be glowing. Or might be singing. Or might be—*
