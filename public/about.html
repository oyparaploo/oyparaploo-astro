      The Reach(ing) is the Chime

      Beginning with Forgiveness — of the maker's work or play, of a bad day ... Blame set down at every door or passage ... What's offered is Mercy ... or belief that anything incomplete still deserves a hand ... We prefer to support rather than go too soon to criticizing ... Every piece and performance was created while proving worth — and the proof came from required effort ... We always want to help ... reaching toward problems with sleeves rolled up ... All of it done with a loose and friendly notion of Unity — cooperation between writing and drawing ... maker and who enters ... Effort put toward common ... We the People. Sized down to a front porch and fresh squeezed glass of timeless lemonade-ah ... What this whole practice hopes to sound like is absolute Harmony ( with a capital H ) ... Shared responsibility to be a little kinder than each day demands of us.

      Built in the open.

      Brian William Otto, also known as Reach Chimes — writer and multidisciplinary artist in the Twin Cities, Minnesota. Born 1959. His work and play move in the register of Wonderful Garbage + occasionally Noir Chanson. Decades of living and arriving at proof ... gathered here under the name Paraploo.
