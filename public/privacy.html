We don't collect your data.

This site is an archive of writing and pictures. We are not interested in who you are, where you came from, or what you look at. There is nothing here that tracks you.

What happens when you browse

The site uses no analytics, no tracking scripts, no pixels, no fingerprinting. We do not profile visits and we do not sell or share anything about them, because we have nothing to sell or share.

Where the pages come from

The pages are plain files served by Cloudflare. Like every hosting company, Cloudflare keeps short-lived server logs — the address your connection comes from, the page asked for, the time — to deliver pages and stop attacks. We do not read these logs and they are not used to identify you. Cloudflare's own privacy notice covers what it keeps and for how long.

Typefaces

The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser asks Google's servers for the font files, and Google sees your connection address in that request. Google says it does not use these requests for anything else.

Music players

On the Favorite Songs pages the songs play through Spotify players. Those players are Spotify's, not ours; they may set their own cookies and note that you visited, under Spotify's privacy notice. If you would rather they did not, you can block third-party cookies in your browser. The rest of the site has no players.

Search

The site search runs inside your browser. What you type is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Our own remembering

The site sets no cookies of its own. The one thing it remembers is your choice of Light, Dark or Auto, which is stored in your browser and never sent to us. Clear your browser's site data and it is gone.

If you write to us

There is no newsletter, no mailing list and no form. If you write to hello@paraploo.com, your message arrives in a Gmail inbox, so Google handles it under Google's privacy notice. We keep your message only as long as it takes to reply and for our own records of the conversation. We do not add you to anything, and we do not pass your address on.

Outside links

The site links to other people's sites. What they collect is their business and is covered by their notices, not this one.

Children

Nothing here is made for children, and we knowingly collect nothing from anyone, children included.

Your rights

If you have written to us and want to know what we hold, want it corrected, or want it deleted, write to hello@paraploo.com and we will do it. That is the whole procedure.

Changes

If any of this changes, this page changes, and the date below changes with it.
