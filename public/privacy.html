We don't collect your data.

We are a small organization based in the Twin Cities, Minnesota. This site doesn't track you, profile you, or sell anything having to do with your visit. If you write to us, we keep your email only to write back. That's it.

What happens when you browse our website

Nothing much. The site is plain HTML pages hosted on Cloudflare. There's no mechanism to track you. We don't run Google Analytics or any tracking scripts. No pixels. No fingerprinting. No heat maps of where your eyes went. The pages load and they're yours alone.

Fonts and players from other companies

The typefaces come from Google Fonts, so Google sees that a page asked for them. The song pages carry players from Spotify; when a player loads, Spotify can set its own cookies and collect data about your visit. We can't control what they do on their end. If this concerns you, most browsers let you block third-party cookies. The writing and pictures on the site work the same either way.

If you write to us

The site has no newsletter and no forms. If you write to hello@paraploo.com, we keep your message to answer it. We don't sell or share any email.

Our site doesn't use cookies

The site's own pages set no cookies. If you see one, it came from an outside player or service, not from us. The one thing the site remembers is your Light, Dark or Auto choice, kept in your own browser and never sent to us.
